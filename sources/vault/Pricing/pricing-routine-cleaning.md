## 📈 Financial Benchmarks
* **Target Net Profit Margin:** 65% - 70%
* **Ideal Crew Size:** 1 - 2 Cleaners
* **Estimated Time:** 1 to 1.5 hours per 1,000 sq. ft.

> FLAG (C-CL01-08, X-19): the "Target Net Profit Margin" line above is an internal, unapproved target with no cost basis. "Net" profit implies an overhead allocation that no file defines, and which margin basis applies (gross, contribution or net) is undecided (Q-28-13). It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements ([28](../AI%20Development%20Plans/28-pricing-estimating-and-job-costing.md) section 8; spec FIN-007). The "Estimated Time" line does not say whether it counts person-hours or crew-elapsed time, and no wage or loaded labor cost rate exists (owner facts 2026-10-02). Added 2026-10-03.

## 📐 Pricing Tiers & Calculation Methods

### Method A: Flat Rate by Layout (Recommended for Consistency)
* **Studio / 1 Bed & 1 Bath:** $90 - $120 *(300 - 450 SAR)*
* **2 Bed & 2 Bath:** $130 - $160 *(500 - 650 SAR)*
* **3 Bed & 2 Bath:** $170 - $210 *(700 - 900 SAR)*
* **4 Bed & 3 Bath+:** $230 - $300+ *(1,000 - 1,300 SAR)*

> FLAG (C-CL01-01, C-CL01-09): below-minimum risk. The "Studio / 1 Bed & 1 Bath" range above lies wholly below the $150 job minimum and the "2 Bed & 2 Bath" range starts below it. FIN-001 applies at estimate level: the estimate applies the minimum or records an authorized exception (spec BIZ-003, FIN-001, CX-001); the owner's proposed default counts the minimum once per separately quoted job or visit bundle, never per line (owner facts 2026-10-02). "(Recommended for Consistency)" is not an owner approval, and which method governs which job is open (Q-28-20). The SAR figures in brackets are legacy artifacts, not prices or conversions; they are never imported or converted ([28](../AI%20Development%20Plans/28-pricing-estimating-and-job-costing.md) PRC-002, PRC-039). No price is changed here. Added 2026-10-03.

### Method B: Hourly Billing (Best for Custom or Unkempt Homes)
* **Base Hourly Rate:** $35 - $50 / hour *(150 - 250 SAR / hr)* per cleaner.
* *Rule:* If a home takes 3 hours with 2 cleaners, total billable hours = 6 hours ($210 - $300).

> FLAG (C-CL01-08, C-CL01-09): the "Base Hourly Rate" is a customer billing rate per cleaner, not a wage or loaded cost rate ([job-costing playbook](../Skills/bookkeeping%2C%20taxes%2C%20profitability%2C%20and%20cash/handyhub-job-costing/references/playbook.md)); hourly quantities are person-hours, as the "Rule" line counts them. Short hourly jobs compute below the $150 job minimum, so FIN-001 applies at estimate level. A switch to hourly billing after the estimate is accepted needs an approved change order ([28](../AI%20Development%20Plans/28-pricing-estimating-and-job-costing.md) section 3.3; spec FIN-008). What counts as a "Custom or Unkempt" home, and whether such homes start with CL-02 instead, is an owner decision (Q-04-06; Q-28-20). The SAR figure is a legacy artifact (C-CL01-09). Added 2026-10-03.

### Method C: Square Footage Billing
* **Standard Rate:** $0.08 - $0.12 per sq. ft. *(3 - 5 SAR per sqm)*.

> FLAG (C-CL01-08, C-CL01-09): "Standard Rate" is a benchmark, not an approved rate, and the area basis (which floor area is measured) is not stated; area is measured in square feet with its basis stated per item ([28](../AI%20Development%20Plans/28-pricing-estimating-and-job-costing.md) section 3.3). Small homes priced by area compute below the $150 job minimum, so FIN-001 applies at estimate level. The per-square-metre SAR rate is a legacy artifact: there is no square-metre unit and no automatic unit or currency conversion at launch (28 PRC-039). Added 2026-10-03.

## 🔄 Frequency Discounts (الاشتراكات الدورية)
- [ ] **Weekly Service:** Deduct 20% from the base flat rate.
- [ ] **Bi-Weekly Service (Every 2 weeks):** Deduct 15% from the base flat rate.
- [ ] **Monthly Service (Every 4 weeks):** Deduct 10% from the base flat rate.

> FLAG (C-CL01-01): minimum evaluated per visit pending Q-28-04 ([28](../AI%20Development%20Plans/28-pricing-estimating-and-job-costing.md) D-28-07). Under that PROPOSED default each recurring visit is a job, and these deductions push more layout tiers below the $150 job minimum. Whether a discount may take a visit below the minimum, and whether it is applied before the minimum comparison, is undecided (Q-28-03, Q-28-08). The deductions name only the "base flat rate", so they are undefined for Methods B and C, and no authorization rule or estimator limit exists (spec EST-004, PERM-004; 28 PRC-019). They are price items of a recurring plan, not ad hoc discounts; "Every 4 weeks" is not a calendar month, so plan periods must state which they mean (28 section 3.8; Q-28-18). The Arabic text in the heading is a legacy artifact (C-CL01-09). Added 2026-10-03.

## 🚨 Surcharges & Add-ons
- [ ] **Pet Hair Surcharge:** +$25 flat per visit (covers extra vacuum filter clearing and time).
- [ ] **High Clutter Fee:** +$30 flat if staff must organize excessive toys/clothes before dusting.

> FLAG (C-CL01-05, X-11): the "Pet Hair Surcharge" and "High Clutter Fee" lines above are unapproved and appear in no agreement, while the agreement's lockout and late-cancellation fees appear nowhere in this file. A fee is used only once it is in the owner-approved fee schedule and shown on the estimate and in the agreement (28 D-28-09, PRC-021; Q-28-07). The clutter fee is decided on site and "excessive" is undefined: a condition found on site changes the price only through a change order the customer approves before the extra work (spec FIN-008, JOB-005; 28 D-28-08, PRC-020), and organizing toys and clothes is outside the agreement's checklist scope. Surcharges are add-ons and must not create a second minimum (04 CL-01 "minimum charge behavior"). The pet surcharge prices extra time; it is not a pet-safety control - pets are a card hazard handled in the [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop%20Routine%20Cleaning%20Checklist.md) (spec CL-01.HAZ; X-11). Added 2026-10-03.
