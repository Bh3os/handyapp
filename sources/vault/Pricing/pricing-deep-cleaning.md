## 📈 Financial Benchmarks
* **Target Net Profit Margin:** 50% - 60% (due to heavy chemical use and extended hours)
* **Ideal Crew Size:** 2 - 3 Cleaners
* **Estimated Time:** 2.5 to 3.5 hours per 1,000 sq. ft.

> FLAG (C-CL02-11; X-19) - "Target Net Profit Margin" line: internal target, unapproved, with no cost basis; "net profit" implies an overhead allocation that no file defines, and the reason given ("heavy chemical use and extended hours") has no chemical allowance behind it (Q-28-13). It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; spec FIN-007). "Ideal Crew Size": the upper end exceeds the two-employee launch team, and the owner is not counted as a worker without confirmation (owner facts 2026-10-02; Q-28-02). "Estimated Time" does not say whether it means person-hours or crew-elapsed time (job-costing playbook: "Distinguish wage rate from customer billing rate and crew elapsed time from person-hours."). Values unchanged.

## 📐 Pricing Tiers & Calculation Methods

### Method A: Square Footage Billing (Safest Industry Standard)
* **Standard Range:** $0.20 - $0.30 per sq. ft. *(8 - 12 SAR per sqm)*.
* *Example (2,000 sq. ft. home):* 2,000 x $0.25 = $500 total charge.

> FLAG (C-CL02-11) - "Safest Industry Standard" in the Method A heading is an unsourced claim: no vault file sources it, and the estimate playbook says "never invent an industry-standard price". It is not for customer use and does not make Method A the governing method; Method B carries a competing label ("Highly Recommended for First-Time Clients"), and which method governs which job type is an owner decision (Q-28-20). FLAG (C-CL02-13): for small units the per-square-foot range computes below the job minimum and below the Method C "Small Apartment" floor; reconcile with Method C - owner decision (spec BIZ-003, FIN-001; arithmetic in [04 Service registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#CL-02) "minimum charge behavior"). The SAR and per-square-metre figures on these lines are legacy artifacts (C-CL02-12). Values unchanged.

### Method B: Hourly Billing (Highly Recommended for First-Time Clients)
* **Base Hourly Rate:** $50 - $70 / hour *(250 - 350 SAR / hr)* per cleaner.
* *Note:* Never give a flat price over the phone for a deep clean if the house hasn't been cleaned in months. Use hourly instead.

### Method C: Flat Rate Estimates by Property Size
* **Small Apartment (Under 1,000 sq. ft.):** $200 - $300 *(800 - 1,100 SAR)*
* **Medium House (1,000 - 2,500 sq. ft.):** $400 - $600 *(1,500 - 2,250 SAR)*
* **Large Luxury Villa (2,500 - 5,000 sq. ft.):** $700 - $1,200+ *(2,600 - 4,500 SAR)*

- [ ] **Blinds Hand-Wiping (Per Window):** +$15 per window blind.

> FLAG (C-CL02-01; C-CL02-10) - SCOPE CONFLICT - included vs add-on undecided: the main plan's process list and the SOP include oven and refrigerator interiors, the main plan includes wall washing, and the SOP includes blinds and wiping inside vanity drawers and cabinets, while this menu sells them as add-ons. The agreement's "Add-ons Approved" checkboxes cover only the oven, fridge and empty cabinets, with no box for wall washing or blinds. Until the owner decides (Q-28-21; task-inclusion matrix, 04 SVC-011), each add-on is shown on the estimate as a separately acceptable option (spec EST-005; RECOMMENDATION of 02 C-CL02-01). Whether an add-on can be booked on its own, and how the job minimum then applies, is an open owner decision; add-ons on a deep clean must not create extra minimum charges (04 CL-02 "minimum charge behavior"). "Gasket sanitization" is claim wording that needs an approved product label before any customer use ([32 Website and customer experience](../AI%20Development%20Plans/32-website-and-customer-experience.md) legal row L5). The Arabic text in the menu heading is a legacy artifact (C-CL02-12). Values unchanged.
