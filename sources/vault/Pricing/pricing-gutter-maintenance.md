## 📐 Linear Pricing Breakdown

### Multi-Tier Gutter Cleaning & Flush Packages
* **Single-Story Ranch Home (Up to 150 linear feet):** $150 - $225 flat.
* **Two-Story Suburban Family Home (Up to 250 linear feet):** $275 - $400 flat.

> FLAG (C-HI06-01): the two-story package above is pending the height/access policy ([02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) G-005); the SOP is on hold. It is not sellable until that policy is approved and the SOP passes competent review. Pending owner and qualified safety professional review (Q-31-09).

### Premium Micro-Mesh Gutter Guards (All-In Supply & Install)
* **Rate:** $5.50 - $7.50 per linear foot.
* *Example (150 linear feet home):* 150 x $6.50 = **$975 total project price** (Includes full complimentary deep flush clean before guard fastening lines are locked down).
