## 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Regional Square Foot Average:** $0.20 - $0.35 / sq. ft.
* **Minimum Equipment Trip Charge:** $150 minimum billable allocation.
* **Target Margin:** 75%

> FLAG (C-HI03-06, X-18) - "Minimum Equipment Trip Charge" line: competing minimum - owner decision (Q-28-06). The label reads as a trip fee that could be added on top of a tier price, while the spec treats \$150 as the minimum job value (BIZ-003, CX-001, FIN-001). The owner's proposed default applies the minimum once per separately quoted job/visit bundle before tax and not to every line (owner facts 2026-10-02); a multi-service visit must not receive several minimum charges (estimate-assistant playbook; 28 D-28-05, PROPOSED). The agreement's separate dry-run cancellation fee has no line here; no-work fees are a fee-schedule decision (Q-28-07). Not a customer charge until approved.

> FLAG (X-19) - "Target Margin" line: internal and unapproved, with no cost basis; the file does not say whether it is a gross, contribution or net margin or a markup. It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; FIN-007; Q-28-13).
