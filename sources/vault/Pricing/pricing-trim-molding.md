## 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Finish Carpentry Baseline:** $4.50 - $9.00 / linear foot for installation.
* **Target Margin:** 60% (Requires precise compound miter cuts, nail filling, and flawless caulk prep lines).

> FLAG (C-HI09-09): the "Finish Carpentry Baseline" range above does not contain the range of the "Custom Crown Molding" rate line below. Whether this headline is a summary of the line rates or a separate quoting rate is an owner decision (Q-28-01); like every figure in this file it is an unapproved benchmark. No price changed.

> FLAG (X-19): the "Target Margin" line above is an internal, unapproved target with no cost basis and no margin definition. Never show it in customer documents, field views or agreements (source: 28 section 8; spec FIN-007). Its rationale wording "flawless caulk prep lines" is internal and must not be reused as a customer promise (C-HI09-04). Owner and CPA decision (Q-28-13).

## 📐 Per-Linear-Foot Finish Trim Matrix

### Standard Baseboards & Door/Window Casings (Up to 4-inch profiles)
* **Rate:** $4.50 - $6.00 per linear foot (Labor only).

> FLAG (C-HI09-02): baseboards and door/window casings are priced on the line above, but the SOP has steps only for crown molding on wavy ceilings and wainscoting box panels, so neither is covered by an SOP; casings are also missing from the agreement scope. Whether both are offered is an owner scope decision, with the attorney for the agreement scope. No price changed. (source: SOP option headings; agreement "Scope Details" clause; 31 section 10 HI-09)

### Custom Crown Molding (Single-Piece Ceiling Layouts)
* **Rate:** $7.00 - $10.50 per linear foot (Labor only).
* *Why:* High ceiling ladder adjustment logistics and complex upside-down compound angle cuts.

### Wainscoting / Accent Box Framing Panels (Priced flat per accent wall face)
* **Rate:** $350 - $600 per standard wall area face (Includes design grid, adhesive bond, and pin nail locks).
