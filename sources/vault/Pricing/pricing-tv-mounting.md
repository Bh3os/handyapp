## 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Standard Mount Average:** $125 - $185 (Client supplies TV and bracket frame)
* **All-In Turnkey Pricing:** $190 - $275 (You supply premium tilt/fixed bracket)
* **Target Margin:** 70%

> FLAG (X-22; C-HI04-10): the two benchmark lines above assume different bracket suppliers ("Client supplies TV and bracket frame" versus "You supply premium tilt/fixed bracket") - customer-provided item rule pending (X-22; 29 section 9.1); no line prices bracket supply or markup (Q-28-14). The "Target Margin" line is an internal, unapproved target with no cost basis and no stated margin or markup basis; it must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; FIN-007; C-HI04-10). Owner and CPA review.

The "Commercial Wall" wording has no commercial-work policy behind it (X-23).

> FLAG (C-HI04-06; C-HI04-05; C-HI04-11): the "In-Wall Low-Voltage Wire Hiding" line covers low-voltage cables only, and only where permitted (spec HI-04.MAT "low-voltage pass-through components if permitted"); it never includes line-voltage work (spec HI-04.SAF "do not perform unlicensed line-voltage work"; C-HI04-06).
