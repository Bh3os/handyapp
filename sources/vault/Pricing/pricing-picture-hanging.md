## 📐 Flat-Rate Matrix by Artwork Demographics

### Standard Frames (Up to 16"x20" / Under 10 lbs on Drywall)
* **Rate:** $35 - $50 per single frame.
* *Bundle:* 5-Frame Gallery Wall Grid = $180 - $250 flat.

### Oversized / Heavy Items (Large Mirrors, Heavy Canvases 20–50 lbs)
* **Rate:** $110 - $160 per item.
* *Note:* Includes heavy-duty zinc toggle bolts and dual-anchor laser balancing.

> FLAG (C-HI01-02): load claim - manufacturer ratings only. The weight band in the heading above and the "heavy-duty zinc toggle bolts" in the note describe a price band and hardware, not a rated capacity. An item's hardware capacity comes from the manufacturer's rating for that weight and substrate, recorded for the job; items above the SOP's own weight bands stop and escalate ([SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop-picture-hanging.md) section 12). No rating is added here. Added 2026-10-03.

### Masonry Premium (Mounting onto Raw Brick / Concrete Fireplaces)
* **Rate:** Add **+$50 flat surcharge** per item to cover hammer-drill bit wear and specialized sleeve anchors.
