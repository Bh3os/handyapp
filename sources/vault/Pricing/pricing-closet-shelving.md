## 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Average System Install Surcharge:** $75 - $125 per linear foot of shelving layout if client buys the kit.
* **Target Margin:** 65%

> FLAG (C-HI08-04): the per-linear-foot "Average System Install Surcharge" above prices the same client-supplied-kit job as the hourly "Base Rate" and its "Flat Rate alternative" below, and no rule says which basis governs; "linear foot of shelving layout" is undefined. Owner decision (Q-28-20); no price changed.

> FLAG (X-19): the "Target Margin" line above is an internal, unapproved target with no cost basis and no margin definition. Never show it in customer documents, field views or agreements (source: 28 section 8; spec FIN-007). Owner and CPA decision (Q-28-13).

## 📐 Structural Pricing Scenarios

### Client-Supplied Modular Kits (e.g., ClosetMaid, IKEA, Elfa tracks)
* **Base Rate:** $85 / hour (Minimum 2 hours billable baseline).
* **Flat Rate alternative:** $150 per standard reach-in closet | $350 - $550 per large walk-in master storage grid.

> FLAG (X-22): client-supplied kits are customer-provided products: a job line only, never HandyHub stock (source: 29 section 3.2). Before installation the worker records their visible condition, model and quantity (source: spec START-009); the acceptance, defect and liability rule for customer-supplied products is pending attorney review (Q-28-14; 29 section 9.1).

> FLAG (X-18): "Minimum 2 hours billable baseline" is a service-level minimum next to the company \$150 job minimum, and a multi-service visit must not receive several minimum charges. Keep it as minimum billable hours, make it a service floor or drop it: owner decision (Q-28-06; 28 section 4.4).

### Custom Heavy-Duty Garage/Pantry Slats (You supply lumber/shelves + labor)
* **Rate Matrix:** $25 - $45 per linear shelf tier face installed.
* *Inclusions:* Multi-wall laser alignment mapping, stud anchoring, and weight load capacity certification.

> FLAG (C-HI08-01): "weight load capacity certification" - do not use - attorney and safety review (C-HI08-01; 28 PRC-041). It conflicts with the agreement's "Load Rating Disclaimer" and with the spec's load controls (spec HI-08.SAF "confirm load path/fastener rating ... do not overload wall/substrate"); no SOP step tests or certifies loads.

> FLAG (C-HI08-05): the HandyHub-supplied lumber and shelves of this scenario have no SOP step, tool list or material cost line, and whether wooden or custom shelving is offered at all is an owner scope decision. "linear shelf tier face" is undefined (Q-28-20).
