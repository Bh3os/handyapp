* **Post Replacement Average:** $175 - $275 per post hole.
* **Minimum Structural Repair Call-Out Fee:** $200 minimum balance.
* **Target Margin:** 60%

> FLAG (C-HI10-03, X-18) - "Minimum Structural Repair Call-Out Fee" line: competing minimum - owner decision (Q-28-06). Unapproved. The line sits under a market-benchmark heading, so the file does not establish whether it is a HandyHub rule or a market reference; read as a HandyHub rule, it competes with the \$150 job minimum (spec BIZ-003, FIN-001, CX-001), and "minimum balance" leaves open whether it is a floor or an additive fee. The owner's proposed default applies one minimum per separately quoted job or visit bundle, never per line (owner facts 2026-10-02); fence repair combined with PT-02 staining on one visit must not receive two minimum charges (estimate-assistant playbook; 28 section 4.4). Not a customer price; value unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner.

> FLAG (X-19, C-HI10-08) - "Target Margin" line: internal and unapproved, with no cost basis; the file does not say whether it is a gross, contribution or net margin or a markup (Q-28-13). It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; spec FIN-007). The agreement names this file as its scope, which would expose this line to the customer (C-HI10-08; see the review notes). Value unchanged.

## 📐 Flat-Rate Component Breakdown

### Leaning Post Stabilization (Full excavation + Plumb + New Concrete Collar)
* **Rate:** $185 - $250 per individual 4x4 wood post hole.
* *Inclusions:* Digging out base, temporary braces, 2 bags red-bag concrete, water flush bar compaction.

> FLAG (C-HI10-V01) - "Leaning Post Stabilization" block: no person-hours, crew size or production rate. The SOP's cure wait before the braces come off implies a wait on site or a return trip that no line prices (28 section 7.1; 04 HI-10 labor assumptions). The concrete quantity in the "Inclusions" line has no stated basis against the SOP trench or the product's yield, and the bracing lumber and post level this work needs are not in the inventory (C-HI10-V01). Excavation is subject to the SOP's utility-locate and property-authorization hold, and a firm quote for digging or structural work needs a site assessment (28 section 5.3; 04 HI-10 estimating method). Owner decision; no price changed.

### Horizontal 2x4 Structural Rail Replacement
* **Rate:** $45 - $65 per single 8ft or 10ft rail section (Includes pressure-treated lumber + heavy galvanized structural hanging clips).

### Privacy Picket Rot Swaps
* **Rate:** $15 per individual picket replaced (Minimum order of 5 pickets or combined with post/rail jobs).

> FLAG (C-HI10-06) - "Privacy Picket Rot Swaps" line: no approved procedure - pending SOP. Picket replacement is priced here, named in the agreement ("picket repair services") and promised in the main plan, but no SOP step covers it; it is not schedulable until a procedure is drafted and competently reviewed, and offering, defining or withdrawing it is an owner decision. The "Minimum order" wording is an order-quantity rule, not a job total: the job total is subject to the approved minimum (FIN-001). Picket species and profile are not stated (04 HI-10 material assumptions). No price changed.

### Gate Re-alignments (Anti-sag cable + heavy hardware swap out)
* **Price:** $125 - $185 flat per single gate opening.

> FLAG (C-HI10-07) - "Gate Re-alignments" heading and line: gate hardware replacement is not covered by the SOP or the inventory, which provide only the anti-sag cable kit; the agreement says "gate tuning" and the main plan promises anti-sag hinges and self-latching drop rods (OPEN QUESTION; the owner aligns the files after deciding the gate scope). The job total is subject to the approved minimum (FIN-001). Whether latch work on a gate in a pool-barrier fence carries any requirement is an open question for the local building official (04 HI-10 permit/license flags). No price changed.
