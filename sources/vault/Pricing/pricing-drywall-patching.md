## 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Handyman Specialist Labor Average:** $60 - $90 / hour
* **Trip Fee Minimum:** $150 - $250 (To cover fuel across I-65 bridges, setup, and first hour)

> FLAG (C-DW01-03, X-18): competing minimum - owner decision (Q-28-06). The "Trip Fee Minimum" line above is a second, ranged, service-level minimum beside the single job minimum of spec BIZ-003, FIN-001 and CX-001. It also covers the "first hour", so adding it to a flat-rate bracket below would charge twice for the same time, and on a visit combined with another service's trip-type charge the minimums would stack. Travel pricing is not approved: [28](../AI%20Development%20Plans/28-pricing-estimating-and-job-costing.md) proposes keeping it disabled at launch (D-28-10, PROPOSED), and the bridge-crossing premise is unverified (Q-28-17). The [agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-drywall-patching.md) repeats this range in its "Trip Minimum Notice" clause. Unapproved; do not quote. Added 2026-10-03.

* **Target Margin:** 75% (Low material cost, high skill/convenience markup)

> FLAG (X-19): internal target, unapproved, no cost basis. The "Target Margin" line above names a margin but justifies it with "markup"; the two use different bases (the [job-costing playbook](../Skills/bookkeeping%2C%20taxes%2C%20profitability%2C%20and%20cash/handyhub-job-costing/references/playbook.md) measures gross margin on net revenue and markup on cost), and whether a gross, contribution or net margin is meant is undefined (Q-28-13). Must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements ([28](../AI%20Development%20Plans/28-pricing-estimating-and-job-costing.md) section 8; spec FIN-007). Added 2026-10-03.

## 📐 Flat-Rate Matrix by Damage Size

### Small Hole Patch (Up to 4" x 4" - e.g., Doorknobs, Fist impacts)
* **Price Range:** $150 - $225 flat 
* *Operational Note:* Includes hot-mud backing, tape, block coat, and sand. Paint is excluded unless client provides matching color spray/can.

> FLAG (C-DW01-06, C-DW01-07, X-22): inclusion conflict - owner decision (Q-28-21). The note above reads as if painting is included when the client supplies matching paint, while the "Paint & Primer Match Add-on" line below charges for that same case, and the [agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-drywall-patching.md) "Exclusions" clause excludes "final aesthetic color-matching paint coats" unless itemized. The note lists no primer, but the [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop%20drywall-patching.md) primes every patch and the spec says "primer if included" (spec DW-01.MAT). Client-supplied paint has no acceptance rule (X-22); under spec START-009 its condition, product and quantity would be recorded before use ([29](../AI%20Development%20Plans/29-inventory-and-procurement.md) section 9.1, PROPOSED). Added 2026-10-03.

### Medium Hole Patch (Up to 12" x 12" - e.g., Minor plumbing/electrical cutouts)
* **Price Range:** $250 - $370 flat
* *Operational Note:* Requires wood backing installation and structural mesh/paper bridging.

### Large Panel Patch (Up to 4' x 4' - e.g., Serious water damage or ceiling leaks)
* **Price Range:** $400 - $550 flat
* *Operational Note:* Includes structural cutting to joists, hanging fresh replacement panels, and multi-coat feathering out to invisible seams.

> FLAG (C-DW01-05): outcome claim - not approved for customer use. "invisible seams" in the note above is not an approved promise; the spec's completion checks are "flat/feathered finish appropriate to scope, texture match within agreed tolerance" (spec DW-01.QAC). Any customer wording needs the owner and an attorney licensed in KY/IN. Added 2026-10-03.

> FLAG (C-DW01-08, C-DW01-01): scope and hazard gap. This bracket ("Serious water damage or ceiling leaks", "structural cutting to joists", "hanging fresh replacement panels") is larger than the SOP's stated "minor to medium" scope, and no SOP step covers large or overhead panels (C-DW01-08). "structural cutting to joists" is ambiguous wording (it may read as cutting framing members) for the reviewer to settle; cutting or altering framing members is structural work on the SOP's Do-not-perform list. Water-damage work first needs the card's checks "confirm leak/source resolved" and "stop on mold/suspect hazardous material or extensive concealed damage" (spec DW-01.SAF). A price line does not clear a safety hold. HUMAN REVIEW: qualified safety professional. Added 2026-10-03.

## ➕ Mandatory Surcharges
- [ ] **Ceiling Repair Premium:** Add a **25% surcharge** on any patch overhead due to gravity-defying mud application and complex texture blending.

> FLAG (C-DW01-01): overhead work. The spec card lists "ladder for ceilings" as a hazard (spec DW-01.HAZ); no ladder, access control or overhead step exists in the SOP or the inventory, and no approved height and access policy exists ([02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) G-005). Until that policy and a reviewed overhead procedure exist, ceiling work that needs a ladder or other access equipment stays blocked ([31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 3.3 entry 5 and section 8.4 rule 4); a price line does not release it. No SOP step covers the "complex texture blending" named above, and this premium does not appear in the agreement. The heading "Mandatory Surcharges" also holds the optional add-on below. Added 2026-10-03.

- [ ] **Paint & Primer Match Add-on:** +$75 flat per patch area (if client supplies paint) or +$125 flat (if your crew has to take a sample to local Sherwin-Williams/Home Depot for color matching).

> FLAG (C-DW01-06, C-DW01-07, X-22): inclusion conflict - owner decision (Q-28-21). The client-paint variant above prices the case that the "Small Hole Patch" note treats as included, and primer is sold here inside an add-on while the SOP primes every patch (spec DW-01.MAT "primer if included"). The retailer names are not an approved vendor list (Q-29-03). Client-supplied paint: see the flag under "Small Hole Patch" (spec START-009; X-22). Added 2026-10-03.
