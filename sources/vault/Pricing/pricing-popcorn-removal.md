* **Scraping & Level 5 Finish Standard:** $2.50 - $4.50 / square foot
* **Containment Overhead:** ~$120 per room in plastic sheeting and tape materials.
* **Target Margin:** 60% (Labor and disposal intensive)

> FLAG (C-DW02-11, X-19) - "Scraping & Level 5 Finish Standard" and "Containment Overhead" lines: unsourced, undated benchmarks (X-19). "Containment Overhead" is an internal material cost estimate per room, not a customer price: a cost-basis candidate once sourced ([28](../AI%20Development%20Plans/28-pricing-estimating-and-job-costing.md) section 11), never customer-visible (28 section 8; spec FIN-007). Values unchanged.

> FLAG (X-19) - "Target Margin" line: internal target, unapproved, with no cost basis; the file does not say whether it is a gross, contribution or net margin or a markup (Q-28-13). Disposal, named as its reason, is neither priced nor costed in this file. It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; spec FIN-007). Value unchanged.

## 📐 Per-Square-Foot Tiered Models

### Tier 1: Standard Popcorn Scraping + Texture Spray (Orange Peel or Knockdown)
* **Rate:** $2.50 - $3.25 per sq. ft.
* *Inclusions:* Full plastic isolation envelope, hydraulic misting, bulk scrape, single coat skim, and pneumatic texture hopper apply.

> FLAG (C-DW02-13, C-DW02-15) - Tier 1 "Inclusions": TIER-DEPENDENT STEPS - owner to confirm. This tier includes a "single coat skim", while the SOP's Phase 4 applies two skim passes to every ceiling (C-DW02-13). The tier covers sprayed Orange Peel or Knockdown on the ceiling only; Skip-Trowel and wall texture matching, which the main plan and the inventory name, have no price line or SOP procedure (C-DW02-15). Owner decision; no price changed.

### Tier 2: Premium Popcorn Scraping + Level 5 Smooth Finish (No Texture)
* **Rate:** $3.50 - $4.75 per sq. ft.
* *Inclusions:* Bulk scrape, two to three consecutive wide skimming blade passes, mechanical HEPA dustless sanding, and paint-ready white prime.

> FLAG (C-DW02-12, C-DW02-13) - Tier 2 "Inclusions": "two to three" skim passes here against the SOP's two passes (C-DW02-13); "paint-ready white prime" needs primer, which the inventory does not list (C-DW02-12; see the inventory's proposed additions). Neither tier prices the Phase 3 repairs that the SOP performs after scraping (re-taping, re-fastening); repairs beyond the approved scope need an approved change order (spec FIN-008; 04 DW-02 estimating method). No price changed.

## 🚨 Essential Safety Clauses
- [ ] **Pre-1980 Asbestos Testing Surcharge:** +$150 flat fee if your company coordinates the lab text before scraping. If asbestos is positive, refer to a hazardous remediation sub-contractor.
- [ ] **High Ceiling Surcharge (Over 10 ft):** Add **+$0.50 / sq. ft.** due to rolling staging/scaffolding logistics.

> FLAG (C-DW02-02, C-DW02-01) - "Pre-1980 Asbestos Testing Surcharge" line: TESTING ROLE UNDEFINED - no HandyHub sampling until the role is defined (C-DW02-02). HUMAN REVIEW: accredited asbestos inspector; attorney licensed in KY/IN. This line says the company "coordinates" the test, while the agreement charges for "Company testing"; no file names who collects samples, with what qualification, which lab or what record releases work (Q-30-18; Q-28-07). "lab text" is probably a typo for "lab test" (HYPOTHESIS; flagged, not corrected). On a positive result this line refers the work out, while the agreement lets work resume once an abatement firm "encapsulates or clears the space" (C-DW02-01); the SOP holds a positive or inconclusive result as BLOCKED until documented clearance from a qualified asbestos professional accepted by the owner. Not a customer charge until approved; no price changed.

> FLAG (X-12, C-DW02-12) - "High Ceiling Surcharge (Over 10 ft)" line: the surcharge prices "rolling staging/scaffolding logistics", but no height and access policy exists ([02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) G-005) and the inventory lists no staging, scaffold or ladder, only scraper poles (C-DW02-12). Steps that need such equipment stay on hold in the SOP until a qualified safety professional approves the access method and equipment (31 section 8.4). Rent or buy, and any pass-through of the equipment cost, are owner decisions (Q-28-15). HUMAN REVIEW: qualified safety professional. No price changed.
