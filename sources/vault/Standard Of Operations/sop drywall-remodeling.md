## 1. Purpose

A professional workflow guiding crews through framing extensions, installing technical panels, and integrating new drywall walls smoothly with old existing residential properties.

- This SOP covers how HandyHub workers lay out and fasten partition framing, select, hang and finish drywall, and blend new drywall into existing walls for **DW-03 Residential Remodeling & Home Additions**, only after the preconditions in section 3 and the holds in section 12 clear (source: spec Appendix B name; [04](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#DW-03) DW-03 customer-facing description, PROPOSED neutral summary; original steps in section 12).
- It states how the work is done and promises no flatness, crack-free, mold-prevention, code-compliance or structural outcome to the customer (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 3.5 rule 5; C-DW03-09).
- Proposed risk class, pending qualified safety review: high-risk / specialist review for framing in additions, headers or any load-bearing change, work at utility interfaces, tie-ins and demolition in older homes, and fire-rated assemblies; elevated for non-structural partitions and board hanging with lifts or stilts (source: safety-liability-manager risk classification; 04 DW-03 active/inactive status, PROPOSED).

> FLAG (C-DW03-14, C-DW03-01): the H1 says "Structural Additions" and the intro says "framing extensions", while the canonical name is "Residential Remodeling & Home Additions". "Structural work" is a hazard on the spec card (DW-03.HAZ), not an offered scope: structural framing is not performed under this SOP unless the owner decides otherwise and a structural engineer or building official approves it (section 2, Do not perform). The H1 and the intro stay verbatim; a title change is made only in a new SOP version (31 section 5). HUMAN REVIEW: owner; structural engineer or building official.

## 2. Scope

### In scope

- Phase 1 as written in section 12: layout with chalk lines and a laser level, fastening top and bottom plates, and installing solid wood blocking for later mounted items, for non-structural partitions only and after the structural, permit, old-property and access holds in section 12 clear (source: original Phase 1; agreement "Project Specifications" clause "Company will install framing"; spec DW-03.INT "framing locations").
- Phases 2 to 5 as written in section 12: board selection and hanging, taping and corner beads, blending tie-ins, sanding, the light test and site cleanup (source: original Phases 2 to 5; agreement "technical gypsum panels, metal corner beads, and finish seams"; spec DW-03.MAT).
- Confirming on site, for every job, the intake facts: approved design/scope, framing locations, utilities, permits/inspections status, drywall type, moisture/fire requirements, finish level (source: spec DW-03.INT; CX-005).

### Out of scope

- Not covered - pending review (C-DW03-03): installing the "clear plastic isolation curtains" that the agreement's "Dust Containment" clause promises; no step sets up containment. The owner decides the containment standard with the qualified safety professional; until then the dust controls in sections 9 and 11 stay pending review.
- Not covered - pending review (C-DW03-04): insulation, which pricing sells as the "Insulation Install" add-on and the agreement offers as "insulation [ ]"; no step, material or PPE exists. It stays separate from HI-07 Attic Insulation. Whether DW-03 includes insulation is an owner decision; whether an inspection is required before cover is a question for the building official.
- Not covered - pending review (C-DW03-02): demolition and gutting, which pricing sells as "Old Drywall Demolition / Gutting"; no step covers demolition, hazardous-material screening or disposal, so demolition stays on hold until a hazardous-material policy and a reviewed demolition procedure exist.
- Not offered under this SOP unless the owner decides otherwise (04 DW-03 exclusions, PROPOSED): electrical, plumbing and HVAC work; structural engineering, headers and load-bearing changes; permit applications and fees (responsibility undecided, C-DW03-01); painting; hazardous-material abatement; mold remediation. Work for a general contractor or developer belongs to DW-04 under a reviewed subcontract, not to DW-03 ([02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) section 5.2, PROPOSED boundary).

### Do not perform

Each entry: prohibited work - reason class - what to do instead (source). PROPOSED wording; HUMAN REVIEW: qualified safety professional and licensing reviewer (31 section 3.3).

1. Electrical, plumbing, gas or HVAC work outside the approved scope or requiring a licensed trade, including relocating any utility exposed by framing or a gut renovation - licensed trade - stop and escalate; relocations only by licensed trades (source: spec START-011 "unapproved electrical/plumbing/gas/structural scope"; master section 1.3; 31 section 10 DW-03).
2. Structural work, meaning cutting, removing, sistering or adding load-bearing members, ledgers, footings or framing, unless it is in the approved scope with the required qualified review - structural - stop and escalate (source: spec START-011; master section 1.3).
3. Suspected asbestos / lead paint / mold: stop and escalate (source: spec START-011). Do not cut, drill, sand or remove painted surfaces, old plaster or other suspect material in a pre-1978 or unknown-age home until a qualified person records a disposition - hazardous material ("Unknown is not safe", owner facts 2026-10-02; 31 sections 7.2 and 10).
4. Work that is not in the approved scope or an approved change order - out of scope - record the customer's request instead (source: spec JOB-005, JOB-010, FIN-008, AT-033).
5. Working at height or using access equipment, including stilts, the panel lift and ladders, beyond the approved height and access policy - policy - stop and escalate (source: HI-06.SAF "beyond approved height/access policy"; DW-04.TLS "scaffolds/stilts only under approved policy").
6. Mixing or sequencing chemicals in any way the approved step and the product SDS do not allow, including joint compounds - hazardous material - stop and escalate (source: 31 section 3.3, from CL-01.SAF "never mix incompatible chemicals").
7. Recording, photographing or sending customer passwords, master codes or site access codes outside the app's secured fields - policy - use the app's secured fields only (source: spec COM-002; owner facts 2026-10-02; 27 AUTH-009).
8. Promising results, warranties, safety, health or code compliance to the customer, including flatness, crack-free joints, mold prevention or code outcomes - policy - refer the question to the owner (source: master sections 1.4, 17 and 29; C-DW03-09).
9. Concealing unapproved utility work - licensed trade and inspection - do not hang board over it; stop and escalate (source: spec DW-03.SAF "do not conceal unapproved utility work").
10. Structural framing changes without approved plans, including headers, load-bearing walls and addition framing - structural - HOLD for a structural engineer or building official (source: 31 section 10 DW-03; C-DW03-01).
11. Demolition or gutting before a hazardous-material policy exists - hazardous material - do not quote or start; escalate (source: 31 section 10 DW-03; C-DW03-02).
12. Covering work that needs an inspection before the inspection is signed off - permit and inspection - stop and escalate (source: spec DW-03.SAF "inspections before concealment where required"; 31 section 8.3 rule 2).

## 10. Stop-work conditions

> **Stop-work rule (all HandyHub services).** You have the right to stop work. For any condition below, stop the affected work and escalate before continuing (source: spec START-011, JOB-004):
> - an unsafe condition for you, the customer, occupants, pets or the public;
> - concealed damage, substrate failure, moisture or an active leak;
> - suspect hazardous material, such as possible asbestos, lead paint or mold; do not disturb it;
> - electrical, plumbing, gas, HVAC or structural work that is not in the approved scope or needs a licensed trade;
> - an aggressive occupant or animal;
> - severe weather or unsafe heat or cold (source: spec BRF-008);
> - any material mismatch between the site and the briefing, approved scope, materials, access or approvals.
>
> Do not continue through an unsafe or restricted condition to keep the schedule (source: master section 12). A customer request, a checkbox or a signature does not clear a safety hold (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; risk-triage playbook L27). In an emergency, move people away and call emergency services first (source: spec BRF-013; risk-triage playbook L7). Then use Stop Work / Report Hazard in the app (source: spec UXE-004) and follow section 19.

Service-specific stop triggers and holds (all pending qualified review; release roles per 31 section 8.3):

- Card guidance, verbatim: "verify approved plans/permit dependencies; do not conceal unapproved utility work; use correct board/system; lifting/team controls; inspections before concealment where required" (source: spec DW-03.SAF).
- HOLD C-DW03-01, preconditions before Phase 1: approved plans and scope on file; permit determination recorded by a reviewer, not assumed; headers, load-bearing and addition framing on hold for a structural engineer or building official; plumbing, electrical and HVAC relocations by licensed trades only. A structural concern and unapproved electrical, plumbing, gas or HVAC scope are critical holds that no role can override (31 section 8.3).
- HOLD C-DW03-01, inspection before concealment, before "Hanging Sequence": required inspections signed off with photos before any board covers framing, utilities or other work; a failed or incomplete inspection is a critical hold (31 section 8.3 rule 2).
- HOLD C-DW03-02, old-property screening, before Phase 1 and before "The Old-to-New Merge": pre-1978 or unknown-age painted surfaces, suspect materials, horsehair plaster and demolition are not disturbed; release only on a recorded disposition by a qualified person accepted by the owner (31 sections 8.3 and 10).
- HOLD X-12, access before Phase 1, and HOLD C-DW03-08, stilts, panel lift and team lifting before "Hanging Sequence": these stay on hold until a qualified safety professional approves the controls; a hold caused by a missing policy cannot be released by override (31 section 8.4 rule 4).
- Suspected mold growth or an active leak: stop; treated as critical until the company mold definition exists (31 section 8.4 rule 2; Q-31-10; X-06).
- Board or system on site that does not match the approved plans, including fire-rated or moisture-resistant assemblies: stop and escalate (spec DW-03.SAF "use correct board/system"; START-011 "any material mismatch").

The HOLD and FLAG lines are additions pending qualified review: a precondition hold sits directly before the step it gates, and a hold or flag that suspends, limits or flags a step sits directly after it ([36](../AI%20Development%20Plans/36-file-modification-plan.md) D-36-17). They never replace or reword a step.*


### 🕒 Phase 1: Structural Framing & Layout Verification

> HOLD (C-DW03-01) - preconditions, before the first step below ("Plumb Line Layout") and before any framing: approved plans and scope on file; permit determination recorded by a reviewer, not assumed; headers, load-bearing and addition framing = HOLD for a structural engineer or building official; plumbing, electrical and HVAC relocations by licensed trades only. Do not start framing until these are recorded for this job (spec START-010). Release only by the named reviewer, with evidence (31 section 8.3). Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10 DW-03; spec DW-03.INT, DW-03.SAF)

> HOLD (C-DW03-02) - before the first step below and before any step that cuts, drills, sands or removes existing finishes: in a pre-1978 or unknown-age home, painted surfaces, old plaster and other suspect materials are not disturbed; stop and escalate. No HandyHub sampling; release only on a recorded disposition by an EPA RRP-certified renovator or an accredited asbestos inspector accepted by the owner. Pending qualified review. (source: 31 sections 7.2 and 10; spec START-011; owner facts 2026-10-02, EPA RRP and asbestos sources; X-13)

> HOLD (X-12) - before the first step below: snapping ceiling lines and fastening top plates is work at height. Ladders and other access equipment are used only under the approved height and access policy, which does not exist yet ([02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) G-005); until it exists this work stays on hold (31 section 8.4 rule 4). Pending qualified review. (source: spec DW-03.HAZ "falls", DW-03.TLS "access equipment"; section 2, Do not perform item 5)

- [ ] **Plumb Line Layout:** Snap blue chalk lines on the floor and ceiling to map new partitions. Use a laser level to verify that new vertical stud tracking is perfectly plumb.
- [ ] **Fastening Tracks:** Secure top and bottom plates using structural wood screws (for wood joists) or concrete anchors (for basement floor slabs). Space studs precisely at 16 inches on-center.

> FLAG (C-DW03-01): UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions. "Space studs precisely at 16 inches on-center" in the step above has no source; stud spacing, plate fasteners and anchors come only from the approved plans, any listed assembly, the board and fastener manufacturers and any applicable code requirement confirmed by the building official (31 section 10 DW-03: "manufacturer, listed assembly and code govern"). The "structural wood screws" and "concrete anchors" stay on the structural HOLD above; anchor type, size and embedment are never estimated (risk-triage playbook "do not invent ... anchor load ratings"). The step text above is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: structural engineer or building official.

- [ ] **Fire-Blocking & Blocking:** Install solid wood blocking sections behind target zones where heavy items will be mounted later (e.g., TV wall mounts, kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities).

> FLAG (G-059): the step above is titled "Fire-Blocking & Blocking" but describes only backing for later mounted items; fireblocking is not addressed and is not performed under this step until the building official's requirements are recorded for the job. The step states no load capacity for the items mounted later. The step text above is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: building official.

> FLAG (C-DW03-04): no insulation step exists between Phase 1 and Phase 2, although pricing sells the "Insulation Install" add-on and the agreement offers "insulation [ ]". Insulation is not installed under this SOP (section 2, Out of scope); if the owner adds it, a reviewed step, materials, PPE and any required inspection before cover come first. Pending owner decision.


### 📦 Phase 2: Technical Panel Selection & Hanging
- [ ] **Board Allocation Map:**
	- **Standard Zones:** 1/2-inch lightweight drywall sheets.
	- **Bathrooms / Wet Kitchens:** Moisture-resistant Greenboard or Purpleboard.
	- **Garage Ceilings / Boiler Rooms:** 5/8-inch Fire-Rated Type X sheets.

> FLAG (spec DW-03.SAF "use correct board/system"): the board types in the step above are a default map, not a specification. The board and system for each area come from the approved plans, the board manufacturer and any listed fire-rated assembly; fire separation for garage ceilings and boiler rooms, and the suitability of moisture-resistant board in wet areas, including behind tile (the main plan's claim, 02 C-DW03-09), are questions for the building official and the board manufacturer (04 DW-03 permit/license flags). If the board on site does not match the approved plans, stop (spec START-011 "any material mismatch"). The step text above is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: building official.

> HOLD (C-DW03-01) - inspection before concealment, before the step below ("Hanging Sequence") and before any board covers framing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC or other work: required inspections signed off with photos before concealment; do not conceal unapproved utility work (spec DW-03.SAF). A failed or incomplete inspection is a critical hold that no role can override (31 section 8.3 rule 2). Which inspections apply, and who arranges them, is recorded for the job by a reviewer (04 DW-03 permit/license flags, OPEN QUESTION). Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10 DW-03; spec DW-03.SAF, DW-03.QAC "penetrations/interfaces documented, inspection prerequisites satisfied")

> HOLD (C-DW03-08) - before the step below and every later step that uses stilts, the panel lift, ladders or team lifting: controls for stilts, the panel lift and team lifting must be approved by a qualified safety professional and in place. None is defined yet, and no height, load or team size is set here; until they exist those steps stay on hold (31 section 8.4 rule 4). Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10 DW-03; spec DW-03.HAZ "heavy panels, lifts", "falls"; DW-03.SAF "lifting/team controls"; X-12)

- [ ] **Hanging Sequence:** Always hang ceiling panels first, then push wall panels tightly up against the ceiling line. Stagger all vertical wall seams—never allow four corners to meet at a single point (prevents settling stress cracks).

> FLAG (31 section 3.5 rule 5): "(prevents settling stress cracks)" in the step above is a working rationale, not a promise to the customer; no crack-free outcome is offered (section 2, Do not perform item 8). The step text above is unchanged.

- [ ] **Fastener Pattern:** Drive screws 8 inches apart along all perimeter edges and 12 inches apart along center interior studs. Keep screws at least 3/8 inch away from the edge of the panel. Ensure heads are recessed without piercing the paper face.

> FLAG (C-DW03-01): UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions. "8 inches apart", "12 inches apart" and "at least 3/8 inch away from the edge" in the step above have no source; fastener type, length and spacing come only from the board manufacturer and, for a fire-rated assembly, the listed assembly (31 section 10 DW-03; spec DW-03.SAF "use correct board/system"). The step text above is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: qualified reviewer; building official for fire-rated assemblies.


### 🧽 Phase 3: Premium Multi-Stage Taping & Mudding
- [ ] **First Stage (Bedding):** Fill all tapered factory joints with multi-purpose joint compound. Embed premium paper joint tape directly into the wet mud using a 6-inch knife, squeezing out trapped air and excess compound.
- [ ] **Corner Bead Application:** Install metal or heavy composite plastic corner beads on all exposed external wall corners. Fasten with a crimper tool or screws, then apply a heavy coat of mud to blend the bead edge into the wall profile.

> FLAG (C-DW03-V02): the bead material differs across files: the step above allows "metal or heavy composite plastic" beads, while the agreement specifies "metal corner beads" only. Use the material on the approved estimate; a substitution that affects specification or customer expectation needs approval (spec JOB-007). The step text above is unchanged. Pending owner decision.

- [ ] **Fastener Filling:** Apply a clean single pass of mud over all recessed screw dimples throughout the boards.

### 🩹 Phase 4: Blending & Skimming Transitions

> HOLD (C-DW03-02) - before the step below ("The Old-to-New Merge"): the tie-in to horsehair plaster or another old finish is held until the build year and the plaster and paint disposition are recorded for the job; in a pre-1978 or unknown-age home, stop and escalate. Release only on a recorded disposition by an EPA RRP-certified renovator or an accredited asbestos inspector accepted by the owner. Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10 DW-03; 04 DW-03 prerequisites "plaster disposition recorded before demolition or tie-ins", PROPOSED)

- [ ] **The Old-to-New Merge:** Where new drywall connects to thick old horsehair plaster, use a wide 12-inch or 14-inch knife to feather the mud layer out at least 18 inches into the old wall area to create a gradual, invisible slope.

> FLAG (C-DW03-11): the step above feathers "at least 18 inches", while the agreement commits to "up to 18 inches"; the owner decides one commitment for the agreement, this SOP and the quality check. "invisible" is an outcome word, not a promise to the customer (31 section 3.5 rule 5). The step text above is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; attorney (agreement wording).

- [ ] **Second Fill Coat:** Apply a wider coat of topping compound over all joint seams, widening the patch to 8–10 inches.
- [ ] **Third Finish Coat:** Apply an ultra-thin tight coat of lightweight mud to fill micro-pinholes and sand tracks.

### ✨ Phase 5: Final Sanding & Inspection Protocol
- [ ] **Edge Sanding:** Sand all joints using a fine 180-grit sponge, taking extreme care not to scuff or fray the raw paper facing of the drywall sheets.

> FLAG (C-DW03-15): the agreement's final draw falls due on "final mechanical sanding", while the step above sands by hand with a sponge; the sanding method is not changed here, and the payment trigger is for the owner and the attorney. Dust controls for sanding are pending qualified safety review (sections 6, 9 and 11). The step text above is unchanged.

- [ ] **The Light Test:** Place a commercial halogen work light close to the wall angle. Meticulously inspect every junction for trowel marks, craters, or uneven ridges. Spot-apply compound to corrections.
- [ ] **Site Cleanup:** Vacuum up all fine sanding debris, damp-wipe window frames, and sweep clean before painter turnover.
