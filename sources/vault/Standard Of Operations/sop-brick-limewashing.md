## 1. Purpose

An intensive masonry procedure focusing on deep chemical washing, extensive architectural masking, and applying high-durability breathable protections.

> FLAG: outcome claim - owner and attorney review (C-PT03-02). The intro above is kept verbatim. It calls the work "high-durability breathable protections" for both options, while the agreement sells Option B as a "(Full moisture barrier seal)" and the pricing file as a "(Full Waterproof Shield)", and the spec card lists "moisture trapping/wrong coating" as a hazard (spec PT-03.HAZ). Whether a coating is breathable depends on the product chosen and its manufacturer data; this SOP makes no such claim. (source: 31 section 10 PT-03 "Flag verbatim")

Purpose (PROPOSED wording, pending review): clean and prepare exterior brick, protect openings and adjacent property, and apply either a limewash finish (Option A) or an elastomeric masonry coating (Option B) for the canonical service PT-03 Brick Limewashing & Elastomeric Masonry Coatings, using a coating system confirmed as compatible with the masonry and its moisture condition, and stopping for an unresolved moisture source, for height or access beyond the approved policy and for any unsafe condition (source: spec Appendix B; spec PT-03.INT, PT-03.SAF; 04 PT-03 "customer-facing description", PROPOSED neutral text; original phases in section 12). This draft authorizes no work and promises no result to the customer (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; 31 section 3.5 rule 5).

## 2. Scope

### In scope

As written in the original procedure (section 12), pending review:
- Phase 1 masonry cleansing: efflorescence scraping, a mildew wash and a pressure wash of the brick envelope (source: original Phase 1; pricing Option A "Includes masonry wash"; agreement payment milestone "completion of full masonry wash"). All three steps carry holds in section 12.
- Phase 2 spray masking of windows, exterior light fixtures, entry doors, utility meters, the roofline, soffits, fascia and gutters (source: original Phase 2). The roofline and soffit steps are held until an access plan is approved (C-PT03-04).
- Option A, limewash: mixing the slaked-lime slurry, pre-wetting the brick, brush application and distressing (source: original Phase 3; pricing and agreement Option A).
- Option B, elastomeric coating: airless spray application with back-rolling by a second painter (source: original Phase 4; pricing and agreement Option B).
- Phase 5 de-masking, glass overspray removal and a ground check before the final walkthrough (source: original Phase 5; agreement payment milestone "de-masking, glass scraping inspection, and clean site handover").
- Options A and B are alternates of one service, chosen on the estimate and never merged with PT-02 or HI-03 work (source: spec EST-005; 04 PT-03 aliases and boundary rules).

### Out of scope

- Not covered - pending review (C-PT03-07): the test section with client sign-off before the full facade that the agreement promises (agreement clause 1). No step performs or records it, and the agreement speaks of "spraying the full facade" while Option A brush-applies limewash. Whether a test section is offered and priced, and how it is applied, are owner decisions (open question recorded with C-PT03-07 in 02; spec PT-03.QAC "approved sample/look").
- Not covered - pending review (C-PT03-08): protection of landscaping, plants, vehicles and neighboring property before spraying. The agreement promises "multi-layered window and landscape masking protection" and the pricing file budgets time for masking landscapes, but Phase 2 has no landscape step and Phase 5 only checks the ground at the end. A wind limit for spraying is an open question; no value is set here (source: agreement clause 2; pricing "Masking & Safety Overhead"; spec PT-03.QAC "openings/landscape protected").
- Not offered under this SOP until the owner decides (source: 04 PT-03 "exclusions", GAP): tuckpointing and mortar repair, structural masonry repair, crack repair beyond what the approved coating system specifies, correction of moisture sources (gutters, grading, plumbing), roof, gutter or window repair, removal of existing paint or sealers, interior brick, partial areas such as a chimney or an accent wall, which are not priced (04 PT-03 minimum charge behavior), and commercial buildings (the agreement's "Commercial/Residential" label has no commercial policy, Q-04-11).

## 10. Stop-work conditions

> **Stop-work rule (all HandyHub services).** You have the right to stop work. For any condition below, stop the affected work and escalate before continuing (source: spec START-011, JOB-004):
> - an unsafe condition for you, the customer, occupants, pets or the public;
> - concealed damage, substrate failure, moisture or an active leak;
> - suspect hazardous material, such as possible asbestos, lead paint or mold; do not disturb it;
> - electrical, plumbing, gas, HVAC or structural work that is not in the approved scope or needs a licensed trade;
> - an aggressive occupant or animal;
> - severe weather or unsafe heat or cold (source: spec BRF-008);
> - any material mismatch between the site and the briefing, approved scope, materials, access or approvals.
>
> Do not continue through an unsafe or restricted condition to keep the schedule (source: master section 12). A customer request, a checkbox or a signature does not clear a safety hold (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; risk-triage playbook L27). In an emergency, move people away and call emergency services first (source: spec BRF-013; risk-triage playbook L7). Then use Stop Work / Report Hazard in the app (source: spec UXE-004) and follow section 19.

Service-specific stop triggers (source: spec PT-03.SAF unless noted; PROPOSED wording pending qualified review):
- Coating system not confirmed as compatible with the masonry and any existing coating or sealer: do not coat ("confirm compatible coating system").
- An active moisture source, or efflorescence that points to one, with no recorded disposition: do not coat; stop and escalate ("address active moisture cause before coating when required"; C-PT03-05).
- Required PPE, including skin and eye protection, not available: do not handle lime or chemicals ("PPE/skin/eye protection").
- Unsuitable weather, or height or access beyond the approved policy: record No Start / Blocked or stop ("weather and height controls"; spec SCH-008, START-011).
- Loose or crumbling mortar or spalling brick found during washing: stop washing that area, photograph it, record it (JOB-004) and report it with the approved message (agreement "Pre-existing Masonry Defects"; Do not perform entry 9).
- People or pets enter the wash or spray zone, or spray drifts toward vehicles, plants or neighboring property: stop (X-11; C-PT03-08).
- Any instruction to mix the masonry cleaner with bleach before an approved product and SDS review: do not mix (C-PT03-01).
- A gas meter, regulator or vent would have to be wrapped or covered to continue: stop and escalate (Do not perform entry 10).

Use, mix and apply only as the approved label allows; no product, ratio or dwell is set here. Plants, openings, people and pets are protected and runoff control is in place before any application. Pending qualified review. ### 🧪 Phase 3: Execution - Option A: Authentic Limewash Look

> FLAG (C-PT03-07): the agreement promises a test section with client sign-off before the full facade and describes the application as "spraying"; this option brush-applies the limewash and has no test-section or sign-off step. Not covered - pending review (section 2); open question recorded with C-PT03-07 in 02.

> HOLD (C-PT03-05, C-PT03-02): before any step of this option - the moisture-source disposition, and the compatibility of the limewash system with this masonry and any existing coating, are recorded by the qualified reviewer from the product's manufacturer data. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-03.SAF "confirm compatible coating system", "address active moisture cause before coating when required")

> HOLD (PT-03.SAF): before the "Slaking Slurry Mix" step below - skin and eye protection selected by the qualified safety professional is worn for mixing, wetting, brushing and distressing lime. The "instructions" named in that step are the approved limewash product's label and data sheet; mix, pre-wet and apply only as they state. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-03.SAF "PPE/skin/eye protection"; PT-03.HAZ "chemical/alkaline materials"; 29 section 8.2 row 8)

- [ ] **Slaking Slurry Mix:** Mix authentic Italian Class-A slaked lime powder with water according to instructions until a milk-like consistency is reached.
- [ ] **Saturating the Masonry:** Spray the brick wall with water right before application—*Limewash requires a wet brick canvas to bind chemically.*
- [ ] **Masonry Brush Apply:** Use a large, thick block brush to work the limewash slurry into the brick pores using cross-hatch strokes.

> HOLD (C-PT03-03, X-08): before the "Artisanal Distressing" step below - the "light pressure washer mist" in that step falls under the same pressure-washing hold as "High-Pressure Cleanse" (method and pressure from the manufacturer instructions and the qualified safety professional; people and pets out of the zone). - [ ] **Artisanal Distressing:** Wait 30 minutes for the limewash to partially set. Use a light pressure washer mist or a damp sponge to selectively wash away wash coats from edges/corners, exposing natural brick hints for an old-world look.

> FLAG: UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions (the approved limewash product's data) for the waiting time in the step above; it stays verbatim until a reviewer confirms or replaces it. (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 3)

### 🛡️ Phase 4: Execution - Option B: Solid Elastomeric Shielding

> HOLD (C-PT03-05, C-PT03-02): before any step of this option - the moisture-source disposition, and the compatibility of the elastomeric coating system with this masonry and any existing coating, are recorded by the qualified reviewer from the product's manufacturer data. (source: spec PT-03.SAF "confirm compatible coating system", "address active moisture cause before coating when required")

> HOLD (X-08, X-11): before the "Airless Spray Setup" step below - operate the sprayer only per its manufacturer's safety instructions, with people and pets out of the spray zone and openings, landscaping, vehicles and neighboring property protected. Tip size, pressure and pass overlap come from the sprayer and coating manufacturer instructions; the values in the steps below stay verbatim and are UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions. Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10 PT-03 "Airless spraying per the sprayer manufacturer's safety instructions"; 31 section 7.2 rank 10)

- [ ] **Airless Spray Setup:** Set up a heavy-duty airless sprayer with a large 517 or 521 tip size to handle thick masonry paint.
- [ ] **Saturating Spray Pass:** Spray a heavy, thick layer of elastomeric paint over the dry brick walls, overlapping sweeps by 50%.
- [ ] **Back-Rolling the Brick:** Have a second painter follow the sprayer with a thick 1-1/4 inch nap roller, forcing the paint deep into porous mortar joints to seal micro-cracks and bridge gaps completely.

> FLAG: outcome claim - owner and attorney review (C-PT03-02). "seal micro-cracks and bridge gaps completely" in the step above depends on the coating's manufacturer data; it is a working description, not a customer promise (section 17). This step needs both workers at once (section 4).
