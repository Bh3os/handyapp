## 1. Purpose
- Repair localized drywall damage (holes, cracks, utility cut-outs) by cutting out, backing, patching, taping, feathering, sanding and priming, as written in section 12, for DW-01 Professional Patching, Repair & Handyman Services (source: spec Appendix B; [04](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#DW-01) customer-facing description, PROPOSED neutral text).
- Texture matching and painting are not covered (section 2); whether primer is included is an owner decision (C-DW01-07; Q-28-21).
- This draft makes no outcome promise to the customer and is not field-approved (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 3.5 rule 5; owner facts 2026-10-02).

Original intro statement, kept verbatim:

This Standard Operating Procedure ensures that all minor to medium wall and ceiling punctures, water damages, and stress cracks are repaired to an invisible finish in a single visit.

> FLAG: outcome claim - owner and attorney review (C-DW01-05). The statement above promises work "repaired to an invisible finish in a single visit". The spec's completion standard is "flat/feathered finish appropriate to scope, texture match within agreed tolerance" (spec DW-01.QAC), finish-level terms have no shared definition (X-26), and no step states the drying or recoat times that a single visit would need. The statement stays verbatim until an approved SOP version replaces it (Q-31-16).

> FLAG (C-DW01-08): scope gap - large panel patch. The statement above limits this SOP to "minor to medium" damage, while the [pricing file](../Pricing/pricing-drywall-patching.md) also sells a "Large Panel Patch" bracket for "Serious water damage or ceiling leaks"; no step covers large or overhead panels (section 2).

## 2. Scope

### In scope
- Localized wall damage within the "minor to medium" scope of the original intro: squaring, cutting out, backing, inserting and fastening a replacement panel, taping, two coats, sanding, light inspection, dust wiping and priming, as written in section 12 (source: original intro; section 12).
- Ceiling punctures named in the original intro: only after an approved height and access policy and reviewed overhead controls exist; until then those tasks stay blocked (source: spec DW-01.HAZ "ladder for ceilings"; [02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) G-005; X-12; section 3).
- Water damages named in the original intro: only after the leak or water source is confirmed resolved and the moisture and mold checks are clear (source: spec DW-01.SAF; HOLDs at the start of section 12).
- Intake facts the estimate and the job rely on: hole/damage size/location, cause of damage, active leak status, wall/ceiling type, texture/finish, paint responsibility (source: spec DW-01.INT).

### Out of scope
- Not covered - pending review (C-DW01-04): texture matching, which the [agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-drywall-patching.md) "Texture Disclaimer" clause promises ("expert texture matching"). No step applies texture; "Dust-Controlled Sanding" only blends the feathered edges into the existing texture (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10).
- Not covered - pending review (C-DW01-08): large panel patches as priced in the [pricing file](../Pricing/pricing-drywall-patching.md) "Large Panel Patch" bracket ("Serious water damage or ceiling leaks", "structural cutting to joists", "hanging fresh replacement panels"), and any overhead panel work. Placeholder: "Large panel / ceiling patch - PENDING competent review" (source: [02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) C-DW01-08).
- Not covered - pending review (C-DW01-07): painting and color matching, sold in the pricing file as the "Paint & Primer Match Add-on" and excluded by the agreement unless itemized; no painting step exists. Whether primer is included is an owner decision (Q-28-21).
- No step covers mold remediation, hazardous-material work, or leak, plumbing or electrical repair; such conditions stop the work and are referred. Customer-facing exclusions are an owner and attorney decision (source: [04](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#DW-01) exclusions row, GAP; spec EST-003; X-06).
- Owner question: whether "Handyman Services" in the canonical name reaches beyond drywall repair (source: [04](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#DW-01) aliases row).

## 10. Stop-work conditions

> **Stop-work rule (all HandyHub services).** You have the right to stop work. For any condition below, stop the affected work and escalate before continuing (source: spec START-011, JOB-004):
> - an unsafe condition for you, the customer, occupants, pets or the public;
> - concealed damage, substrate failure, moisture or an active leak;
> - suspect hazardous material, such as possible asbestos, lead paint or mold; do not disturb it;
> - electrical, plumbing, gas, HVAC or structural work that is not in the approved scope or needs a licensed trade;
> - an aggressive occupant or animal;
> - severe weather or unsafe heat or cold (source: spec BRF-008);
> - any material mismatch between the site and the briefing, approved scope, materials, access or approvals.
>
> Do not continue through an unsafe or restricted condition to keep the schedule (source: master section 12). A customer request, a checkbox or a signature does not clear a safety hold (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; risk-triage playbook L27). In an emergency, move people away and call emergency services first (source: spec BRF-013; risk-triage playbook L7). Then use Stop Work / Report Hazard in the app (source: spec UXE-004) and follow section 19.

DW-01 stop triggers (spec DW-01.SAF first: "confirm leak/source resolved; scan before cutting; isolate dust; stop on mold/suspect hazardous material or extensive concealed damage"; then the register holds; all pending qualified review):
- The leak or water source is active or not confirmed resolved: do not start, cut, patch or close the wall (source: spec DW-01.SAF; C-DW01-01).
- The scan has not been done, has found a service in the cut path, or gives an uncertain result: do not cut (source: spec DW-01.SAF "scan before cutting"; spec DW-01.HAZ "hidden utilities").
- Dust cannot be isolated: do not cut or sand (source: spec DW-01.SAF "isolate dust").
- Suspected mold, suspect hazardous material or extensive concealed damage: stop. Suspected mold is treated as a critical hold until the company mold definition exists (source: spec DW-01.SAF; [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 8.4 rule 2; X-06; Q-31-10).
- Build year unknown or pre-1978 with no recorded disposition: do not cut, score or sand painted surfaces (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10; X-13).
- Ceiling or overhead work without an approved height and access policy: the task stays blocked (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 3.3 entry 5 and section 8.4 rule 4; [02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) G-005; X-12).
- Structural, electrical or plumbing work turns out to be needed: stop (section 2, Do not perform).
- Client-supplied paint missing, damaged or unidentified when the approved estimate relies on it: the affected task is blocked (source: spec START-009; spec JOB-004 "missing customer-supplied item").
- Every HOLD in section 12 names who clears it; critical holds clear only with a recorded qualified assessment, never by override or customer request (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 8.3).

## 11. Setup
- On arrival: confirm the address, work area and contact; identify pets and occupants; inspect the actual site against the briefing and record differences; material differences trigger Risk Triage or supervisor review (source: spec APXA-002, START-007).
- Capture the existing-condition photos of the damaged area and its surroundings, including any water staining (source: spec APXA-002, START-008; section 16).
- Protect the work area: "Drop Cloths" in section 12 is the original protection step; dust isolation beyond it is set by the qualified safety reviewer (source: spec APXA-002 "protect work area"; spec DW-01.SAF "isolate dust").
- Keep children and pets out of the work area (source: X-11; spec CX-005).
- Identify utilities and emergency constraints relevant to the task (source: spec APXA-002).
- Before starting: complete the required safety, PPE and site checks; acknowledge the SOP and hazards; start only when Ready or an authorized exception is documented (source: spec APXA-003, START-004).
- The original setup phase, "Phase 1: Site Protection & Damage Evaluation", stays in section 12, verbatim and in order (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 9.2 step 6).

## 12. Step-by-step procedure
*Original procedure, verbatim and in its original order; the five original phase headings are demoted one level. Lines starting with HOLD or FLAG were added on 2026-10-03 as separate lines next to the affected steps: a hold that must be met first sits directly before the step it gates; a flag, or a hold that limits a step, sits directly after the step it refers to ([36](../AI%20Development%20Plans/36-file-modification-plan.md) D-36-17). They are pending qualified review and change no original step. ### 🕒 Phase 1: Site Protection & Damage Evaluation

> HOLD (C-DW01-01): before "Drop Cloths" below and every later step, confirm that any leak or water source behind the damage is resolved, and record the cause of damage and the active-leak status. A leak that is active or not confirmed resolved: do not start; STOP and escalate (sections 10 and 19). Cleared by: TBD - qualified safety professional. Pending qualified review. (source: spec DW-01.SAF "confirm leak/source resolved"; spec DW-01.INT "cause of damage, active leak status")

> HOLD (C-DW01-01, X-06): before "Drop Cloths" below, check the damaged area for wet material or suspected mold growth without disturbing it; the moisture check method is TBD by the qualified reviewer. Wet material or suspected mold: do not start; STOP and escalate. Until the company mold definition exists, suspected mold is a critical hold (Q-31-10). Cleared by: TBD - qualified mold assessor. Pending qualified review. (source: spec DW-01.HAZ "possible mold/moisture"; spec DW-01.SAF "stop on mold"; [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 8.4 rule 2)

> HOLD (C-DW01-01, X-12): ceiling or other overhead patch: do not start "Drop Cloths" below or any later step overhead while no approved height and access policy and no reviewed overhead procedure exist; the task stays blocked. Cleared by: TBD - qualified safety professional, after the policy is approved. Pending qualified review. (source: spec DW-01.HAZ "ladder for ceilings"; [02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) G-005; [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 8.4 rule 4)

- [ ] **Drop Cloths:** Lay canvas drop cloths directly under the repair area. Tape lightweight plastic sheeting below the wall zone if working on painted or wallpapered surfaces.
- [ ] **Structural Scan:** Use a stud finder to locate the nearest wood/metal studs on either side of the hole. Scan for hidden electrical wires or copper plumbing pipes behind the gypsum core.

> FLAG (C-DW01-11): the step above relies on a stud finder that the inventory file does not list; whether a device detects wiring or pipes comes from its manufacturer documentation. The label "Structural Scan" describes a stud and service scan, not a structural assessment. Pending qualified review. (source: [02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) C-DW01-11; [inventory file](../Required%20inventory/inventory-drywall-patching.md))

- [ ] **Squaring the Patch:** Use a framing square and a pencil to draw a perfect rectangle around the damage. Avoid erratic shapes to ensure clean replacement joints.

### 🔨 Phase 2: Surgical Extraction & Framing Backing

> HOLD (C-DW01-01): do not start "Clean Cut" below until the scan in "Structural Scan" above has covered the drawn rectangle and the surrounding wall where backing screws will be driven, and has found no wiring, pipe or other service there. A service in that area or an uncertain scan result: STOP and escalate; moving or repairing wiring or plumbing is licensed-trade work (section 2, Do not perform). Cleared by: TBD - qualified safety professional. Pending qualified review. (source: spec DW-01.SAF "scan before cutting"; spec DW-01.HAZ "hidden utilities"; spec START-011)

> HOLD (C-DW01-01, X-13): before "Clean Cut" below, record the approximate build year or "unknown". Build year unknown or pre-1978: do not cut, score or sand painted drywall, plaster, compound or texture until a qualified person records a disposition (HOLD for RRP review); suspect material is not disturbed. This is a conservative screening hold, not a legal conclusion. Cleared by: TBD - EPA RRP-certified renovator; accredited asbestos inspector for suspect asbestos. Pending qualified review. (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10 and section 7.2 rank 2; spec DW-01.SAF "suspect hazardous material"; Q-31-07)

> HOLD (C-DW01-01): before "Clean Cut" below, isolate dust from the rest of the property. Whether the drop cloths and plastic sheeting of "Drop Cloths" are enough, how dust is isolated, whether the stocked HEPA vacuum is used and which PPE applies (no respirator class is named here) are set by the qualified safety professional (section 6). Cleared by: TBD - qualified safety professional. Pending qualified review. (source: spec DW-01.SAF "isolate dust"; spec DW-01.HAZ "silica/dust"; spec DW-01.TLS "PPE")

- [ ] **Clean Cut:** Cut out the drawn rectangle cleanly using a drywall hand saw or utility knife. Keep the saw blade vertical at a 90-degree angle to avoid crumbling the surrounding drywall plaster.

> HOLD (C-DW01-01, X-06): after the step above and before "Stud Clearing", inspect the opened cavity without disturbing it further. Extensive concealed damage, wet or deteriorated material, suspected mold, damaged framing, or a service in or behind the opening: STOP, photograph and record it with its severity (spec JOB-004) and escalate. Continue only after the hold is cleared and, where scope or price changes, an approved change order exists (spec FIN-008, JOB-005). Cleared by: TBD - qualified safety professional; structural engineer for framing concerns; qualified mold assessor for suspected mold. Pending qualified review. (source: spec DW-01.SAF "stop on mold/suspect hazardous material or extensive concealed damage"; [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 8.3)

- [ ] **Stud Clearing:** If a stud is present, cut the drywall back exactly to the center line of the stud to leave a ledge for the new piece.
- [ ] **Wood Backing Insertion:** If no stud is reachable, cut a piece of 1x3 lumber or plywood slat 4 inches longer than the hole. Insert it inside the cavity and secure it tightly from the outside using 1-1/4 inch drywall screws through the existing wall.

### 🎨 Phase 3: Hanging & Structural Bridging
- [ ] **Sizing the Insert:** Measure the cut opening and deduct 1/8 inch from the width and height. Cut a matching thickness replacement panel (1/2" or 5/8") from a fresh sheet.
- [ ] **Screw Fastening:** Screw the patch panel tightly into the wood backing or stud. Counter-sink the screws slightly (1/32 inch) to form a shallow dimple without breaking the paper facing.
- [ ] **Beveling Edges:** Use a utility knife to score a slight V-groove bevel along the seams where the old and new drywall meet (improves compound penetration).

### 🧽 Phase 4: Hot-Mud Taping & Feathering Layers
- [ ] **Mesh Tape Application:** Apply high-strength self-adhesive fiberglass mesh tape centered directly over all joint seams without overlapping or wrinkling.
- [ ] **First Coat (Setting Mud):** Mix a batch of fast-drying setting compound (Sheetrock 20 or 45). Use a 6-inch taping knife to firmly press the hot-mud through the mesh, completely filling the V-groove. Skim flat.

> FLAG: the step above mixes a chemical product. Mix and use it only as the product label and SDS direct; the product names are kept verbatim and are not an approved product list, and a product without a reviewed SDS is not carried to a job (PROPOSED interim rule). No mixing ratio is added here. Pending qualified review. (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 3.5 rules 3 and 8; [29](../AI%20Development%20Plans/29-inventory-and-procurement.md) section 8.3)

- [ ] **Second Coat (Feather Coat):** Once the hot-mud is solid (approx. 30 mins), scrape off minor ridges with your knife blade. Switch to a 10-inch or 12-inch knife and apply a thin layer of lightweight ready-mix mud, feathering the edges 4 inches outward past the original seam.

> FLAG: UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions. The "approx. 30 mins" set time in the step above is given for two different setting compounds; set, dry and recoat times come only from the product manufacturer's instructions. The step stays unchanged until a qualified reviewer replaces the figure. (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10; [02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) G-044)

### ✨ Phase 5: Micro-Sanding & Priming (The Invisible Finish)

> FLAG (C-DW01-05, X-26): "The Invisible Finish" in the heading above is an outcome term with no defined finish level. Quality is checked against the spec wording "flat/feathered finish appropriate to scope" (spec DW-01.QAC; section 13). Heading kept verbatim; owner and attorney review.

> HOLD (C-DW01-01, X-13): before "Dust-Controlled Sanding" below, the build-year disposition recorded before "Clean Cut" must also cover sanding into the existing painted wall at the feathered boundaries; without it, do not sand. Dust isolation and any PPE as set by the qualified safety professional (section 6). Cleared by: TBD - EPA RRP-certified renovator. Pending qualified review. (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10; spec DW-01.HAZ "silica/dust")

- [ ] **Dust-Controlled Sanding:** Use a fine-grit (220-grit) sanding sponge to gently smooth the dry edges. Meticulously blend the feathered boundaries into the texture of the original wall.

> FLAG (C-DW01-04): blending the feathered edges into the existing texture in the step above is not a texture-matching procedure. Texture matching, which the agreement promises, is not covered by this SOP (section 2). Pending owner decision.

- [ ] **Flashlight Inspection:** Hold a high-powered LED light flat against the wall profile. Check for minor shadow hollows, pinholes, or tool lines. Touch up with spot معجون if needed.

> FLAG (C-DW01-13): the last sentence of the step above contains a word in Arabic, kept verbatim. Proposed English gloss for owner and bilingual-reviewer confirmation: "joint compound" ([02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) C-DW01-13). This is not a translation; Spanish text comes only through the translation workflow (section 22).

- [ ] **Dry Wiping:** Wipe down the repair zone with a clean, dry microfiber cloth to remove chalky powder.

> FLAG (C-DW01-11): dry wiping in the step above is the only dust-removal step, and no step uses the stocked cordless HEPA vacuum. The cleanup method for sanding dust and the use of the vacuum are set by the qualified safety professional (section 14). (source: spec DW-01.QAC "dust cleaned"; [inventory file](../Required%20inventory/inventory-drywall-patching.md))

- [ ] **Sealing Primer:** Apply a uniform coat of high-quality drywall primer-sealer. *Crucial:* Never allow painters to apply final paint over raw compound without primer; it will absorb differently and leave a flashing silhouette blotch.

> FLAG (C-DW01-07): the step above primes every patch, while the spec says "primer if included" (spec DW-01.MAT, DW-01.QAC) and the pricing file sells primer inside an add-on. Whether primer is included is an owner decision (Q-28-21); the step and its quality rationale stay unchanged. Apply the primer only as the product label and SDS direct. (source: [02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) C-DW01-07; [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 3.5 rule 8)

## 19. Escalation

> 1. Stop the affected work and keep yourself, occupants and pets away from the hazard; do not try to fix, move or disturb it (source: spec START-011; risk-triage playbook L7).
> 2. Emergency (fire, gas odor, injury, electrical contact, structural movement): call emergency services first, then `<owner decision: emergency contact>` (source: spec BRF-013).

> 4. Report in the app with Stop Work / Report Hazard: type, photos and severity (source: spec JOB-004, UXE-004). Before the job has started, record No Start / Blocked instead of starting (source: spec SCH-008, AT-030).

> 7. Tell the customer only what the approved "work blocked" message says; never send incident details or access codes by text or email (source: spec NTF-007, COM-002).
> 8. Resume only after the briefing is updated and re-acknowledged, with a change order if scope or price changes (source: spec START-002, FIN-008, JOB-005).
