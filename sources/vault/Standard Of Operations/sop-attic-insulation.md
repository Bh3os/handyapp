## 1. Purpose

- Install blown-in loose-fill fiberglass or cellulose insulation in an attic to the coverage and depth in the approved estimate, with soffit ventilation baffles and approved fixture protection, after an on-site assessment (source: spec Appendix B "Attic Insulation Upgrades"; 04 HI-07 customer-facing description, PROPOSED neutral summary; original steps in section 12).
- This SOP makes no outcome, energy-saving, R-value or code-compliance promise (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 5).
- Proposed risk class: high-risk / specialist review, because of attic wiring and heat, fall-through and suspect existing material (source: 04 HI-07 status, PROPOSED; safety-liability-manager risk classification).

## 2. Scope

### In scope

- Protecting the floor under the attic hatch, installing soffit ventilation baffles and blowing loose-fill fiberglass or cellulose insulation to the target in the approved estimate, as section 12 describes (source: original steps; spec HI-07.QAC "target coverage/depth per approved plan").
- Fixture protection around recessed lights, only by a method a licensed electrician approves; the step is on HOLD (source: spec HI-07.SAF, HI-07.MAT; C-HI07-03).
- Confirming on site, for every job, the intake facts: attic access, area, existing insulation, moisture/pest history, ventilation, recessed lights, wiring, target insulation plan, material type (source: spec HI-07.INT; CX-005).

### Out of scope

- Not covered - pending review (C-HI07-V01): sealing electrical wire holes through top plates with the stocked "Fire-Rated Fireproofing Caulk & Foam Spray". No step, price line or agreement scope covers it. HUMAN REVIEW: licensed electrician and local building official.
- Not covered - pending review (C-HI07-08): wrapping "the access hatch room in plastic sheeting", which the agreement promises; the "Site Masking" step covers the floor under the hatch only. The owner decides the containment standard.
- Not covered - pending review (04 HI-07 quality checklist): hatch insulation and weatherstripping restoration; spec HI-07.QAC "access/weatherstripping restored" has no step.
- Not offered under this SOP unless the owner decides otherwise (04 HI-07 exclusions, PROPOSED): removal of existing insulation; air sealing; ventilation or roof changes; roof or rafter repair (no catalog service; referral policy is Q-30-15); mold or pest remediation; hatch replacement. Wall-cavity batt insulation belongs to DW-03, not this service.

### Do not perform

Each entry: prohibited work - reason class - what to do instead (source). PROPOSED wording; HUMAN REVIEW: qualified safety professional and licensing reviewer (31 section 3.3).

1. Electrical, plumbing, gas or HVAC work outside the approved scope or requiring a licensed trade - licensed trade - stop and escalate (source: spec START-011 "unapproved electrical/plumbing/gas/structural scope"; master section 1.3).
2. Structural work, meaning cutting, removing, sistering or adding load-bearing members, ledgers, footings or framing, unless it is in the approved scope with the required qualified review - structural - stop and escalate (source: spec START-011; master section 1.3).
3. Suspected asbestos / lead paint / mold: stop and escalate (source: spec START-011). Do not disturb suspect material, including unknown existing insulation - hazardous material ("Unknown is not safe", owner facts 2026-10-02).
4. Work that is not in the approved scope or an approved change order - out of scope - record the customer's request instead (source: spec JOB-005, JOB-010, FIN-008, AT-033).
5. Working at height or using access equipment, including the hatch ladder and joist travel, beyond the approved height and access policy - policy - stop and escalate (source: HI-06.SAF "beyond approved height/access policy"; DW-04.TLS "scaffolds/stilts only under approved policy").
6. Mixing or sequencing chemicals in any way the approved step and the product SDS do not allow - hazardous material - stop and escalate (source: 31 section 3.3, from CL-01.SAF "never mix incompatible chemicals").
7. Recording, photographing or sending customer passwords, master codes or site access codes outside the app's secured fields - policy - use the app's secured fields only (source: spec COM-002; owner facts 2026-10-02; 27 AUTH-009).
8. Promising results, warranties, safety, health or code compliance to the customer, including R-value, code-target or energy-saving outcomes - policy - refer the question to the owner (source: master sections 1.4, 17 and 29; C-HI07-05).
9. Covering unsafe fixtures or vents - licensed trade and fire hazard - stop, keep insulation out of the clearance zone, escalate (source: spec HI-07.SAF "do not cover unsafe fixtures/vents"; C-HI07-03).
10. Electrical, combustion, flue or gas-appliance work - licensed trade - stop and refer to a licensed electrician or an HVAC or gas professional (source: 31 section 10; spec HI-07.HAZ "electrical", "combustion/ventilation issues").
11. Removing existing insulation - out of scope and possible hazardous material - stop and escalate (source: 31 section 10).
12. Mold or pest remediation - hazardous material and out of scope - stop and refer (source: 31 section 10; spec HI-07.HAZ "pests/mold").

### 📋 Standard Operational Execution Flow

> HOLD (C-HI07-01) - pre-work inspection gate, before the first step below ("Site Masking") and before any work in the attic: pre-inspect moisture/electrical/combustion concerns and record what is found with photos (spec HI-07.SAF; JOB-004). Stop on mold, active leak or unsafe wiring (spec HI-07.SAF). Release only by a recorded qualified assessment (31 section 8.3). Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-07.SAF; 31 section 10)

> HOLD (C-HI07-01) - before the first step below: unknown or suspect existing insulation, for example vermiculite: do not disturb; stop; escalate. No HandyHub sampling; release only on documented clearance from a qualified asbestos professional accepted by the owner (31 section 7.2). Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10; spec START-011; owner facts 2026-10-02, EPA asbestos source)

- [ ] **Site Masking:** Cover the entire floor hallway directly beneath the attic hatch entry with plastic drops to trap loose blown fibers.

> HOLD (C-HI07-02) - before the step below ("Soffit Ventilation Baffle Placement") and every later attic step ("Fixtures Isolation", "Pneumatic Blow-In Application"): attic travel and fall-through, heat-stress, low-clearance and respiratory and skin protection controls must be approved by a qualified safety professional and in place. None is defined yet, and no temperature limit or respirator type is set here; until they exist the attic steps stay on hold (31 section 8.4). Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-07.HAZ, HI-07.SAF, HI-07.TLS; 31 section 10; X-12, X-27)

- [ ] **Soffit Ventilation Baffle Placement:** Crawl into the low attic perimeter edges. Install plastic or styrofoam insulation baffles (rafter vents) directly into every single joist bay near the soffit vents. *Crucial: This ensures fresh airflow is not blocked by loose insulation, which causes roof ice dams.*
- [ ] **Fixtures Isolation:** Build protective fire-rated drywall boxes around the top of hot recessed can lights to isolate them from loose insulation elements.

> HOLD (C-HI07-03): the step above is held. Do not build or install fixture protection until a licensed electrician approves the method, materials and clearances from the fixture manufacturer's instructions and applicable requirements. This hold also limits the blow-in step below: if a fixture's type or rating is unknown, or it is damaged or not approved, stop, keep insulation out of the clearance zone and escalate; do not cover unsafe fixtures/vents (spec HI-07.SAF). Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-07.SAF, HI-07.MAT "approved light/penetration protection materials"; spec section 17 intro; 31 section 10)

- [ ] **Pneumatic Blow-In Application:** Feed premium fiberglass or cellulose insulation bags into the commercial blowing machine hopper outside. The attic technician will direct the hose uniformly, filling the floor joists to a depth of 16–20 inches to hit the regional code target (R-49 to R-60 value).

> FLAG (C-HI07-06, C-HI07-05): UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions. "to a depth of 16–20 inches to hit the regional code target (R-49 to R-60 value)": the depth comes from the approved estimate and the selected product's coverage chart (spec HI-07.QAC "target coverage/depth per approved plan"), and the code-target wording is an unverified regulatory claim until local requirements are verified for each Kentucky and Indiana municipality. The step text above is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; local building official; attorney.

- Suspect hazardous material is not bagged, moved or removed (source: section 2, Do not perform; spec START-011).

## 16. Photo requirements (before / during / after)

- Before: arrival and existing-condition photos; start is blocked until they are captured or an authorized reason is recorded (source: spec START-008). Include what the pre-work inspection finds: moisture, wiring or fixture concerns, combustion concerns and existing insulation (source: spec HI-07.SAF; JOB-004).
- During: concealed conditions recorded with photos and severity (source: spec APXA-004; JOB-004); baffle and depth-marker placement (source: 04 HI-07 completion evidence, PROPOSED).
- After: photos mapped to work areas or tasks (source: spec QA-003): depth markers per area, baffles per eave run, fixture protection, hatch and weatherstripping restored, living space clean (source: 04 HI-07 completion evidence, PROPOSED); cleanup photos where required (source: spec QA-002).
- Privacy: internal-only by default; avoid people, documents and valuables not needed for the work (source: spec REC-002; 31 section 3.5 rule 7).

## 17. Customer communication

- Arrival and completion updates use approved templates; a blocked job uses only the approved "work blocked" message, with no incident details or access codes by text or email (source: spec NTF-007, COM-002).
- Rot, water stains, active leaks, mold, wiring or combustion concerns: stop and escalate first (section 19); the customer is then told in approved wording. The agreement's notify-only clause is under attorney review (source: spec HI-07.SAF; C-HI07-01).
- Walkthrough: record the outcome as accepted, accepted with punch items, unavailable or disputed (source: spec QA-004).
- Care and handoff notes come only from approved product data (source: spec QA-008; quality-assistant playbook "Prepare handoff instructions from approved product data").
- Do not state that the work meets a code, reaches an R-value standard or will lower energy bills, and make no warranty statement (source: section 2, Do not perform item 8; C-HI07-05).
- Explain the invoice and payment next step without unauthorized promises (source: spec APXA-006).

## 18. Completion criteria

Done means all of the following, recorded in the job (source: spec QA-001 to QA-004; HI-07.QAC; APXA-005 to APXA-007):

- Every section 13 check answered, or an exception recorded (QA-001).
- After photos mapped to areas or tasks (QA-003).
- Protection removed and cleanup done, with no insulation contamination in the living space (QA-002; HI-07.QAC).
- Hatch and access restored (HI-07.QAC).
- Tools and materials accounted for; rentals returned (APXA-005, APXA-007).
- Walkthrough outcome recorded, with punch items (QA-004).
- Unresolved issues, incidents and change requests submitted before the job is closed (APXA-007).
- Final completion is a human sign-off; the quality review only recommends readiness (source: quality-assistant playbook "Recommend readiness for human sign-off; do not set final completion").

## 19. Escalation

> 1. Stop the affected work and keep yourself, occupants and pets away from the hazard; do not try to fix, move or disturb it (source: spec START-011; risk-triage playbook L7).
> 2. Emergency (fire, gas odor, injury, electrical contact, structural movement): call emergency services first, then `<owner decision: emergency contact>` (source: spec BRF-013).
> 3. Only if policy allows and it is safe, take the minimum action needed to prevent immediate injury or property damage, and record why and what you did (source: spec JOB-006).
> 4. Report in the app with Stop Work / Report Hazard: type, photos and severity (source: spec JOB-004, UXE-004). Before the job has started, record No Start / Blocked instead of starting (source: spec SCH-008, AT-030).
> 5. Escalate with one tap to `<owner decision: escalation order>`; the item enters the owner/supervisor queue (source: spec UXE-002, JOB-012).
> 6. Wait for release by the role named in the hold, with evidence recorded. Critical holds clear only with a recorded qualified assessment (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; 27 Roles and permissions section 6.3).
> 7. Tell the customer only what the approved "work blocked" message says; never send incident details or access codes by text or email (source: spec NTF-007, COM-002).
> 8. Resume only after the briefing is updated and re-acknowledged, with a change order if scope or price changes (source: spec START-002, FIN-008, JOB-005).

Service roles for HI-07 (contacts and escalation order are owner decisions, Q-31-05):

- Wiring, junction boxes or recessed fixtures: licensed electrician (C-HI07-03).
- Flues, vents or gas appliances: HVAC or gas professional (spec HI-07.HAZ).
- Unknown or suspect existing insulation: accredited asbestos inspector (C-HI07-01).
- Mold: qualified mold assessor or industrial hygienist (X-06; Q-31-10).
- Heat, falls, low clearance and respiratory protection: qualified safety professional (C-HI07-02).
- Roof leaks or rafter rot: owner, for the referral decision (Q-30-15).
- Code, permit or R-value questions: owner, with the local building official (C-HI07-05).

## 20. Incident reporting

> - Report every injury, property damage, near miss, customer conflict, tool or equipment failure and suspected hazardous-material exposure on the incident form (source: spec JOB-008).
> - An injured person comes first: call emergency services; give only the first aid that the company's approved first-response arrangements cover (not yet defined; pending qualified safety review); medical decisions belong to emergency services and medical professionals (source: master section 1.3; safety-liability-manager skill "prioritize immediate safety and emergency response over paperwork").
> - Record facts, photos and actions taken. Do not guess causes or discuss fault or liability with the customer; refer those questions to the owner (source: spec AIR-012 boundary "liability admissions require human review").
> - Keep injury and medical details out of customer-visible notes and messages (source: spec PERM-003, COM-002).
> - Report as soon as it is safe and before the job is closed (source: spec Appendix A APXA-007). High-severity incidents go to the escalation queue (source: spec AT-035, JOB-012).

- For this service, report at least a fall or step-through of the ceiling, heat illness, insulation or dust exposure, and blower or hose failure (source: spec JOB-008; HI-07.HAZ "heat stress", "falls through ceiling", "dust/fibers").
