## 1. Purpose

Clean concrete driveways and walkways of organic growth and treat motor-oil stains with pressure washing and approved stain pre-treatment, for the canonical service HI-03 Driveway Pressure Washing & Surface Restoration, while protecting people, pets, adjacent property and drainage (source: spec Appendix B; spec HI-03.INT, HI-03.SAF; 04 HI-03 "customer-facing description", PROPOSED neutral summary; original procedure Options A and B in section 12). No outcome is promised: some stains may not lift fully (source: agreement "Stain Limitations"; 31 section 3.5 rule 5). This SOP has no repair or sealing step even though the canonical name says "Surface Restoration" (C-HI03-10).

## 2. Scope

### In scope

As written in the original procedure (section 12), pending review:
- Option A: chemical pre-treatment and rotary surface cleaning of concrete with organic growth; it is the only whole-surface path, so a driveway without growth has no written path (source: original Option A; C-HI03-08).
- Option B: spot treatment of motor-oil stains on concrete, matching the pricing line "Heavy Motor Oil Extraction Surcharge" (source: original Option B; pricing file).
- Surfaces: concrete only, as the steps are written; any other hardscape surface needs its own approved method (source: 04 section 5.4 rule (b), PROPOSED; C-HI03-08).

### Out of scope

- Not covered - pending review (C-HI03-03): perimeter wand detailing, which the agreement includes "as outlined in" this SOP; no step, nozzle or quality check exists.
- Not covered - pending review (C-HI03-05): the post-wash bio-shield spray, sold as an option in pricing and described as included in the main plan; no product, SDS or step exists.
- Not covered - pending review: rust-line treatment promised in the main plan; no product or step exists (source: 31 section 10).
- Not covered - pending review (C-HI03-08): sidewalks, entry brick steps, patios and courtyards of other materials, and a general wash path for surfaces without growth or oil, all sold in the main plan or pricing; pavers and brick are not offered unless assessed (source: 04 HI-03 "exclusions").
- Not covered - pending review (C-HI03-V01): old or set-in oil saturation; Option B is written for a fresh oil spot.
- Not offered under this SOP until the owner decides (source: 04 HI-03 "exclusions", GAP): sealing, crack repair, painted or coated surfaces, wood decks and fences (PT-02 preparation or an owner-decided line, Q-04-09), siding and roofs.

## 10. Stop-work conditions

> **Stop-work rule (all HandyHub services).** You have the right to stop work. For any condition below, stop the affected work and escalate before continuing (source: spec START-011, JOB-004):
> - an unsafe condition for you, the customer, occupants, pets or the public;
> - concealed damage, substrate failure, moisture or an active leak;
> - suspect hazardous material, such as possible asbestos, lead paint or mold; do not disturb it;
> - electrical, plumbing, gas, HVAC or structural work that is not in the approved scope or needs a licensed trade;
> - an aggressive occupant or animal;
> - severe weather or unsafe heat or cold (source: spec BRF-008);
> - any material mismatch between the site and the briefing, approved scope, materials, access or approvals.
>
> Do not continue through an unsafe or restricted condition to keep the schedule (source: master section 12). A customer request, a checkbox or a signature does not clear a safety hold (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; risk-triage playbook L27). In an emergency, move people away and call emergency services first (source: spec BRF-013; risk-triage playbook L7). Then use Stop Work / Report Hazard in the app (source: spec UXE-004) and follow section 19.

Service-specific stop triggers (source: spec HI-03.SAF unless noted; PROPOSED wording pending qualified review):
- People or pets enter the wash zone: stop washing until they are clear (X-11).
- The test on an inconspicuous area shows etching, striping or other damage: stop and escalate (HI-03.SAF "test inconspicuous area"; HI-03.QAC "without etching/striping").
- The pressure is not confirmed as appropriate for the surface: do not start washing (HI-03.SAF "use surface-appropriate pressure"; C-HI03-08).
- Electrical fixtures, outlets, openings or landscaping cannot be protected: do not wash toward them; stop and escalate (HI-03.SAF "protect electrical/openings/landscape").
- Runoff cannot be managed: stop (HI-03.SAF "manage runoff").
- Damaged substrate: stop, record (JOB-004) and escalate (HI-03.SAF "stop for damaged substrate").
- Water frozen or unavailable, or severe weather: record No Start / Blocked instead of starting (spec SCH-008, START-011; agreement "Water Access Mandatory").

- Arrival and completion updates are drafts; the worker or an administrator controls the actual send according to policy (source: spec UXE-008).
- Record the walkthrough outcome: accepted, accepted with punch items, unavailable or disputed (source: spec QA-004).
- Care notes come only from approved product data (source: spec QA-008).
- Blocked work: use only the approved "work blocked" message (source: spec NTF-007).
- Do not tell the customer that every stain will come out (agreement "Stain Limitations"), that products are "eco-safe" or harmless (C-HI03-V02), that the surface is safe or that mold is killed (X-06; Do not perform entry 8), and do not offer the bio-shield until it is approved (C-HI03-05).

## 18. Completion criteria

Definition of done (source: spec QA-001 to QA-006; HI-03.QAC; APXA-005, APXA-006):
- Every section 13 check answered, or an exception recorded (QA-001).
- After photos mapped to areas (QA-003).
- Cleanup done and protection removed (QA-002).
- The customer's water connection and anything else altered within scope restored (APXA-005).
- Punch items recorded with an owner and a due date (QA-005).
- Walkthrough outcome recorded (QA-004).
- No hold left open; any incident reported (section 20; APXA-007).

## 19. Escalation

> 1. Stop the affected work and keep yourself, occupants and pets away from the hazard; do not try to fix, move or disturb it (source: spec START-011; risk-triage playbook L7).
> 2. Emergency (fire, gas odor, injury, electrical contact, structural movement): call emergency services first, then `<owner decision: emergency contact>` (source: spec BRF-013).
> 3. Only if policy allows and it is safe, take the minimum action needed to prevent immediate injury or property damage, and record why and what you did (source: spec JOB-006).
> 4. Report in the app with Stop Work / Report Hazard: type, photos and severity (source: spec JOB-004, UXE-004). Before the job has started, record No Start / Blocked instead of starting (source: spec SCH-008, AT-030).
> 5. Escalate with one tap to `<owner decision: escalation order>`; the item enters the owner/supervisor queue (source: spec UXE-002, JOB-012).
> 6. Wait for release by the role named in the hold, with evidence recorded. Critical holds clear only with a recorded qualified assessment (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; 27 Roles and permissions section 6.3).
> 7. Tell the customer only what the approved "work blocked" message says; never send incident details or access codes by text or email (source: spec NTF-007, COM-002).
> 8. Resume only after the briefing is updated and re-acknowledged, with a change order if scope or price changes (source: spec START-002, FIN-008, JOB-005).

## 20. Incident reporting

> - Report every injury, property damage, near miss, customer conflict, tool or equipment failure and suspected hazardous-material exposure on the incident form (source: spec JOB-008).
> - An injured person comes first: call emergency services; give only the first aid that the company's approved first-response arrangements cover (not yet defined; pending qualified safety review); medical decisions belong to emergency services and medical professionals (source: master section 1.3; safety-liability-manager skill "prioritize immediate safety and emergency response over paperwork").
> - Record facts, photos and actions taken. Do not guess causes or discuss fault or liability with the customer; refer those questions to the owner (source: spec AIR-012 boundary "liability admissions require human review").
> - Keep injury and medical details out of customer-visible notes and messages (source: spec PERM-003, COM-002).
> - Report as soon as it is safe and before the job is closed (source: spec Appendix A APXA-007). High-severity incidents go to the escalation queue (source: spec AT-035, JOB-012).
