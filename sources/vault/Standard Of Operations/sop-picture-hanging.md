## 1. Purpose
- Hang framed art, canvases and mirrors on the wall types this SOP covers, at a placement the customer approves, using hardware whose manufacturer rating fits the item weight and the substrate (source: spec Appendix B; spec HI-01.INT, HI-01.SAF, HI-01.QAC; [agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-picture-hanging.md) "Inclusions" clause).
- The original SOP has no intro statement. This draft makes no outcome promise and is not field-approved (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 3.5 rule 5; owner facts 2026-10-02).

## 2. Scope
### In scope
- Standard hollow drywall walls: Option A in section 12 (source: original SOP heading "Problem Option A").
- Raw brick or concrete fireplace mantel faces: Option B in section 12, only after the on-site assessment required under "Do not perform" (source: original SOP heading "Problem Option B"; [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10).
- Per item: weight check and hardware selection, a level line at the customer's preferred height, fastening and bumper placement as written in section 12 (source: original SOP steps).
- Intake facts the estimate relies on: wall type and thickness; item dimensions/weight; exact placement; stud/anchor availability; number of items; height/access; customer-supplied hardware (source: spec HI-01.INT).

### Out of scope
- Not covered - pending review (C-HI01-05): plaster, including historic plaster and plaster on lath, "soft brick", tile, metal-stud and other substrates without an approved procedure. Stop and request an on-site assessment (source: [02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) C-HI01-05; [agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-picture-hanging.md) "Substrate Disclaimer" clause; [main plan](../Main%20Plans/Home%20Improvement%20Business.md) section 1).
- Not covered - pending review (C-HI01-02): items heavier than the Option A weight bands in section 12, and masonry items without the anchor manufacturer's rating recorded for the job (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10).
- Not covered - pending review (G-046): gallery-wall (multi-item) layout, which the [pricing file](../Pricing/pricing-picture-hanging.md) sells as a "Bundle" and the main plan markets, but no step covers (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10; [02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) G-046).
- Owner decision pending (OPEN QUESTION in the [04](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#HI-01) exclusions row): ceiling-hung items; wall repair or painting; frame repair; removal or patching of old holes; patching or touch-up unless the approved scope includes it (source: spec HI-01.MAT "patching touch-up materials if included").

### Do not perform
Universal entries (PROPOSED wording, [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 3.3; HUMAN REVIEW: qualified safety professional and licensing reviewer):
1. Electrical, plumbing, gas or HVAC work outside the approved scope or requiring a licensed trade (source: spec START-011 "unapproved electrical/plumbing/gas/structural scope"; master section 1.3).
2. Structural work, meaning cutting, removing, sistering or adding load-bearing members, ledgers, footings or framing, unless it is in the approved scope with the required qualified review (source: START-011; master section 1.3).
3. Disturbing suspect asbestos, lead paint or mold: stop and escalate (source: START-011 "suspect hazardous materials"; owner facts L20 "Unknown is not safe").
4. Work that is not in the approved scope or an approved change order; record customer requests instead (source: JOB-005, JOB-010, FIN-008, AT-033).
5. Working at height or using access equipment beyond the approved height and access policy (source: HI-06.SAF "beyond approved height/access policy"; DW-04.TLS "scaffolds/stilts only under approved policy").
6. Mixing or sequencing chemicals in any way the approved step and the product SDS do not allow (source: CL-01.SAF "never mix incompatible chemicals"; X-09).
7. Recording, photographing or sending customer passwords, master codes or site access codes outside the app's secured fields (source: COM-002; owner facts L17; [27](../AI%20Development%20Plans/27-roles-and-permissions.md) AUTH-009).
8. Promising results, warranties, safety, health or code compliance to the customer (source: master sections 1.4, 17 and 29; CL-07.SAF "do not make unsupported indoor-air-quality claims").

Service entries (seeds from [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10 and the register; pending the same review):
- Electrical work of any kind for this service, including picture lights or any wiring. Reason: licensed trade. Instead: stop and escalate; the owner refers the request (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10; spec START-011).
- Fireplace-mantel installs before an on-site assessment of heat and gas-appliance proximity. Reason: hazard (RISK). Instead: stop and escalate; whether fireplace mounting is offered at all is Q-31-08 (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10).
- Suspected asbestos / lead paint / mold: stop and escalate (source: spec START-011). This service drills into walls that may carry older paint or historic plaster (source: [agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-picture-hanging.md) "Substrate Disclaimer" clause; X-13).
- Hanging an item heavier than the SOP's own Option A weight bands, or a masonry item without the anchor manufacturer's rating recorded for the job. Reason: out of scope and safety. Instead: stop and escalate (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10; C-HI01-02).

## 10. Stop-work conditions

> **Stop-work rule (all HandyHub services).** You have the right to stop work. For any condition below, stop the affected work and escalate before continuing (source: spec START-011, JOB-004):
> - an unsafe condition for you, the customer, occupants, pets or the public;
> - concealed damage, substrate failure, moisture or an active leak;
> - suspect hazardous material, such as possible asbestos, lead paint or mold; do not disturb it;
> - electrical, plumbing, gas, HVAC or structural work that is not in the approved scope or needs a licensed trade;
> - an aggressive occupant or animal;
> - severe weather or unsafe heat or cold (source: spec BRF-008);
> - any material mismatch between the site and the briefing, approved scope, materials, access or approvals.
>
> Do not continue through an unsafe or restricted condition to keep the schedule (source: master section 12). A customer request, a checkbox or a signature does not clear a safety hold (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; risk-triage playbook L27). In an emergency, move people away and call emergency services first (source: spec BRF-013; risk-triage playbook L7). Then use Stop Work / Report Hazard in the app (source: spec UXE-004) and follow section 19.

Service-specific stop triggers (source: spec HI-01.SAF "verify substrate; confirm fastener capacity; two-person lift when required; protect floor/wall; stop if hidden-service risk or wall condition is uncertain"):
- Hidden-service risk or the wall condition is uncertain: stop (source: spec HI-01.SAF; C-HI01-01).
- The substrate cannot be verified or is not one this SOP covers: stop and request an on-site assessment (source: spec HI-01.SAF "verify substrate"; C-HI01-05).
- Fastener capacity cannot be confirmed from the hardware manufacturer's rating for the item weight and substrate, or the item is heavier than the SOP's own bands: stop and escalate (source: spec HI-01.SAF "confirm fastener capacity"; C-HI01-02).
- A two-person lift is required and the second person is not available: stop the lift (source: spec HI-01.SAF).
- Floor and wall cannot be protected: do not start the affected task (source: spec HI-01.SAF "protect floor/wall").
- Fireplace-mantel install without the on-site assessment of heat and gas-appliance proximity: do not start (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 10).
- A ladder is needed and no approved height/access policy exists: the task stays blocked (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 3.3 entry 5; [02](../AI%20Development%20Plans/02-gap-analysis.md) G-005).
- Every HOLD in section 12 names who clears it; critical holds clear only with a recorded qualified assessment, never by override or customer request (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 8.3).

## 17. Customer communication
- Confirm the placement with the customer before drilling and record the approval (source: spec HI-01.QAC; [agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-picture-hanging.md) "Final Placement Approval" clause).
- At the walkthrough, record the outcome: accepted, accepted with punch items, unavailable or disputed (source: spec QA-004, APXA-006).
- Explain the invoice and payment next step without making unauthorized promises (source: spec APXA-006).
- If work is blocked, use only the approved "work blocked" message (source: spec NTF-007; [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 8.5).
- Do not promise load capacity, perfect alignment or that an item will never move, and do not discuss fault with the customer; refer such questions to the owner (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 3.3 entry 8 and section 8.5; C-HI01-02, C-HI01-V01).
- Care notes only from approved product data (source: spec QA-008; [quality playbook](../Skills/LLC%2C%20legal%20administration%2C%20and%20risk/handyhub-quality-assistant/references/playbook.md)).

- After photos captured and mapped to work areas (source: spec QA-003).

## 19. Escalation

> 1. Stop the affected work and keep yourself, occupants and pets away from the hazard; do not try to fix, move or disturb it (source: spec START-011; risk-triage playbook L7).
> 2. Emergency (fire, gas odor, injury, electrical contact, structural movement): call emergency services first, then `<owner decision: emergency contact>` (source: spec BRF-013).
> 3. Only if policy allows and it is safe, take the minimum action needed to prevent immediate injury or property damage, and record why and what you did (source: spec JOB-006).
> 4. Report in the app with Stop Work / Report Hazard: type, photos and severity (source: spec JOB-004, UXE-004). Before the job has started, record No Start / Blocked instead of starting (source: spec SCH-008, AT-030).
> 5. Escalate with one tap to `<owner decision: escalation order>`; the item enters the owner/supervisor queue (source: spec UXE-002, JOB-012).
> 6. Wait for release by the role named in the hold, with evidence recorded. Critical holds clear only with a recorded qualified assessment (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; 27 Roles and permissions section 6.3).
> 7. Tell the customer only what the approved "work blocked" message says; never send incident details or access codes by text or email (source: spec NTF-007, COM-002).
> 8. Resume only after the briefing is updated and re-acknowledged, with a change order if scope or price changes (source: spec START-002, FIN-008, JOB-005).

- For this service the path above applies before any further work on the affected item when: hidden-service risk is uncertain; an item exceeds the SOP's bands or has no recorded rating; the substrate is not covered; a fireplace mantel has not been assessed; or older finishes may contain lead paint or asbestos (source: sections 10 and 12).
- Contacts and escalation order: `<owner decision: escalation order>` (Q-31-05).

## 20. Incident reporting

> - Report every injury, property damage, near miss, customer conflict, tool or equipment failure and suspected hazardous-material exposure on the incident form (source: spec JOB-008).
> - An injured person comes first: call emergency services; give only the first aid that the company's approved first-response arrangements cover (not yet defined; pending qualified safety review); medical decisions belong to emergency services and medical professionals (source: master section 1.3; safety-liability-manager skill "prioritize immediate safety and emergency response over paperwork").
> - Record facts, photos and actions taken. Do not guess causes or discuss fault or liability with the customer; refer those questions to the owner (source: spec AIR-012 boundary "liability admissions require human review").
> - Keep injury and medical details out of customer-visible notes and messages (source: spec PERM-003, COM-002).
> - Report as soon as it is safe and before the job is closed (source: spec Appendix A APXA-007). High-severity incidents go to the escalation queue (source: spec AT-035, JOB-012).

- For this service, report among others: a dropped or falling item, a drill strike on a hidden service, and damage to customer artwork, glass or walls (source: spec JOB-008; HI-01.HAZ).
