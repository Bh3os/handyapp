## 1. Purpose

- Clean and reset a short-term rental between guest stays for the canonical service CL-05 Airbnb Turnover & Vacation Rental Management: pre-clean inspection with damage photos, linen rotation, cleaning, restocking, staging and completion photos (source: spec Appendix B; original procedure in section 12; agreement "Standard Turnover" clause).
- Intended result, as the spec card's quality checks define it: "clean/reset checklist, linens/amenities, documented damage, staging standard, property locked and host notified of exceptions" (source: spec CL-05.QAC).
- The "Vacation Rental Management" part of the canonical name (co-hosting or property management) has no defined scope and is not covered by this SOP (source: [04](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#CL-05) CL-05 exclusions row; [28](../AI%20Development%20Plans/28-pricing-estimating-and-job-costing.md) section 3.13).

## 2. Scope

### In scope
- Phase 1: pre-clean damage scan, damage and extreme-mess photos, amenity check, thermostat setting and router check; photo delivery is held (section 12) (source: original procedure, section 12).
- Phase 2: stripping beds, collecting towels and starting a wash where laundry is done on site (source: original procedure, section 12; spec CL-05.TLS "laundry resources as applicable").
- Phase 3: high-touch disinfection, fridge shelves and handle bars (the guest-food discard is held, C-CL05-10) and bathroom cleaning (source: original procedure, section 12).
- Phase 4: bed making, amenity and kitchen restocking, trash bags and staging; who supplies restock items is undecided (Q-29-13) (source: original procedure, section 12).
- Phase 5: floors and completion photos; photo delivery is held (section 12) (source: original procedure, section 12).
- Lock-up check and exception notice to the host before leaving (hold added in section 12) (source: spec CL-05.SAF, CL-05.QAC).

### Out of scope
- Not covered - pending review (C-CL05-V01): kitchen deep cleaning, which the main plan sells ("Deep cleaning kitchens and bathrooms"); the only kitchen cleaning step here is the fridge in "Kitchen Check" (source: main plan section 5; 31 section 10).
- Not covered - pending review (OPEN QUESTION): pool and hot-tub areas of the "Luxury / Pool Property" type that the pricing file sells (source: pricing file; 31 section 10).
- Not covered - pending review (C-CL05-12): off-site laundering and linen supply, which the inventory file ("pre-pressed stock linens stored in your car vehicle") and Phase 4 ("professionally laundered sheets") assume; the laundry model is undecided (source: inventory file; 02 C-CL05-12).
- Not covered - pending review: co-hosting or property-management work and restock purchasing for the host, which the pricing file sells as add-ons ("Option A", "Option B"); not offered until legal and licensing review (source: pricing file; 28 section 3.13; Q-28-18, Q-29-13).
- Out of scope until the owner decides (candidates, PROPOSED): repairs (report and refer to a separate estimate), pest treatment, biohazard remediation, deep cleaning (CL-02) and guest communication (source: [04](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#CL-05) CL-05 exclusions row).

## 10. Stop-work conditions

> **Stop-work rule (all HandyHub services).** You have the right to stop work. For any condition below, stop the affected work and escalate before continuing (source: spec START-011, JOB-004):
> - an unsafe condition for you, the customer, occupants, pets or the public;
> - concealed damage, substrate failure, moisture or an active leak;
> - suspect hazardous material, such as possible asbestos, lead paint or mold; do not disturb it;
> - electrical, plumbing, gas, HVAC or structural work that is not in the approved scope or needs a licensed trade;
> - an aggressive occupant or animal;
> - severe weather or unsafe heat or cold (source: spec BRF-008);
> - any material mismatch between the site and the briefing, approved scope, materials, access or approvals.
>
> Do not continue through an unsafe or restricted condition to keep the schedule (source: master section 12). A customer request, a checkbox or a signature does not clear a safety hold (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; risk-triage playbook L27). In an emergency, move people away and call emergency services first (source: spec BRF-013; risk-triage playbook L7). Then use Stop Work / Report Hazard in the app (source: spec UXE-004) and follow section 19.

Service-specific stop triggers and holds for CL-05 (pending qualified review):

## 17. Customer communication

- The customer is the host or property manager. Arrival and completion updates are drafts, and the worker or admin controls the send (source: spec UXE-008; agreement preamble).
- Exceptions - damage, missing or broken amenities, guest items left behind, urgent maintenance and any lock-up problem - are reported to the host through the approved channel and logged on the job (source: spec CL-05.QAC "host notified of exceptions", JOB-009; Q-27-16).
- Guest communication is a candidate exclusion for the owner to decide (source: 04 CL-05 exclusions row).
- Never send access codes, incident details or internal notes by SMS or email (source: spec COM-002).
- No promises of review outcomes, completion times or absolute results to hosts or guests (source: 31 section 3.3 entry 8; 31 section 3.5 rule 5).
- Blocked work: use only the approved "work blocked" message (source: spec NTF-007).
- Walkthrough: where the host or a representative is present, record the outcome - accepted, accepted with punch items, unavailable or disputed (source: spec QA-004).

## 18. Completion criteria

- Every check in section 13 is answered or has a recorded exception (source: spec QA-001).
- The property is locked and the host is notified of exceptions (source: spec CL-05.SAF, CL-05.QAC).
- Damage is documented, and completion photos are captured in the app and mapped to rooms (source: spec CL-05.QAC, QA-003).
- No guest property was discarded without policy; items left behind are recorded (source: spec CL-05.SAF).
- No access code appears in any note, photo or message (source: spec COM-002; 27 AUTH-009, PROPOSED).
- Cleanup is done and the walkthrough outcome is recorded (source: spec QA-002, QA-004).
- Host-supplied restock and linens have their START-009 record (source: spec START-009).
- Closeout: unresolved issues and incident or change follow-ups are submitted; the job closes only when the required operational and financial steps are complete (source: spec APXA-006, APXA-007).

## 19. Escalation

> 1. Stop the affected work and keep yourself, occupants and pets away from the hazard; do not try to fix, move or disturb it (source: spec START-011; risk-triage playbook L7).
> 2. Emergency (fire, gas odor, injury, electrical contact, structural movement): call emergency services first, then `<owner decision: emergency contact>` (source: spec BRF-013).
> 3. Only if policy allows and it is safe, take the minimum action needed to prevent immediate injury or property damage, and record why and what you did (source: spec JOB-006).
> 4. Report in the app with Stop Work / Report Hazard: type, photos and severity (source: spec JOB-004, UXE-004). Before the job has started, record No Start / Blocked instead of starting (source: spec SCH-008, AT-030).
> 5. Escalate with one tap to `<owner decision: escalation order>`; the item enters the owner/supervisor queue (source: spec UXE-002, JOB-012).
> 6. Wait for release by the role named in the hold, with evidence recorded. Critical holds clear only with a recorded qualified assessment (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; 27 Roles and permissions section 6.3).
> 7. Tell the customer only what the approved "work blocked" message says; never send incident details or access codes by text or email (source: spec NTF-007, COM-002).
> 8. Resume only after the briefing is updated and re-acknowledged, with a change order if scope or price changes (source: spec START-002, FIN-008, JOB-005).

## 20. Incident reporting

> - Report every injury, property damage, near miss, customer conflict, tool or equipment failure and suspected hazardous-material exposure on the incident form (source: spec JOB-008).
> - An injured person comes first: call emergency services; give only the first aid that the company's approved first-response arrangements cover (not yet defined; pending qualified safety review); medical decisions belong to emergency services and medical professionals (source: master section 1.3; safety-liability-manager skill "prioritize immediate safety and emergency response over paperwork").
> - Record facts, photos and actions taken. Do not guess causes or discuss fault or liability with the customer; refer those questions to the owner (source: spec AIR-012 boundary "liability admissions require human review").
> - Keep injury and medical details out of customer-visible notes and messages (source: spec PERM-003, COM-002).
> - Report as soon as it is safe and before the job is closed (source: spec Appendix A APXA-007). High-severity incidents go to the escalation queue (source: spec AT-035, JOB-012).
