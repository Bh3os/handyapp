## 1. Purpose
- This SOP covers how HandyHub workers clean gutters and downspouts, re-pitch sagging sections, reseal leaking joints and install aluminum micro-mesh guards for **HI-06 Gutter Maintenance & Leaf Protection**, only within the approved height and access policy (source: spec Appendix B name; [04](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#HI-06) HI-06 card, PROPOSED neutral service summary).
- It states how the work is done and promises no outcome to the customer (source: [31](../AI%20Development%20Plans/31-sop-standard.md) section 3.5 rule 5). The original file has no intro statement, so no original line is moved into this section.

## 2. Scope
### In scope
- Option A as written in section 12: manual clearance of gutters and downspout flushing, pitch realignment of sagging sections, and resealing of leaking corners and end caps (source: original SOP; agreement "Inclusions" clause).
- Option B as written in section 12: sizing, placing and fastening aluminum micro-mesh guard screens (source: original SOP; pricing "Premium Micro-Mesh Gutter Guards" line).
- Proposed estimate line types: `cleaning`, `minor-repair` (approved add-on) and `guards` (project), all under one height and access policy (source: 04 section 5.7, PROPOSED DECISION D-04-13).
- Leaks and sags are addressed only within the approved scope (source: spec HI-06.QAC "leaks/sags addressed only in approved scope").

### Out of scope
- Fascia and soffit carpentry, including fascia board replacement: outside the catalog; refer out (source: agreement "Fascia Board Decay" clause; 04 section 5.9; X-05).
- Roofing and roof repair: refer out to a roofing professional (source: 04 section 5.9).
- Gutter replacement, seamless-gutter fabrication and underground drain excavation (source: 04 HI-06 card, exclusions row, GAP; owner to confirm).
- Not covered - pending review (C-HI06-01): two-story work until the height/access policy exists, although pricing sells a two-story package (source: pricing "Two-Story Suburban Family Home" line; 31 section 10).
- Not covered - pending review (GAP, 04 HI-06 card): pre-cleaning before guard installation. The pricing guard example includes a "complimentary deep flush clean", but Option B has no cleaning step (source: pricing guard example line; 31 section 10).
- Not covered - pending review (C-HI06-08): stainless-steel mesh guards, which the main plan offers but the inventory does not stock and no step covers (source: main plan "Gutter Guard Mounting" bullet; inventory file).

### Do not perform

3. Disturbing suspect asbestos, lead paint or mold: stop and escalate (source: START-011 "suspect hazardous materials"; owner facts L20 "Unknown is not safe").
4. Work that is not in the approved scope or an approved change order; record customer requests instead (source: JOB-005, JOB-010, FIN-008, AT-033).
5. Working at height or using access equipment beyond the approved height and access policy (source: HI-06.SAF "beyond approved height/access policy"; DW-04.TLS "scaffolds/stilts only under approved policy").
6. Mixing or sequencing chemicals in any way the approved step and the product SDS do not allow (source: CL-01.SAF "never mix incompatible chemicals"; X-09).
7. Recording, photographing or sending customer passwords, master codes or site access codes outside the app's secured fields (source: COM-002; owner facts L17; [27](../AI%20Development%20Plans/27-roles-and-permissions.md) AUTH-009).

## 10. Stop-work conditions
> **Stop-work rule (all HandyHub services).** You have the right to stop work. For any condition below, stop the affected work and escalate before continuing (source: spec START-011, JOB-004):
> - an unsafe condition for you, the customer, occupants, pets or the public;
> - concealed damage, substrate failure, moisture or an active leak;
> - suspect hazardous material, such as possible asbestos, lead paint or mold; do not disturb it;
> - electrical, plumbing, gas, HVAC or structural work that is not in the approved scope or needs a licensed trade;
> - an aggressive occupant or animal;
> - severe weather or unsafe heat or cold (source: spec BRF-008);
> - any material mismatch between the site and the briefing, approved scope, materials, access or approvals.
>
> Do not continue through an unsafe or restricted condition to keep the schedule (source: master section 12). A customer request, a checkbox or a signature does not clear a safety hold (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; risk-triage playbook L27). In an emergency, move people away and call emergency services first (source: spec BRF-013; risk-triage playbook L7). Then use Stop Work / Report Hazard in the app (source: spec UXE-004) and follow section 19.

## 12. Step-by-step procedure
All original procedure lines appear below verbatim and in their original order; the two original "Problem Option" headings are demoted one level. Added lines start with `HOLD` or `FLAG` inside a quote block and are pending qualified review.

> HOLD (C-HI06-01): whole-SOP hold. Every step below is done at gutter or roof-edge height, and no approved height/access, fall-protection, ladder, power-line or weather control exists yet. Do not perform any step below until the approved height/access policy exists and this SOP passes competent review. Cleared only by recorded approval of the qualified safety professional and the owner. (source: spec HI-06.HAZ, HI-06.SAF; 31 section 10; owner facts 2026-10-02 "Unknown is not safe")

> HOLD (C-HI06-01): before any ladder is raised for the steps below, complete the section 11 checks: weather and ground, overhead power lines (no clearance distance is set here), ladder setup per the approved policy, and the exclusion zone. Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-06.SAF; 31 section 10)

### 🚨 Problem Option A: Fixing Sagging Pitches & Seam Miter Leaks
- [ ] **Manual Clearance:** Scoop out packed leaves from the gutter lines, then flush the downspouts with a high-volume water line to clear subterranean blockages.

> HOLD (31 section 10): if a downspout's underground drain backs up while flushing in the step above, stop flushing and escalate (section 19). Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10; RISK, unverified)

> HOLD (C-HI06-04): before the "Pitch Realignment" step below, inspect and photograph the fascia behind each sagging section. If the fascia is rotted or its condition is uncertain, do not drive fasteners: stop, record the condition (JOB-004) and escalate for a change order or referral; fascia carpentry is outside the catalog (X-05). Pending owner and qualified carpentry review. (source: agreement "Fascia Board Decay" clause; 02 C-HI06-04)

- [ ] **Pitch Realignment:** Locate sagging gutter sections. Drive heavy-duty, reinforced 7-inch aluminum gutter spikes or structural timber screws straight through the gutter face into solid fascia board framing to fix the water-flow pitch toward downspouts.

> FLAG (C-HI06-V01): the step above has no slope-measurement step and no flow test, while the main plan says "We measure gutter slopes" and spec HI-06.QAC requires "flow verified". Pitch values come only from the gutter manufacturer or a qualified reviewer; none is set here. See the flow check in section 13. Pending owner review.

- [ ] **Joint Resealing:** Scrape away old, cracked caulking from leaking corners and end caps using a wire wheel brush. Dry the aluminum surface completely and apply a heavy bead of commercial self-leveling waterproof gutter sealant.

### 🚨 Problem Option B: Mounting Heavy-Gauge Micro-Mesh Gutter Guards
- [ ] **Section Sizing:** Measure and cut 4-foot lengths of premium aluminum micro-mesh screens using heavy metal tin snips.

> HOLD (C-HI06-01): before the "Roofline Slide" step below, confirm that the approved height/access policy covers roof-edge work; until it does, do not perform the step. If roof conditions are unsafe, stop (spec HI-06.SAF). The word "safely" in the step is not a safety determination. Whether roof-edge guard installation is offered at all, and how shingle disturbance is handled with the customer, is open (Q-31-08). Pending qualified safety professional and roofing professional review. (source: 31 section 10; spec HI-06.HAZ "roof edge")

- [ ] **Roofline Slide:** Slide the back flange of the gutter guard screen safely underneath the first layer of roof shingles.
- [ ] **Fastener Locking:** Secure the front lip of the guard screen firmly onto the outer aluminum lip of the gutter frame using self-tapping hexagonal zip screws spaced every 12 inches.
