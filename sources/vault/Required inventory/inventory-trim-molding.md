## 🧳 A. Saws, Nailers & Joining Hardware (ماكينات وأدوات النجارة)
- [ ] **12-inch Dual-Bevel Sliding Compound Miter Saw:** Mounted on a rolling stand platform for high-accuracy angle adjustments.
- [ ] **20V Cordless Brad Nailer (18-Gauge) & Pin Nailer (23-Gauge):** Eliminates hose dragging inside luxury homes.
- [ ] **Flexible Steel Caulking Spatulas & Putty Knives:** For dressing nail holes flat.
- [ ] **Contour Coping Saw:** Handheld saw with fine carbon steel blades for manual coping cut profiles.

## 🧴 B. Adhesives & Finish Fillers (المعجون والمواد اللاصقة)
- [ ] **Premium Paintable Latex Caulk (Elastic formulation):** Seals long seam lines between trim moldings and walls without cracking.
- [ ] **High-Strength Liquid-Nails Construction Adhesive:** For bonding wainscoting squares flat onto drywall planes.
- [ ] **Professional Wood Putty / Vinyl Joint Filler:** Non-shrinking compound to flush out countersunk brad head holes.
- [ ] **Premium Wood Glues (Polyurethane or PVA base):** For clamping outside miter corners together cleanly.
