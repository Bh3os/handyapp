## 🧴 A. The Chemical Arsenal (المنظفات الكيميائية)
- [ ] **Luxury Signature Room Scent Mists:** Lavender, linen, or white-tea essential oils to mist rooms after closure.
- [ ] **Premium Leather Conditioner & Cleaner:** For high-end luxury leather sofas, chairs, and desk pads.
- [ ] **Pure Wood Conditioning Beeswax / Polish:** For maintaining exposed authentic wood grain furniture.
- [ ] **Stainless Steel Oil-Based Polish:** Leaves a rich protective film that resists fingerprints on upscale luxury kitchen appliances.

> FLAG (C-CL06-02, C-CL06-12): none of the four products above has an SDS on file; a chemical product without a reviewed SDS is not carried to a job (29 section 8.3 interim rule, PROPOSED; HUMAN REVIEW: qualified safety professional). The essential-oil scent mists are used only after a recorded fragrance preference and allergy check covering occupants and pets, pending safety review (C-CL06-02; spec CL-06.INT "preferred products"). The leather conditioner and the stainless steel polish have no SOP step (NO SOP STEP - define or retire after owner review), and whether the SOP's "Vanity Polish" step uses the beeswax polish is to be confirmed (C-CL06-12). Each product is used only on a surface whose care instructions allow it (spec CL-06.SAF "follow garment/surface care labels").

## 🧳 B. Hardware & Essential Tools (الأدوات والمعدات)
- [ ] **Professional Clothes Steamer (Handheld & Stand Vertical):** High-wattage steamer for removing creases from suits, silk dresses, and long bed skirts.
- [ ] **Ultra-Quiet Commercial Vacuum Cleaner:** To perform silent carpet maintenance without disrupting residents working from home.
- [ ] **Premium Linen Sheet Straps & Tuck Paddles:** Plastic paddles used to slide under heavy mattresses to tuck sheets without lifting, ensuring tight hospital corners.
- [ ] **Fine Camel-Hair Dusting Brushes:** For dusting intricate art frames, crystals, sculptures, and piano keyboards without leaving scratches.

> FLAG (C-CL06-02): the steamer line above describes high-wattage equipment; burn and electrical-load controls are pending safety review and come from the manufacturer instructions (31 section 10 CL-06), and each garment is steamed only within its care label (spec CL-06.SAF). The tuck paddles are the PROPOSED control for mattress lifting (the SOP's draft hold before "Hospital Corners"). "without leaving scratches" in the brush line is the file's own description, not a verified result; which art, crystal, sculptures or instruments a worker may touch is the customer's written authorization (spec CL-06.QAC "high-value items undisturbed except authorized care"), and items above reach wait for a height and access policy (Q-31-09).
