## 🧳 A. Insulation Media & Containment Elements
- [ ] **Premium Compressed Blowing Cellulose / Fiberglass Insulation Bags:** Sourced in bulk pallets.
- [ ] **Styrofoam Soffit Ventilation Baffles (Rafter Vents):** Fixed channels to guarantee continuous roof ventilation airflow.
- [ ] **Fire-Rated Fireproofing Caulk & Foam Spray:** For sealing electrical wire drill wire holes through top plates.
- [ ] **Thick Poly Masking Tarps:** 4-mil clear rolls to wrap floor entries directly below the ceiling access hatch.

## 🛠️ B. Pneumatic Machinery & Protective Gear (ماكينات النفخ وأدوات الحماية)
- [ ] **Commercial Pneumatic Insulation Blowing Machine (Hopper Unit):** High-output agitation blower (can be rig-mounted or rented).
- [ ] **Flexible Lightweight Insulation Delivery Hose (100-foot):** Fitted with heavy-duty interlocking couplings.
- [ ] **NIOSH-Approved N95 / P100 Disposable Dust Respirators:** Mandatory to shield workers from airborne glass fiber particulates.
- [ ] **High-Intensity Cordless LED Work Lights / Headlamps:** For illuminating dark joist bays inside attics safely.
