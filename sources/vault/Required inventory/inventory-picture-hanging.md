## 🧳 A. Precision Hardware & Fasteners (المسامير وأدوات التثبيت)
- [ ] **Heavy-Duty Zinc Self-Drilling Toggle Anchors:** Rated for 50–100 lbs on hollow drywall.
- [ ] **Standard Plastic Expansion Sleeves & Anchor Assortment:** For light to medium frames.
- [ ] **Monkey Hooks / Gorilla Hooks Steel Wire Assortment:** For tool-free drywall hanging under 10 lbs.
- [ ] **Heavy-Duty Galvanized Masonry Screws (Tapcon style):** For secure brick/concrete mounting.
- [ ] **Adhesive Clear Rubber Bumper Pads:** Placed on frame back corners to prevent wall scuffing.

## 🛠️ B. Alignment & Mechanical Tools (أدوات القياس والثقب)
- [ ] **Self-Leveling Cross-Line Green Laser Level:** With a heavy-duty tripod or telescoping pole mount.
- [ ] **20V Cordless Brushless Hammer Drill & Impact Driver Combo:** For fast substrate boring.
- [ ] **Carbide-Tipped Masonry Drill Bits:** 3/16-inch and 1/4-inch bits specifically for brick fireplace mantels.
- [ ] **Deep-Sensing Stud Finder:** Capable of reading center columns through thick sheetrock and plaster.
- [ ] **Magnetic Pocket Bubble Levels & 24-inch Box Level:** For physical cross-checking.
