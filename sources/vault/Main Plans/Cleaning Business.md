## 🧹 1. Routine House Cleaning

> Canonical service: **CL-01** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#CL-01) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-routine-cleaning.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-routine-cleaning.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-routine-cleaning.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop%20Routine%20Cleaning%20Checklist.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Consistent, Reliable Upkeep for a Beautiful, Stress-Free Home*

### 📣 Service Overview
Maintaining a tidy home shouldn't take over your weekends. Our Routine House Cleaning service is designed for busy families and professionals who want to come home to a fresh, organized space every single week. We handle the essential, day-to-day upkeep—dusting, vacuuming, sanitizing, and tidying—with consistent precision so you can reclaim your free time without sacrificing a clean living environment.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
- [ ] **Dusting and wiping:** Removing dust from accessible surfaces including tables, shelves, countertops, and home appliances.
- [ ] **Floor care:** Vacuuming carpets and rugs alongside sweeping and mopping hard floor surfaces throughout the house.
- [ ] **Bathroom sanitization:** Cleaning and disinfecting essential fixtures such as toilets, sinks, showers, tubs, and mirrors.
- [ ] **Kitchen tidying:** Wiping down exterior surfaces of large appliances, cleaning countertops, and washing the sink area.
- [ ] **Waste management:** Emptying trash bins across all rooms and replacing them with fresh liners.

## ✨ 2. Elite Deep Cleaning Service

> Canonical service: **CL-02** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#CL-02) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-deep-cleaning.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-deep-cleaning.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-deep-cleaning.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop%20Deep%20Cleaning%20CheckList.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Rejuvenate Your Space with a Meticulous, Top-to-Bottom Reset*

### 📣 Service Overview
When routine wiping isn't enough, your home needs a deep structural reset. Over time, grease accumulates in the kitchen, soap scum etches into bathroom tiles, and dust hides underneath heavy furniture. Our Elite Deep Cleaning service targets these neglected, hard-to-reach areas. We recommend this intensive service once every few months, before major holidays, or as a baseline first-time visit to restore your home to showroom condition.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
- [ ] **Grease elimination:** Scrubbing accumulated grease, grime, and oil splatters from kitchen backsplashes and cooking areas.
- [ ] **Interior appliance washing:** Cleaning the hidden interior compartments of ovens, refrigerators, and microwaves.
- [ ] **High and low detailing:** Washing walls, baseboards, ceiling fans, and removing cobwebs from high corners.
- [ ] **Grout and tile scrubbing:** Removing stubborn soap scum, limescale, and mildew from bathroom tiles and grout lines.
- [ ] **Sub-surface cleaning:** Moving light furniture to vacuum and mop areas that are typically hidden or neglected.

## 🏗️ 4. Post-Construction & Post-Renovation Cleaning

> Canonical service: **CL-04** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#CL-04) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-post-construction.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-post-construction.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-post-construction.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop%20Post-Construction%20Cleaning%20Checklist.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Eradicate Construction Dust and Turn Your Renovation into a Masterpiece*

### 📣 Service Overview
Once the contractors leave, a layer of ultra-fine, abrasive drywall dust, sawdust, and plaster residue settles on every single surface—including inside light fixtures and inside vents. Standard house cleaning tools will simply spread this white film around. Our industrial-grade Post-Construction division utilizes specialized HEPA filtration vacuums and scraping tools to extract fine particulates and make your brand-new renovation safe, clean, and ready for immediate occupancy.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
- [ ] **Fine dust extraction:** Vacuuming and wiping down microscopic drywall and sawdust that settles on all surfaces.
- [ ] **Paint and adhesive removal:** Carefully scraping off paint splatters, plaster droplets, and tape residue from floors and glass.
- [ ] **Label and sticker peeling:** Removing protective films, barcodes, and manufacturing stickers from newly installed fixtures.
- [ ] **Airborne particle cleanup:** Wiping down light fixtures, vents, and walls where construction dust clings.
- [ ] **Final polish:** Buffing and polishing brand new surfaces, metals, and floors so they are ready for immediate occupancy.

## 💨 7. Air Duct & Ventilation Cleaning

> Canonical service: **CL-07** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#CL-07) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-air-duct-cleaning.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-air-duct-cleaning.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-air-duct-cleaning.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop%20Air%20Duct%20and%20Vent%20Cleaning%20Checklist.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Clean Air, Lower Energy Bills—Professional HVAC & Vent Restoration*

### 📣 Service Overview
Out of sight should not mean out of mind. Your HVAC system continuously circulates air throughout your home, and over time, main air ducts become heavy breeding grounds for dust mites, pet dander, mold spores, and allergens. This ruins interior air quality and forces your AC unit to work harder, increasing your energy bills. Our ventilation cleaning service uses high-powered negative pressure extraction to clear your ductwork, letting your family breathe pure, clean air.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
- [ ] **Filter replacement:** Removing clogged, dusty air filters and installing brand new HVAC or AC filters.
- [ ] **Grille and register scrubbing:** Washing the metal vent covers and intake grilles to eliminate visible dust buildup.
- [ ] **Hose and vacuum extraction:** Utilizing specialized vacuum equipment to pull dust and debris out of main air ducts.
- [ ] **Exhaust fan clearing:** Cleaning out greasy or dusty exhaust fans in bathrooms and kitchen range hoods.
- [ ] **Air quality optimization:** Removing allergens, mold spores, and pet dander from the ventilation pathway to improve breathing conditions.
