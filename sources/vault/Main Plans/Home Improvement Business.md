## 🖼️ 1. Precision Picture Hanging & Art Installation

*Target Headline for Website: Secure, Laser-Level Placement for Fine Art, Heavy Mirrors, and Gallery Walls*

### 📣 Service Overview
A beautiful piece of art or a grand statement mirror can instantly elevate a room—but hanging them incorrectly can lead to crooked frames, damaged drywall, or catastrophic falls. Our professional art installation service eliminates the guesswork. Whether you need a heavy, ornate floor mirror mounted safely above a mantel, a seamless grid-pattern gallery wall of family photos, or fragile canvas paintings secured, we deliver pinpoint, laser-level accuracy that protects both your artwork and your walls.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
- [ ] **Laser-Guided Layout Planning:** We map out exact dimensions using self-leveling laser lines to guarantee perfect symmetry and spacing before making any wall punctures.
- [ ] **Substrate & Stud Verification:** We scan your walls to locate structural studs or determine substrate composition (drywall, plaster, or raw brick) to select the perfect anchoring hardware.
- [ ] **Heavy-Duty Anchor Fastening:** We install professional toggle bolts, heavy-duty drywall anchors, or masonry sleeves rated to hold up to 100+ lbs safely without sagging.
- [ ] **Final Symmetrical Tuning:** We mount the artwork, verify level alignment under secondary light checks, and add bumper pads to protect your wall paint from back-frame scratching.

## 🥩 2. Deep-Clean Barbecue & Grill Maintenance

> Canonical service: **HI-02** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#HI-02) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-grill-cleaning.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-grill-cleaning.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-grill-cleaning.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop-grill-cleaning.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Restore Your Outdoor Grill to Factory Shine—Eco-Safe, Carbon-Stripping Service*

### 📣 Service Overview
Over months of grilling, stubborn grease layers, burnt food particles, and toxic carbon crust build up inside your barbecue pit. This buildup doesn't just create sudden flare-ups and uneven heating zones; it actively alters the flavor of your food and cuts your expensive grill’s lifespan in half. Our premium barbecue detailing service completely tears down, degreases, and sanitizes your grill using non-toxic, eco-safe methods, returning your outdoor cooking setup to showroom condition.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
- [ ] **Dismantling & Component Soak:** We carefully remove grill grates, flavorizer bars, heat deflectors, and internal drip pans to submerge them in a heavy, carbon-liquefying hot bath.
- [ ] **Mechanical Scraping & Detailing:** We scrub the inner cookbox down to raw metal, utilizing specialized degreasing scrapers and steam brushes to dissolve baked-on grease webs.
- [ ] **Venturi Tube & Burner Clearance:** We clean out internal burner tube ports and clear obstructions from insect nests or ash blocks, ensuring a safe, consistent gas flow and uniform flame pattern.
- [ ] **Stainless Steel Exterior Polish:** We dry-wipe and buff all external hoods, side shelves, and control knobs using premium, streak-free stainless steel protective oils.
