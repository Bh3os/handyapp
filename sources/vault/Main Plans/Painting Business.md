## 🏠 1. Professional Residential Interior Painting

> Canonical service: **PT-01** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#PT-01) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-interior-painting.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-interior-painting.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-interior-painting.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop-interior-painting.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Transform Your Living Spaces with Flawless, Crisp Cut-Lines*

### 📣 Service Overview
Your home is your sanctuary, and nothing updates a room faster or adds more immediate value than a premium coat of paint. Whether you want to brighten up a dark living room, create a modern accent wall in your master bedroom, or refresh your entire home with a clean, cohesive color palette, our interior painting specialists deliver showroom-quality results. Operating with maximum care, we treat your home with the utmost respect, ensuring crisp lines, rich colors, and absolutely zero mess left behind.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
* **White-Glove Site Protection:** We move heavy furniture to the center of the room and cover all flooring, fixtures, and personal belongings with premium canvas drop cloths and protective plastic sheeting.
* **Surface Defect Repair:** Before a single drop of paint is applied, our team patches wall dents, repairs minor drywall cracks, and sands surfaces perfectly flat to ensure a smooth finish.
* **Precision Edge Cutting:** We don't rely on sloppy shortcuts. Our artisans execute sharp, laser-straight cut-lines along all baseboards, trim moldings, window frames, and ceiling borders.
* **Premium Multi-Coat Finish:** We apply a high-hiding foundation primer followed by a minimum of two coats of premium, eco-friendly (Zero-VOC) latex paint, providing rich color depth and long-lasting washability.

## 🪵 2. Outdoor Deck & Fence Staining and Sealing

> Canonical service: **PT-02** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#PT-02) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-deck-staining.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-deck-staining.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-deck-staining.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop-deck-staining.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Restore and Protect Your Backyard Oasis—Professional Wood Sealing*

### 📣 Service Overview
The harsh weather patterns of the Kentuckiana region can quickly turn your beautiful wooden deck or privacy fence into a gray, weathered, and splintered hazard. Unprotected exterior wood absorbs rainwater, leading to structural rot, warping, and costly structural failures. Our specialized staining and sealing service deep-cleans weather-beaten lumber and seals the pores with premium, UV-resistant oils, restoring the rich natural wood grain while extending its lifespan for years to come.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
* **Chemical Wood Stripping:** We apply specialized, eco-safe wood conditioners and brighteners to break down failed older stains, ground-in dirt, and dark mold spores.
* **Hydraulic Pressure Cleansing:** We use calibrated, low-pressure power washing to deeply flush away gray cellulose wood cells and dirt without gouging or tearing the delicate soft grain of the wood.
* **Surface De-Splinter Sanding:** Once fully dry, we lightly sand heavy handrail areas and footpaths to eliminate painful splinters and ensure a comfortable, smooth-to-the-touch surface.
* **Industrial Oil Saturation:** We spray and brush deep-penetrating transparent, semi-transparent, or solid oil-based stains deep into the dry wood fibers, providing a heavy moisture barrier and advanced UV sun protection.

## 🧱 3. Brick Limewashing & Elastomeric Masonry Coatings

> Canonical service: **PT-03** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#PT-03) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-brick-limewashing.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-brick-limewashing.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-brick-limewashing.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop-brick-limewashing.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Classic European Aesthetics—Breathable Protection for Your Brick Home*

### 📣 Service Overview
Painting raw exterior brick requires specialized masonry knowledge. Standard exterior paints trap moisture inside the brick's porous core, causing the paint to blister, peel, and eventually crack the masonry during winter freeze-and-thaw cycles. Our specialized brick coatings division offers authentic European Limewash and advanced elastomeric formulas. These architectural coatings chemically fuse with your brick, transforming your home's curb appeal with a luxury finish while allowing the masonry to breathe naturally.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
* **Industrial Masonry Wash:** We power wash the entire brick exterior with specialized stone cleansers to remove caked soot, salt efflorescence, and organic mildew layers.
* **Elite Architectural Masking:** We meticulously tape off, seal, and wrap all windows, doors, gutters, and adjacent landscapes in protective films to completely isolate the spray zone.
* **Authentic Limewash Application:** For a rustic look, we apply Class-A old-world European Limewash made of slaked lime and natural pigments, which can be artistically distressed to show hints of the underlying brick.
* **Elastomeric Waterproof Shielding:** For solid modern upgrades, we spray thick elastomeric masonry coatings that bridge hairline cracks, flexing with your home's structural movement while preventing wind-driven rain penetration.

## 🩹 4. Wallpaper Removal & Surface Restoration

> Canonical service: **PT-04** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#PT-04) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-wallpaper-removal.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-wallpaper-removal.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-wallpaper-removal.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop-wallpaper-removal.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Say Goodbye to Dated Wallpaper—Restore Your Walls to Fresh Canvas*

### 📣 Service Overview
Stripping away decades-old, stubborn wallpaper can quickly turn into a home renovation nightmare. Tearing the paper dry will rip away the underlying drywall's structural paper face, causing deep gouges that require extensive, expensive drywall texturing to repair. Our professional wallpaper removal service takes a patient, damage-free approach. We chemically dissolve old adhesives and restore the raw drywall beneath back to a smooth finish, giving you a perfect canvas for modern paint.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
* **Enzymatic Slurry Softening:** We lightly score the vinyl face of the wallpaper and apply professional warm-steam or enzyme-based adhesive-dissolving solution to liquefy the vintage paste hidden behind.
* **Damage-Free Peeling:** We use wide, flexible, rounded scrapers to gently peel away the vinyl sheets and backing fibers without piercing or scratching the fragile drywall core below.
* **Adhesive Scraping & Cleanse:** We scrub and flush the bare walls to completely eradicate residual sticky glue paste, preventing future paint bubbling or peeling flaws.
* **Level 4 Skim Coat Restoration:** We apply thin coats of lightweight compound (معجون) over old wall seams or imperfections, sanding the surface to a flawless, paint-ready Level 4 finish.
