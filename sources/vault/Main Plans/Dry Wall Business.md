## 🩹 1. Professional Patching, Repair & Handyman Services

> Canonical service: **DW-01** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#DW-01) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-drywall-patching.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-drywall-patching.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-drywall-patching.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop%20drywall-patching.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Flawless Wall & Ceiling Repairs in a Single Day*

### 📣 Service Overview
Life happens, and your walls bear the brunt of it. Whether a plumber had to cut into your ceiling to fix a pipe leak, a moving crew gouged a hallway corner, or a heavy door handle punched a neat hole through your drywall, leaving damaged drywall exposed ruins your home’s visual appeal. Our rapid-response patching and repair service is designed to fix these structural blemishes cleanly, quickly, and invisibly. 

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
* **Precision Extraction:** We cut away crumbling, unstable, or water-damaged drywall back to the center of the structural studs to ensure a rock-solid foundation for the fix.
* **Structural Bridging:** We secure sturdy wood backing or mesh-tape support bridges across the opening to prevent future sagging or cracking.
* **Seamless Blending:** We apply high-performance setting compounds (hot-mud) in thin, layered skins, meticulously feathering out the edges so the transition patch is completely undetectable.
* **Surface Priming:** We sand the patch perfectly flat and apply a high-quality primer coat, leaving your wall fully prepped for final paint matching.

## 🪒 2. Texture Matching & Popcorn Ceiling Removal

> Canonical service: **DW-02** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#DW-02) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-popcorn-removal.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-popcorn-removal.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-popcorn-removal.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop%20popcorn-removal.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Modernize Your Home—Ditch the Outdated Popcorn Ceilings*

### 📣 Service Overview
Old, heavy "popcorn" or acoustic spray-on ceiling textures are relic design choices of past decades. Not only do they make modern interiors look dated, but they also act as aggressive traps for dust, cooking grease, allergens, and spiderwebs, making them almost impossible to clean. Our comprehensive texture removal and remodeling service safely strips away these old finishes, dramatically modernizing your home while improving interior air quality.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
* **Elite Dust Containment:** We isolate the target rooms by installing heavy, thick plastic sheeting barriers across entryways, completely sealing floors, walls, and light fixtures to trap all scraping dust.
* **Safe Hydraulic Stripping:** We use a calculated water-misting system to soften the old acoustic texture, allowing us to safely slice and scrape the substrate clean off without gouging the delicate drywall backing beneath.
* **Level 5 Skim Coating:** Once bare, we apply multiple thin coats of premium joint compound over the entire ceiling area, converting it into a smooth, luxurious Level 5 mirror finish.
* **Artisanal Texture Matching:** If your walls require a specific texture blend (such as Orange Peel, Knockdown, or Skip-Trowel) to match existing layouts, we utilize calibrated hopper spray lines to match the pattern perfectly.

## 🔨 3. Residential Remodeling & Home Additions

> Canonical service: **DW-03** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#DW-03) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-drywall-remodeling.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-drywall-remodeling.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-drywall-remodeling.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop%20drywall-remodeling.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Custom Drywall Installations for Renovations & Extensions*

### 📣 Service Overview
Taking on a home remodel—like turning an unfinished, cold basement into a vibrant home theater, dividing a massive room into separate spaces, or tackling a complete kitchen/bathroom gut renovation—requires precise framing and clean drywall installation. Our residential remodeling service bridges the complex gap between old structural elements and crisp, new drywall additions, providing straight corners, square walls, and perfectly level ceilings.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
* **Custom Wood/Metal Framing:** We build precise wood or steel stud wall partitions based on your new layout designs, incorporating true-square headers and door frames.
* **Moisture & Mold Protection:** We install dense, moisture-resistant "Greenboard" or ultra-protective "Purpleboard" panels in bathrooms, kitchens, and laundry rooms to actively prevent mold growth behind the tile lines.
* **Architectural Tie-Ins:** We expertly tape, mud, and feather structural joints where your new home addition connects to older lath-and-plaster or existing drywall structures, eliminating joint shifting or structural settling cracks.
* **Corner Bead Defending:** We fit heavy-duty steel or impact-resistant composite corner beads along all exposed wall edges to ensure impact protection against daily household traffic.

## 🏗️ 4. High-Volume Residential New Construction

> Canonical service: **DW-04** · [Registry](../AI%20Development%20Plans/04-service-registry.md#DW-04) · [Pricing](../Pricing/pricing-new-construction.md) · [Inventory](../Required%20inventory/inventory-new-construction.md) · [Agreement](../Service%20Agreements/contract-new-construction.md) · [SOP](../Standard%20Of%20Operations/sop%20new-construction.md)
*Target Headline for Website: Production-Grade Drywall Contracting for Developers & Builders*

### 📣 Service Overview
For custom luxury single-family builds, new neighborhood developments, or multi-family apartment complexes, maintaining tight build schedules while keeping finish standards exceptional is a constant balancing act. Our new construction drywall division is built strictly for high-volume, rapid-production environments. We combine organized material logistics with specialized crews to hang, tape, and finish thousands of square feet of drywall every single week with flawless, paint-ready accuracy.

### ⚙️ What We Do (The Process)
* **High-Velocity Material Layout:** We map out bulk drywall delivery deliveries across multiple rooms, minimizing installation waist cutoffs and optimizing installation speed.
* **Automated Mechanical Taping:** Our finishing crews use industrial automatic taping tools (Bazookas and mechanical skimming boxes) to apply embedded joint tape and multiple coats of mud at lightning speed.
* **Scaffolding & Stilt Operations:** We utilize rolling scaffolding lines and drywall stilts to fluidly handle high-ceiling spaces, soaring entryways, and intricate multi-layered tray ceilings without slowing building momentum.
* **Strict Quality Walkthroughs:** Every finished room undergoes a rigorous inspection under high-intensity construction lighting to identify and resolve joint flashing flaws, ensuring painters get a perfect wall template.
