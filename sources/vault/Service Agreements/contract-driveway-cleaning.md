This Agreement governs the high-pressure concrete cleaning and surface restoration services executed by **[Your Company Name]** ("Company") for **[Client Full Name]** ("Client").

## 1. Scope of Hydraulic Cleansing
* **Inclusions:** Targeted chemical pre-treatment, uniform rotary deck surface cleaning, and perimeter wand detailing as outlined in `sop-driveway-cleaning.md`.
* **Stain Limitations:** Client acknowledges that while professional surface decks lift organic mold, algae, and deep mud caking, certain chemical blemishes—such as decades-old automotive motor oil saturation, heavy rust, or battery acid burns—may leave permanent concrete shading shadows despite aggressive chemical lifting.

## 2. Pre-Service Site Requirements
* **Vehicle Clearing:** Client warrants that all vehicles will be removed from the driveway and parked at least 50 feet away from the active wash zones prior to crew arrival. 
* **Water Access Mandatory:** Client must provide access to an unrestricted external spigot with standard residential water pressure. If water access is frozen or unavailable, a **\$50 dry-run cancellation fee** will apply.
