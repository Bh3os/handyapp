This Agreement governs the heavy containment scraping and ceiling updating services executed by **[Your Drywall Company Name]** ("Company") for **[Client Full Name]** ("Client").

## 1. Hazardous Materials & Pre-1980 Waiver
* **Asbestos Testing Clause:** For any residential property constructed prior to 1980 in the Kentuckiana region, Client must provide verified negative asbestos test logs, or agree to pay a **\$150 testing surcharge** for Company testing prior to any scraping.
* **Positive Remediation Policy:** If asbestos testing returns positive, this contract is instantly suspended until a certified hazardous abatement sub-contractor encapsulates or clears the space, at the Client's separate expense.

## 2. Environment Isolation & Client Responsibilities
* **Total Room Evacuation:** Client agrees to completely remove all electronics, personal items, clothes, and furniture from the target rooms before the crew arrives.
* **HVAC Shutdown Consent:** Client grants permission for the Company to fully turn off the residential HVAC network during the scraping and sanding phases to prevent static dust flow through building ducts.
* **Pre-existing Cracks:** The Company is not responsible for structural ceiling joist cracks or structural shifting that becomes visible only *after* the heavy acoustic popcorn texture is scraped away.

## 3. Pricing, Milestones & Terms
* **Total Cleanable Area:** Based on a calculated **[Insert Sq. Ft.]** totaling a contract price of **\$[Amount]**.
* **Retainer Deposit:** A non-refundable **25% schedule retainer** is required to lock the calendar date.
* **Final Walkthrough:** Final payment is due upon successful completion of the specified finish (Level 5 Smooth or Sprayed Texture) before the final containment plastic sheets are removed from the walls.
