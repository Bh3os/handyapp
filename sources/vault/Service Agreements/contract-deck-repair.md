This Service Agreement is entered into by and between **[Your Company Name]** ("Company") and **[Client Full Name]** ("Client") for safety-focused residential deck repairs.

## 1. Scope & Structural Code Compliance
* **Inclusions:** Extraction of failed floorboards, installing pressure-treated structural lumber updates, post through-bolting, and stair tightening as defined in `sop-deck-repair.md`.
* **Hidden Frame Rot Clause:** If during the removal of old floorboards the primary joist framework or home ledger board is discovered to have deep, structural wood rot making screw fastening impossible, work will be paused. A structural remediation change order will be itemized to bring the frame back up to regional safety codes before fresh floorboards can be locked down.

## 2. Material Discoloration Waiver
* **Lumber Variance:** Fresh pressure-treated replacement lumber possesses a natural green or bright blonde hue that will not immediately match the dark, weathered gray of the client's older existing deck floorboards. The Company recommends a full cleaning and oil staining cycle (minimum 60 days post-cure) to blend tones uniformly.
