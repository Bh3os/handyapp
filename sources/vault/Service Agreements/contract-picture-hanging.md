This Service Agreement is entered into by and between **[Your Company Name]** (hereinafter "Company") and **[Client Full Name]** (hereinafter "Client") for localized artwork and mirror mounting services.

## 1. Scope of Work & Structural Limits
* **Inclusions:** Service strictly covers the leveling, anchoring, and secure mounting of designated frames, canvases, or mirrors as outlined in the `sop-picture-hanging.md` micro-checklist.
* **Substrate Disclaimer:** Client must inform the technician of hidden wall utilities or specialized wall substrates (such as historic plaster or soft brick). The Company utilizes professional digital scanners but is not liable for piercing deep unmapped utility lines run outside local building codes.
* **Final Placement Approval:** The technician will establish a green laser guide line for Client approval. Once the Client signs off on the physical visual layout, any modification or moving of the frame after anchor setting will be billed as a paid change order.
