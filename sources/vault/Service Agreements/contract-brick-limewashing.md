This Commercial/Residential Agreement is executed by **[Your Painting Company Name]** ("Company") and **[Client Full Name]** ("Client") for advanced architectural brick coatings or old-world limewashing [1.1.1، 1.1.7].

## 1. Scope of Application & Custom Aesthetics
* **Project Specifications:** Company will install premium breathable coatings according to the checked option within `pricing-brick-coatings.md`:
	- [ ] **Option A: Authentic Italian Slaked Lime Wash** (With custom artistic distress wear)
	- [ ] **Option B: High-Build Solid Elastomeric Shield** (Full moisture barrier seal) [1.1.1، 1.1.7]
* **The Artisanal Distress Waiver:** For Limewash projects, the Client acknowledges that distressing is an artistic, hand-crafted visual application. The level of underlying brick exposure is subjective, and the crew will execute an initial 5x5 test section for Client sign-off before spraying the full facade.

## 2. Site Protection & Masking Warranty
* **Overspray Prevention:** High-volume airless spraying creates architectural mist. The Company provides multi-layered window and landscape masking protection [1.1.1، 1.1.4]. Client agrees to keep vehicles parked at least 50 feet away from the building during active spray windows.
* **Pre-existing Masonry Defects:** The Company is not responsible for crumbling mortar joints, structural spalling brick, or moisture trapped behind interior walls prior to our arrival. Loose mortar discovered during power washing will be reported to the Client immediately.

## 3. Progress Payout Milestones
Total project value is fixed at **\$[Amount]**, to be paid as follows:
* **Milestone 1:** 40% upfront deposit to secure the operational block and chemical inventory.
* **Milestone 2:** 30% due immediately upon completion of full masonry wash, mechanical masking, and setup.
* **Milestone 3:** 30% due upon de-masking, glass scraping inspection, and clean site handover.
