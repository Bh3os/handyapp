This Finish Carpentry Agreement governs the relationship between **[Your Company Name]** ("Company") and **[Client Full Name]** ("Client") for decorative wood trim installations.

## 1. Craftsmanship Standards & Room Anomalies
* **Scope Details:** Installation of crown molding, baseboards, or wainscoting as outlined in `sop-trim-molding.md` for a total contract value of **\$[Amount]**.
* **The Contour Waiver:** Standard residential walls and ceilings are rarely perfectly level or square. The Company utilizes precision compound miter cutting and custom wood shims to simulate visual straightness. Client acknowledges that wide ceiling dips will be filled cleanly with flexible paintable latex caulk to bridge lines seamlessly.

## 2. Finish Paint Prep Exclusions
* **Prep vs Painting:** This carpentry contract covers mechanical installation, brad counter-sinking, wood putty filling, and continuous edge caulking. Final finish color paint coats are completely excluded unless explicitly wrapped into a separate painting division contract.
