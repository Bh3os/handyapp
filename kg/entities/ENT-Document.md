A generated or uploaded file: uploaded estimate originals (private) and customer PDFs, approval receipts, terms PDFs, invoices, receipts, change orders, care-note summaries.

**Fields:** kind; owner record and version; file hash; visibility; staging TEST mark; created at.

**Invariants:** customer documents are English; issued documents are immutable; current, approved, expired and superseded versions are distinguished; served by short-lived authorized links.

**Who may change:** generated by the server; staff upload estimate documents.
