A worker's personal confirmation of a packet, scope or SOP version.

**Fields:** worker; packet, scope and SOP versions; type; time; superseded by.

**Invariants:** personal, never for another person; a critical change invalidates it; it never releases a hold.

**Who may change:** the worker creates their own; nobody edits.
