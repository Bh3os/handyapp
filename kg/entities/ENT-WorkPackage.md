The assignable unit of approved scope, normally one service in named work areas.

**Fields:** service; pinned SOP version; work areas; quantities and specifications; tasks ([[ENT-Task]]); tools, materials and PPE with criticality and loaded state; dependencies.

**States:** planned, ready, in progress, complete, blocked.

**Who may change:** approval and change orders create and change it; workers update progress.
