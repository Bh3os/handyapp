**Who acts:** a visitor (guest, [[ROLE-GUEST]]); nobody on staff. **Site:** the public website, English only.

1. The visitor lands on the home page. It shows the owner-approved trust facts, exactly: "Free estimates—online or at your property.", "Every project individually priced to match its scope.", "Visits every day, 8 AM–10 PM, by appointment." and the section "Why HandyHub" — "Professional care, from estimate to completion." with its six items ([[OWN-DEC-86]]). No prices, completion times, 24/7 wording, guarantees, credentials or reviews appear; the public phone and email show as labelled placeholders and the "Call" button stays hidden until a real number is set ([[FACT-PUBLIC-CONTACT]]).
2. The visitor opens one of the four categories (Home Improvement, Drywall, Painting, Cleaning) and a service page. Each of the 26 pages shows its description, inclusions and exclusions, estimate approach (remote or site visit), safe preparation steps, relevant considerations (for example the home-age limits of [[FACT-SVC-HOME-AGE-LIMITS]]), 3–5 FAQs and related services ([[OWN-DEC-84]]). On staging the drafted content shows directly; production shows only owner-approved versions ([[OQ-SERVICE-DESCRIPTIONS]]).
3. Where payment methods are listed, cards are named with Square and no surcharge is mentioned ([[OWN-DEC-89]]).
4. The visitor types a ZIP into the coverage check ([[OWN-DEC-88]]) and sees exactly one of:
   - Served: "We serve your area. Tell us about your project."
   - Near the boundary: "You may be within our service area. Send your request and we'll confirm the address."
   - Outside the usual area: "Outside our usual coverage? You can still request an estimate—we'll check whether we can help."
   - Unknown: "We couldn't confirm this ZIP. You can still submit your request for review."
   Every outcome offers "Continue with Request".
5. The footer links to the privacy policy and the SMS terms ([[HH-COM-12]]).
6. The visitor presses "Request a Free Estimate" (header, sticky bar, or a service page, which preselects that service). A dialog asks "How would you like to continue?" with **Continue as Guest** (primary), **Create an Account** and "Already have an account? Sign in" ([[OWN-DEC-87]]). It can be dismissed to keep browsing.

**Automatic:** the coverage outcome is computed only from the configured rules ([[FACT-SERVICE-AREA]]) and carried, with the ZIP, into the form.

**States:** none stored yet except the coverage result and ZIP held in the browser for the request.

**On failure:** an invalid ZIP format shows a correction message; a failed lookup explains the problem, records the outcome as unknown and still allows the request. Coverage never confirms a booking, price or travel charge. The check must work behind the staging access gate.
