**Who acts:** the assigned worker ([[ROLE-WORKER]]) on their own phone (it is a website, not an app).

1. **My Day** lists today's assigned visits in order with arrival window, address, a directions link, access notes, preparation, readiness and the next action ([[OWN-DEC-121]]).
2. While online, each job packet downloads; it shows its last update and "Ready for offline use" only after the required content is verified saved on the phone.
3. On departure the worker taps "On my way" ([[OWN-DEC-107]], [[C07]]).
4. At the property: an **online readiness check** before Start Work, then the fixed sequence safety checks → before photos → operation checklist → after photos → walkthrough → submit for quality review ([[OWN-DEC-109]], [[C08]], [[C09]]). Arrived and Finished timestamps are recorded per worker.
5. Access codes show only to the assigned worker on the visit date after an online permission check, and the displayed copy is removed after the visit ([[OWN-DEC-40]]).
6. Offline, checklists, notes, photos and timestamps keep working; each item shows "Saved on this phone", "Pending upload" or "Synced".

**Automatic:** sync retries without duplicates when connectivity returns; schedule changes and reassignments appear then, and a reassigned visit's packet leaves the phone.

**States:** packet downloading → ready for offline use → stale; item saved on this phone → pending upload → synced (or conflict flagged).

**On failure:** offline use never starts new work, never bypasses a hold and never approves extra work ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]); a job-level Hold blocks Start Work until the Owner sets Ready ([[OWN-DEC-137]]); conflicting changes are flagged for review, never overwritten; pending uploads keep the job visibly incomplete; signing out or clearing data with unsynced work warns first; access codes are never in an offline download.
