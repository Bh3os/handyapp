**Who acts:** the owner; office staff for their own audit view.

1. The owner invites a staff member by email, assigns roles (Owner, Office/Dispatcher, Estimator, Worker; one person may hold several) and sets working days, hours and time off ([[OWN-DEC-123]]).
2. Availability feeds scheduling capacity ([[S05]]); a change that conflicts with booked visits flags them for review, never cancels them.
3. **Offboarding:** the owner disables the account; access is blocked at once, sessions are revoked, job history stays and upcoming assignments become reassignment items in the queue ([[S01]]). The owner can also revoke sessions or start password recovery ([[OWN-DEC-114]]).
4. **Audit log:** the owner sees every recorded action (sign-ins, approvals, payment entries and corrections, customer merges, role changes, safety events), each with who, when, the record and the changes; office staff see their own actions.

**Automatic:** audit entries are written by the system for every listed action, with passwords, tokens, access codes and sensitive message contents redacted.

**States:** account invited / active / disabled; audit entries append-only (corrections are new entries).

**On failure / limits:** audit entries cannot be edited or deleted through the app; audit records are kept 7 years, then go to the owner's review queue ([[FACT-RETENTION-SCHEDULE]]); role permissions are enforced on the server.
