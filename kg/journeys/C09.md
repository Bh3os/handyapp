**Who acts:** the worker; the customer or authorized representative; the owner as quality reviewer.

1. The worker takes after photos of the same areas, paired with the before photos ([[OWN-DEC-109]]).
2. **Walkthrough** with the customer on the worker's phone: "Everything looks good", "Accepted with follow-up items", "I have a concern" or "Customer unavailable", with comments and the participant's identity ([[OWN-DEC-75]]). A signature is optional unless the agreement requires one.
3. **Finished:** the worker's finish timestamp is recorded and the job becomes "Awaiting quality review" — never complete by itself.
4. **Owner quality review** ([[OWN-DEC-64]]) of scope, checklist, before/after evidence and walkthrough; when the owner did the work it is marked "self-review". Problems become punch items with owner, due date and required correction evidence. Minor finish issues: the job stays open and the customer sees "Follow-up work". Major defects, incomplete scope, failed checks or safety concerns block the final invoice and send tasks back.
5. **Handoff:** selected before/after photos and approved, job-specific care notes ([[OWN-DEC-81]]) appear in the workspace.
6. A satisfaction-check draft is prepared two days after the reviewed completion; staff release it.

**Automatic:** nothing is sent to the customer automatically at completion; the invoice draft is prepared ([[C10]]).

**States:** job `in_progress → ready_for_qa → operationally_complete → closed`; QA `pending → in_review → passed / punch_required → pending_recheck → passed`. Customer labels "Completion review / Follow-up work", "Work complete · Review care notes and invoice".

**On failure:** "Customer unavailable" or silence is never acceptance — the completion summary and approved photos are shared through the secure link for later review; a disputed walkthrough goes to the owner and opens a case ([[C11]], [[S10]]); a concern suppresses the satisfaction check; pending photo uploads keep the job visibly incomplete; the job cannot close until every punch item passes re-check, and closing needs payment settled. Care notes without approved product guidance are omitted and flagged ([[OQ-CARE-NOTES-APPROVAL]]). Sharing photos never grants marketing permission.
