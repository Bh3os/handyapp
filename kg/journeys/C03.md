**Who acts:** the customer; the system. Staff see the new request at once ([[S02]]).

1. Only after the request is saved, the receipt page shows "**Your request is in.** Thank you for choosing HandyHub." and "Request reference: HH-…" (the actual reference) with a Copy button ([[OWN-DEC-94]]).
2. "Your project at a glance" summarizes services, description, property address, preferred timing and contact details. "Your photos" lists received, processing and failed photos; failed ones can be retried here.
3. "What happens next?": "We'll review your project and contact you using your preferred method. We aim to respond within one hour during our daily hours, 8 AM–10 PM. Requests received outside those hours are reviewed when we reopen." and "Your request is received. Pricing and your appointment will be confirmed separately."
4. The receipt shows the masked email/phone, code entry, Resend and Change Contact ([[OWN-DEC-96]]). The customer enters the one-time code; email and phone are verified separately.
5. Any business phone or email on the receipt page and in the receipt email is a labelled placeholder ([[FACT-PUBLIC-CONTACT]]).
6. Actions: **View My Request** (opens the project after verification), **Create an Account** (optional, passwordless code, [[OWN-DEC-63]]) and **Return Home**.

**Automatic** ([[FACT-AUTOMATIC-MESSAGES]]):
- The project record is created with the request; it is not a job or appointment ([[OWN-DEC-97]]).
- One receipt email (reference, services, summary, next steps, how to view the request) sends once per request ([[OWN-DEC-95]], [[INT-Resend]]).
- The one-time code and the secure project-access instructions send immediately (email via Resend, text via [[INT-Bird]]).
- If the customer registers later, the verified request is linked to the account without duplicates.

**States:** request `received`; contact verification `unverified → pending → verified` (or `expired` / `failed`) per channel; customer label "Request received · HandyHub will review the details".

**On failure:** a failed receipt email keeps the request saved and gives staff a delivery issue with Retry; codes expire and attempts and resends are limited; a photo failure never loses the request. A reference number alone grants no access; a matching email or phone never reveals earlier projects; an expired link shows a neutral page with a safe way to request new access ([[HH-ID-09]]). Changing a contact requires verifying the new value.
