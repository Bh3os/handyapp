**Who acts:** office staff record cases; workers report incidents from the job; the owner decides and handles professional referrals.

1. A complaint from a walkthrough ([[C09]]), a message or a call becomes a case linked to the project with severity, assigned person, due date, notes and photos ([[OWN-DEC-124]]); if an open case of the same category exists, staff add to it.
2. A worker reports an incident quickly from the job; a safety issue starts the stop-work and critical-hold path ([[C08]], [[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]); an ordinary complaint does not stop unrelated work.
3. Damage, injury and disputes notify the owner at once (queue item plus notification). The owner handles any insurer, attorney or other professional referral, recorded as an owner action ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]).
4. The customer sees a neutral acknowledgement, progress updates and the next step.
5. Corrective work is tracked as assigned follow-up items (punch or return-visit work, [[HH-QA-07]]).
6. Closing the case needs a recorded resolution and any necessary re-check.

**Automatic:** the owner notification; review invitations and satisfaction checks pause for the project while the case is open; related records are kept under a hold.

**States:** complaint `open → acknowledged → investigating → action_pending → resolved → closed` (reopening permitted); incident `reported → triaged → action_pending → resolution_review → closed`.

**On failure / limits:** the system never decides fault, admits liability or promises compensation; restricted incident details (injury, damage, disputes) are visible only to the owner and the worker who reported them; office staff see only that a case exists and its status ([[OWN-DEC-143]]); a deletion request during a case deletes unrelated eligible data and explains which records remain ([[HH-PRIV-03]]); case history is kept after closing.
