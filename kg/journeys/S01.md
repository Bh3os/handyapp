**Who acts:** office staff, estimators and the owner; workers see My Day instead ([[S07]]).

1. After sign-in, one list shows what needs action now ([[OWN-DEC-115]]), ordered: safety issues; today's appointment changes; overdue customer responses and other time-sensitive actions; then everything else by due time.
2. Item types: new requests, unverified contacts, customer replies, message drafts awaiting release (reminders), reschedule/cancel requests, deposits and checks to verify, delivery failures, safety holds, quality reviews, overdue invoices, and those other modules add (Needs Linking messages, cases, availability conflicts, assignments to reassign).
3. Each item shows the project/customer reference, reason, assigned staff member, age or due time and one action button that opens the right screen ([[S02]], [[S04]], [[S05]], [[S06]], [[S10]]). Filters by task type and assignee.
4. New requests show the 1-hour response target: remaining time or overdue, counted only 8 AM–10 PM in the business time zone, paused outside those hours, labelled as an internal target ([[FACT-RESPONSE-TARGET]]).
5. The owner also sees **Owner Decisions**: deposit waivers, administrative exceptions ([[OWN-DEC-68]]), refunds, deletion requests and "Overdue—owner decision" invoices ([[OWN-DEC-112]]).

**Automatic:** items appear, update and disappear as their records change; the list refreshes without interrupting an open form.

**States:** each item is open until its subject is resolved; one entry per subject.

**On failure:** delivery failures appear as their own items with Retry; roles are enforced on the server, so sensitive incident and financial details stay hidden from roles without permission ([[RULE-SERVER-AUTHZ]]).
