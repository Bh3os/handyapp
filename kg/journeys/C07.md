**Who acts:** the customer; office staff ([[S05]]); the assigned worker ([[S07]]).

1. The confirmed visit shows date, arrival window, address and preparation steps in Appointments, after the automatic confirmation ([[OWN-DEC-105]]).
2. **Reminder** ([[OWN-DEC-107]]): a draft is prepared 24 hours before (immediately if the appointment was confirmed less than 24 hours ahead) with date/window, address and approved preparation steps; staff release it within 8 AM–10 PM.
3. **Customer requests:** "Request to Reschedule" or "Request Cancellation". The customer sees "Request received—awaiting staff confirmation." and the existing appointment stays visible. Staff handle the request in the queue; no cancellation fee is shown ([[OQ-DEPOSIT-REFUND-CANCELLATION]]).
4. **Changes:** when staff commit a reschedule or reassignment ([[OWN-DEC-119]]) the customer gets the automatic appointment-changed notice and the workspace shows the new visit; history is kept.
5. **On my way:** on the day, the assigned worker taps "On my way" when actually departing (allowed from three hours before). The customer gets the automatic notice with the workers' first names and the confirmed arrival window — no ETA, no live tracking.

**Automatic:** appointment-changed and on-the-way notices ([[FACT-AUTOMATIC-MESSAGES]]); the worker sees the address from confirmation through the service day ([[OWN-DEC-40]]) and access codes only on the visit date after an online check ([[OWN-DEC-121]]).

**States:** visit `confirmed → en_route → arrived` (or `canceled / no_access`); reschedule creates a new committed version, never erasing the old. Customer labels "Scheduled · View date/window and preparation", "On the way".

**On failure:** reminders are rechecked before release and outdated, cancelled or duplicate ones suppressed; a failed reschedule leaves the current booking intact; repeated "On my way" taps send once; notices for cancelled or superseded visits are blocked; delivery failures are recorded for staff. A customer not present: the worker waits 15 minutes, tries twice and calls the owner before recording "customer not there" (owner answer 2026-10-05T18:17). Deposit or refund questions go to the owner.
