**Who acts:** office staff or the owner issue the invoice; the customer pays; authorized staff (owner or field employees) collect and record.

1. The invoice draft is built automatically from approved estimate lines, approved change orders and the recorded deposit credit ([[OWN-DEC-110]]). It shows "Handyhub LLC" (no address line), no tax line, no minimum and no surcharge.
2. After the owner quality review passes, staff issue it — even while the team is still onsite. The customer gets the automatic "Your HandyHub invoice is ready" notice (no amounts) and sees the invoice and balance in Documents and Payments.
3. Balance due on receipt ([[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]]). The customer chooses ([[OWN-DEC-111]]):
   - **Pay by Card**: opens the invoice-specific Square payment link staff entered;
   - Square Tap to Pay on the worker's phone onsite;
   - cash, check, Zelle, Cash App or Chime, with instructions; the Zelle, Cash App, Chime and payee details are labelled placeholders until the owner supplies them ([[FACT-PAYMENT-INSTRUCTIONS]]).
4. Staff verify and record each payment (invoice, method, amount, date, collector, reference); onsite payments get their receipt immediately.
5. When confirmed payments plus credits bring the balance to $0.00 the invoice shows Paid and the receipt sends automatically.
6. **Unpaid** ([[OWN-DEC-112]]): reminder drafts 24 hours and 3 days after issue, refreshed before release and released by staff within 8 AM–10 PM; after the final reminder is actually sent the invoice moves to "Overdue—owner decision" ([[S11]], [[S01]]).

**Automatic:** invoice-ready notice; payment receipt at $0.00 ([[FACT-AUTOMATIC-MESSAGES]]). Reminders stop when paid.

**States:** invoice `draft → issued → partially_paid / paid` (controlled `voided` and correction flow); check `received → deposited → cleared` (or `returned`); "Overdue—owner decision" is a condition, not a fee. On staging every document is marked "TEST — not a real invoice or estimate" and payments are sandbox only.

**On failure:** issuing before the quality review is blocked; a payment-link redirect or a customer's claim never marks paid; a pending check never shows as cleared; duplicates and double-crediting of the deposit are prevented; a pending payment or billing dispute flags staff review instead of a reminder; a failed delivery is a staff issue, not non-response. No late fees, interest, collections, saved-card charges or automatic write-offs. Recognition never waits for Square's bank payout.
