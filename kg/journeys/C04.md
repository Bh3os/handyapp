**Who acts:** office staff or an estimator ([[S02]]); the customer in the project workspace.

1. **Questions** ([[OWN-DEC-98]]). Staff publish a question in the project. The customer gets the automatic notice "HandyHub has a question about your request. View and reply securely." through the permitted, preferred channel, linking to the question. The workspace shows "Waiting on you" with Reply (text, optional photos, "I'm not sure" for measurements). After sending: "Reply received—HandyHub will review it"; the assigned staff member is notified. Customers are never asked to inspect hazardous areas.
2. **No reply** ([[OWN-DEC-101]]). Reminder drafts at 24 h and 48 h after the notice's successful send; staff release them within 8 AM–10 PM. A customer reply stops them. 24 h after the final reminder was actually sent, staff may close an unbooked request as "Closed—no response"; a reply within 30 days ([[FACT-REOPEN-PERIOD]]) reopens the same request without restoring an expired estimate or appointment.
3. **Approach** ([[OWN-DEC-99]]). Staff choose "Estimate online" (go to [[C05]]) or "Site visit needed" with a short explanation, considering the customer's preference.
4. **Site visit.** Staff offer 2–3 windows reflecting capacity and travel, each held for 24 hours ([[OWN-DEC-144]]); the customer picks one or asks for another time. On selection availability is rechecked and the slot reserved; then the project shows the confirmed date, arrival window, property address, visit purpose and preparation instructions. The appointment type is "Estimate visit": no job, no price approval, no fee ([[FACT-VISIT-FEE]]).
5. **Not a fit** ([[OWN-DEC-100]]). If staff decline after assessing the actual work, the customer sees "Thank you for considering HandyHub. We're unable to take on this project because [plain-language reason]." with no named-company referral; the project stays visible.

**Automatic** ([[FACT-AUTOMATIC-MESSAGES]]): the question notice; the visit-options notice; the visit-confirmed notice; the not-a-fit notice. No duplicates on retries.

**States:** request `reviewing → needs_information / needs_site_visit / ready_for_estimate`, or terminal `declined / outside_scope / not_served`; "Closed—no response"; visit `tentative → confirmed`. Customer labels "More information needed · Answer the listed questions" and "Site visit needed · Confirm details with HandyHub".

**On failure:** a failed notice appears in the staff queue and never counts as customer non-response; a taken slot offers alternatives (concurrency-safe); a reply draft and successful photos survive a failed upload. If work was accepted, an appointment exists or money was received, a not-a-fit goes to the owner for cancellation and financial review instead of closing. Accepted, scheduled or paid work is never cancelled automatically.
