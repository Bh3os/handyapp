**Who acts:** the office/dispatcher ([[ROLE-OFFICE]]) or the owner.

1. The board shows day and week views by worker (a simple list on phones), with estimate visits distinct from work visits, arrival windows, expected duration, travel/setup buffers (staff-entered travel estimates), assigned workers and capacity from worker availability ([[OWN-DEC-123]]).
2. **Offer windows:** for an estimate visit ([[OWN-DEC-99]]) or a work visit ([[OWN-DEC-105]]) staff publish 2–3 feasible windows to the customer; each holds the needed workers and resources for 24 hours ([[OWN-DEC-144]]).
3. **Reserve:** when the customer picks a window, or staff confirm one, the server rechecks capacity and turns its hold into the reservation of every worker the visit needs (a two-person job occupies both); the other offered windows are released and the visit is confirmed. Unselected holds release automatically after 24 hours.
4. **Gates:** work visits need the required deposit satisfied or an owner waiver ([[OWN-DEC-104]]); estimate visits never do. Unresolved critical safety holds and explicit blocking readiness holds block confirmation; ordinary notes do not.
5. **Requests from customers** ([[OWN-DEC-107]]): reschedule and cancellation requests arrive in the queue; staff confirm or decline them here.
6. **Reschedule or reassign** with forms/buttons or by dragging: the new resources are validated first; the previous booking stays in history with actor and reason.

**Automatic:** the customer's confirmation or appointment-changed notice after the change is committed ([[FACT-AUTOMATIC-MESSAGES]]); affected workers are notified and their packets and access refreshed (the old worker loses the packet and access codes).

**States:** visit `tentative → confirmed`, `confirmed → canceled / no_access`, reschedule = new committed version; scheduling task `open → planning → fulfilled / canceled`.

**On failure:** overlapping bookings are refused on the server, including two staff confirming at the same moment; a failed change keeps the current appointment; delivery failures are shown without duplicating reservations or notices; travel times are never invented.
