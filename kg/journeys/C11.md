**Who acts:** the customer; staff release messages; the owner reviews deletion requests and cases.

1. **Repeat service** ([[OWN-DEC-113]]): only if the customer agreed to a schedule, reminder drafts follow it; staff release them; the customer changes or stops the schedule in the workspace, effective for visits more than 24 hours away; a visit within 24 hours follows [[FACT-CANCELLATION-RULES]] ([[OWN-DEC-148]]).
2. **"Request This Service Again"** reuses the authorized property details, asks the customer to confirm they are still current and creates a new linked request ([[C02]]); new pricing and appointments need review.
3. **Reviews:** staff may release a review request; every customer is eligible on the same rules whatever their satisfaction score — no incentives, no pressure, no positive-review filtering. Channel permissions, separate marketing consent and opt-outs are checked first.
4. **Concerns** after completion open a case ([[OWN-DEC-124]], [[S10]]); the customer sees an acknowledgement, progress and the next step. Review invitations and satisfaction checks pause until the case is resolved.
5. **Records:** the customer downloads retained, customer-visible documents through verified access, and can request a data export or deletion.
6. **Deletion request:** shown as a request for owner review, with identity confirmation; the owner sees what will be deleted, what is retained, why, and the review date ([[OWN-DEC-71]]); the customer is told the outcome.

**Automatic:** none of these messages send automatically; deletion-outcome notices are staff-released.

**States:** deletion request pending → approved (eligible data deleted, protected records retained) or partly blocked by a hold; login access closed separately from business records; retained records enter the review queue when their periods end ([[FACT-RETENTION-SCHEDULE]]).

**On failure / limits:** an unpaid balance never prevents closing login access; a hold blocks deletion of the affected records but not account disablement; issued invoices, payments and signed approvals are archived, voided or corrected by audit, never rewritten; unrelated eligible data is still deleted when a case holds other records, with an explanation ([[HH-PRIV-03]]).

**Next:** the journey ends; a new request starts again at [[C01]] or from "Request This Service Again".
