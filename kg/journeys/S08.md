**Who acts:** nobody has to act for decided values; the owner fills genuinely missing facts in the business-details form.

1. Every decided value is seeded as active, versioned, audited configuration citing its decision ([[OWN-DEC-122]], [[RULE-DECIDED-VALUES-ACTIVE]]): hours, time zone, service area, deposit rule, validity, no tax line ([[OWN-DEC-128]]), payment methods, reminder cadences, reopen period, retention schedule, visit fee (none) and the automatic-message list. There is no Activate step and no separate Business Settings setup.
2. The owner opens the simple **business-details form** ([[OWN-DEC-110]], [[OWN-DEC-126]]) to see the issuer name "Handyhub LLC" and to enter, when known, the mailing address ([[OQ-MAILING-ADDRESS]]), the payment details behind the four placeholders ([[OQ-PAYMENT-HANDLES]]) and the public phone and email ([[OQ-PUBLIC-CONTACT]]).
3. A change to any value creates a new version with who, when and why; documents and approvals keep the version they used.

**Automatic:** staging shows labelled TEST values for missing facts and marks documents "TEST — not a real invoice or estimate".

**States:** policy version active (current) / superseded; a missing fact is "missing" only on the output that needs it.

**On failure:** a missing fact never blocks unrelated workflows and is never invented; safety, access and customer-approval protections cannot be changed here ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]).
