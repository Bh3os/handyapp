**Who acts:** office staff (unmatched messages are visible only to authorized office staff); the customer on the other side.

1. Inbound emails (via [[INT-Resend]]) and texts (via [[INT-Bird]], [[OWN-DEC-129]]) and project messages land in one inbox ([[OWN-DEC-120]]).
2. Each message is matched by a reliable reference: for email the per-project reply token or the In-Reply-To/References headers, then a verified contact email; for texts the verified phone number plus the latest open conversation. Names alone never match.
3. A message that could belong to several projects, or to none, goes to **Needs Linking**; staff link it, and the correction is recorded while the original stays unchanged.
4. Staff open the project conversation and reply. Pressing **Send** is the release; there is no second approval.
5. The reply goes by the customer's preferred permitted channel; if it is unavailable (no permission, unverified, opted out), staff see the other permitted options and choose.
6. Each message shows queued, sent, delivered (where the provider reports it) or failed.

**Automatic:** STOP and opt-out keywords update the customer's channel permissions; a customer reply to a published question also notifies the assigned staff member ([[OWN-DEC-98]]).

**States:** notification `queued → sending → sent → delivered`, or failed → retry / permanent failure; inbound message matched / Needs Linking / linked.

**On failure:** reply drafts are kept; retries never send twice; the channel never switches silently; while a channel's inbound webhook secret is unset the inbox shows "Inbound not configured" and never pretends messages arrived; webhooks reject bad signatures, replays and oversized payloads. Internal notes never reach the customer; attachments are validated and follow project permissions.
