**Who acts:** office staff; the owner for reversals.

1. Staff open a customer record ([[OWN-DEC-117]]): contact details with separate email/phone verification, channel permissions, marketing permission, authorized properties, projects, documents, payments and activity history (staff interface English/Spanish; customer content English).
2. A property record holds the full address, access instructions, pets, parking and the date each changeable detail was last confirmed. Access codes sit in a separately protected field shown only to permitted roles, with every read audited, and never go into ordinary messages.
3. **Merge or relink:** office staff propose or perform a permitted merge or correct a mistaken request link, entering a required reason. A preview shows both records and the affected projects, documents and payments before confirming.
4. The merge keeps a survivor and all original IDs, history, approvals and financial evidence; the owner can reverse it without erasing later legitimate activity.
5. A changed property address keeps the old address on historic records with an old-address notice.

**Automatic:** customer access is rechecked after every merge or relink; correcting a mistaken link revokes affected sessions, guest links and account links. Every merge, relink and access-code read is audited.

**States:** record active / merged into survivor (link, never destructive) / reversed.

**On failure:** nothing is merged by matching email, phone, name or address alone; a mistaken link never exposes another customer's history; internal notes and restricted incidents stay private to authorized roles.
