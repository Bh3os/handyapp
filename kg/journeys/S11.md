**Who acts:** the owner ([[ROLE-OWNER]]).

1. The owner opens the dashboard ([[OWN-DEC-125]]) and sees:
   - **Overview:** new requests, estimates awaiting approval, scheduled jobs, jobs awaiting quality review, overdue invoices (including "Overdue—owner decision", [[OWN-DEC-112]]) and open cases, each linking to its action;
   - **Customer response:** requests received, time to first meaningful staff response ([[OWN-DEC-116]]) and the share within the 1-hour target, in business hours only;
   - **Estimates:** sent, approved, declined, expired, awaiting response (revisions counted once);
   - **Job quality:** completed jobs, review pass rates, outstanding punch items, return visits;
   - **Payments:** invoices issued, confirmed payments by method (Square link, Tap to Pay and the others), pending checks, refunds, credits, outstanding and overdue balances;
   - **Cases:** open complaints, damage/injury reports, assignees, overdue follow-ups.
2. The owner sets a date range, compares with the previous period and sees the last-updated time.
3. Any figure opens its supporting records.
4. The owner exports the filtered report as CSV; its totals equal the dashboard.

**Automatic:** figures come from recorded data only; no configuration step is needed.

**States:** none of its own; it reads the other records' states.

**On failure / limits:** missing data shows "unavailable", never zero; deposits are never counted twice and invoiced amounts are kept apart from money received; sensitive case details stay restricted; CSV cells are protected against formula injection.

**Next:** the staff day starts again at [[S01]].
