**Who acts:** an estimator or the owner ([[ROLE-ESTIMATOR]], [[ROLE-OWNER]]); publishing permission is a setting the owner controls ([[OWN-DEC-118]]).

1. From the request ([[S02]]) the estimator chooses ([[OWN-DEC-102]]):
   - **Write an Estimate**: scope, line items and prices in cents, options, exclusions and any deposit;
   - **Upload an Estimate**: a PDF or Word file, previewed, with the total, currency, deposit and validity entered as structured fields (Word is converted to a customer PDF; the original stays private).
2. The estimate carries terms v1.0 ([[OWN-DEC-127]]), the 14-day validity, the deposit computed by [[FACT-DEPOSIT-RULE]], "Payment due on receipt" and "Handyhub LLC"; no tax line, no minimum, no surcharge.
3. Pre-publish validation checks scope, prices, total, options, exclusions, terms, validity and deposit. For an upload the estimator ticks that the document and the structured amounts agree.
4. **Publish** sends the automatic estimate-ready notice ([[C05]]). No owner approval is needed per estimate.
5. **Revise:** any change, including the owner's, creates a new version; published or accepted versions are never edited silently. The version history shows creator, reviser and publisher.
6. **Change orders** drafted by workers in the field ([[OWN-DEC-108]]) are reviewed and published here for the customer's approval ([[C08]]).

**Automatic:** audit of create, revise and publish; the notice on publish.

**States:** estimate revision `draft → published → accepted / declined / expired / withdrawn / superseded`.

**On failure:** a role without publishing permission is refused on the server; an upload without the agreement confirmation cannot be published; amounts are never parsed from a document; a Word file that cannot be converted faithfully blocks publishing until a PDF is supplied. Deposit waivers and refunds stay owner-only.
