**Who acts:** an estimator or the owner publishes ([[S04]]); the verified customer approves; staff record the deposit.

1. **Estimate ready.** Publishing a written or uploaded estimate ([[OWN-DEC-102]]) sends the automatic notice "Your HandyHub estimate is ready" with no amounts. The project shows each line (every line above $0.00) or the published PDF, options and exclusions, any discount as a separate row before the total ([[FACT-PRICING-DISCOUNTS]]), the total, the valid-until date (14 days, [[FACT-ESTIMATE-VALIDITY]]), any deposit, "Payment due on receipt" ([[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]]), the business name "Handyhub LLC" and the full terms v1.0 ([[OWN-DEC-127]]). No tax line, no minimum, no surcharge.
2. **Review and approve** ([[OWN-DEC-103]]). Verified access is required (an expired verification offers a new code without losing selected options). The customer selects options, ticks "I have read and accept the estimate terms" (unchecked by default), types their full name and presses **Approve Estimate**. The server rechecks validity, revision, options and total and records revision, terms version and hash, payment-terms version, options, total, name, verified contact and server time.
3. Alternatives: **Request Changes** (a message to staff) or **Decline** (optional reason).
4. After approval the screen says "Estimate approved—scheduling is next." and then the right next step ([[OWN-DEC-105]]):
   - deposit required: "Estimate approved—pay your deposit to proceed with scheduling."
   - no deposit, satisfied or owner-waived: "Estimate approved—choose your work appointment."
5. **Deposit** ([[OWN-DEC-104]]): for totals over $1,000.00 (after any discount), "Deposit due: $X. Please pay your deposit so we can confirm your work appointment. Payment does not guarantee a particular date." ($X = 20%, [[FACT-DEPOSIT-RULE]]). The customer pays by Square link or another decided method ([[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]]); staff verify and record it. Only the owner can waive it, job-specifically with a reason ([[OWN-DEC-76]]). If the job is cancelled, the deposit is refunded by [[FACT-CANCELLATION-RULES]].
6. **Work windows.** Staff propose 2–3 feasible windows, each held for 24 hours ([[OWN-DEC-144]]); the customer picks one or asks for others; capacity is rechecked and all workers and resources reserved before confirmation; then [[C07]].

**Automatic:** estimate-ready notice; unanswered-estimate reminder drafts 24 hours and 3 days after sending, released by staff at any hour and stopped by acceptance, decline, expiry or any customer response ([[HH-EST-14]]); on approval exactly one job and one scheduling task; the approval receipt with a PDF of the approved estimate and terms; the work-appointment confirmation ([[FACT-AUTOMATIC-MESSAGES]]).

**States:** estimate revision `published → accepted / declined / expired / superseded`; scheduling task `open → planning → fulfilled`; deposit pending → satisfied (verified, check cleared) or owner-waived; customer label "Approved · Scheduling is next". On staging, estimates and approvals are marked "TEST — not a real invoice or estimate" and the terms carry the pending-approval banner.

**On failure:** an expired or superseded estimate is refused, showing the current version or "Request an Updated Estimate"; double clicks give one approval and one job; a received check stays pending until cleared; work scheduling stays blocked until the deposit is satisfied or waived; a lost slot offers alternatives. Approval never confirms an appointment or marks the deposit paid; a waiver never marks it paid.
