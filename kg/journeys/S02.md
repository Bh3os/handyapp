**Who acts:** office staff or an estimator.

1. Staff open the request from the queue ([[OWN-DEC-116]]) and see the original submission: services, answers, description, full address, timing preferences, photos with upload status, coverage result and separate email/phone verification badges ("Contact unverified" requests can still be reviewed).
2. Possible duplicate customers or properties show as suggestions only.
3. Staff link verified matching records or create new ones ([[S03]]); nothing is merged automatically and linking grants no access to earlier projects.
4. Staff assign one responsible staff member (reassignments recorded).
5. Staff choose one action:
   - **Ask a Question** ([[OWN-DEC-98]]) → [[C04]];
   - **Prepare Online Estimate** ([[OWN-DEC-102]]) → [[S04]];
   - **Arrange Estimate Visit** ([[OWN-DEC-99]]) → [[S05]];
   - **Not a Fit** with a reason category ([[OWN-DEC-100]]).
6. Internal notes stay separate from customer-visible messages. The screen shows the next step, responsible person and follow-up due time.

**Automatic:** the 1-hour clock stops only when a meaningful staff response is successfully sent or a completed customer conversation is recorded (for example a logged call with an outcome).

**States:** request `received → reviewing → needs_information / needs_site_visit / ready_for_estimate`, or `declined / outside_scope / not_served`.

**On failure:** opening, assigning, drafting or the automatic receipt never stop the clock; a failed response delivery stays an action item; outside-area requests are decided by staff, not the ZIP check; requests with accepted work, appointments or money go to the owner instead of a plain "Not a Fit".
