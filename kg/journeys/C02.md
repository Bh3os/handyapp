**Who acts:** the guest or a signed-in customer. **Site:** public site, request wizard.

1. **Choose services** ([[OWN-DEC-90]]). The form opens with the service from the originating page preselected and its category open. "Choose a category" shows four cards: Home Improvement — "Repairs and installations"; Drywall — "Wall repairs and drywall projects"; Painting — "Painting and surface finishes"; Cleaning — "Home and property cleaning". "Select services" lists that category's services as checkbox rows. The counter reads "Selected services — 0 of 5"; at most 5 distinct services, with a summary and Remove. "Not sure what service I need" lets the customer describe the project instead. The form explains that several services form one project request, with scope, pricing and scheduling confirmed after review.
2. **About the job** ([[OWN-DEC-91]]). Short questions per selected service, shared questions once, units beside measurements, approximate values allowed, and "I don't know" (stored as unknown, never zero). One project description plus optional per-service notes. Up to 10 photos in total ([[FACT-UPLOAD-LIMITS]]), optional, each with preview, Remove/Retry and its own state (uploading, processing, complete, failed). Shown: "Please don't upload payment cards, IDs, passwords, or unrelated personal information."
3. **Property and timing** ([[OWN-DEC-92]]). Full address (street, city, state, ZIP; unit optional) and property type ([[OQ-PROPERTY-TYPES]]); the ZIP is prefilled from [[C01]] and can be corrected with a coverage recheck. Preferred date (tomorrow or later, property local time) or "I'm flexible"; optional "Need help today?" for staff review (no charge shown, [[OQ-SAME-DAY-CHARGE]]); windows Mornings, Afternoons, Evenings, Weekends or Any time inside 8 AM–10 PM (Any time clears the others). Shown: "These are your preferences. Your appointment is confirmed only after we send a date and arrival window."
4. **Contact and send** ([[OWN-DEC-93]]). Name, email and phone all required ([[OWN-DEC-61]]); preferred contact by email, phone call or text; marketing permission as a separate choice; estimate preference Online Estimate, Site Visit or "Let HandyHub decide". A final summary with Edit per section, the privacy-notice link (its version is saved), then **"Send Estimate Request"**.

**Automatic:** server-side validation of every field and file; an idempotency key so double taps and retried sends create one request; the draft text is kept in the same browser with Resume and Clear Draft (never passwords, codes or payment data) and cleared after a successful send. Photos upload privately ([[INT-R2]]) through an expiring capability scoped to the draft.

**States:** browser draft → submitted request `received` with the coverage result and contact `unverified`.

**On failure:** field errors show beside the field and nothing typed is lost; a failed photo keeps all text and other photos and the request can be sent without it, with a clear notice; outside, boundary or unknown coverage never blocks submission (the request is flagged for staff review); more than 5 services are described in free text without blocking.
