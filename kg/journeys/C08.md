**Who acts:** the assigned worker ([[S07]]); authorized staff publish change orders ([[S04]]); the customer approves changes; the owner handles holds.

1. The worker arrives and sees the approved scope, site information, hazards, safety checklist, tools and materials ([[OWN-DEC-108]]).
2. **Start Work gate:** the worker cannot start until the required safety checks and before photos pass, an online readiness check succeeds and no critical hold remains ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]). The documented administrative exceptions of [[OWN-DEC-68]] (customer not present with authorized unattended access, before-photo upload failed, deposit verified but bookkeeping pending) need owner approval and never waive safety, legal, qualification, scope, work-authorization or access requirements. A job-level "Hold" from [[OWN-DEC-82]] blocks the affected work.
3. The job-specific checklist opens in six stages: site preparation and protection; setup and measurements/checks; service-specific steps; quality checks; cleanup and waste; final checks, completion photos and walkthrough. Steps come only from the vault SOPs (staging shows "Pending competent review"). Progress saves and resumes; required steps cannot be skipped silently.
4. **Something new is found:** the worker drafts a change order (scope, price in cents, reason, photos); authorized staff publish it; the customer approves the exact version in the project (verified, terms box, full name) or in person through a flow that identifies the customer (for example a code to the verified contact). A signature alone is not enough. Change-dependent steps wait; unaffected safe approved work continues.
5. **Hazard or emergency:** Stop Work / Report Hazard is always available. A critical hazard stops the affected work and writes a critical hold in the same transaction as the report, under the job lock; the owner is alerted by text and app alert. The customer sees only "Work is paused for safety review." Gas smell: evacuate and call emergency services or the gas utility from a safe place; never touch gas repairs or exposed live wiring ([[OWN-DEC-80]]).
6. **Resume:** only after a documented resolution, updated instructions and renewed safety checks (the gate passes again).

**Automatic:** the hold write and owner escalation; checklist progress saved to the server and the offline packet.

**States:** job `ready → in_progress ↔ paused`; hold `open → under_review → resolved`; change revision `draft → reviewed → issued → approved / rejected / expired / superseded`. Customer labels "Work in progress", "Work paused".

**On failure:** offline the worker can still record notes, photos and a stop, but cannot start new work, resume after a critical hold or approve changes ([[OWN-DEC-121]]); a stale change order cannot be approved; licensed-trade or hazardous work gets no procedure and goes to a hold ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]).
