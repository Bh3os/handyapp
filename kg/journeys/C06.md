**Who acts:** the customer (a guest on one verified project, or an account customer switching between linked projects); staff feed it from the staff app. The workspace is open from [[C03]] onward and is where every later customer step happens.

1. The customer opens the project through verified access. On a phone **Overview** comes first.
2. **Overview:** plain-language status, one clear next action, latest update, confirmed appointment and payment summary (operational status and payment summary shown side by side, never one combined status).
3. **Messages:** two-way conversation with HandyHub, permitted attachments, send/upload status, retry without losing drafts.
4. **Documents:** estimate revisions, the approved estimate and terms, approval receipt, change orders, invoices, receipts and care notes, each marked current, approved, expired or superseded.
5. **Photos:** the customer's uploads plus before/after photos staff chose to share.
6. **Appointments:** estimate and work visits with date/window, preparation and "Request to Reschedule" / "Request Cancellation" ([[C07]]).
7. **Payments:** approved total, deposit status, recorded payments, credits and remaining balance.

**Automatic:** status and next action follow the server state; drafts survive tab switches; empty sections explain what will appear.

**States:** shown with the customer labels of guide 23.1 (Request received, More information needed, Site visit needed, Estimate ready, Approved · Scheduling is next, Scheduled, On the way, Work in progress, Work paused, Completion review / Follow-up work, Work complete, Canceled / Outside service area).

**On failure / limits:** internal photos are inaccessible on the server (direct URL or ID refused), not merely hidden; a guest link never reaches another project; payments are never marked paid from a customer claim (an "I paid" action only creates a staff task); expired sessions, denied permissions, partial uploads and stale documents each have an explicit state. Customer content is English.
