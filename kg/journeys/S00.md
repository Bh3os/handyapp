**Who acts:** a staff member ([[ROLE-OWNER]], [[ROLE-OFFICE]], [[ROLE-ESTIMATOR]], [[ROLE-WORKER]]); the owner manages accounts ([[S09]]).

1. On staging the person first passes the staging access gate, which is separate from staff login ([[OWN-DEC-73]]).
2. A new employee opens the owner's invite link (sent automatically by email) and sets their own password. There is no public staff registration.
3. The employee signs in on the staff website with email and password ([[OWN-DEC-114]]); no ZITADEL redirect. If they enrolled optional MFA (authenticator app; passkeys if available), they enter the code. Owner and Office accounts without MFA see a recommendation banner.
4. Roles decide what they see next: the action queue ([[S01]]) for office roles and the owner, My Day ([[S07]]) for workers.
5. "Forgot password?" emails a short-lived, single-use reset link; after a reset all sessions are revoked and the person signs in again.
6. Sessions end after the configured inactivity period; Sign Out is always available.

**Automatic:** invite and reset emails ([[FACT-AUTOMATIC-MESSAGES]], [[INT-Resend]]); audit entries for sign-in, failed sign-in, reset, disable and role change ([[OWN-DEC-123]]).

**States:** account invited → password set → active → disabled; session active → expired / revoked.

**On failure:** wrong credentials give one generic error; login and reset are rate-limited; the reset response never reveals whether an email exists; a disabled account is blocked at once with its sessions revoked; nobody, the owner included, can view a password. RISK: privileged accounts without MFA (recommendation: the owner enables MFA).
