- The end-of-job walkthrough is recorded with the customer or their authorized representative. Outcomes (worker labels): accepted ("Everything looks good"), accepted with punch items ("Accepted with follow-up items"), disputed ("I have a concern"), customer unavailable.
- The record holds participant identity, worker, timestamp, comments and linked punch items.
- "Customer unavailable" and no response are never acceptance or a signature. The completion summary and approved photos are shared through the secure project link for later review.
- Disputed work goes to the Owner and opens or joins a case ([[HH-QA-06]]).
- Customer acceptance never bypasses the quality review or closes open punch items.
- A signature is optional unless the approved service agreement requires it; where acknowledgment is needed, the authorized representative and the exact reviewed record are verified.
