- Staff uploads and notes are internal or restricted by default. They become customer-visible only through an explicit, audited share action by an authorized staff member choosing an approved share.
- Customer uploads are visible to that customer and to authorized staff.
- Sharing with the customer never grants marketing or publication permission; that is a separate recorded permission per asset, purpose and channel ([[OWN-DEC-109]]).
- Unshared items are inaccessible to the customer by URL and ID ([[OWN-DEC-106]]).
