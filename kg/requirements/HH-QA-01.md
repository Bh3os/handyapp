- Each service or work package defines its observable quality checks, required after-photos, cleanup and waste handling, protection removal, functional checks where appropriate, care instructions and handoff requirements. They come from the service's SOP and quality-checklist files; none are invented.
- Required answers and evidence cannot be skipped silently: a missing required item blocks the "Finished" submission or is shown to the reviewer as missing, never as a pass.
- Care instructions shown at handoff are only the owner-approved version ([[HH-QA-05]]).
