- WCAG 2.2 AA is the target for every implemented customer and staff journey: accessible names and labels, errors, focus management, status announcements, contrast, text zoom and reflow, reduced motion and meaningful semantics.
- Testing is done by AI with automated scans and real-browser tools before launch ([[OWN-DEC-85]]). Committed end-to-end tests (for example Playwright with axe on Chromium) keep running in CI and check:
  - keyboard-only traversal of each workflow: every control reachable and operable in a logical order, with a visible focus indicator that is not obscured (2.4.7, 2.4.11);
  - dialogs: focus moves in and is trapped, Escape closes, focus returns to the trigger;
  - forms: an error summary receives focus, field messages are linked with `aria-describedby`, input is kept;
  - accessible names, roles and states from the accessibility tree for controls and images;
  - contrast in light and dark themes;
  - reflow at 320 px and at 200% and 400% text zoom without horizontal scrolling or clipped content ([[HH-A11Y-02]]);
  - target size ([[HH-A11Y-03]]); reduced motion; status messages exposed through live regions (`role=status` or `alert`).
- Issues found are fixed and the checks rerun; actual results are recorded per workflow and check (pass, fail, fixed, with the proving test).
- A separate "Not verified" record lists what the tools cannot check: screen-reader testing (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack) and physical-device testing (iOS and Android hardware, switch access, voice control), with the remaining limitation stated. Keyboard or browser testing is never reported as screen-reader testing.
- No claim of full accessibility conformance is made from automated checks. **RISK:** screen-reader and device behaviour stays unverified until a person tests it.

**Changed by [[OWN-DEC-181]] (2026-10-21):** Lighthouse joins the automated checks; manual screen-reader and device tests stay UNVERIFIED for launch.
