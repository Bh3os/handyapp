The homepage trust facts are exactly:
- "Free estimates—online or at your property."
- "Every project individually priced to match its scope."
- "Visits every day, 8 AM–10 PM, by appointment."

The "Why HandyHub" section has the subtitle "Professional care, from estimate to completion." and exactly these items (bold label, then text):
- **Clear pricing:** Written scope and price for your approval.
- **Respect for your time:** Confirmed date, arrival window, and updates.
- **Prepared team:** Tools, materials, and instructions planned for your job.
- **Your approval matters:** No added work or price changes without your agreement.
- **Careful completion:** Quality review, walkthrough when available, and documented follow-up.
- **Easy project access:** Estimates, updates, photos, and invoices through one secure link.

Rules:
- These sentences are the owner-approved claims; the claims register and the copy-lint allow-list contain them with source "owner, 2026-10-12".
- No completion times, no 24/7 availability, no invented prices, guarantees, credentials or reviews anywhere on the page.
- The premium design is kept; the page is English only. Completed edits show on private staging without a draft queue; production changes only on the owner's go decision.
