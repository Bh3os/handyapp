- A punch item has task description, severity (minor or major), assigned owner, due date, state, evidence and a completion re-check by the reviewer.
- Punch items stay linked to the job and visible to the right audiences even after an invoice is issued. Issuing an invoice never erases unfinished work or implies a resolved complaint.
- Open punch items appear in the staff action queue with their due dates.
