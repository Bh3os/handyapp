- Every core journey works at 320 px width and with 200% text zoom; reflow is also checked at larger zoom (400%).
- No action depends only on hover, color or dragging. The scheduling board offers forms and buttons as alternatives to dragging calendar entries ([[OWN-DEC-119]]); uploads have an accessible file-picker alternative to drag-and-drop.
- Dialogs return focus to their trigger when closed.

**Built by [[TSK-0104]] (2026-10-26):** every e2e screen state is checked at 320 px and with 200% text (no sideways scrolling); wide tables scroll inside a named, focusable region; the shared dialog returns focus to its trigger also when it is removed while open.
