- Project goal: main controls at least 44 px and primary worker actions at least 48 px where practical. Every target meets at least the WCAG 2.2 minimum of 24 px. Meeting the size goal is never a substitute for the full WCAG criteria.
- Sticky action bars and headers never cover field errors, the focused element or required content.

**Built by [[TSK-0104]] (2026-10-26):** every e2e screen state is checked on a 320 px phone for controls of at least 44 px (axe checks the 24 px minimum); My Day's actions keep their 48 px; the app has no sticky bars, and the keyboard checks assert the focused control is not covered.

**Limitation (process requirement; reviewer: a person testing on real devices):** Target sizes are measured in emulated desktop Chromium; real touch, on-screen keyboards covering fields and use with gloves outdoors are UNVERIFIED (`docs/accessibility-report.md`).
