- Staff choose the estimating route after review: "Estimate online" or "Site visit needed" ([[HH-LEAD-07]]). The customer's stated preference ([[HH-INT-03]]) is shown to staff and considered, but it never overrides a required site visit or qualification review.
- The several services a customer selects (up to five, plus any described extra work; [[HH-INT-15]]) form one project request. The customer is told that scope, pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
- After approval each service becomes a distinct work package with its own readiness, dependencies, SOP checklist and quality checks.
