- Return work is classified as one of: open punch, workmanship callback, approved warranty, new scope, customer change or damage, product or manufacturer issue, unresolved classification.
- Authorized staff (the Owner) decide coverage and pricing under actual reviewed policy. No warranty policy exists, so "approved warranty" is never applied automatically; legal or financial exposure goes to the Owner for professional review.
