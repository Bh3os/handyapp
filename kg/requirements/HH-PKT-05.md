- The loadout groups shop and vehicle tools, consumable materials, PPE, rental equipment and customer-supplied products.
- Each item is marked "required" or "nice to have".
- Workers confirm what is loaded and available, report missing or damaged items, and attach evidence where needed.
- Item lists come from the service's inventory files and the approved scope; quantities are never invented.
