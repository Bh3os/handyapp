- The worker's screens keep approved scope, proposed extra work and unapproved or prohibited tasks visibly distinct.
- A worker can draft a change order ([[HH-CHG-02]]) but cannot bind a price or perform extra unapproved work through a field note.
- Checklist steps that depend on a pending change are marked blocked until the change is approved; unaffected, safe, approved steps continue.
