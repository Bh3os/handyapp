## 1. Site preparation and property protection

### From "5. Customer and property preparation"

- The agreement's "Condition of Property" clause requires the client to have the property vacant of furniture, personal items and heavy trash bags before the team arrives. That clause is a customer warranty flagged for attorney review (30 section 5 row 17); HandyHub's own arrival checks are never moved onto the customer (source: agreement; 30 section 4.1 clause 5).
- PROPOSED for the preparation checklist (not yet in the agreement): electricity and water left on; access method and site contact; who authorizes entry, payment and any disposal; the desired end state of the refrigerator and freezer; the landlord or lease checklist, if any; movers and contractors finished or scheduled around the visit (source: 04 CL-03 "customer preparation requirements"; spec CL-03.INT; agreement "Access Delays" clause).
- Every job re-confirms access, occupants, pets, parking, hazards and work-area conditions (source: spec CX-005).

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🕒 Phase 1: High structural Clearance"

> HOLD (precondition; C-CL03-07; spec CL-03.SAF "initial walkthrough for damage/hazards"): before "Ceilings & Corners", the first cleaning step, complete the initial walkthrough of every room (section 11) and confirm the property is vacant (section 3). Left-behind items, unknown debris or sharps, biohazard, droppings or infestation, suspected mold, damaged fixtures or an occupied property: stop the affected area and follow section 10. Pending review.

> HOLD (precondition; C-CL03-07; spec CL-03.SAF "photograph pre-existing issues if required"; pending the START-008 configuration): before "Ceilings & Corners", record existing-condition photos and notes of pre-existing damage room by room - walls, floors, cabinets, appliances, fixtures, windows and doors - so that pre-existing damage is separated from the cleaning result (spec CL-03.QAC; section 16). Pending review.

> HOLD (precondition; C-CL03-07, C-CL03-02; spec CL-03.SAF "control sharps/chemicals"): before "Ceilings & Corners", only approved products with a reviewed SDS are on site (29 section 8.3), and the PPE selected by the qualified safety professional is available; none is selected yet, so the chemical and debris tasks stay blocked. Sharps are not picked up or bagged until the reviewer's sharps rule and container exist: stop and escalate. Pending review.

> HOLD (precondition; 31 section 7.2 rank 1, ceiling textures of unknown composition, RISK; spec START-011): before "Ceilings & Corners", look at the ceilings. Do not brush, wipe, scrape or vacuum a textured, damaged or deteriorating ceiling of unknown composition; leave it undisturbed and escalate (section 10). Cleared only by an accredited asbestos inspector's or abatement professional's assessment accepted by the owner. Pending review.

> HOLD (precondition; X-12; spec CL-03.HAZ "ladder"): before "Ceilings & Corners" and "Fixtures", reach skylight frames, high corners, ceiling fans, hanging fixtures and track lighting only with extension tools from floor level. A ladder or other access equipment is used only under the approved height and access policy, which does not exist yet (02 G-005), so tasks that need one stay blocked (job-planner playbook). Pending review.

- [ ] **Ceilings & Corners:** Clear all cobwebs, cocoons, and dust tracking from ceilings, skylight frames, and high corners.

> HOLD (31 section 10 CL-03, RISK): the step above does not cover droppings, nests or live infestation. If any are found here or in a later step (for example in cabinets, drawers or window tracks), stop that area and escalate (section 10). Pending review.

> HOLD (precondition; 31 section 10 CL-03 "power off first", RISK; X-07): before "Fixtures", power to every ceiling fan, hanging light fixture and track lighting system is off before it is dusted or damp-wiped. How power is isolated and verified, and by whom, is TBD - requires qualified safety reviewer review; power is restored as found at closeout (section 14). Damaged fixtures, exposed wiring or a power state that cannot be confirmed: do not wipe; escalate (Do not perform 1). Pending review.

- [ ] **Fixtures:** Dust and damp-wipe every ceiling fan, hanging light fixture, and track lighting system.
- [ ] **Vents:** Vacuum out all heating/cooling return vents and wipe the outer grilles clean.

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🪟 Phase 2: Built-ins & Glass Detailing"

- [ ] **Closets & Shelving:** Wipe down the top shelves, clothing rods, drawers, and corners of every single empty closet and pantry.

> HOLD (precondition; 04 CL-03 "hazards", painted sills, doors and ceilings of unknown composition in older homes; 04 section 6.2 old-property screening; Q-31-07): before "Windows" and "Doors & Frames", look for peeling, chipping or deteriorating paint on sills, tracks, frames and doors. Do not scrape or sand it; leave it undisturbed and escalate (section 10). Whether any lead-safe cleaning practice applies to these steps is an open question for an EPA RRP-certified renovator. Interior glass only: exterior glass is not covered (Do not perform 12; C-CL03-05). Pending review.

- [ ] **Windows:** Meticulously scrub interior window glass, clean out dead insects from window tracks, and wipe down sills.
- [ ] **Doors & Frames:** Hand-wash all interior doors, paying attention to the area around the handles where hand grime accumulates.

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🍳 Phase 3: Kitchen Deep Clean (Vacant Standard)"

- [ ] **Cabinets & Drawers:** Vacuum out loose crumbs inside all cabinets and drawers; wipe down all interior shelves, drawer runners, and exterior cabinet faces.
- [ ] **Oven & Range:** Pull out the storage drawer under the oven and clean it; deep-clean the main oven interior, stovetop, and grease filters.

> FLAG (C-CL03-06): no oven cleaner or degreaser is stocked for the step above; use only an approved product per its label and SDS (29 section 8.2 item 8). Gas odor at any time: emergency (section 19, step 2). Pending review.

- [ ] **Refrigerator & Freezer:** Turn off freezer to wipe down completely; clean all fridge interior compartments, plastic bins, and the external top surface.

> HOLD (31 section 10 CL-03; X-07; spec APXA-005 "restore utilities/components altered within scope"): the step above turns the freezer off, and no step restores it. Before leaving, set the refrigerator and freezer to the end state the customer recorded (on or off; doors as instructed) and record it (section 18); if no instruction is recorded, escalate before leaving. Defrost water is contained so that floors do not become slippery (method TBD - requires qualified safety reviewer review). Pending review.

> HOLD (precondition; 04 CL-03 "hazards", garbage-disposal splash guard; RISK, the same rule 31 section 10 proposes for CL-01): before "Countertops & Sinks", the garbage disposal is switched off and cannot be started while the splash guard is scrubbed; hands never go inside the disposal. The isolation method is TBD - requires qualified safety reviewer review. Pending review.

- [ ] **Countertops & Sinks:** Sanitize counters, clean the sink basin, and scrub around the garbage disposal rubber splash guard.

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🛁 Phase 4: Bathroom Deep Sanitization"

- [ ] **Toilet:** Meticulously scrub the bowl, clean under the rim, disinfect the hinges of the seat, and wash the external porcelain base.
- [ ] **Shower & Tub:** Strip away soap scum layers from tiles/glass and scrub bath rims, soap dishes, and drains completely clean.

> FLAG (31 section 10 CL-03 "Not covered"): in the step above, "drains completely clean" means cleaning drain surfaces only. Blocked drains are not cleared, mechanically or with the drain-opening gel listed in the inventory; record the condition and refer (Do not perform 10). Pending review.

- [ ] **Mirrors & Vanities:** Wipe down all medicine cabinet shelves, polish mirrors, and clean vanity countertops.

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🧹 Phase 5: Absolute Floor Restoration"

> FLAG (31 section 3.5 rule 5; C-CL03-03): "Absolute" in the heading above is outcome wording, kept verbatim; it is not a standard and is not repeated to the customer (section 17). Owner and attorney review (Q-31-16).

- [ ] **Edge Vacuuming:** Vacuum all carpets and hard floor edges using a crevice tool to pull dust from baseboard gaps.

> FLAG (C-CL03-04): the step above vacuums baseboard gaps only. Baseboard washing, which the agreement's "Turnover Standard" and the main plan promise, has no step; it is not covered pending the owner's scope decision (section 2). Pending review.

> HOLD (precondition; C-CL03-07; spec CL-03.QAC "waste removed to agreed location"; spec APXA-005): before "Deep Mopping", which ends at the front door, finish the closeout items that need access to the rooms: waste goes only to the location agreed with the customer and recorded, and no disposal destination is approved yet (section 15); left-behind items stay unless authority and a change order are recorded (Do not perform 9); the refrigerator and freezer are in the recorded end state; power switched off for "Fixtures" and the garbage disposal is restored as found (section 14); HandyHub products, bags and tools are removed except the mopping kit. Pending review.

- [ ] **Deep Mopping:** Mop all hard floors twice using an industrial cleaner, working your way backward out of the front door so no footprints remain.

> FLAG (C-CL03-06; C-CL03-02): "an industrial cleaner" in the step above is not an approved product. Use only an approved floor cleaner per its label and SDS, with the PPE the qualified safety professional selects (none selected yet); the inventory's "Concentrated Floor Stripper / Tile Cleaner" is not used for mopping unless the reviewer approves it for that purpose. Wet floors: wet-floor sign (proposed in the inventory). Pending review.

> HOLD (C-CL03-07; spec CL-03.SAF "secure property at exit"; 31 section 8.4 rule 3, critical): at exit, secure the property - doors and windows locked as found or as instructed, key or lockbox handled per the secured access record - and record the lock-up (section 18). If the property cannot be secured, do not leave it unsecured: escalate at once and follow the owner's recorded instruction. Pending review.

- Restore utilities and components altered within scope: power switched off for "Fixtures" and the garbage disposal is restored as found, by the method the reviewer approves (X-07); the refrigerator and freezer are left in the recorded end state (source: spec APXA-005; section 12 holds).

### From "15. Waste handling"

- No disposal destination or method is approved: waste goes only to the location agreed with the customer and recorded, by a method the reviewer approves for the municipality (source: spec CL-03.QAC; 31 section 3.1 row 15; Q-29-10; 02 G-157; HUMAN REVIEW: local waste authority; qualified safety professional).

  - Evidence: note
- Sharps: not handled until the reviewer's sharps rule, container and disposal route exist (source: spec CL-03.SAF "control sharps/chemicals"; 29 section 5.2).
- Chemical residues and rinse water: per the product SDS and the municipality's rules (source: 29 section 8.2 item 10).

### From "20. Incident reporting"

General path: [[FACT-SVC-INCIDENT-REPORTING]].
