### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🚨 Problem Option A: Removing Deep Organic Moss & Black Mold Spores"

> FLAG (X-06): the mold wording in this option is kept verbatim. The stop-work rule in section 10 lists possible mold; which exterior growth this option may treat is decided by the qualified reviewer once the company definition that separates cleanable mildew, algae and staining from suspected mold growth exists (Q-31-10). Until then the stop-work rule is not relaxed. (source: 31 section 5.8)

> HOLD (C-HI03-02, X-11): before the chemical pre-treat step below - do not mix or apply any product until its SDS has been reviewed and the product approved, PPE has been selected by the qualified safety professional, people and pets are out of the work area, plants and landscaping, electrical fixtures, outlets, doors and windows are protected, and runoff control is in place. Mix and apply only as the approved product label allows; no strength, ratio or dwell is set here. Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-03.SAF; 29 section 8.2 rows 1 and 8; 31 section 10)

- [ ] **Chemical Pre-Treat:** Mix a specialized sodium hypochlorite solution (soft-wash mix) with a foaming surfactant inside a pump sprayer. Apply a generous wet coat over dark mold areas.
- [ ] **Dwell Period:** Allow the solution to dwell for 15 minutes to liquefy microscopic mold roots deep inside concrete pores. Keep the surface damp.

> FLAG: UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions (product label and SDS) for the dwell time in the step above; it stays verbatim until a reviewer confirms or replaces it. (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 3)

> HOLD (C-HI03-01, X-08, X-11, X-27): before the rotary surface cleaning step below - test an inconspicuous area and record the result, keep people and pets out of the wash zone, protect electrical fixtures, outlets and openings, wear PPE selected by the qualified safety professional, and confirm surface-appropriate pressure and acceptable weather under the pressure-washing and weather modules (neither exists yet). Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-03.SAF; HI-03.TLS "PPE"; HI-03.INT "weather"; 31 sections 5.8 and 10)

- [ ] **Rotary Surface Cleaning:** Hook up a 20-inch circular flat surface cleaning deck to a 3,000 PSI gas pressure washer. Glide the deck across the concrete in slow, overlapping parallel rows to prevent streak lines.

> FLAG: UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions for the equipment size and the pressure in the step above; the pressure is not confirmed as appropriate for any surface (C-HI03-08). (source: spec HI-03.SAF "use surface-appropriate pressure"; 31 section 3.5 rule 3)

> HOLD (C-HI03-02): this option ends without a final rinse of the site, adjacent surfaces and plants and without runoff control or a wash-water destination; a reviewed method must be added before activation. Pending qualified review; runoff and disposal rules go to the local authority, and no conclusion is drawn here. (source: spec HI-03.QAC "runoff controlled", "site safe and rinsed"; 31 section 10)

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🚨 Problem Option B: Lifting Stubborn Automotive Motor Oil Stains"

> FLAG (C-HI03-V01): the steps below are written for a fresh oil spot; old or set-in oil saturation, which the agreement says may leave permanent shading, has no step. Record the stain type, age and treatment (spec HI-03.QAC "stain treatment documented"); scope pending owner decision.

> HOLD (C-HI03-02, X-11): before the absorbent lift step below - use only a degreaser or poultice product approved with a reviewed SDS, wear PPE selected by the qualified safety professional, keep people and pets away, and control residue and runoff. Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-03.MAT; HI-03.SAF "manage runoff"; 29 section 8.3 interim rule, PROPOSED)

- [ ] **Absorbent Lift:** Apply a heavy coat of liquid degreaser or poultice clay powder directly over the fresh oil spot. Let it sit for 20 minutes to draw oil out of the porous concrete.

> FLAG: UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions (product label and SDS) for the contact time in the step above. (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 3)

- [ ] **Stiff Aggressive Scrub:** Scrub the spot aggressively using a stiff-bristled wire deck brush and warm water.

> HOLD (C-HI03-01, X-08, X-11, X-27): before the turbo nozzle step below - the test area, the exclusion of people and pets from the wash zone, protection of electrical fixtures, outlets and openings, PPE selected by the qualified safety professional and acceptable weather under the weather module (which does not exist yet) must be in place; the nozzle method in that step is itself suspended (next hold). Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-03.SAF; HI-03.HAZ "high-pressure injection injury", "electrical proximity"; HI-03.INT "weather")

- [ ] **Turbo Nozzle Blast:** Rinse the area using a high-pressure 0-degree oscillating turbo nozzle wand, holding the tip 6 inches away from the stain to lift residual shadowing.

> HOLD (C-HI03-01): the step above is suspended pending safety review. Its "0-degree oscillating turbo nozzle wand, holding the tip 6 inches away" method is not confirmed for any surface; nozzle, pressure and distance come only from the washer and nozzle manufacturer instructions and the qualified safety professional. No replacement value is set here. (source: spec HI-03.SAF "use surface-appropriate pressure", HI-03.QAC "without etching/striping"; 31 section 10)

> HOLD (C-HI03-02): this option ends without a final rinse of the treated area and adjacent surfaces and without collection of degreaser, oil residue and wash water; a reviewed method must be added before activation. Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-03.QAC "runoff controlled", "site safe and rinsed")

## Stop-work triggers, escalation and incidents (service-specific)

### From "10. Stop-work conditions"

The general stop-work rule is [[FACT-SVC-STOP-WORK-RULE]]. Service-specific triggers:

Service-specific stop triggers (source: spec HI-03.SAF unless noted; PROPOSED wording pending qualified review):
- People or pets enter the wash zone: stop washing until they are clear (X-11).
- The test on an inconspicuous area shows etching, striping or other damage: stop and escalate (HI-03.SAF "test inconspicuous area"; HI-03.QAC "without etching/striping").
- The pressure is not confirmed as appropriate for the surface: do not start washing (HI-03.SAF "use surface-appropriate pressure"; C-HI03-08).
- Electrical fixtures, outlets, openings or landscaping cannot be protected: do not wash toward them; stop and escalate (HI-03.SAF "protect electrical/openings/landscape").
- Runoff cannot be managed: stop (HI-03.SAF "manage runoff").
- Damaged substrate: stop, record (JOB-004) and escalate (HI-03.SAF "stop for damaged substrate").
  - Evidence: note
- Water frozen or unavailable, or severe weather: record No Start / Blocked instead of starting (spec SCH-008, START-011; agreement "Water Access Mandatory").
  - Evidence: note

Service holds (inserted in section 12). Critical holds cannot be overridden (31 section 8.3); a hold that exists because a required module or policy is missing (pressure washing, chemicals and SDS, weather) behaves as a critical hold until that module is approved (31 section 8.4 rule 4, PROPOSED):

| Hold | Where in section 12 | Release role and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| C-HI03-01: test area, exclusion zone, PPE and protection of fixtures, outlets and openings before washing; turbo-nozzle method suspended | Before the rotary and turbo-nozzle steps; after the turbo-nozzle step | Qualified safety professional: reviewed method from the washer and nozzle manufacturer instructions, recorded approval |
| C-HI03-02: SDS, PPE, landscape protection, final rinse and runoff control for the chemical program | Before the chemical pre-treat and absorbent lift steps; after the last step of each option | Qualified safety professional (SDS and PPE); local authority for discharge rules |
| X-08 pressure-washing module; X-11 occupants and pets; X-27 weather module | In the holds before the washing steps | Qualified safety professional, once the modules are approved |
