### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🕒 Phase 1: Structural Framing & Layout Verification"

> HOLD (C-DW03-01) - preconditions, before the first step below ("Plumb Line Layout") and before any framing: approved plans and scope on file; permit determination recorded by a reviewer, not assumed; headers, load-bearing and addition framing = HOLD for a structural engineer or building official; plumbing, electrical and HVAC relocations by licensed trades only. Do not start framing until these are recorded for this job (spec START-010). Release only by the named reviewer, with evidence (31 section 8.3). Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10 DW-03; spec DW-03.INT, DW-03.SAF)

> HOLD (C-DW03-02) - before the first step below and before any step that cuts, drills, sands or removes existing finishes: in a pre-1978 or unknown-age home, painted surfaces, old plaster and other suspect materials are not disturbed; stop and escalate. No HandyHub sampling; release only on a recorded disposition by an EPA RRP-certified renovator or an accredited asbestos inspector accepted by the owner. Pending qualified review. (source: 31 sections 7.2 and 10; spec START-011; owner facts 2026-10-02, EPA RRP and asbestos sources; X-13)

> HOLD (X-12) - before the first step below: snapping ceiling lines and fastening top plates is work at height. Ladders and other access equipment are used only under the approved height and access policy, which does not exist yet (02 G-005); until it exists this work stays on hold (31 section 8.4 rule 4). Pending qualified review. (source: spec DW-03.HAZ "falls", DW-03.TLS "access equipment"; section 2, Do not perform item 5)

- [ ] **Plumb Line Layout:** Snap blue chalk lines on the floor and ceiling to map new partitions. Use a laser level to verify that new vertical stud tracking is perfectly plumb.
- [ ] **Fastening Tracks:** Secure top and bottom plates using structural wood screws (for wood joists) or concrete anchors (for basement floor slabs). Space studs precisely at 16 inches on-center.

> FLAG (C-DW03-01): UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions. "Space studs precisely at 16 inches on-center" in the step above has no source; stud spacing, plate fasteners and anchors come only from the approved plans, any listed assembly, the board and fastener manufacturers and any applicable code requirement confirmed by the building official (31 section 10 DW-03: "manufacturer, listed assembly and code govern"). The "structural wood screws" and "concrete anchors" stay on the structural HOLD above; anchor type, size and embedment are never estimated (risk-triage playbook "do not invent ... anchor load ratings"). The step text above is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: structural engineer or building official.

- [ ] **Fire-Blocking & Blocking:** Install solid wood blocking sections behind target zones where heavy items will be mounted later (e.g., TV wall mounts, kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities).

> FLAG (G-059): the step above is titled "Fire-Blocking & Blocking" but describes only backing for later mounted items; fireblocking is not addressed and is not performed under this step until the building official's requirements are recorded for the job. The step states no load capacity for the items mounted later. The step text above is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: building official.

> FLAG (C-DW03-04): no insulation step exists between Phase 1 and Phase 2, although pricing sells the "Insulation Install" add-on and the agreement offers "insulation [ ]". Insulation is not installed under this SOP (section 2, Out of scope); if the owner adds it, a reviewed step, materials, PPE and any required inspection before cover come first. Pending owner decision.

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 📦 Phase 2: Technical Panel Selection & Hanging"

- [ ] **Board Allocation Map:**
	- **Standard Zones:** 1/2-inch lightweight drywall sheets.
	- **Bathrooms / Wet Kitchens:** Moisture-resistant Greenboard or Purpleboard.
	- **Garage Ceilings / Boiler Rooms:** 5/8-inch Fire-Rated Type X sheets.

> FLAG (spec DW-03.SAF "use correct board/system"): the board types in the step above are a default map, not a specification. The board and system for each area come from the approved plans, the board manufacturer and any listed fire-rated assembly; fire separation for garage ceilings and boiler rooms, and the suitability of moisture-resistant board in wet areas, including behind tile (the main plan's claim, 02 C-DW03-09), are questions for the building official and the board manufacturer (04 DW-03 permit/license flags). If the board on site does not match the approved plans, stop (spec START-011 "any material mismatch"). The step text above is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: building official.

> HOLD (C-DW03-01) - inspection before concealment, before the step below ("Hanging Sequence") and before any board covers framing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC or other work: required inspections signed off with photos before concealment; do not conceal unapproved utility work (spec DW-03.SAF). A failed or incomplete inspection is a critical hold that no role can override (31 section 8.3 rule 2). Which inspections apply, and who arranges them, is recorded for the job by a reviewer (04 DW-03 permit/license flags, OPEN QUESTION). Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10 DW-03; spec DW-03.SAF, DW-03.QAC "penetrations/interfaces documented, inspection prerequisites satisfied")

> HOLD (C-DW03-08) - before the step below and every later step that uses stilts, the panel lift, ladders or team lifting: controls for stilts, the panel lift and team lifting must be approved by a qualified safety professional and in place. None is defined yet, and no height, load or team size is set here; until they exist those steps stay on hold (31 section 8.4 rule 4). Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10 DW-03; spec DW-03.HAZ "heavy panels, lifts", "falls"; DW-03.SAF "lifting/team controls"; X-12)

- [ ] **Hanging Sequence:** Always hang ceiling panels first, then push wall panels tightly up against the ceiling line. Stagger all vertical wall seams—never allow four corners to meet at a single point (prevents settling stress cracks).

> FLAG (31 section 3.5 rule 5): "(prevents settling stress cracks)" in the step above is a working rationale, not a promise to the customer; no crack-free outcome is offered (section 2, Do not perform item 8). The step text above is unchanged.

- [ ] **Fastener Pattern:** Drive screws 8 inches apart along all perimeter edges and 12 inches apart along center interior studs. Keep screws at least 3/8 inch away from the edge of the panel. Ensure heads are recessed without piercing the paper face.

> FLAG (C-DW03-01): UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions. "8 inches apart", "12 inches apart" and "at least 3/8 inch away from the edge" in the step above have no source; fastener type, length and spacing come only from the board manufacturer and, for a fire-rated assembly, the listed assembly (31 section 10 DW-03; spec DW-03.SAF "use correct board/system"). The step text above is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: qualified reviewer; building official for fire-rated assemblies.

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🧽 Phase 3: Premium Multi-Stage Taping & Mudding"

- [ ] **First Stage (Bedding):** Fill all tapered factory joints with multi-purpose joint compound. Embed premium paper joint tape directly into the wet mud using a 6-inch knife, squeezing out trapped air and excess compound.
- [ ] **Corner Bead Application:** Install metal or heavy composite plastic corner beads on all exposed external wall corners. Fasten with a crimper tool or screws, then apply a heavy coat of mud to blend the bead edge into the wall profile.

> FLAG (C-DW03-V02): the bead material differs across files: the step above allows "metal or heavy composite plastic" beads, while the agreement specifies "metal corner beads" only. Use the material on the approved estimate; a substitution that affects specification or customer expectation needs approval (spec JOB-007). The step text above is unchanged. Pending owner decision.

- [ ] **Fastener Filling:** Apply a clean single pass of mud over all recessed screw dimples throughout the boards.

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🩹 Phase 4: Blending & Skimming Transitions"

> HOLD (C-DW03-02) - before the step below ("The Old-to-New Merge"): the tie-in to horsehair plaster or another old finish is held until the build year and the plaster and paint disposition are recorded for the job; in a pre-1978 or unknown-age home, stop and escalate. Release only on a recorded disposition by an EPA RRP-certified renovator or an accredited asbestos inspector accepted by the owner. Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10 DW-03; 04 DW-03 prerequisites "plaster disposition recorded before demolition or tie-ins", PROPOSED)

- [ ] **The Old-to-New Merge:** Where new drywall connects to thick old horsehair plaster, use a wide 12-inch or 14-inch knife to feather the mud layer out at least 18 inches into the old wall area to create a gradual, invisible slope.

> FLAG (C-DW03-11): the step above feathers "at least 18 inches", while the agreement commits to "up to 18 inches"; the owner decides one commitment for the agreement, this SOP and the quality check. "invisible" is an outcome word, not a promise to the customer (31 section 3.5 rule 5). The step text above is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; attorney (agreement wording).

- [ ] **Second Fill Coat:** Apply a wider coat of topping compound over all joint seams, widening the patch to 8–10 inches.
- [ ] **Third Finish Coat:** Apply an ultra-thin tight coat of lightweight mud to fill micro-pinholes and sand tracks.

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / ✨ Phase 5: Final Sanding & Inspection Protocol"

- [ ] **Edge Sanding:** Sand all joints using a fine 180-grit sponge, taking extreme care not to scuff or fray the raw paper facing of the drywall sheets.

> FLAG (C-DW03-15): the agreement's final draw falls due on "final mechanical sanding", while the step above sands by hand with a sponge; the sanding method is not changed here, and the payment trigger is for the owner and the attorney. Dust controls for sanding are pending qualified safety review (sections 6, 9 and 11). The step text above is unchanged.

- [ ] **The Light Test:** Place a commercial halogen work light close to the wall angle. Meticulously inspect every junction for trowel marks, craters, or uneven ridges. Spot-apply compound to corrections.
- [ ] **Site Cleanup:** Vacuum up all fine sanding debris, damp-wipe window frames, and sweep clean before painter turnover.
