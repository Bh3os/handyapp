### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🕒 Phase 1: Deep Masonry Cleansing"

- [ ] **Efflorescence Scrape:** Use a stiff wire brush to scrub away chalky white salt deposits (efflorescence) from old brick faces.

> HOLD (C-PT03-05): the step above removes efflorescence but does not address its cause. Photograph and record where efflorescence was found (spec PT-03.QAC "defects/limitations documented"), and do not start any step of Phase 3 or Phase 4 until a qualified reviewer has recorded the moisture-source disposition. No moisture threshold is set here. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-03.SAF "address active moisture cause before coating when required"; PT-03.INT "efflorescence/cracks, moisture source")

> HOLD (C-PT03-01): before the "Mildew Wash" step below - NO MIXING - pending SDS-based product selection. Do not mix the masonry cleaner with bleach or any other product, and do not apply it, until a named product has been selected and its SDS, and the SDS of anything it would be combined with, reviewed and approved by the qualified safety professional. Use, mix and apply only as the approved label allows; no product, ratio or dwell is set here. Plants, openings, people and pets are protected and runoff control is in place before any application. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-03.MAT "approved masonry cleaner"; PT-03.QAC "openings/landscape protected"; 04 PT-03 "permit/license flags" (runoff); 29 section 8.3; 31 section 10 PT-03; X-11)

- [ ] **Mildew Wash:** Spray a specialized masonry cleaner mixed with bleach over dark mildew zones.

> FLAG (X-06): the step above treats "dark mildew zones". The stop-work rule in section 10 lists possible mold; which exterior growth this step may treat is decided by the qualified reviewer once the company definition that separates cleanable mildew and staining from suspected mold growth exists (Q-31-10). Until then the stop-work rule is not relaxed. (source: 31 section 5.8)

> HOLD (C-PT03-03, X-08, X-11, X-27): before the "High-Pressure Cleanse" step below - keep people and pets out of the wash zone; protect electrical fixtures, outlets, meters, doors and windows from direct spray (in the original order the Phase 2 masking comes after this step, and that sequence is under review); confirm the washing method and pressure for this masonry from the manufacturer instructions and the qualified safety professional; and confirm acceptable weather under the pressure-washing and weather modules (neither exists yet). If loose or crumbling mortar or spalling brick appears, stop washing that area and follow section 10. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-03.HAZ "pressure wash", PT-03.INT "weather"; agreement "Pre-existing Masonry Defects"; 31 sections 5.8 and 10)

- [ ] **High-Pressure Cleanse:** Power wash the entire brick envelope at 2,500-3,000 PSI to clear out deep dirt, mud splatters, and organic residue from porous masonry joints.

> FLAG (C-PT03-03): the pressure in the step above is UNVERIFIED - confirm per masonry condition and product instructions (UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions). It stays verbatim until a qualified reviewer confirms or replaces it; no value is set here, and no pressure washer is listed in the inventory yet. (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 3; 31 section 10 PT-03)

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🧪 Phase 3: Execution - Option A: Authentic Limewash Look"

> FLAG (C-PT03-07): the agreement promises a test section with client sign-off before the full facade and describes the application as "spraying"; this option brush-applies the limewash and has no test-section or sign-off step. Not covered - pending review (section 2); open question recorded with C-PT03-07 in 02.

> HOLD (C-PT03-05, C-PT03-02): before any step of this option - the moisture-source disposition, and the compatibility of the limewash system with this masonry and any existing coating, are recorded by the qualified reviewer from the product's manufacturer data. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-03.SAF "confirm compatible coating system", "address active moisture cause before coating when required")

> HOLD (PT-03.SAF): before the "Slaking Slurry Mix" step below - skin and eye protection selected by the qualified safety professional is worn for mixing, wetting, brushing and distressing lime. The "instructions" named in that step are the approved limewash product's label and data sheet; mix, pre-wet and apply only as they state. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-03.SAF "PPE/skin/eye protection"; PT-03.HAZ "chemical/alkaline materials"; 29 section 8.2 row 8)

- [ ] **Slaking Slurry Mix:** Mix authentic Italian Class-A slaked lime powder with water according to instructions until a milk-like consistency is reached.
- [ ] **Saturating the Masonry:** Spray the brick wall with water right before application—*Limewash requires a wet brick canvas to bind chemically.*
- [ ] **Masonry Brush Apply:** Use a large, thick block brush to work the limewash slurry into the brick pores using cross-hatch strokes.

> HOLD (C-PT03-03, X-08): before the "Artisanal Distressing" step below - the "light pressure washer mist" in that step falls under the same pressure-washing hold as "High-Pressure Cleanse" (method and pressure from the manufacturer instructions and the qualified safety professional; people and pets out of the zone). Until that hold is released, only the damp-sponge method named in the same step is available. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-03.HAZ "pressure wash"; 31 section 10 PT-03)

- [ ] **Artisanal Distressing:** Wait 30 minutes for the limewash to partially set. Use a light pressure washer mist or a damp sponge to selectively wash away wash coats from edges/corners, exposing natural brick hints for an old-world look.

> FLAG: UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions (the approved limewash product's data) for the waiting time in the step above; it stays verbatim until a reviewer confirms or replaces it. (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 3)

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🛡️ Phase 4: Execution - Option B: Solid Elastomeric Shielding"

> HOLD (C-PT03-05, C-PT03-02): before any step of this option - the moisture-source disposition, and the compatibility of the elastomeric coating system with this masonry and any existing coating, are recorded by the qualified reviewer from the product's manufacturer data. Whether the coating would trap moisture in this wall is part of that review (spec PT-03.HAZ "moisture trapping/wrong coating"). Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-03.SAF "confirm compatible coating system", "address active moisture cause before coating when required")

> HOLD (X-08, X-11): before the "Airless Spray Setup" step below - operate the sprayer only per its manufacturer's safety instructions, with people and pets out of the spray zone and openings, landscaping, vehicles and neighboring property protected. Tip size, pressure and pass overlap come from the sprayer and coating manufacturer instructions; the values in the steps below stay verbatim and are UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions. Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10 PT-03 "Airless spraying per the sprayer manufacturer's safety instructions"; 31 section 7.2 rank 10)

- [ ] **Airless Spray Setup:** Set up a heavy-duty airless sprayer with a large 517 or 521 tip size to handle thick masonry paint.
- [ ] **Saturating Spray Pass:** Spray a heavy, thick layer of elastomeric paint over the dry brick walls, overlapping sweeps by 50%.
- [ ] **Back-Rolling the Brick:** Have a second painter follow the sprayer with a thick 1-1/4 inch nap roller, forcing the paint deep into porous mortar joints to seal micro-cracks and bridge gaps completely.

> FLAG: outcome claim - owner and attorney review (C-PT03-02). "seal micro-cracks and bridge gaps completely" in the step above depends on the coating's manufacturer data; it is a working description, not a customer promise (section 17). This step needs both workers at once (section 4).

## Stop-work triggers, escalation and incidents (service-specific)

### From "10. Stop-work conditions"

The general stop-work rule is [[FACT-SVC-STOP-WORK-RULE]]. Service-specific triggers:

Service-specific stop triggers (source: spec PT-03.SAF unless noted; PROPOSED wording pending qualified review):
- Coating system not confirmed as compatible with the masonry and any existing coating or sealer: do not coat ("confirm compatible coating system").
- An active moisture source, or efflorescence that points to one, with no recorded disposition: do not coat; stop and escalate ("address active moisture cause before coating when required"; C-PT03-05).
- Required PPE, including skin and eye protection, not available: do not handle lime or chemicals ("PPE/skin/eye protection").
- Unsuitable weather, or height or access beyond the approved policy: record No Start / Blocked or stop ("weather and height controls"; spec SCH-008, START-011).
  - Evidence: note
- Loose or crumbling mortar or spalling brick found during washing: stop washing that area, photograph it, record it (JOB-004) and report it with the approved message (agreement "Pre-existing Masonry Defects"; Do not perform entry 9).
  - Evidence: note
- People or pets enter the wash or spray zone, or spray drifts toward vehicles, plants or neighboring property: stop (X-11; C-PT03-08).
- Any instruction to mix the masonry cleaner with bleach before an approved product and SDS review: do not mix (C-PT03-01).
- A gas meter, regulator or vent would have to be wrapped or covered to continue: stop and escalate (Do not perform entry 10).

Service holds (inserted in section 12). Critical holds cannot be overridden (31 section 8.3); a hold that exists because a required module or policy is missing (height and access, pressure washing, chemicals and SDS, weather) behaves as a critical hold until that module is approved (31 section 8.4 rule 4, PROPOSED):
