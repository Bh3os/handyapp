## 3. Service-specific work steps

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🕒 Phase 1: Valet, Laundry & Closet Organization"

> HOLD (C-CL06-01): before the garment sorting step below - the customer's privacy boundaries, off-limits rooms and the items workers may handle are on record for this visit (section 3). Collect clothing only where the customer allows. Cash, jewelry, keys, mail, documents or other personal records found among clothing are not opened, read, photographed or kept; they are handled only as the customer has authorized in writing, and valuables out of place, missing items and any damage are reported (section 19). Pending qualified review and privacy-owner review. (source: spec CL-06.INT "privacy boundaries", "delicate items"; CL-06.SAF "strict privacy; do not photograph/access personal records without need", "escalation for valuables/damage"; 31 section 10 CL-06)

- [ ] **Garment Sorting:** Collect discarded clothing items left around the room; separate dirty laundry from clothes requiring dry cleaning or pressing.

> FLAG (C-CL06-04): the step above separates dirty laundry from clothes "requiring dry cleaning or pressing", but no step washes, dries or folds laundry, hand-washes delicates or takes garments to a dry cleaner. Those tasks are sold in pricing and the main plan and are not covered (section 2); under this SOP, garments stay in the residence where the customer keeps them. Off-site custody needs insurance broker and attorney review. (source: 02 C-CL06-04; 31 section 10 CL-06; 31 SOP-029, PROPOSED)

> HOLD (C-CL06-01, C-CL06-02): before the ironing and steaming step below - (1) read each garment's care label; a garment without a clear care label, or whose label does not allow the planned ironing or steaming, is not pressed and is set aside and reported (section 17); (2) inspect the steamer or iron and its cord before use and use it only as its manufacturer instructions allow. Burn controls and the electrical load of high-wattage equipment on household circuits are pending safety review; a damaged cord or plug or a tripped circuit stops the step, with no work on electrical components (Do not perform entry 1). Pending qualified review. (source: spec CL-06.SAF "follow garment/surface care labels"; inventory "High-wattage steamer" line; 02 C-CL06-02; 31 section 10 CL-06)

- [ ] **Ironing & Steaming:** Iron or steam requested shirts, suits, or delicate dresses to remove all wrinkles.

> FLAG: outcome wording - "to remove all wrinkles" in the step above is kept verbatim but is not a promise. What may be done to a garment is limited by its care label; a garment that cannot be pressed further within its label is reported, not forced. Owner review of the wording (Q-31-16). (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 5; spec CL-06.SAF)

- [ ] **Uniform Hanging:** Hang clothing items uniformly inside the wardrobe (all hangers facing the same direction, items spaced evenly).
- [ ] **Shoe Presentation:** Align shoes neatly on the closet floor or shoe racks, grouped by style and color, facing forward.

> FLAG (C-CL06-V01): tier scope undefined - pricing reserves closet organization and garment steaming for the Gold tier and above, while the steps of this phase make no tier distinction and the agreement gives the same scope to every tier. The tasks of each visit come from the accepted plan version (section 3); the per-tier task list is an owner decision (Q-28-21), and agreement wording changes only after attorney review. (source: 02 C-CL06-V01)

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🛌 Phase 2: Luxury Bed Turndown & Linen Care"

> HOLD (C-CL06-02): before the hospital corners step below - lift and tuck method pending safety review. The inventory's tuck paddles, "used to slide under heavy mattresses to tuck sheets without lifting", are the PROPOSED control; any lifting limit or two-person rule comes only from the qualified safety professional (TBD). Pending qualified review. (source: spec CL-06.HAZ "ergonomic strain"; inventory "Premium Linen Sheet Straps & Tuck Paddles" line; 02 C-CL06-02)

- [ ] **Hospital Corners:** Make the bed using hospitality-grade sheets, tucking the bottom edges into tight 45-degree hospital corners.
- [ ] **Symmetry Check:** Pull the duvet/comforter completely straight and symmetrical on both sides; fluff sleeping and accent pillows to stand vertically.
- [ ] **Towel Rolling:** Replace used bath linens with fresh, warm-dried towels folded into precise hotel squares or rolled neatly into vanity displays.

> FLAG (C-CL06-04, C-CL06-12): "warm-dried" in the step above implies drying laundry, which no step covers (section 2). Where the fresh towels and bed linens come from, the customer's stock or HandyHub, is undecided (supply responsibility, C-CL06-12). (source: 02 C-CL06-04; 29 section 5.4 row CL-06)

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / ✨ Phase 3: High-End Dusting & Ambiance Staging"

> HOLD (C-CL06-01): before the clutter step below - organize only items in areas and of kinds the customer has authorized. Do not open, read, sort or photograph mail, documents or other personal records; stacking magazines does not extend to mail or papers. Cash, jewelry and other valuables are not moved beyond written authorization (Do not perform entries 9 and 10). Pending privacy-owner review. (source: spec CL-06.SAF; CL-06.QAC "high-value items undisturbed except authorized care"; 31 section 10 CL-06)

- [ ] **Clutter Management:** Organize daily clutter: neatly bundle charging cables with ties, stack magazines chronologically, and remove empty cups/dishes.

> HOLD (C-CL06-01, C-CL06-02): before the vanity polish step below - dust and wipe delicate and high-value items (glass trays, perfume bottles, furniture finishes) only where the customer has authorized care, with a product and method suited to the surface per its care instructions; an unknown finish is left untreated and reported, and any damage is reported at once (section 19). Pending qualified review. (source: spec CL-06.SAF "follow garment/surface care labels", "escalation for valuables/damage"; CL-06.HAZ "chemical/surface damage"; CL-06.QAC)

- [ ] **Vanity Polish:** Meticulously dust and wipe delicate glass makeup trays, perfume bottles, nightstands, and dressing tables.

> HOLD (C-CL06-02): before the scent and decor step below - mist linens, use a room scent or refresh a diffuser only when the customer's fragrance preference and an allergy or sensitivity check covering occupants and pets are on record, and only with a product approved with a reviewed SDS (29 section 8.3 interim rule, PROPOSED). If the customer declined scenting or the check is missing, skip the scenting parts of the step and record why. Pending qualified review. (source: 02 C-CL06-02; spec CL-06.INT "preferred products"; 04 CL-06 "customer preparation requirements"; 31 section 10 CL-06)

- [ ] **Scent & Decor:** Refresh room reed diffusers, spray a light fabric mist over linens, and trim/water any fresh-cut flowers in vases.

> FLAG (C-CL06-V01, C-CL06-12): tier scope undefined - pricing reserves "ambient scent refreshing" for the Gold tier and above, while the step above runs on every visit; the per-tier task list is an owner decision (Q-28-21). Who supplies and pays for diffuser refills and fresh flowers is undecided (C-CL06-12). (source: 02 C-CL06-V01, C-CL06-12)

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🧹 Phase 4: Invisible Waste & Floor Care"

- [ ] **Immediate Trash Clear:** Empty all trash bins even if they contain minor waste; wipe the bin interior and use premium, snug-fitting liners.

> FLAG: sharps - broken glass, dishes or other sharp items in bins are a hazard that the registry lists as an ASSUMPTION pending safety review; the handling method and any container are TBD by the qualified safety professional. Until then, a sharp or biohazard item found while emptying or wiping a bin stops the step above and is reported (section 10). (source: 04 CL-06 "hazards"; 29 section 5.2 `cleaning-core`)

> HOLD (C-CL06-02, X-11): before the floor care step below - (1) use a cleaning product and method suited to the floor finish per its care instructions; a stone or wood floor whose care instructions are unknown is not wet-mopped until the customer confirms them; (2) keep occupants and pets off damp floors and mark the wet area until it is dry (a wet-floor sign is proposed in the inventory). Pending qualified review. (source: spec CL-06.SAF "follow garment/surface care labels"; CL-06.HAZ "chemical/surface damage"; 02 C-CL06-02; 29 section 5.2 `cleaning-core`)

- [ ] **Silent Floor Care:** Use a low-noise vacuum on carpets and rugs; damp-mop hard stone or wood floors using a high-gloss microfiber pad for a streak-free sheen.

> HOLD (C-CL06-01): secure closeout - after the floor care step above and before leaving the residence: return moved items to their agreed places; leave doors, windows, locks and the alarm as the customer instructed, using access details only from the app's secured fields (never written down, photographed or texted); return or log keys as custody items; confirm that high-value items were left undisturbed except for authorized care; record the closeout check. If the residence cannot be secured as instructed, escalate before leaving (section 19), unless your own safety requires you to leave. Pending qualified review and privacy-owner review. (source: spec CL-06.QAC "high-value items undisturbed except authorized care, secure closeout"; 31 section 10 CL-06; 31 section 8.4 rule 3; 27 AUTH-009; spec COM-002)

## Stop-work triggers, escalation and incidents (service-specific)

### From "10. Stop-work conditions"

The general stop-work rule is [[FACT-SVC-STOP-WORK-RULE]]. Service-specific triggers:

Service-specific stop triggers (source: spec CL-06.SAF unless noted; PROPOSED wording pending qualified review):
- A task would need opening, reading or photographing mail, documents or other personal records: do not; escalate (CL-06.SAF "strict privacy; do not photograph/access personal records without need").
  - Evidence: measurement, note
- A garment has no clear care label, or its label does not allow the planned pressing; a surface's finish or care instructions are unknown: do not treat that item; set it aside and report it (CL-06.SAF "follow garment/surface care labels").
  - Evidence: note
- Valuables found out of place or missing, or damage found or caused: stop handling the item and escalate (CL-06.SAF "escalation for valuables/damage"; spec JOB-008 for damage).
- A fragrance allergy or sensitivity is reported, or the fragrance check is not on record: no scent products (02 C-CL06-02).
  - Evidence: note
- No reviewed SDS for a product: do not use it (29 section 8.3).
- An electrical fault, such as a tripped circuit or a damaged cord, plug or outlet: stop using the appliance; do not work on electrical components (Do not perform entry 1; 02 C-CL06-02).
- Occupants or pets cannot be kept off a wet floor: do not mop that area until they can be (02 C-CL06-02).
- A sharp or biohazard item in a bin, linens or clutter: stop and report (04 CL-06 "hazards", ASSUMPTION).
  - Evidence: note
- The residence cannot be secured as the customer instructed at closeout: escalate before leaving, unless your own safety requires you to leave (CL-06.QAC "secure closeout"; 31 section 8.4 rule 3).
