### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🚨 Problem Option A: Fixing to Standard Drywall Hollow Walls"

> HOLD (C-HI01-01): before any hole or fastener goes into the wall in this option (the monkey-hook path of "Weight Audit" and the pilot hole of "Anchor Driving"), check each marked position for hidden electrical, plumbing or other services with the stocked scanner, used per its manufacturer instructions. Whether the stocked scanner detects wiring or pipes is not stated (section 7); where its manufacturer instructions do not cover them, a scan that finds nothing does not clear the risk, which counts as uncertain. If hidden-service risk or the wall condition is uncertain, STOP and escalate (sections 10 and 19). Cleared by: TBD - qualified safety professional. Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-01.SAF "stop if hidden-service risk or wall condition is uncertain"; 31 section 10; owner facts 2026-10-02 "Unknown is not safe")
>
> GATE (C-HI01-04): before any hole or fastener goes into the wall in this option, record the customer's approval of the layout set in "Laser Level Setup" (photo of the laser line or marked positions plus the customer's acknowledgement). Without it, do not drill or fasten. Pending review. (source: 02 C-HI01-04; spec HI-01.QAC "customer-approved placement"; agreement "Final Placement Approval" clause)

- [ ] **Weight Audit:** Weigh the artwork. If under 10 lbs, execute using heavy-duty monkey hooks. If 10–50 lbs, utilize professional self-drilling zinc toggle anchors.
  - Evidence: measurement

> HOLD (C-HI01-02): the step above covers only its own weight bands. An item heavier than these bands is not hung: STOP and escalate. Hardware capacity for the item weight and this substrate comes from the hardware manufacturer's rating recorded for the job; no rating is added here, and the maximum accepted weight is an open owner question. Cleared by: TBD - qualified safety professional. Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-01.SAF "confirm fastener capacity"; 31 section 10)
>
> HOLD (HI-01.SAF): if the weight audit above shows that the item needs a two-person lift, do not lift or hang it alone; stop that task until the second person is present. No weight threshold for a two-person lift is set yet (open owner question). Cleared by: TBD - qualified safety professional. Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-01.SAF "two-person lift when required")

- [ ] **Laser Level Setup:** Snap a self-leveling horizontal green laser line at the client's preferred eye-level height (standard center is 57 to 60 inches from the floor).

> FLAG: UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions. The "standard center is 57 to 60 inches from the floor" figure in the step above is an unsourced convention; the client's preferred height named in the same step governs. Pending review. (source: 31 section 10)

- [ ] **Anchor Driving:** Drill a clean pilot hole. Insert the toggle bolt through the bracket, push it into the drywall cavity until the wings snap open behind the board, and drive the screw flush.

> FLAG (C-HI01-11): verify the step above against the hardware manufacturer's installation instructions. The stocked anchor is a self-drilling toggle anchor, while this step describes a toggle bolt set through a pilot hole; the match is unverified. Pending qualified review. (source: 02 C-HI01-11; inventory file)

- [ ] **Bumper Placement:** Apply soft rubber adhesive bumper pads to the bottom back corners of the picture frame to prevent scratching or floating dust shadows on the wall.

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🚨 Problem Option B: Fixing to Raw Brick / Concrete Fireplace Mantels"

> HOLD (31 section 10, fireplace mantel): before "Substrate Drilling" below, every fireplace-mantel install needs an on-site assessment of heat and gas-appliance proximity; without it, do not start this option. Whether fireplace mounting is offered at all is an open owner question (Q-31-08). Cleared by: TBD - qualified safety professional. Pending qualified review.
>
> HOLD (C-HI01-02): this option gives no weight band. Before "Substrate Drilling" below, record the item weight and the anchor manufacturer's rating for that weight in this masonry; without both, STOP and escalate. If the item needs a two-person lift, do not lift or hang it alone (spec HI-01.SAF). No rating is added here. Cleared by: TBD - qualified safety professional. Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10; spec HI-01.SAF)
>
> GATE (C-HI01-04): before "Substrate Drilling" below, record the customer's approval of the marked positions (photo plus the customer's acknowledgement). Without it, do not drill. Pending review. (source: 02 C-HI01-04; spec HI-01.QAC "customer-approved placement")
>
> HOLD (C-HI01-01): before "Substrate Drilling" below, check each marked position for hidden electrical, plumbing or other services with the stocked scanner, used per its manufacturer instructions. Whether the stocked scanner detects wiring or pipes is not stated (section 7); where its manufacturer instructions do not cover them, a scan that finds nothing does not clear the risk, which counts as uncertain. If hidden-service risk or the wall condition is uncertain, STOP and escalate (sections 10 and 19). Cleared by: TBD - qualified safety professional. Pending qualified review. (source: spec HI-01.SAF; 31 section 10; owner facts 2026-10-02 "Unknown is not safe")

- [ ] **Substrate Drilling:** Use a hammer drill fitted with a carbide-tipped masonry bit. Drill a clean hole directly into the center of the solid brick face. *Warning: Avoid drilling into soft mortar joints as they crumble over time.*
- [ ] **Sleeve Setting:** Drive a plastic or lead expansion masonry anchor sleeve into the hole using a rubber mallet until it sits perfectly flush with the brick face.

> FLAG (hazardous material): the step above names a "lead expansion masonry anchor sleeve". Lead-containing hardware is a hazardous-material handling item (SDS need and hygiene per the qualified safety reviewer), not a lead-paint item. Pending qualified review. (source: 31 section 10)
>
> FLAG (C-HI01-11): verify the step above against the anchor manufacturer's installation instructions. The listed plastic sleeves are not stated as masonry-rated, and lead sleeves are not stocked. Pending qualified review. (source: 02 C-HI01-11; inventory file)

- [ ] **Bracket Fastening:** Thread a heavy-duty galvanized structural screw through the hanger hook and drive it tightly into the sleeve until fully anchored.

> FLAG (C-HI01-11): verify the step above against the hardware manufacturer's installation instructions. The stocked masonry fastener is a Tapcon-style screw, not the screw-into-sleeve combination this step describes; the match is unverified. Pending qualified review. (source: 02 C-HI01-11; inventory file)

## Stop-work triggers, escalation and incidents (service-specific)

### From "10. Stop-work conditions"

The general stop-work rule is [[FACT-SVC-STOP-WORK-RULE]]. Service-specific triggers:

Service-specific stop triggers (source: spec HI-01.SAF "verify substrate; confirm fastener capacity; two-person lift when required; protect floor/wall; stop if hidden-service risk or wall condition is uncertain"):
- Hidden-service risk or the wall condition is uncertain: stop (source: spec HI-01.SAF; C-HI01-01).
- The substrate cannot be verified or is not one this SOP covers: stop and request an on-site assessment (source: spec HI-01.SAF "verify substrate"; C-HI01-05).
- Fastener capacity cannot be confirmed from the hardware manufacturer's rating for the item weight and substrate, or the item is heavier than the SOP's own bands: stop and escalate (source: spec HI-01.SAF "confirm fastener capacity"; C-HI01-02).
- A two-person lift is required and the second person is not available: stop the lift (source: spec HI-01.SAF).
- Floor and wall cannot be protected: do not start the affected task (source: spec HI-01.SAF "protect floor/wall").
- Fireplace-mantel install without the on-site assessment of heat and gas-appliance proximity: do not start (source: 31 section 10).
- A ladder is needed and no approved height/access policy exists: the task stays blocked (source: 31 section 3.3 entry 5; 02 G-005).
- Every HOLD in section 12 names who clears it; critical holds clear only with a recorded qualified assessment, never by override or customer request (source: 31 section 8.3).
  - Evidence: note
