If the oven or range is on or hot, or its state cannot be confirmed, do not start the oven part of either step; escalate (section 19). Pending review.

- [ ] **Kitchen Prep:** Spray heavy-duty degreaser onto the range hood filters, stove grates, and interior oven walls.

> HOLD (precondition; C-CL02-03): CHEMICAL COMPATIBILITY NOT REVIEWED - do not combine or sequence products until SDS review. - [ ] **Bathroom Prep:** Apply thick toilet bowl cleaner under the rim; spray anti-limescale/mildew remover onto shower tile grout, glass doors, and faucet bases.

> HOLD (31 section 10 CL-02; 04 section 5; Q-04-15): in the step above, removing vent grilles and dusting inside the opening are not performed: they overlap CL-07 Air Duct & Ventilation Cleaning, and the boundary is pending the owner's decision. > HOLD (precondition; C-CL02-06; spec BRF-003 "approximate age if known"): before "Baseboards & Trim", "Doors" and "Wall Spots", check the painted surfaces. - [ ] **Baseboards & Trim:** Hand-wipe all baseboards, chair rails, and crown moldings with a damp microfiber cloth to remove caked dust.
- [ ] **Doors:** Clean the top ledge of all doors, wipe door panels, sanitize knobs, and scrub grime around door frames.
- [ ] **Wall Spots:** Meticulously inspect and spot-clean walls to remove fingerprints, scuff marks, and crayon lines around outlets.

## 5. Cleanup and waste handling

### From "14. Cleanup"

- Clean the area, remove waste and protection, and account for tools and materials (source: spec APXA-005; QA-002).
- Restore components altered within scope, such as drain stoppers (source: spec APXA-005; C-CL02-08).
- Close the products and keep the chlorine-based product apart from the acid products for transport; transport rules are set by the reviewer (source: 29 section 8.3 interim rule; 29 section 8.2 item 6).
- Return unused materials (source: spec APXA-007).

### From "15. Waste handling"

- Waste streams: mop and rinse water with chemical residue; used cloths and pads; drain hair and debris; used razor blades; empty product containers (source: 29 section 8.2 item 10; section 12 steps).
- No disposal method or destination is approved: contain chemical residues and dispose of them only by the method the reviewer approves for the municipality (source: 31 section 3.1 row 15; Q-29-10; HUMAN REVIEW: local waste or sewer authority; qualified safety professional).
- Used blades: disposal method TBD - requires qualified safety reviewer review; a sharps container is proposed in the inventory file (source: inventory file; spec CL-02.HAZ).
- The inventory's contractor bags are described for "heavy, damp kitchen or bathroom waste"; their destination follows the same open question (source: inventory file; Q-29-10).
