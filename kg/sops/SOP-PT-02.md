## 3. Service-specific work steps

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🧪 Phase 2: Chemical Stripping & Slurry Flushing"

> HOLD (C-PT02-05, X-27): before the stripper application step below - confirm that the weather and temperature are suitable for the work and for the approved products, per the product instructions and the agreement's weather clause; if not, postpone and record No Start / Blocked. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-02.SAF "postpone unsuitable weather"; spec SCH-008; 31 section 10 PT-02)

> HOLD (soundness, 04 D-04-11; older coatings and treated lumber, OPEN QUESTION Q-31-07): before the stripper application step below - walk the deck for rot, loose boards and structural defects, route any finding to HI-10 or HI-11 and do not coat over it; where the property was built before 1978 or its age is unknown, do not strip or wash an existing coating until a qualified person records a disposition; older treated lumber of unknown treatment waits for the qualified safety professional. Pending qualified review. (source: agreement "Structural Integrity"; 31 section 7.2 rank 2; 31 section 10 PT-02; risk-triage playbook L27)

> HOLD (C-PT02-02, X-11): before the stripper application step below - do not pour or apply the sodium hydroxide stripper until its SDS has been reviewed and the product approved, PPE for pouring, spraying and washing it off has been selected by the qualified safety professional, the sprayer is dedicated and labeled for it (acid and caustic products never share a sprayer unless both SDSs allow it - RISK), and people and pets are out of the work area. No strength, dilution or dwell is set here. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-02.HAZ "chemicals"; 29 section 8.2 rows 1, 3, 4 and 8; 31 section 10 PT-02)

- [ ] **Stripper Application:** Pour heavy-duty sodium hydroxide wood stripper into a plastic pump sprayer. Apply a uniform wet coat over gray, weather-beaten wood sections.
- [ ] **Dwell Monitor:** Allow the chemical to sit for 15-20 minutes. *Never let the wood dry out; mist lightly with water if it begins to evaporate.*

> FLAG: UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions (product label and SDS) for the dwell time in the step above; it stays verbatim until a reviewer confirms or replaces it. (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 3; 31 section 10 PT-02)

> HOLD (C-PT02-09, X-08): before the power-wash step below - confirm a pressure method appropriate for the wood from the washer manufacturer instructions and the reviewer, keep people and pets out of an exclusion zone, and wear PPE selected by the qualified safety professional; the pressure washing module does not exist yet. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-02.SAF "pressure appropriate"; 02 C-PT02-09; 31 section 5.8)

- [ ] **Low-Pressure Power Wash:** Use a pressure washer fitted with a wide 40-degree fan nozzle. Keep the wand tip 12 inches away from the wood and sweep fluidly *with the grain*. Scrape away the liquefied gray pulp without tearing soft wood fibers.

> FLAG: UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions for the nozzle angle and stand-off distance in the step above. "Low-pressure" is not tied to a stated pressure, and the listed washer's rated pressure is not confirmed as appropriate for wood (C-PT02-09). The step stays as written. (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 3; 31 section 10 PT-02)

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🧴 Phase 3: Wood Brightening & Neutralization"

> HOLD (C-PT02-02): before the brightener step below - apply the oxalic acid brightener only with its SDS reviewed and PPE selected by the qualified safety professional (the inventory's gloves and goggles line stays mandatory), from a sprayer dedicated and labeled for the acid; never the stripper's sprayer unless both SDSs allow it (RISK). Pending qualified review. (source: inventory "Chemical-Resistant Elbow Gloves & Splash Goggles" line; 29 section 8.2 rows 3 and 4; 31 section 10 PT-02)

- [ ] **pH Reset:** Spray an oxalic acid wood brightener over the freshly washed wet lumber. This instantly neutralizes the harsh alkaline stripper, kills deep mold spores, and returns the wood to its original bright blonde tone.

> FLAG (X-06): "kills deep mold spores" in the step above is kept verbatim. It is an unsubstantiated antimicrobial claim and is not repeated to customers; which exterior growth this step may treat waits for the company definition that separates cleanable mildew from suspected mold growth (Q-31-10). The stop-work rule in section 10 for possible mold is not relaxed meanwhile. (source: 31 section 5.8; 04 PT-02 "permit/license flags")

- [ ] **Dwell & Deep Rinse:** Let it sit for 10 minutes, then thoroughly rinse the entire deck structure and adjacent plants with a high-volume garden hose.

> FLAG: UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions (product label and SDS) for the dwell time in the step above. Where the rinse water and residue may go is decided for the municipality (Q-29-10); no conclusion is drawn here. (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 3; 04 PT-02 "permit/license flags")

- [ ] **Moisture Check:** Allow the wood to dry for a minimum of 48 hours. Check wood moisture levels using a digital meter—**must be below 12% to 15%** before applying oil stain.
  - Evidence: measurement

> FLAG (C-PT02-08): UNSOURCED - verify against manufacturer instructions for the drying time and the moisture range in the step above. The range is ambiguous and is stated differently from the agreement's moisture clause; the limit must come from the approved product's instructions (spec PT-02.SAF "verify wood dry enough per product"). The existing check stays in force until a qualified reviewer replaces it. Record the meter reading as completion evidence; the meter is not in the inventory (proposed there). (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 3; 36 section 3.4 rule 8)

### From "12. Step-by-step procedure / 🧳 Phase 4: Staining Application & Back-Brushing"

> HOLD (C-PT02-05, C-PT02-02, X-27): before the perimeter cut step below - start staining only when the moisture check above has passed and is recorded, the weather and temperature suit the approved stain per its instructions and the agreement's weather clause, the stain's SDS has been reviewed, and PPE for brushing and spraying it has been selected by the qualified safety professional. Pending qualified review. (source: spec PT-02.SAF "verify wood dry enough per product", "postpone unsuitable weather"; 31 section 10 PT-02)

- [ ] **Perimeter Cut:** Use a 4-inch wide thick stain brush to paint edges, vertical posts, railings, and spaces between deck boards.

> HOLD (X-08, X-11): before the spray/roll step below - if the airless sprayer is used, follow the sprayer manufacturer's safety instructions, keep people and pets out of the spray zone, protect adjacent property from overspray, and confirm that conditions allow spraying without drift; the method waits for the pressure washing and airless spraying module. The step itself also names a roller. Pending qualified review. (source: original step "using a roller or airless sprayer"; 31 section 5.8; 31 section 7.2 rank 10)

- [ ] **Bulk Spray/Roll:** Apply the oil-based stain over flat floorboards using a roller or airless sprayer.
- [ ] **Mandatory Back-Brushing:** Immediately follow the sprayer/roller with a wide stain brush, manually pushing the liquid deep into the wood pores to prevent pooling, tracking, or surface flashing blotches.

> FLAG (C-PT02-04): the steps above apply oil-based stain only. Solid acrylic stain, which pricing sells as a premium, and any sealer have no approved step, product or quality check; they are not covered (section 2). (source: 31 section 10 PT-02)

## Stop-work triggers, escalation and incidents (service-specific)

### From "10. Stop-work conditions"

The general stop-work rule is [[FACT-SVC-STOP-WORK-RULE]]. Service-specific triggers:

Service-specific stop triggers (source: spec PT-02.SAF unless noted; PROPOSED wording pending qualified review):
- Unsuitable weather for the work or the product: postpone; record No Start / Blocked instead of starting, and stop if conditions change during work (PT-02.SAF "postpone unsuitable weather"; spec SCH-008, START-011; C-PT02-05).
  - Evidence: note
- Wood not dry enough for the approved product, or no meter reading recorded: do not apply stain (PT-02.SAF "verify wood dry enough per product"; C-PT02-08).
- Plants and adjacent surfaces cannot be protected: do not apply chemicals or spray (PT-02.SAF "protect plants/adjacent surfaces").
- Pressure not confirmed as appropriate for the wood, or anyone in the wash or spray zone: do not wash or spray (PT-02.SAF "pressure appropriate"; C-PT02-09; X-08).
- Oily rags or brush cleaners cannot be controlled as the oily-rag step requires: stop (PT-02.SAF "manage oily rags").
- Rot, loose boards, protruding fasteners or other structural defects found: stop, record (JOB-004) and route to HI-10 or HI-11 (agreement "Structural Integrity"; 04 section 5.4 rule (a)).
  - Evidence: note
- Existing coating at a property built before 1978 or of unknown age: do not strip, wash or sand it; escalate (Q-31-07; 31 section 7.2 rank 2).
- No reviewed SDS or no selected PPE for a chemical step: do not start that step (C-PT02-02; 29 section 8.3).

Service holds (inserted in section 12). Critical holds cannot be overridden (31 section 8.3); a hold that exists because a required module or policy is missing (pressure washing, chemicals and SDS, weather, old-property screening) behaves as a critical hold until that module is approved (31 section 8.4 rule 4, PROPOSED):
