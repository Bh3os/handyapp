## 1. Site preparation and property protection

### From "5. Customer and property preparation"

- The agreement requires that "All primary trades (painters, floor installers, plumbers) must be completely finished with their work in target zones before the Company begins detailed polishing" and that the client keep the site safe, with active power and running water; verify both on arrival and record any difference (source: agreement "Construction Complete" and "On-Site Safety"; spec START-007). The site-safety clause is under attorney review (see the agreement review notes).
  - Evidence: note
- Missing preparation items (GAP): area release per area, access and keys, HVAC status, paint cure dates, labels that must stay, debris or dumpster location, parking, general-contractor walkthrough date, property age (source: 04 CL-04 customer preparation requirements). A PROPOSED customer checklist is sent with the appointment reminder once the owner and attorney approve the wording (source: spec NTF-004, NTF-005).
- Occupied or partly occupied property: occupants, children and pets are kept out of the active work area; the occupant and pet module is pending qualified safety review (source: spec CL-04.INT "occupancy readiness"; APXA-002; X-11; 31 section 5.8).
- A customer's or contractor's statement or signature never clears a hold (source: owner facts 2026-10-02 "acknowledgment cannot bypass critical holds"; risk-triage playbook L27).

> HOLD (C-CL04-04) - before "HEPA Vacuuming" below and every later step in an area: the area is released for cleaning by all trades, and no unsafe active construction is under way in it; power and water are verified safe before any corded tool or wet step; sharps are managed by the reviewer's method before debris is gathered or blades are used. If an area is not released, construction is still active, or power or water is unsafe, do not start: record No Start / Blocked with evidence and escalate (spec SCH-008). (source: spec CL-04.SAF "confirm area released for cleaning; do not work amid unsafe active construction; respiratory/dust control; sharps management; verify power/water safety"; CL-04.HAZ; START-011; 31 section 10)

> HOLD (X-13) - before "HEPA Vacuuming" below and every later step that vacuums, sweeps, wipes or bags renovation dust or debris: in a pre-1978 or unknown-age home where the renovation disturbed painted surfaces or older materials, or wherever debris is suspected to contain asbestos, do not disturb the dust or debris; stop and escalate. HEPA capture does not clear suspect material. Release only by the qualified reviewer named in section 10. Pending review by an EPA RRP-certified renovator and an accredited asbestos inspector. (source: spec START-011 "suspect hazardous materials"; 04 CL-04 hazards; risk-triage playbook "A pre-1978 painted surface is not cleared by a customer saying it looks safe."; owner facts 2026-10-02 "Unknown is not safe"; 29 section 8.1)

> HOLD (C-CL04-03) - DUST-CONTROL METHOD PENDING SAFETY REVIEW, before "HEPA Vacuuming" below and every step of this "Dry Dust Pull" phase that vacuums, sweeps or gathers dust: the dust-control method (HEPA capture, wet methods or dry sweeping) and respiratory protection must be decided by a qualified safety professional; none is selected here. The inventory's "Mandatory" respirator statement stays and is not weakened. HEPA vacuuming and the dust-binding rinse are engineering controls already in the steps; they do not replace the reviewer's decision and do not clear suspect lead or asbestos. Until the decision exists these steps stay on hold (31 section 8.4). Pending qualified review. (source: spec CL-04.SAF "respiratory/dust control"; CL-04.TLS "PPE/respiratory protection as plan requires"; inventory "Mandatory to protect workers' lungs from airborne silica dust particles."; 31 section 10)

> HOLD (C-CL04-01, X-12) - HEIGHT WORK - PENDING SAFETY REVIEW, before "HEPA Vacuuming" below (ceilings), before "Fixtures & Hardware" in Phase 2 (light bulbs), and before any work "Above 12 ft" that the pricing file prices: access only with equipment and methods approved under a height and access policy, which does not exist yet (02 G-005); the inventory lists no ladder or scaffold. Until the policy exists, the steps or parts of steps that need a ladder, platform or scaffold stay on hold (31 section 8.4). Pending qualified review. - [ ] **Bulk Debris:** Sweep and gather any remaining construction debris, drywall pieces, wood shavings, and sawdust.

> FLAG (C-CL04-03): DUST-CONTROL METHOD PENDING SAFETY REVIEW. The step above sweeps dry, without the "Fine Dust Sweeping Compound" that the inventory stocks to be "sprinkled on floors before sweeping to lock fine dust down". Whether dry sweeping is acceptable or HEPA or wet methods are required is the qualified safety professional's decision (02 C-CL04-03). The gathered debris may include sharp pieces (sharps hold above), and its destination is undefined (section 15). The step text is unchanged. (source: spec CL-04.HAZ "fine dust, silica/wood dust, sharp debris"; inventory file; 31 section 10)
