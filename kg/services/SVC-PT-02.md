### Draft plan copy (`Main Plans/Painting Business.md`; not approved, claims unverified)

*Target Headline for Website: Restore and Protect Your Backyard Oasis—Professional Wood Sealing*

The harsh weather patterns of the Kentuckiana region can quickly turn your beautiful wooden deck or privacy fence into a gray, weathered, and splintered hazard. Unprotected exterior wood absorbs rainwater, leading to structural rot, warping, and costly structural failures. Our specialized staining and sealing service deep-cleans weather-beaten lumber and seals the pores with premium, UV-resistant oils, restoring the rich natural wood grain while extending its lifespan for years to come.

* **Chemical Wood Stripping:** We apply specialized, eco-safe wood conditioners and brighteners to break down failed older stains, ground-in dirt, and dark mold spores.
* **Hydraulic Pressure Cleansing:** We use calibrated, low-pressure power washing to deeply flush away gray cellulose wood cells and dirt without gouging or tearing the delicate soft grain of the wood.
* **Surface De-Splinter Sanding:** Once fully dry, we lightly sand heavy handrail areas and footpaths to eliminate painful splinters and ensure a comfortable, smooth-to-the-touch surface.
* **Industrial Oil Saturation:** We spray and brush deep-penetrating transparent, semi-transparent, or solid oil-based stains deep into the dry wood fibers, providing a heavy moisture barrier and advanced UV sun protection.

## Included work (SOP section 2, "In scope")

As written in the original procedure (section 12), pending review:
- Phase 1: masking of adjacent siding, brick and low window glass, and pre-wetting of plants (source: original Phase 1).
- Phase 2: sodium hydroxide stripper application, dwell and low-pressure washing of weathered wood (source: original Phase 2).
- Phase 3: oxalic acid brightening, rinse, drying and a moisture check before staining (source: original Phase 3).
- Phase 4: oil-based stain on deck floorboards, edges, vertical posts and railings by brush, roller or airless sprayer, with back-brushing (source: original Phase 4).
- Phase 5: oily-rag control, drying signs and keep-off instructions to the homeowner (source: original Phase 5).
- Surfaces: structurally sound exterior wood, as the agreement's "Structural Integrity" clause assumes; composite decking and painted surfaces are not addressed by any step (source: agreement "Structural Integrity"; 04 PT-02 "exclusions", GAP).
