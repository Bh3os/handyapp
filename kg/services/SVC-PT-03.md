### Draft plan copy (`Main Plans/Painting Business.md`; not approved, claims unverified)

*Target Headline for Website: Classic European Aesthetics—Breathable Protection for Your Brick Home*

Painting raw exterior brick requires specialized masonry knowledge. Standard exterior paints trap moisture inside the brick's porous core, causing the paint to blister, peel, and eventually crack the masonry during winter freeze-and-thaw cycles. Our specialized brick coatings division offers authentic European Limewash and advanced elastomeric formulas. These architectural coatings chemically fuse with your brick, transforming your home's curb appeal with a luxury finish while allowing the masonry to breathe naturally.

* **Industrial Masonry Wash:** We power wash the entire brick exterior with specialized stone cleansers to remove caked soot, salt efflorescence, and organic mildew layers.
* **Elite Architectural Masking:** We meticulously tape off, seal, and wrap all windows, doors, gutters, and adjacent landscapes in protective films to completely isolate the spray zone.
* **Authentic Limewash Application:** For a rustic look, we apply Class-A old-world European Limewash made of slaked lime and natural pigments, which can be artistically distressed to show hints of the underlying brick.
* **Elastomeric Waterproof Shielding:** For solid modern upgrades, we spray thick elastomeric masonry coatings that bridge hairline cracks, flexing with your home's structural movement while preventing wind-driven rain penetration.

## Included work (SOP section 2, "In scope")

As written in the original procedure (section 12), pending review:
- Phase 1 masonry cleansing: efflorescence scraping, a mildew wash and a pressure wash of the brick envelope (source: original Phase 1; pricing Option A "Includes masonry wash"; agreement payment milestone "completion of full masonry wash"). All three steps carry holds in section 12.
- Phase 2 spray masking of windows, exterior light fixtures, entry doors, utility meters, the roofline, soffits, fascia and gutters (source: original Phase 2). The roofline and soffit steps are held until an access plan is approved (C-PT03-04).
- Option A, limewash: mixing the slaked-lime slurry, pre-wetting the brick, brush application and distressing (source: original Phase 3; pricing and agreement Option A).
- Option B, elastomeric coating: airless spray application with back-rolling by a second painter (source: original Phase 4; pricing and agreement Option B).
- Phase 5 de-masking, glass overspray removal and a ground check before the final walkthrough (source: original Phase 5; agreement payment milestone "de-masking, glass scraping inspection, and clean site handover").
