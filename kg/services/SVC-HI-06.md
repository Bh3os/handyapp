### Draft plan copy (`Main Plans/Home Improvement Business.md`; not approved, claims unverified)

*Target Headline for Website: Structural Water Diversion—Premium Seamless Gutter Guards and Cleaning*

Clogged gutters do far more damage than just creating overflow waterfalls. When rainwater spills over your rooflines, it pools right at your foundation, causing severe basement leaks, drywall mold, and devastating structural shifting. Our complete gutter maintenance service flushes your drainage pathways clear, re-aligns sagging spikes, and installs premium gutter guards to shield your home from falling leaves and twigs permanently.

- [ ] **Debris Manual Flushing:** We clear out caked dirt and compacted tree leaves down to bare aluminum, flushing downspouts out with high-volume water lines.
- [ ] **Pitch Realignment Tuning:** We measure gutter slopes and drive reinforced structural timber screws to pull sagging gutters flush against the fascia boards.
- [ ] **Seam Miter Sealing:** We dry out mitered corners and joint brackets, scraping off dry caulking and injecting heavy-duty waterproof gutter sealants.
- [ ] **Gutter Guard Mounting:** We secure heavy-gauge stainless steel micro-mesh leaf screens or aluminum guards firmly across the gutter line to lock out future debris.
