Purpose (SOP section 1):

Install closet and shelving track systems, either wire track systems secured into drywall hollows with toggle anchors (Option A) or tracks and standards screwed into wood framing studs (Option B), so that the installed components are level, fastened per the system and anchor manufacturer's instructions and installed within the manufacturer's rated load and spacing (source: spec Appendix B HI-08; spec HI-08.SAF, HI-08.QAC; original Option A and B headings in section 12). This draft authorizes no work and promises no result to the customer (source: owner facts 2026-10-02; 31 section 3.5 rule 5).

*Target Headline for Website: Reclaim Your Spaces—Custom-Fit Closet Systems & Heavy-Duty Shelving*

Cluttered walk-in closets, chaotic pantries, and stacked garage floors are a direct result of inadequate vertical storage layout designs. Our custom shelving installation transforms unorganized wall spaces into highly functional storage centers. We secure high-quality wire shelving arrays or modern wooden modular closet systems, allowing you to maximize every square inch of available wall space cleanly.

- [ ] **Wall Layout Blueprinting:** We measure your interior closet spaces and sketch an optimized storage blueprint detailing shelving tiers, hanging rods, and basket lines.
- [ ] **Anchor Track Levelling:** We install heavy-gauge steel top tracks along the drywall, using commercial self-leveling lasers to verify absolute horizontal straightness.
- [ ] **Stud Locking Fasteners:** We drive heavy anchoring toggle bolts or structural screws directly into wood studs to guarantee the tracking frame can support heavy bulk loads.
- [ ] **Component Modular Hanging:** We fit the vertical standards, hook adjustable wire brackets or wooden shelves into position, and test structural weight tolerances.

### SOP section 2, "Do not perform"

1. Electrical, plumbing, gas or HVAC work outside the approved scope or requiring a licensed trade. Reason: licensed trade. Instead: stop and escalate (source: spec START-011 "unapproved electrical/plumbing/gas/structural scope"; master section 1.3).
2. Structural work, meaning cutting, removing, sistering or adding load-bearing members, ledgers, footings or framing, unless it is in the approved scope with the required qualified review. Reason: structural. Instead: stop and escalate (source: spec START-011; master section 1.3).
3. Disturbing suspect asbestos, lead paint or mold, including older painted or textured wall surfaces in the drilling path (source: 04 HI-08 hazards; owner facts 2026-10-02 "Unknown is not safe"). Reason: hazardous material. Suspected asbestos / lead paint / mold: stop and escalate (source: spec START-011).
4. Work that is not in the approved scope or an approved change order, including designing or changing a layout on site. Reason: out of scope. Instead: record the customer's request for a change order (source: spec JOB-005, JOB-010, FIN-008, AT-033).
5. Working at height or using access equipment beyond the approved height and access policy. No such policy exists yet (02 G-005), so work that needs a ladder or step stool is held until it does (31 section 8.4 rule 4, PROPOSED). Reason: policy. Instead: stop and escalate (source: HI-06.SAF "beyond approved height/access policy"; DW-04.TLS "scaffolds/stilts only under approved policy"; spec HI-08.HAZ "ladder").
6. Mixing or sequencing chemicals in any way the approved step and the product SDS do not allow. Reason: hazardous material. Instead: stop and escalate (source: CL-01.SAF "never mix incompatible chemicals"; X-09).
7. Recording, photographing or sending customer passwords, master codes or site access codes outside the app's secured fields. Reason: policy (privacy). Instead: use the app's secured fields only (source: spec COM-002; owner facts 2026-10-02; 27 Roles and permissions AUTH-009).
8. Promising results, warranties, safety, health or code compliance to the customer, including any load capacity or load "certification". Reason: policy. Instead: refer questions to the owner and hand over only the manufacturer's rated limits as care notes (section 17) (source: master sections 1.4, 17 and 29; C-HI08-01).
9. Overloading the wall or substrate: no installation outside, and no load or fit check beyond, the system and anchor manufacturer's rated load and spacing. Reason: safety. Instead: stop and escalate (source: spec HI-08.SAF "do not overload wall/substrate"; HI-08.QAC "basic load/fit check").
10. Moving, removing or rewiring closet lights or outlets that are in the way. Reason: licensed trade. Instead: stop and escalate; any referral to a licensed electrician is an owner decision (source: 31 section 10 HI-08; spec START-011).
