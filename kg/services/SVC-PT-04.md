### Draft plan copy (`Main Plans/Painting Business.md`; not approved, claims unverified)

*Target Headline for Website: Say Goodbye to Dated Wallpaper—Restore Your Walls to Fresh Canvas*

Stripping away decades-old, stubborn wallpaper can quickly turn into a home renovation nightmare. Tearing the paper dry will rip away the underlying drywall's structural paper face, causing deep gouges that require extensive, expensive drywall texturing to repair. Our professional wallpaper removal service takes a patient, damage-free approach. We chemically dissolve old adhesives and restore the raw drywall beneath back to a smooth finish, giving you a perfect canvas for modern paint.

* **Enzymatic Slurry Softening:** We lightly score the vinyl face of the wallpaper and apply professional warm-steam or enzyme-based adhesive-dissolving solution to liquefy the vintage paste hidden behind.
* **Damage-Free Peeling:** We use wide, flexible, rounded scrapers to gently peel away the vinyl sheets and backing fibers without piercing or scratching the fragile drywall core below.
* **Adhesive Scraping & Cleanse:** We scrub and flush the bare walls to completely eradicate residual sticky glue paste, preventing future paint bubbling or peeling flaws.
* **Level 4 Skim Coat Restoration:** We apply thin coats of lightweight compound (معجون) over old wall seams or imperfections, sanding the surface to a flawless, paint-ready Level 4 finish.

### SOP section 2, "Out of scope"

- A "flawless, paint-ready Level 4 finish" over whole walls, as the main plan describes: Not covered - pending review (C-PT04-06). This SOP skims "rough spots, indents, and seams" only; the deliverable and its acceptance criteria are an owner decision (X-26).
- Drywall reconstruction after hidden mold (agreement "Inclusions" clause, "paid change order"): Not covered - pending review (C-PT04-02). Suspected mold stops the work (section 10); assessment, remediation and any reconstruction need a qualified mold professional and an owner decision on referral.

The threshold between minor and wide tearing is undefined; any added repair goes through a change order that the customer approves before the work (spec FIN-008, JOB-005).

No other product or method is added here; another method needs its product instructions and review.
- Border removal along the ceiling (pricing "Border-Only Stripping Surcharge") and any other work beyond floor-standing reach: ladder work only under an approved height and access policy, which does not exist yet (X-12; 02 G-005).
- Plaster walls, ceiling paper, wallpaper hanging, fixture removal, furniture moving and texture matching: no step covers them; the owner decides which become customer-visible exclusions (source: 04 PT-04 "exclusions", GAP).
