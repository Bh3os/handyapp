### Draft plan copy (`Main Plans/Home Improvement Business.md`; not approved, claims unverified)

*Target Headline for Website: Secure Your Property Bounds—Expert Fence Post Stabilization and Panel Repairs*

Heavy storms and humid soil transitions across the Kentuckiana region eventually compromise the structural integrity of your privacy or picket fences. Leaning posts, broken horizontal rails, and detached rotted wood panels look unpolished and present immediate safety hazards for pets and children. Our targeted fence repair service focuses on structural stabilization—avoiding the massive cost of a full replacement by reinforcing existing structures to like-new strength.

- [ ] **Post Structural Stabilization:** We dig around leaning, unstable posts and install heavy-gauge steel reinforcement brackets or pour fast-setting concrete collars below the frost line.
- [ ] **Horizontal Rail Structural Re-tie:** We cut out decayed or split horizontal structural wood 2x4 rails, replacing them with thick pressure-treated lumbers secured with exterior structural framing screws.
- [ ] **Picket Replacement Matching:** We remove individual cracked or warped privacy pickets, installing matching wood species (Cedar or Pressure-Treated) aligned perfectly with the existing fence line.
- [ ] **Gate Hinge Calibration:** We re-square sagging fence gates, swapping out worn-out hardware for heavy-duty galvanized anti-sag hinges and self-latching drop rods.
