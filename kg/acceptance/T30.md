**Scenario:** staff publish two questions; the customer answers one with a photo and "I'm not sure" for a measurement; later the request is closed as no response and the customer replies within the reopen period; another request is marked Not a Fit.

**Expected:** the same project is updated; the original submission and staff decisions stay; the other question stays open; the reply reopens the same request without restoring an expired estimate; Not a Fit keeps the history visible.
