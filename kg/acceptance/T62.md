**Scenario:** a worker with the collect permission records cash on site; a worker without it tries; a check is recorded, deposited and later cleared by the Owner.

**Expected:** the cash record holds collector, time, amount, invoice and receipt number and the receipt sends at once; the worker without permission is refused; the check shows pending until marked cleared, and the collector cannot mark their own collection cleared.
