**Scenario:** a device resends the same events several times on reconnect; one event conflicts with a newer server scope change; a revoked worker's device syncs.

**Expected:** each client UUID is ingested once; the conflict goes to review, never last-write-wins; the revoked worker's new writes are refused and their evidence quarantined for Owner review.
