**Scenario:** a satisfaction draft and a review request are prepared for a project with an open case; another customer opts out of marketing texts after a review request was queued; a third gave a low satisfaction answer.

**Expected:** the first two are held until the case is resolved; the opt-out blocks the queued send at execution; the low score does not change review eligibility; repeat-service reminders go only on the customer-agreed schedule and stop when the customer stops them.
