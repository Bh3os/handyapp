**Scenario:** a customer session stays idle 30 minutes and another stays open 8 hours; a customer logs out, recovers access, changes their verified email, and the Owner deletes their login account.

**Expected:** the idle and the 8-hour sessions end ([[FACT-SESSION-TIMINGS]]); sessions are revoked or renewed as designed; the old contact keeps no access; account deletion revokes sign-in, sessions and project links while protected records stay.
