**Scenario:** a worker reports a suspected gas leak during work; another reports a non-critical issue they can fix within their training and SOP.

**Expected:** the gas report creates a critical hold in the same transaction under the job lock, shows the evacuate and call guidance, offers no resolve-on-site option, raises a top-priority Owner item, and the customer sees only "Work is paused for safety review."; the minor issue records steps, materials and the check, and notifies the Owner.
