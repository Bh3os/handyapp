**Scenario:** staff write an estimate whose lines total a small amount (for example one line of $35.00) and publish it; the customer approves; an invoice is drafted. Another estimate has a $0.00 line, a negative line and a military discount.

**Expected:**
- Estimate, approval record, job snapshot and invoice all show exactly the calculated total.
- No minimum line, floor, disclosure, validation or top-up appears in the API, documents, seeds or calculators.
- The $0.00 and negative lines are refused at publishing; the discount shows as a separate totals row before the total, computed half-up in cents, and the deposit base is the total after it ([[OWN-DEC-145]]).
