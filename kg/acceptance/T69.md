**Scenario:** automated Playwright and axe runs over every customer and staff workflow (including the scheduling board's non-drag alternatives) at 320 px, 200% and 400% text, light and dark themes.

**Expected:** every control is reachable and operable by keyboard with visible focus; dialogs trap and return focus; errors are linked and announced via live regions; no horizontal scroll; recorded results. Screen-reader and physical-device checks are recorded as unverified; no full-conformance claim.

**Limitation (process requirement; reviewer: a person testing with assistive technology):** As the scenario expects, screen-reader and physical-device checks are recorded as UNVERIFIED (`docs/accessibility-report.md`); the automated runs cover Chromium only.
