**Scenario:** a guest with no account completes the request form with valid services, address, timing and contact details and presses "Send Estimate Request".

**Expected:**
- Exactly one durable request and one project record exist (the project is not a job or appointment).
- One new-request item appears in the staff action queue with the first-response clock running.
- The receipt page shows "Your request is in." with the real reference and the project at a glance, only after the request is saved.
- Exactly one English receipt email is sent automatically; no booking wording anywhere.
