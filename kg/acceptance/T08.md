**Scenario:** county page drafts exist, some without approved coverage and authentic local content.

**Expected:** those are absent from navigation and the sitemap and return not found; a published county page has factual, useful local detail only.

**Limitation (owner input; reviewer: Owner):** No county has approved local content, so the "published county page" half of the scenario cannot happen yet; only the domain rule that would publish one is tested ([[HH-CUS-04]]).
