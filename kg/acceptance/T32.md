**Scenario:** an estimate is published, edited (creating version 2) and published again; the customer tries to approve version 1 and, later, an expired version.

**Expected:** version 1 is kept as superseded; edits never change a published version; both approvals are refused with the current version shown or "Request an Updated Estimate"; the version history shows creator, reviser and publisher.
