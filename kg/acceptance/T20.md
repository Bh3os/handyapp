**Scenario:** a guest with a verified project creates an optional account with a one-time code.

**Expected:** only the verified, authorized project attaches; its job, estimate and invoice keep the same IDs; nothing is duplicated; other projects with the same email stay unlinked until verified. Organization accounts (P2) follow the same explicit-membership rule.
