**Scenario:** two staff confirm overlapping visits needing the same worker at the same moment; two customers select the same offered window; a held window is offered to another customer; an offer is left unselected for 24 hours.

**Expected:** one success and one conflict each time, enforced in the database; the loser is offered alternatives; nothing partial is written; a held window is not offered to anyone else; selecting a window confirms it and releases the other offered windows; unselected holds release after 24 hours ([[OWN-DEC-144]]).
