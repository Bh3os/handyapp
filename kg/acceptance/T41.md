**Scenario:** visits booked at a local time inside the spring-forward gap and inside the repeated fall-back hour, and a visit moved across a DST change.

**Expected:** the gap time is refused with a clear message; the repeated hour is stored with its offset; customer and worker see the chosen local window with its zone; UTC instants are correct.
