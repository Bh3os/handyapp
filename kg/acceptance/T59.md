**Scenario:** a deposit, a partial cash payment, an uncleared check and an overpayment are recorded on one invoice, two of them concurrently.

**Expected:** confirmed paid, pending and remaining balance show separately; the deposit is credited once; allocations never exceed the invoice; the overpayment becomes an unallocated credit for an Owner decision; Paid appears only when confirmed payments and credits reach $0.00, then the receipt sends once.
