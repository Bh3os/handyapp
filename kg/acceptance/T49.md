**Scenario:** a worker downloads packets for today's jobs and goes offline; the device storage is inspected.

**Expected:** "Ready for offline use" appears only after the content is verified on the device; version and last sync show; no access codes, finance exports, unneeded customer history or restricted incidents are stored; data for ended assignments is purged.
