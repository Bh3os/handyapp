**Scenario:** two workers on one job check off steps, take photos and tap Arrived and Finished; one forgets Finished; the Owner corrects a timestamp; a mandatory step is skipped with a reason.

**Expected:** each record carries its worker and work package; the missing timestamp is flagged, not invented; the correction keeps the original and the reason; the skipped mandatory step blocks completion and is visible to the reviewer; checklist progress resumes after reopening the app.
