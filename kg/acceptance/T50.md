**Scenario:** offline, a worker records notes, photos, Arrived, a stop and checklist steps, then tries Start Work, resuming after a hold, approving a change order, recording a payment and submitting for quality review.

**Expected:** drafts show "Saved on this phone" and "Not yet synchronized"; the stop shows at once; every listed critical action is refused until online; the sequence order is enforced offline.
