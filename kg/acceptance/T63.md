**Scenario:** a cleared deposit check is returned; a card payment is disputed; the Owner issues a credit and records a Square refund; a wrong payment amount is corrected.

**Expected:** no payment is edited or deleted; adjustments and reversing allocations are appended; the balance and deposit status update; the receipt shows voided; reminders pause for the dispute; each change carries reason, actor and link to the original.
