**Scenario:** uploads of a 15 MB JPEG (accepted), a JPEG over 15 MiB, a PDF, a file whose content does not match its extension, and an image containing a malicious payload; an 11th photo.

**Expected:** each is rejected or quarantined with a clear message; none can be fetched as approved evidence; the limits of [[FACT-UPLOAD-LIMITS]] (JPEG, PNG, WebP; 15 MiB per file; 10 photos per request) are enforced on the server ([[OWN-DEC-146]]).
