**Scenario:** a Worker, Office user and Estimator each try a refund, write-off, deposit waiver, job reopen and bulk export; the Owner tries each without and with a fresh sign-in and a reason.

**Expected:** every non-Owner attempt is refused; the Owner's attempts succeed only with fresh sign-in and a reason, and each is audited.
