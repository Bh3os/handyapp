**Scenario:** the Owner issues an invoice while a minor punch item is open; the customer then reports a complaint from a message; a return visit is scheduled; staff try to close the job.

**Expected:** the punch item and the case stay visible after the invoice; a second complaint in the same category is offered "add to existing case"; the return visit is linked with its own scope and cost, and the original completion evidence is unchanged; closing is refused while the punch item or case is open.
