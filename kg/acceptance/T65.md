**Scenario:** an estimate is published while the email provider is down; reminder drafts exist for an estimate later accepted, a visit later rescheduled and an invoice later paid; a question reminder and an estimate reminder are released at 11 PM.

**Expected:** the business change commits and its outbox event is retried until sent once; each stale reminder is suppressed at release or send; the 11 PM question reminder queues until opening hours while the estimate reminder sends at once ([[OWN-DEC-132]]); cadences match [[FACT-REMINDER-CADENCES]]; a failed delivery is never counted as customer non-response.
