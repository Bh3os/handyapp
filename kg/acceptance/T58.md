**Scenario:** a job with an approved estimate, one approved and one rejected change order and a paid deposit passes the Owner's quality review; staff issue the invoice.

**Expected:** lines equal the approved estimate plus the approved change only; the deposit appears once as a credit; no tax line; issuer "Handyhub LLC" with no address line; payment term and instructions shown (card named as Square, no surcharge, placeholders labelled); English; on staging marked "TEST — not a real invoice or estimate"; the invoice-ready notice sends once with no amounts; issuing before the review is refused.
