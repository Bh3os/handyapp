**Scenario:** a job with an approved estimate, one approved and one rejected change order and a paid deposit passes the Owner's quality review; staff issue the invoice.

**Expected:** lines equal the approved estimate plus the approved change only; the deposit appears once as a credit; no tax line; issuer "Handyhub LLC" with no address line; business phone and email labelled placeholders; any discount as a totals row; the 90-day workmanship warranty; payment term and instructions shown (card named as Square, no surcharge, placeholders labelled); English; on staging marked "TEST — not a real invoice or estimate"; the invoice-ready notice sends once with no amounts; issuing before the review is refused.

**Limitation (owner input; reviewer: Owner):** The invoice shows labelled placeholders for the public phone and email and for the Zelle, Cash App, Chime and check details, and leaves out the mailing address, until the owner supplies them ([[OQ-PAYMENT-HANDLES]], [[FACT-PUBLIC-CONTACT]]); "Handyhub LLC" is unconfirmed against the registered entity ([[OQ-LEGAL-ENTITY-NAME]]).
