**Scenario:** a customer submits with missing and invalid fields; an attacker posts altered values (extra services, bad ZIP, oversized text) straight to the API.

**Expected:** field-specific accessible errors with focus to the summary and every entered value and upload kept; the server independently rejects the manipulated payload with field errors and creates nothing.
