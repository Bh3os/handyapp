**Scenario:** a project moves through estimate ready, approved with deposit due, deposit recorded, visit confirmed, on the way, work complete and invoice issued.

**Expected:** at each step the English Overview shows the plain-language status and one next action (for example "Estimate approved—pay your deposit to proceed with scheduling."), the public timeline, the current appointment and a payment summary, all from the same records staff see.
