**Scenario:** an isolated restore from backup after deletions and suppressions were executed.

**Expected:** customers, projects, versions, media and payment relationships match; the deletions and suppressions recorded after the backup are re-applied before a controlled resume; integrity results and missing data are recorded.

**Limitation (process requirement; reviewer: Owner):** The restore rehearsal runs on a local PostgreSQL with `pg_dump` and `pg_restore`; restoring the production backups is a production-only check that has not been run ([[OQ-GO-DECISION]]).
