**Scenario:** an Estimator writes an estimate with work packages, quantities, options and exclusions; another uploads a PDF with structured amounts; a Word upload; one publish attempt without the agree-confirmation.

**Expected:** server totals are half-up per line and equal the sum of displayed lines; no tax line, no minimum, no surcharge; the deposit appears only when the rule applies; validity is the decided period; the unconfirmed upload is refused; the Word original stays private with a PDF for the customer; no rate, tax or deposit value appears that the owner did not decide.
