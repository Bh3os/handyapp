**Scenario:** the Owner disables a worker who has an open browser session, an offline device with pending items and upcoming assignments.

**Expected:** the next server call from that session fails; sessions are revoked; offline sync of new writes is refused and pending evidence quarantined for Owner review; upcoming assignments are flagged for reassignment; job history stays.
