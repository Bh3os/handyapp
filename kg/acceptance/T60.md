**Scenario:** a customer returns from the Square link "success" page and presses an "I paid" action; staff paste a non-Square link; staff record a Square payment, then record the same reference again.

**Expected:** nothing is marked paid by the redirect or the claim (the claim creates a staff verification item); the non-Square or non-https link is refused; the first recording is accepted and the duplicate reference refused. There is no callback endpoint to attack.

**Limitation (owner decision; reviewer: Owner):** There is no customer "I paid" action ([[OQ-PAYMENT-LINK-DETAILS]], PROPOSED). A customer's claim arrives as a project message: it opens the conversation's "customer replied" staff item and changes no payment or balance (tested); no dedicated payment-verification item exists.
