**Scenario:** a customer picks Mornings and Weekends, then picks Any time; another picks "I'm flexible" and "Need help today?".

**Expected:** the five choices are offered; Any time clears the others; Weekends aligns with the date preference; the form shows "These are your preferences. Your appointment is confirmed only after we send a date and arrival window."; nothing creates a visit or confirmation; "Need help today?" is a request for staff review and no same-day charge appears ([[OWN-DEC-140]]).
