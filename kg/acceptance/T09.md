**Scenario:** a customer picks Mornings and Weekends, then picks Any time; the day windows show their hours ([[FACT-WINDOW-HOURS]]); another picks "I'm flexible" and "Need help today?".

**Expected:** the five choices are offered; Any time clears the others; Weekends aligns with the date preference; the form shows, below the choices, "These are your preferred times only. Your appointment is not confirmed until HandyHub sends you a confirmed date and arrival window." ([[OWN-DEC-170]]); nothing creates a visit or confirmation; "Need help today?" is a request for staff review and no same-day charge appears ([[OWN-DEC-140]]).
