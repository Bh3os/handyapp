**Scenario:** estimates approved at exactly $1,000.00, at $1,000.01, and over the threshold only because of a selected option; deposits paid by a cleared card payment, an uncleared check, and waived by the Owner; an Office user and another administrator try to waive.

**Expected:**
- no deposit at $1,000.00; a deposit of 20% of the final approved total including options, half-up to the cent, above it (values from [[FACT-DEPOSIT-RULE]]);
- work-visit confirmation is blocked until the deposit is cleared or waived; estimate visits are never blocked; an uncleared check does not satisfy it;
- only the Owner can waive, with a fresh sign-in and a reason; the waiver does not mark the deposit paid or change the price; others are refused.
