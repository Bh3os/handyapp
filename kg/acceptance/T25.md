**Scenario:** a customer presses "Request to Reschedule" on a confirmed visit; staff later decline one request and confirm another. Two customers with paid deposits request cancellation, one 48 hours and one 12 hours before the visit; HandyHub moves a third customer's visit for weather.

**Expected:** "Request received—awaiting staff confirmation."; the visit stays confirmed and visible until staff commit; a declined request leaves it intact; the confirmed change keeps history and notifies the customer automatically once; rescheduling is free; staff see the outcome of [[FACT-CANCELLATION-RULES]]: a full deposit refund at 48 hours, $50.00 kept and the rest refunded at 12 hours, and a choice of free reschedule or full refund for the weather move; only the Owner records each refund, nothing is refunded automatically ([[OWN-DEC-134]]).
