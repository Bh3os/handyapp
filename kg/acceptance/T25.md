**Scenario:** a customer presses "Request to Reschedule" on a confirmed visit; staff later decline one request and confirm another.

**Expected:** "Request received—awaiting staff confirmation."; the visit stays confirmed and visible until staff commit; a declined request leaves it intact; the confirmed change keeps history and notifies the customer automatically once.
