**Scenario:** staff share two after-photos with the customer; another user and an anonymous client request them; staff try to mark one for marketing; care instructions exist as an unapproved draft and as an approved version with one product line missing guidance.

**Expected:** shared photos are reachable only by the authorized customer through short-lived links; internal photos are inaccessible by URL or ID; marketing use needs a recorded publication permission; the customer sees only the approved care version, without the line lacking product guidance, which is flagged to staff.
