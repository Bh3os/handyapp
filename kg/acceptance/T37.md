**Scenario:** a site-estimate visit is booked for a project, the estimate is approved, and a work visit is booked for the job; the customer's original timing preference is on the request.

**Expected:** the estimate visit is "Estimate visit" linked to the project with no job or price approval; the work visit links to the job with its scope; neither is confused with the timing preference.
