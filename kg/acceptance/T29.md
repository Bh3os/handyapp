**Scenario:** office staff merge two duplicate customers after the preview, then correct a request linked to the wrong customer; the Owner reverses a link.

**Expected:** original IDs, history, approvals and payments are kept; access is rechecked and the mistaken link's access is revoked; the reversal keeps later legitimate activity; each step is audited with a reason.
