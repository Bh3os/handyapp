**Scenario:** a guest submits a request using an email that belongs to an existing customer with earlier projects and an account.

**Expected:** the receipt, verification screens and emails reveal no earlier projects or account status; staff see a duplicate suggestion only, labelled as a suggestion; nothing links until staff link verified matching records.
