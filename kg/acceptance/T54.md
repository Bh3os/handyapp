**Scenario:** a worker reports an injury incident with photos; another worker, Office and the customer try to read it; the reporter tries to release the hold; the Owner resolves it with evidence.

**Expected:** only authorized roles see details; the customer sees a neutral acknowledgment; the Owner is notified; the reporter cannot release their own hold; resuming needs the resolution, a refreshed packet, personal review and the gate passing again; no fault or compensation wording appears.
