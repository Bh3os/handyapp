**Scenario:** the Owner Dashboard runs over a synthetic dataset with known answers, including revisions, deposits, pending checks, refunds, missing costs and requests answered inside and outside business hours.

**Expected:** each figure matches the known answer; revisions count once; deposits once; invoiced is distinct from received; missing data shows "unavailable"; response-time math counts only business hours; the previous-period comparison and drill-down lists match; CSV totals equal the dashboard.
