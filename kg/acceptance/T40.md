**Scenario:** a job carries a job-level Hold with a note about an unresolved permit or licensing requirement; another job's scope includes licensed trade work routed to a hold.

**Expected:** neither can be confirmed or started by any role until the Hold is cleared with a note or the critical hold is resolved; a non-blocking note does not block scheduling. No license or credential module exists.
