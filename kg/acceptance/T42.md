**Scenario:** dispatch reschedules a visit and reassigns it to another worker; a second editor saves with the old version; one proposed change fails capacity.

**Expected:** customer and worker screens, capacity and history update; the customer is notified automatically once after commit; the new worker's packet refreshes and the old worker loses access and the packet; the stale save conflicts; the failed change keeps the booking intact.
