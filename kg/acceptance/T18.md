**Scenario:** a customer opens an expired link, a revoked link, enters a code after its 10 minutes, and enters wrong codes until attempts are exhausted.

**Expected:** a neutral recovery page with a safe way to request new access; no project data leaks; the expired code and the attempt limit hold ([[FACT-SESSION-TIMINGS]]).
