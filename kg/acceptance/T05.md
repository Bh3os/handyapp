**Scenario:** a visitor clicks "Request a Free Estimate" on a service page, continues as guest; another visitor starts from the header and chooses "Not sure what service I need".

**Expected:** the first form opens with that service's category open and the immutable service ID preselected; the second submits with no service forced, the description kept for staff classification.
