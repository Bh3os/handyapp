**Scenario:** a worker finishes with an after-photo missing; the walkthrough is "Customer unavailable"; the Owner's review finds a minor and a major defect.

**Expected:** the job is "Awaiting quality review", not complete; unavailable is not acceptance and the summary is shared through the secure link; punch items are created with owners and due dates; the minor one shows the customer "Follow-up work"; the major one blocks the final invoice and returns tasks for correction.
