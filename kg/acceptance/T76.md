**Scenario:** one guest submits a request leaving the SMS box unchecked; a second checks it, later replies STOP, then HELP, then START; a visitor follows the privacy-policy and SMS-terms links from the request form and the footer.

**Expected:** the box is unchecked by default and separate from the terms and marketing choices; no text goes to the first number; the second number's consent record holds the disclosure version, time and source; STOP blocks the next queued text and records the withdrawal; HELP returns the help text; START records a new opt-in; the privacy policy has the SMS section saying mobile numbers and opt-in data are never shared or sold for marketing; the SMS terms list the program, message types, frequency, "Message and data rates may apply", STOP, HELP and the support contact; both links work from the form and the footer; the same business and brand name appears on the site, the privacy policy and the SMS terms.

**Limitation (owner input; reviewer: Owner and attorney):** "HELP returns the help text" is not met: the HELP reply is NOT CONFIGURED (`sms.help_reply`, [[OQ-ATTORNEY-PRIVACY]]), so HELP is recorded and changes nothing. STOP and START are tested with synthetic inbound texts; carrier delivery waits for A2P 10DLC ([[OQ-BIRD-10DLC]]).
