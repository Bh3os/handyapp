**Scenario:** an assigned worker opens My Day and a job packet for a multi-service job.

**Expected:** every packet section is present (property, scope, SOP checklist, hazards, PPE, tools, materials, evidence, escalation and emergency, completion preview) with version, last-updated time and sources; SOP steps trace to their vault source lines and missing stages say "No SOP step recorded for this stage — ask the office"; no access code is in the packet.
