**Value:** no estimate or visit fee. Online and onsite estimates are free ("Free estimates—online or at your property.", [[OWN-DEC-86]]).

- No visit fee is displayed or charged. Only an explicit owner change to this policy can introduce one, and any future fee is disclosed before booking ([[OWN-DEC-99]]).
