## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)

#### 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Renovation Average:** $2.00 - $3.50 / square foot of wall surface
* **Hourly Contractor Rate:** $65 - $120 / hr for specialized architectural tie-ins
* **Target Margin:** 55% - 65%

> FLAG (X-19, C-DW03-07) - "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks" block: unsourced, undated market benchmarks, not approved HandyHub rates (owner facts 2026-10-02: numeric rates, wages and profit targets not supplied). The "Target Margin" line is an internal target, unapproved, with no cost basis and no stated margin basis (gross, contribution or net) or markup basis (Q-28-13); it must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (spec FIN-007; 28 section 8). The agreement names this file as its specification, which would expose this line to the customer (C-DW03-07; see the review notes). The "Hourly Contractor Rate" line has no rule for when it applies and no person-hour basis (02 G-059). Values unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; CPA (margin basis).

#### 📐 Unified Estimation Methods

##### Method A: Square Footage of Wall/Ceiling Surface Area (Best for Open Spaces)
* **Standard Level 4 Hang & Tape:** $2.25 - $3.00 per sq. ft.
* **Moisture-Resistant Zones (Bathrooms/Greenboard):** $2.75 - $3.50 per sq. ft.

> FLAG (X-26) - Method A lines: "Standard Level 4" is the only finish level named in any DW-03 file, and no file defines it; finish-level acceptance criteria are to be defined once, from a cited source, and used by every drywall and painting service (02 X-26; spec DW-03.INT "finish level", DW-03.QAC). Method A states no board, corner-bead or sanding inclusion, and the area basis (wall face gross or net of openings, ceiling) is not stated (28 section 3.3: "Area basis must be stated per item"). Values unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; experienced drywall finisher.

##### Method B: Flat Unit Sheet Pricing (Best for Basements & Closed Additions)
* Includes hanging, taping, 3 coats of mud, corner beads, and final sanding.
* **Per 4x8 Sheet (1/2" Standard Drywall):** $110 - $140 per sheet installed.
* **Per 4x12 Sheet (Ceilings / Fewer Seams):** $165 - $200 per sheet installed.

> FLAG (C-DW03-05) - framing not priced. The agreement's "Project Specifications" clause says "Company will install framing" and the main plan sells "Custom Wood/Metal Framing", but neither method nor the add-on menu prices framing; studs and track, plate fasteners, fire-rated board, disposal and equipment rental have no line either (28 section 7.2; 02 G-059). GAP line: Framing (unit and basis TBD by owner). No number is invented (estimate-assistant playbook: "never invent an industry-standard price"). Framing that touches headers, load-bearing walls or an addition also stays behind the SOP's structural hold until a structural engineer or building official approves it (C-DW03-01). HUMAN REVIEW: owner.

> FLAG (Q-28-20) - Method A ("Best for Open Spaces") and Method B ("Best for Basements & Closed Additions") have no selection rule, and their inclusions differ or are unstated; the "Unified Estimation Methods" heading does not make them interchangeable. An estimate uses the method the owner sets for the job type, or shows the methods as separately identifiable alternates (spec EST-005; 04 DW-03 estimating method, PROPOSED). Method B's "Includes" line names corner beads (see the "Corner Bead Matrix" flag below). Values unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner.

#### ➕ Micro-Add-on Menu
- [ ] **Insulation Install (R-13/R-15 Fiberglass Batt per wall cavity):** +$0.65 / sq. ft.

> FLAG (C-DW03-04) - "Insulation Install" line: insulation is sold here and offered in the agreement ("insulation [ ]"), but no SOP step, inventory item or PPE covers it, so it is not schedulable until the owner decides whether DW-03 includes insulation and a reviewed procedure exists; it stays separate from HI-07 Attic Insulation (02 C-DW03-04). Whether the batt type named in the line fits the applicable energy code for an addition, and whether an inspection is required before cover, are questions for the local building official per Kentucky and Indiana municipality (04 DW-03 permit/license flags). Price unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; building official; qualified safety professional (fiberglass handling).

- [ ] **Corner Bead Matrix:** First 4 outside corners are free. Every additional metal/composite corner bead installed is **+$25 flat**.

> FLAG (C-DW03-12, C-DW03-V02) - "Corner Bead Matrix" line: Method B's "Includes" line lists "corner beads" without a limit, while this line makes only the first four outside corners free; included or add-on is an owner decision (Q-28-21). The bead material also differs across files: this line charges "metal/composite" beads, the SOP allows "metal or heavy composite plastic" beads, the inventory stocks vinyl archway beads, and the agreement specifies "metal corner beads" only; a substitution that affects specification or customer expectation needs approval (spec JOB-007). No price changed. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; attorney (agreement wording).

- [ ] **Old Drywall Demolition / Gutting:** +$1.00 to $2.00 / sq. ft. (includes packing out debris to dump trailers).

> FLAG (C-DW03-02) - "Old Drywall Demolition / Gutting" line: HOLD pending lead and asbestos screening. Not to be quoted or sold until a hazardous-material policy and a reviewed demolition procedure exist: no SOP step covers demolition, no build-year or suspect-material screening exists, and disposal is described only as "packing out debris to dump trailers" (02 C-DW03-02; X-13). In a pre-1978 or unknown-age home, painted surfaces, old plaster and other suspect materials are not disturbed until a qualified person records a disposition (31 sections 7.2 and 10). Suspected hazardous material is a stop-and-refer condition, not a disposal line (28 section 3.5); whether the dump trailer is owned or rented is Q-29-08. Price unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: EPA RRP-certified renovator; accredited asbestos inspector; attorney; owner.


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Method A per square foot of wall and ceiling surface ("Best for Open Spaces"), with a standard and a moisture-resistant line; Method B flat per sheet installed, by sheet size ("Best for Basements & Closed Additions"), whose "Includes" line lists hanging, taping, three coats, corner beads and final sanding; an "Hourly Contractor Rate" for specialized architectural tie-ins; add-ons per square foot (insulation, demolition) and per corner bead beyond the first four. Under the "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks" heading, a per-square-foot "Renovation Average" with no source or date (X-19). No rule selects Method A or B (Q-28-20); framing is priced nowhere (C-DW03-05) (source: 28 section 7.2 row DW-03; 04 DW-03 pricing model; headings "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks", "Unified Estimation Methods", "Micro-Add-on Menu")
- Units: Square foot of wall or ceiling surface, with the area basis (gross or net of openings) not stated; sheet by size; hour; corner bead; square foot for the insulation and demolition add-ons. No unit for framing, disposal, equipment rental, return visits for drying between coats, or travel (source: 28 sections 3.3 and 7.2)
