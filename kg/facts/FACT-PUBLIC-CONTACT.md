**Value ([[OWN-DEC-131]]):** not provided. The public phone number and the public email are both clearly labelled placeholders on the website, receipts, invoices and messages until the owner provides the real values.

- A placeholder is never a working link: no page links to a phone number or an email address, the "Call" button stays hidden and the structured data carries no telephone or email until a real value is set ([[OWN-DEC-47]]).
- The business number will be on Bird ([[OWN-DEC-129]], [[INT-Bird]]); email on [[FACT-DOMAIN]] goes through Resend ([[INT-Resend]]).
