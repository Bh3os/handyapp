## Inventory checklist as written (`Required inventory/inventory-driveway-cleaning.md`)

#### 🧴 A. Deep Stain Extractors (المنظفات ومزيلات بقع الزيوت)
- [ ] **Sodium Hypochlorite (Soft-Wash Mix 12.5%):** Commercial bleach compound to eradicate black mold spores.
- [ ] **Industrial Concrete Degreaser & Poultice Clay Powder:** For pulling deeply embedded automotive motor oil out of concrete.
- [ ] **Foaming Chemical Surfactants:** Additives to keep cleaner sprays wet on sloped concrete driveways.

#### 🧳 B. Hydraulic Washers & Rotary Surface Machinery (الماكينات ومعدات الغسيل)
- [ ] **Gas-Powered Commercial Pressure Washer:** Delivering a minimum of 3,000 PSI @ 3.0 GPM with a thermal relief valve.
- [ ] **20-inch Stainless Steel Circular Flat Surface Cleaner Deck:** Eliminates zebra striping and forces fast, uniform rows [1.1.1، 1.1.5].
- [ ] **0-Degree Oscillating Turbo Nozzle Wand Attachment:** High-velocity pinpoint blast for oil stain lifting.
- [ ] **Industrial 2-Gallon Chemical Pump Sprayer:** Fitted with Viton seals to resist corrosive bleaching chemicals.
- [ ] **Heavy-Duty 50-foot High-Pressure Hose Extensions (3/8-inch diameter):** Steel-braid non-marring hoses.

Proposed additions in the vault file (from the SOP and spec cross-check; pending owner and safety review, nothing approved for purchase or use): PPE for pressure washing and chemical application - selection by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); `vehicle-safety` kit reference (first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, eyewash, spill kit as review items; contents by the qualified safety professional) (PROPOSED); SDS register field for every chemical product above (PROPOSED); Stiff-bristled wire deck brush (PROPOSED); Warm-water supply for the scrub step (PROPOSED (owner decides the source)); Perimeter or edge nozzle per the washer manufacturer - selection by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED (conditional)); Supply hose to the customer's outdoor spigot (PROPOSED); Barriers and exclusion-zone items - types per safety reviewer (PROPOSED); Rinse-water and runoff controls - types per safety reviewer and local authority (PROPOSED); Landscape, electrical-fixture and opening protection (coverings) (PROPOSED); Absorbent pads (PROPOSED); Engine fuel for the gas washer, as specified by the washer manufacturer (PROPOSED); Approved fuel container - type per safety reviewer (PROPOSED); Bio-shield product (OPEN QUESTION - not proposed until the owner keeps the line (Q-28-21)); Rust-stain treatment product (OPEN QUESTION - not proposed until the owner decides the scope).
