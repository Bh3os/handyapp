## Clauses as written

This Agreement details the contract parameters between **[Your Company Name]** ("Company") and **[Client Full Name]** ("Client") for blown-in thermal attic insulation upgrades.

#### 1. Volumetric Metrics & Code Compliance
* **Scope Criteria:** The Company will install loose-fill cellulose or fiberglass media down to a pre-calculated depth of **[Insert Inches]** to satisfy local regional code targets (R-49 to R-60 baseline), totaling a contract price of **\$[Amount]**.
  - Governed now by: price: set per job on the estimate, [[FACT-PRICING-PER-JOB]].
* **Airflow Protection:** Crew will explicitly install foam soffit baffles across every single joist bay near eave vents to protect historical structural roof ventilation.

#### 2. Structural Access & Contamination Waiver
* **Hatch Isolation:** The crew will wrap the access hatch room in plastic sheeting. Drywall attic dust is highly particulate; Client releases the Company from liability for fine airborne dust migration outside the direct plastic containment boundaries.
  - Governed now by: liability, insurance and waiver wording: attorney and insurance-broker review ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]); no insurance or liability statement is made from this draft ([[RULE-NO-INVENTED-FACTS]]).
* **Pre-existing Leaks:** Blown insulation cannot fix active roof leaks. If active wood rot or water stains are discovered on structural roof rafters, Client will be notified immediately.
