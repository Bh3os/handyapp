Required tools, materials and PPE for [[SVC-HI-09]] and [[SOP-HI-09]]. The vault inventory is a draft checklist (`Required inventory/inventory-trim-molding.md`, `owner_approved: false`): quantities, stock levels, vendors, reorder points and ratings are not defined, and brand or rating words are kept as written, not approved. A missing critical tool, material or PPE item blocks the affected tasks ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]).

## Inventory checklist as written (`Required inventory/inventory-trim-molding.md`)

#### 🧳 A. Saws, Nailers & Joining Hardware (ماكينات وأدوات النجارة)
- [ ] **12-inch Dual-Bevel Sliding Compound Miter Saw:** Mounted on a rolling stand platform for high-accuracy angle adjustments.
- [ ] **20V Cordless Brad Nailer (18-Gauge) & Pin Nailer (23-Gauge):** Eliminates hose dragging inside luxury homes.
- [ ] **Flexible Steel Caulking Spatulas & Putty Knives:** For dressing nail holes flat.
- [ ] **Contour Coping Saw:** Handheld saw with fine carbon steel blades for manual coping cut profiles.

#### 🧴 B. Adhesives & Finish Fillers (المعجون والمواد اللاصقة)
- [ ] **Premium Paintable Latex Caulk (Elastic formulation):** Seals long seam lines between trim moldings and walls without cracking.
- [ ] **High-Strength Liquid-Nails Construction Adhesive:** For bonding wainscoting squares flat onto drywall planes.
- [ ] **Professional Wood Putty / Vinyl Joint Filler:** Non-shrinking compound to flush out countersunk brad head holes.
- [ ] **Premium Wood Glues (Polyurethane or PVA base):** For clamping outside miter corners together cleanly.

## Service-card defaults (spec section 17, HI-09)

- Tools and equipment (HI-09.TLS): miter saw, nailer/compressor or approved fastening system, measuring/angle tools, level, caulk tools, PPE/dust control.
- Materials and consumables (HI-09.MAT): trim/molding, fasteners, adhesive/caulk as specified, filler, shims, finishing materials if included.

## SOP section 8, "Materials and consumables" (`Standard Of Operations/sop-trim-molding.md`)

- Trim and molding stock of the approved profile and material; who supplies it (HandyHub stock, job purchase or the customer) is undefined (source: spec HI-09.MAT "trim/molding"; 02 G-158; Q-29-06; Q-28-14).
- Fasteners: brad nails and pins of the type the approved nailers take, per the nailer manufacturer; not yet in the inventory (source: spec HI-09.MAT "fasteners"; section 12; C-HI09-05).
- Shims ("thin wooden wedges"), named in "The Shimming Fix"; not yet in the inventory (source: spec HI-09.MAT "shims"; section 12).
- Adhesive and caulk as specified: the listed paintable latex caulk and construction adhesive. Brand names in the original text are kept verbatim and are not approved products (source: spec HI-09.MAT "adhesive/caulk as specified"; inventory file; 31 section 3.5 rule 8).
- Filler: the listed wood putty or vinyl joint filler (source: spec HI-09.MAT "filler"; inventory file).
- The inventory also lists wood glues, which no step uses (source: inventory file).
- Finishing materials only if included in the approved scope; paint coats are excluded by the agreement (source: spec HI-09.MAT "finishing materials if included"; agreement "Prep vs Painting" clause).
- A chemical product without a reviewed SDS is not carried to a job; the caulk, construction adhesive, filler and glue each need one (source: 29 section 8.3, PROPOSED; 02 G-008).
- Substitutions need approval according to policy (source: spec JOB-007).
