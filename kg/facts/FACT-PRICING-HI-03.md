## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)

#### 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Regional Square Foot Average:** $0.20 - $0.35 / sq. ft.
* **Minimum Equipment Trip Charge:** $150 minimum billable allocation.
* **Target Margin:** 75%

> FLAG (C-HI03-06, X-18) - "Minimum Equipment Trip Charge" line: competing minimum - owner decision (Q-28-06). The label reads as a trip fee that could be added on top of a tier price, while the spec treats \$150 as the minimum job value (BIZ-003, CX-001, FIN-001). The owner's proposed default applies the minimum once per separately quoted job/visit bundle before tax and not to every line (owner facts 2026-10-02); a multi-service visit must not receive several minimum charges (estimate-assistant playbook; 28 D-28-05, PROPOSED). The agreement's separate dry-run cancellation fee has no line here; no-work fees are a fee-schedule decision (Q-28-07). Not a customer charge until approved.

> FLAG (X-19) - "Target Margin" line: internal and unapproved, with no cost basis; the file does not say whether it is a gross, contribution or net margin or a markup. It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; FIN-007; Q-28-13).

#### 📐 Tiered Square Footage Matrix

> FLAG (C-HI03-07, C-HI03-08) - tier matrix below: the tier bounds overlap (each shared boundary area falls into two tiers), and the second tier's stated per-square-foot basis reproduces only its upper bound; nothing says whether the tiers or the per-square-foot benchmark govern. Tiers must be contiguous and non-overlapping before any activation (28 PRC-004, PROPOSED). The tier headings name patio, sidewalk and courtyard areas without a surface material, while the SOP and the agreement cover concrete only. Owner decision; no price is changed here.

#### 🚨 Stain Surcharges (زيادة الأسعار للبقع الصعبة)

> FLAG (C-HI03-V01) - "Heavy Motor Oil Extraction Surcharge" line: stain age and type scope unresolved. The SOP oil procedure is written for a fresh oil spot, while the agreement says decades-old saturation may leave permanent shading. Owner decision on which stains the surcharge covers; an oil-only visit priced from this line alone would fall below the \$150 minimum, so FIN-001 would apply (28 section 7, OPEN QUESTION).

> FLAG (C-HI03-05) - "Post-Wash Chemical Bio-Shield Shielding Spray" line: included vs optional unresolved - not sellable pending review. The main plan presents the bio-shield as an included process step; no product, SDS or SOP step exists, and the effect described here is not carried into price items or customer text (28 PRC-041, PROPOSED). Owner decision (Q-28-21); HUMAN REVIEW: attorney; state pesticide regulator (claim and label).


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Per-square-foot regional benchmark ("Regional Square Foot Average" line); three flat area tiers ("Price Range" lines under the small, standard and large tier headings); a "Minimum Equipment Trip Charge" line; two stain add-ons. Nothing says whether the tiers or the per-square-foot benchmark govern, and the tier bounds overlap (C-HI03-07). The registry's estimating basis is measured area per surface type x condition x stain treatments x drainage, water source and access, with add-ons separately identifiable (EST-005) (source: 28 section 7 row HI-03; 04 HI-03 "estimating method")
- Units: Square feet and area tier; a flat area charge (oil surcharge) and a flat option (bio-shield). The tier headings name the area type but no surface material (C-HI03-08). No square metres (source: 28 section 7 row HI-03; PRC-039)
