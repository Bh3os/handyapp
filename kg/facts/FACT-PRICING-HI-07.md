## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)

#### 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Regional Standard per Square Foot:** $1.50 - $2.50 / sq. ft. (to transition an under-insulated attic up to an R-49 / R-60 value matrix).
* **Target Margin:** 50% - 60% (Higher machine asset and continuous material inventory costs).

> FLAG (X-19): internal target, unapproved, with no cost basis and no stated margin definition; never shown to customers or field roles (spec FIN-007; 28 section 8). The margin basis and any target are owner and CPA decisions (Q-28-13). Value unchanged.

#### 📐 Square Footage Variable Volume Rates

##### Small Attic Space Footprint (Under 1,000 sq. ft.)
* **Rate:** $2.00 - $2.50 per sq. ft.

##### Large Attic Space Footprint (1,000 - 2,500 sq. ft.)
* **Rate:** $1.50 - $1.90 per sq. ft.
* *Example (1,500 sq. ft. attic footprint):* 1,500 x $1.70 = **$2,550 total contract cost**.

#### 📋 Standard Mandatory Prep Costs (Included or Itemized)

> FLAG (C-HI07-10): "Included or Itemized" is undecided. Whether these prep items sit inside the per-square-foot rate or are added per unit is an owner decision (Q-28-21); the worked example above multiplies area by rate only and leaves them out, so it is not a complete job total. The amounts are unapproved benchmarks. No price changed. HUMAN REVIEW: owner.

- [ ] **Soffit Ventilation Baffle Placement:** +$12 per rafter vent channel bay installed.
- [ ] **Recessed Can Light Fireproofing Box Shields:** +$25 flat per hot can light enclosure assembly.

> FLAG (C-HI07-03, C-HI07-V01): licensed electrician review; do not cover unsafe fixtures (HI-07.SAF; C-HI07-03). The SOP step that builds these boxes is on HOLD until a licensed electrician approves the method and materials from the fixture manufacturer's instructions (spec section 17: "Service-card defaults never override a site-specific hazard, manufacturer instruction, permit/licensing requirement, or approved change order."). The box material is not stocked (C-HI07-V01) and "Fireproofing" is an unverified performance term. Not to be quoted or performed until approved; the price is unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: licensed electrician; local building official.


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Area-based: a per-square-foot rate tiered by attic footprint (a small and a large tier), plus per-unit prep items per baffle bay and per recessed-light enclosure, labelled "Included or Itemized" (C-HI07-10). The "Regional Standard per Square Foot" line presents an unsourced benchmark range as a standard (X-19) and links it to an R-value target that has no jurisdiction source; the target belongs in the approved estimate. No rate above the large tier's upper bound; all-units tiers let an attic just under the tier boundary price above one at the boundary (RISK; owner tier rule needed) (source: 28 section 7.1 row HI-07; 04 HI-07 pricing model; headings "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks", "Square Footage Variable Volume Rates", "Standard Mandatory Prep Costs (Included or Itemized)")
- Units: Square feet of attic footprint; "rafter vent channel bay" for baffles, which the agreement and SOP call a "joist bay" (one countable unit needs one name); "hot can light enclosure assembly" per recessed fixture (source: 28 section 7.1; 04 HI-07 aliases)
