**Value ([[OWN-DEC-146]]):** each uploaded photo or document may be up to 15 MB, implemented as 15 MiB (15 × 1,048,576 bytes) so no file the owner allows is refused. A request accepts up to 10 photos in total.

- Customer request photos: JPEG, PNG or WebP; no PDFs; photos optional ([[OWN-DEC-62]], [[OWN-DEC-91]]).
- Validated on the server by content signature, size and count.
- Staff estimate documents (PDF or Word) are a separate staff-only upload with their own type validation, under the same 15 MB per-file limit ([[OWN-DEC-102]]).
