## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)

tags: #finance/pricing #drywall/production #market/louisville

#### 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Louisville Metro Residential Subcontractor Baseline:** $1.38 - $1.50 / sq. ft. (Strictly hang and tape, basic Level 4, material bulk ordered by GC)
* **All-In Builder Rate (You supply board + labor + tape):** $1.85 - $2.65 / sq. ft.
* **Target Margin:** 35% - 45% (Lower margin but balanced by massive volume and fast cash flow turnover draws)

> FLAG (X-19) - "Target Margin" line and the two benchmark lines above it: the "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks" figures name no source, date or approver and are not HandyHub rates (28 section 2.2). The "Target Margin" line is an internal target, unapproved, with no cost basis, and the file does not say whether it is a gross, contribution or net margin or a markup (Q-28-13). It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; spec FIN-007). Its rationale assumes "massive volume", while HandyHub starts with two employees (owner facts 2026-10-02), and "fast cash flow turnover draws", while the agreement's billing clause sets net payment terms (28 section 8; Q-28-19). Values unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; CPA (margin basis).

#### 📐 Commercial/Developer Volume Breakdown

> FLAG (C-DW04-09) - heading "Commercial/Developer Volume Breakdown": the canonical service is "High-Volume Residential New Construction" (spec Appendix B; 04). Commercial or multi-family projects may carry different licensing, code, insurance and safety obligations; that is an OPEN QUESTION, and nothing here states what Kentucky or Indiana requires. Whether builder or commercial work is offered at launch is undecided (Q-30-04; Q-28-19). Heading unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; attorney; building official.

##### Production Hanging Only (No Taping)
* **Rate:** $0.45 - $0.75 per sq. ft.
* *Operational Note:* Fast auto-feed screw gun line, boards pinned to raw studs.

##### Production Taping & Mudding Only (Bazooka & Flat Box Lines)
* **Rate:** $0.55 - $0.90 per sq. ft.
* *Operational Note:* Three mechanical passes over joints to clear a paint-ready Level 4 finish.

##### Total Turnkey Package (Board + Hang + Tape + Sand)
* **10,000 sq. ft. or less (Custom Builds):** $2.25 - $2.75 per sq. ft.
* **10,000 - 50,000 sq. ft. (Track Housing/Apartments):** $1.85 - $2.15 per sq. ft.
* **50,000+ sq. ft. (Large Subdivisions):** $1.50 - $1.75 per sq. ft.

> FLAG (C-DW04-11) - "Total Turnkey Package" tiers: the tier bounds overlap at exactly 10,000 and 50,000 sq. ft., so a job of exactly that size matches two tiers. Before any value is imported, the tiers of one item must be contiguous and non-overlapping, lower bound inclusive and upper bound exclusive (28 PRC-004, PROPOSED). Which bounds apply is an owner decision (Q-28-01); no price is changed. HUMAN REVIEW: owner.

#### 🚨 Volume Guard Clauses
- [ ] **GC Material Discrepancy Surcharge:** If the General Contractor provides the boards but has shorted the material count, delaying your production line, a **$45 / hour crew standby penalty** applies.

> FLAG (C-DW04-03) - "GC Material Discrepancy Surcharge" line: basis and trigger conflict with the agreement. This line charges standby per crew-hour when the general contractor has shorted a board count; the agreement's "Standby Crew Fee" charges per worker-hour when the production line is locked out or delayed by delivery trucks, late framing adjustments or other trades' debris, so the amount invoiced depends on which document is applied. The "penalty" wording is held for attorney review, and the application would use "fee" (28 section 3.7). A standby charge could be invoiced only from an owner-approved fee schedule (28 PRC-021), with the delay time logged (spec JOB-002) and the short delivery recorded with photos (spec JOB-004, START-009). Amount unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; attorney (Q-28-07).

- [ ] **Winter Heat Surcharge:** If working between November and March in Kentuckiana and the build site lacks functional temporary heating (below 50°F), add a **15% surcharge** to offset the extended drying times of setting mud layers.

> FLAG (C-DW04-04) - "Winter Heat Surcharge" line: this surcharge does not relax the SOP temperature precondition (C-DW04-04). The SOP's "Climate Verification" step requires a sealed building envelope and the stated internal temperature for the stated period before mud application, and the agreement's "Drying Temperature Clause" places that heat on the contractor through the sanding phase; this line instead prices work on a site that lacks temporary heat. Until the owner sets one winter rule (attorney for the remedy wording), the SOP precondition governs field work, and a surcharge is never a reason to proceed without it (31 section 10). Temporary heating on site is a safety matter for the qualified safety professional (SOP section 9). Amount unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; attorney (remedy wording).

> FLAG (C-DW04-10) - charges missing from "Volume Guard Clauses": the agreement adds two charges that this file does not define, an emergency rate for board removal forced by late mechanical changes ("Pre-requisite Trades Sign-off") and monthly late interest on draws ("Commercial Billing & Net Draw Terms"). Neither is in this price list or approved. Any fee needs an owner-approved fee schedule entry (28 PRC-021), and late interest is a payment-terms decision, not a price item (28 section 3.13). HUMAN REVIEW: owner; attorney and CPA for the interest (Q-28-07, Q-28-19).


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Builder per-square-foot packages under "Commercial/Developer Volume Breakdown": "Production Hanging Only (No Taping)", "Production Taping & Mudding Only (Bazooka & Flat Box Lines)" and "Total Turnkey Package (Board + Hang + Tape + Sand)", the last in three volume tiers (C-DW04-11). Two market reference lines under "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks" ("Louisville Metro Residential Subcontractor Baseline", "All-In Builder Rate") have no matching HandyHub package, and their relation to the turnkey tiers is not explained (owner decision). "Volume Guard Clauses" adds an hourly standby charge and a percentage winter surcharge. No rule says which package or tier governs a job, and the agreement carries a single unit-rate placeholder (source: 28 section 7.2 row DW-04; 04 DW-04 pricing model; agreement "Scope Metrics")
- Units: Square feet (written "sq. ft."), with no stated area basis (board area, wall face or floor area; 28 section 3.3 requires the basis per item); volume tiers by total square feet, overlapping at the two boundary values (C-DW04-11); crew-hour for standby, while the agreement uses worker-hours (C-DW04-03); a percentage of an unstated base for the winter surcharge (source: 28 sections 3.3 and 7.2; 28 PRC-004, PRC-039)
