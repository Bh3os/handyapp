Required tools, materials and PPE for [[SVC-HI-07]] and [[SOP-HI-07]]. The vault inventory is a draft checklist (`Required inventory/inventory-attic-insulation.md`, `owner_approved: false`): quantities, stock levels, vendors, reorder points and ratings are not defined, and brand or rating words are kept as written, not approved. A missing critical tool, material or PPE item blocks the affected tasks ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]).

## Inventory checklist as written (`Required inventory/inventory-attic-insulation.md`)

#### 🧳 A. Insulation Media & Containment Elements
- [ ] **Premium Compressed Blowing Cellulose / Fiberglass Insulation Bags:** Sourced in bulk pallets.
- [ ] **Styrofoam Soffit Ventilation Baffles (Rafter Vents):** Fixed channels to guarantee continuous roof ventilation airflow.
- [ ] **Fire-Rated Fireproofing Caulk & Foam Spray:** For sealing electrical wire drill wire holes through top plates.
- [ ] **Thick Poly Masking Tarps:** 4-mil clear rolls to wrap floor entries directly below the ceiling access hatch.

#### 🛠️ B. Pneumatic Machinery & Protective Gear (ماكينات النفخ وأدوات الحماية)
- [ ] **Commercial Pneumatic Insulation Blowing Machine (Hopper Unit):** High-output agitation blower (can be rig-mounted or rented).
- [ ] **Flexible Lightweight Insulation Delivery Hose (100-foot):** Fitted with heavy-duty interlocking couplings.
- [ ] **NIOSH-Approved N95 / P100 Disposable Dust Respirators:** Mandatory to shield workers from airborne glass fiber particulates.
- [ ] **High-Intensity Cordless LED Work Lights / Headlamps:** For illuminating dark joist bays inside attics safely.

Proposed additions in the vault file (from the SOP and spec cross-check; pending owner and safety review, nothing approved for purchase or use): PPE per the card's tools and safety rows (respiratory and skin protection, coveralls) - selection by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); Eye protection for fiber and dust exposure - selection by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); Head protection for low-clearance work - selection by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); `vehicle-safety` kit reference: first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, eyewash, spill kit and SDS access as review items - contents by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); SDS register entry for each chemical product in this list (insulation; fire-rated caulk and foam; fuel if the blower is engine-driven) (PROPOSED); Measuring tools and depth markers (PROPOSED); Hand tools, including cutting tools (PROPOSED); Containment tape or fasteners for the plastic sheeting (PROPOSED); Baffle fasteners and fastening tool, as the baffle manufacturer specifies (PROPOSED); Vacuum; dust class set by the safety reviewer (PROPOSED); Hatch access ladder - per approved height/access policy (none exists, 02 G-005) (PROPOSED); Attic walk boards or another joist-travel aid - per approved height/access policy (none exists, 02 G-005) (PROPOSED); Heat-stress supplies as the reviewer's heat plan requires (PROPOSED - pending qualified safety review); Hopper-to-attic communication means (PROPOSED - pending qualified safety review); Blower power supply, as the blower manufacturer specifies (PROPOSED); Hatch weatherstripping material, if hatch restoration is in scope (PROPOSED); Waste bags for empty insulation bags and used sheeting (PROPOSED); Approved light/penetration protection material (HOLD - not added until a licensed electrician approves the method and materials (C-HI07-03)).

## Service-card defaults (spec section 17, HI-07)

- Tools and equipment (HI-07.TLS): respiratory PPE, coveralls, lighting, insulation blower where used, measuring/depth markers, hand tools, vacuum.
- Materials and consumables (HI-07.MAT): approved insulation, baffles, markers, containment/protection, approved light/penetration protection materials.

## SOP section 8, "Materials and consumables" (`Standard Of Operations/sop-attic-insulation.md`)

- Approved insulation: loose-fill fiberglass or cellulose (original step: "premium fiberglass or cellulose insulation bags"); product, coverage chart and bag count come from the approved estimate and manufacturer data; no product is approved yet (source: spec HI-07.MAT "approved insulation"; Q-28-14).
- Baffles: the SOP allows "plastic or styrofoam", the agreement says "foam" and the inventory stocks styrofoam only; the approved material is an owner decision (source: spec HI-07.MAT "baffles"; C-HI07-12).
- Markers: depth markers, not stocked (source: spec HI-07.MAT "markers").
- Containment and protection: plastic sheeting ("Thick Poly Masking Tarps"); tape or fasteners to hold it are not stocked (source: spec HI-07.MAT "containment/protection"; inventory file).
- Approved light/penetration protection materials: none approved. The "fire-rated drywall boxes" in section 12 are not stocked, and the stocked fire-rated caulk and foam have no step (C-HI07-V01); HOLD until a licensed electrician approves the method and materials (C-HI07-03) (source: spec HI-07.MAT).
- Substitutions that affect specification, safety, cost or customer expectation need approval (source: spec JOB-007).
- Every chemical product needs a reviewed SDS before it is carried to a job (source: 29 section 8.3, PROPOSED; 02 G-008).
