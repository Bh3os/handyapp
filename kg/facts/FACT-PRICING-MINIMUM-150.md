Text as written in the status callout of the vault pricing files (for example `Pricing/pricing-picture-hanging.md`): "The $150 job minimum (spec BIZ-003, FIN-001, CX-001) applies unless an authorized exception is recorded; how it applies to recurring visits, callbacks, estimate visits and multi-service visits is an open owner decision."

The rule came from the owner facts of 2026-10-02 ([[OWN-DEC-06]], "Minimum charge of $150 USD"). Every service-level minimum, trip charge, call-out minimum or minimum-hours line in the price lists depended on or competed with it; all are superseded with their price lists.
