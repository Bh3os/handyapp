## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)


#### 📈 Financial Benchmarks
* **Target Net Profit Margin:** 65% - 75%
* **Ideal Customer Profile:** High-net-worth families, busy corporate executives, penthouses.
* **Business Model:** 100% Contractual Monthly Subscriptions (Paid upfront on the 1st of the month).

> FLAG (X-19) - "Target Net Profit Margin" line: internal target, unapproved, with no cost basis. "Net" would need an overhead allocation that no file defines, and the file does not say whether the figure is a margin or a markup. It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; FIN-007; Q-28-13). Because the agreement defines its packages by this file's name, the line could reach a customer through the agreement (C-CL06-10). 28 section 8 also notes that at the file's top-tier price and daily hours little room would remain for labor cost if the target held (ESTIMATE; a RISK, not a contradiction).

> FLAG (C-CL06-06) - "Business Model" line: payment timing and method pending owner, payment-provider and attorney decisions. Paying a full month upfront is a plan term, not a deposit (28 section 3.10). The agreement turns it into "billed and captured" card-style collection although no payment provider is selected; until one is, payments are electronic invoices, cash and check (owner facts 2026-10-02; spec BIZ-007; 28 PRC-043, PROPOSED). Proration of partial months, refunds on termination and the accounting of prepaid months are open (Q-28-10, Q-28-18; HUMAN REVIEW: attorney, CPA). Not a customer term until approved.

#### 🗓️ Subscription Package Matrix

##### 🥈 Silver Tier (2x Visits Per Week)
* **Monthly Cost:** $400 - $600 / month *(1,500 - 2,250 SAR / mo)*.
* *Inclusions:* Light daily tidying, hospital-corner bed making, standard laundry wash, trash clearing.

> FLAG (C-CL06-03, C-CL06-04, C-CL06-V01) - Silver "Inclusions" line: "Light daily tidying" on a tier of two visits a week - frequency wording pending owner decision (C-CL06-03). "standard laundry wash" has no SOP washing, drying or folding step, so it is not sellable until a reviewed step exists (C-CL06-04; 31 SOP-029, PROPOSED). The agreement's single scope sentence and the tier-free SOP give Silver the closet, steaming and scent tasks this file reserves for Gold (C-CL06-V01); the per-tier task list is an owner decision (Q-28-21; 04 SVC-011 task-inclusion matrix).

##### 🥇 Gold Tier (3x Visits Per Week)
* **Monthly Cost:** $700 - $1,000 / month *(2,600 - 3,750 SAR / mo)*.
* *Inclusions:* Silver tier items + closet color-coding organization, premium garment valet steaming, interior fridge maintenance, ambient scent refreshing.

> FLAG (C-CL06-07, C-CL06-V01) - Gold "Inclusions" line: "interior fridge maintenance" has no SOP step (NO SOP STEP), and what may be discarded from a fridge is undecided; it is not sellable until a reviewed step exists or the owner withdraws it (31 SOP-029, PROPOSED; Q-28-21). "ambient scent refreshing" depends on a recorded fragrance preference and allergy check under the SOP's draft hold (31 section 10 CL-06). The agreement and the SOP give these tasks to every tier, not only Gold and above (C-CL06-V01).

##### 👑 Elite Presidential Tier (Daily Maintenance - Mon-Fri)
* **Monthly Cost:** $1,500 - $2,200 / month *(5,600 - 8,250 SAR / mo)*.
* *Inclusions:* Absolute resort-style daily upkeep. Dedicated personal housekeeper on property for up to 3 hours daily, luxury bed adjustments, custom closet curation, daily trash disposal, organizing mail/packages.

> FLAG (C-CL06-01, C-CL06-07, C-CL06-03) - Elite "Inclusions" line: "organizing mail/packages" has no SOP step (NO SOP STEP) and needs a privacy boundary first; what may be touched, opened or moved is a privacy-owner and attorney decision (C-CL06-01, C-CL06-07; 30 section 8 row 21; Q-30-17). "luxury bed adjustments" may fall under the SOP's bed-making phase; the owner confirms (C-CL06-07). The "Dedicated personal housekeeper on property" commits a large share of one of the two employees per Elite client and means lone work in a private residence: capacity is an owner decision (28 section 9.1 P13; Q-28-02) and lone-work controls are pending safety review (04 CL-06 "hazards"). "Absolute resort-style daily upkeep" is outcome wording that is not carried into customer price text (28 PRC-041, PROPOSED). "Daily" here means the Monday-to-Friday visits of the tier heading, not the main plan's "every single day" (C-CL06-03).

#### 🚨 Subscription Terms & Fees
- [ ] **Cancellation/Skip Policy:** Subscriptions cover a monthly window. If a client skips a day, no refunds are issued; they can schedule a makeup visit within 7 days.
- [ ] **Fine Garments Surcharge:** +$50/mo if the valet service involves luxury dry-cleaning drop-offs or hand-washing pure silk/cashmere items.

> FLAG (C-CL06-05) - "Cancellation/Skip Policy" line: terms conflict - owner and attorney. The agreement, which defines its packages by this file, says a skipped visit earns "no partial monetary credit" and offers a makeup "hour block" within a business-day window "subject to crew availability", while this line says "no refunds are issued" and offers a makeup visit within a calendar-day window. One policy must be chosen, and no rule covers visits that HandyHub itself misses. Consumer refund and credit terms need attorney review for Kentucky and Indiana separately (30 section 5 row 71; Q-28-18, Q-28-27; Q-30-22). Not a customer term until approved.

> FLAG (C-CL06-04) - "Fine Garments Surcharge" line: the services it charges for, dry-cleaning drop-offs and hand-washing silk or cashmere, have no SOP step and no agreement line, and the agreement's "Valet Limitation" says staff "are not professional dry cleaners". Not sellable until the owner decides to offer them and reviewed steps (care-label check, custody log) exist (31 SOP-029, PROPOSED; 28 PRC-042, PROPOSED). Garments leaving the residence raise custody and insurance questions (HUMAN REVIEW: insurance broker, attorney). The surcharge is a monthly add-on, not a standalone price (28 section 4.5). No amount is changed here.


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Monthly prepaid subscription tiers by visits per week, Silver, Gold and Elite Presidential ("Monthly Cost" lines), with a monthly "Fine Garments Surcharge" and a skip and makeup rule ("Cancellation/Skip Policy" line). Tier choice is the only estimating input: no residence size, room count or task-time driver (04 CL-06 "estimating method"). PROPOSED in 28: a recurring plan is a ServicePlan record holding frequency, per-period price, proration, skip and makeup rules and the minimum evaluation scope, and each visit is still a Job (28 section 3.12) (source: 28 section 7.4 row CL-06; 28 sections 3.3 and 3.12)
- Units: Month (plan period); tiers are defined by visits per week; the Elite line also states daily on-site hours. No square metres (source: 28 section 7.4 row CL-06; PRC-039)
