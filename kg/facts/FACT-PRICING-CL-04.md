## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)


#### 📈 Financial Benchmarks
* **Target Net Profit Margin:** 40% - 50% (High risk, specialized equipment like HEPA vacuums and glass scrapers required).
* **Ideal Crew Size:** 3 - 5 Cleaners
* **Billing Structure:** Contractors usually pay per square foot/meter.

> FLAG (X-19) - "Target Net Profit Margin" line: internal target, unapproved, with no cost basis. "Net" implies an overhead allocation that the file does not define, and the file does not say whether the figure is a margin or a markup (Q-28-13). Its rationale names high risk and specialized equipment, but the file defines no control or cost basis for that risk; hazard controls belong in the SOP under qualified safety review. It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; spec FIN-007). Value unchanged.

> FLAG (C-CL04-07) - "Ideal Crew Size" line: LABOR ASSUMPTION CONFLICT - OWNER DECISION. The crew size exceeds current staffing (owner facts 2026-10-02: initially two employees, subcontractors later). The file states no productivity, person-hours or loaded labor cost (Q-28-02). Launch crew, maximum project size and subcontractor use are owner decisions. Value unchanged.

> FLAG (C-CL04-11, C-CL04-13) - "Billing Structure" line: it names contractors as the paying customer, while the main plan markets this service to homeowners and the agreement is a commercial contract whose party placeholder includes a property owner (C-CL04-11; Q-28-19; HUMAN REVIEW: attorney licensed in KY/IN). In "foot/meter" the metre option is a legacy artifact (C-CL04-13); estimates use square feet with the area basis stated per item (28 section 3.3; PRC-039). Value unchanged.

#### 📐 The 3-Phase Milestone Pricing Model

##### Phase 1: Rough Interior Clean (Dust Pull & Debris Removal)
* **Rate:** $0.15 - $0.25 per sq. ft. *(6 - 10 SAR per sqm)*.
* *Inclusions:* Bulk vacuuming fine drywall powder, pulling construction trash.

> FLAG (X-19, C-CL04-13) - Phase 1 "Rate" and "Inclusions" lines: the rate is an unsourced benchmark (X-19), and its SAR and per-square-metre parenthetical is a legacy artifact that is never imported or converted (C-CL04-13; 28 PRC-002, PRC-039). "pulling construction trash" includes debris removal, but the file prices no disposal and no waste policy exists, so disposal items stay inactive until it does (28 sections 3.5 and 7.4; 02 G-157; Q-28-15). Cleanup that belongs to a HandyHub drywall or painting job is not sold again as a CL-04 phase line (04 section 5.2 rules (a) and (d), PROPOSED). The SOP holds the dust work of this phase until a qualified safety professional decides the dust-control method and respiratory protection. No price changed.

##### Phase 2: Detail Clean (Scraping, Peeling, Wiping)
* **Rate:** $0.25 - $0.40 per sq. ft. *(11 - 16 SAR per sqm)*.
* *Inclusions:* Scraping paint/stucco off glass, peeling protective films, washing new cabinets inside/out.

> FLAG (C-CL04-10, X-19, C-CL04-13) - Phase 2 lines: "washing new cabinets inside/out" - the SOP's "Cabinetry Interior" step covers drawer and shelf interiors only; cabinet exteriors have no SOP step and are not covered, pending an owner decision to add a reviewed step or exclude them (C-CL04-10). "Scraping paint/stucco off glass": the SOP holds blade scraping until a pre-work glass inspection with photos is recorded, and interior versus exterior glass is not defined (04 estimating method). The rate is an unsourced benchmark with a legacy SAR and per-square-metre parenthetical (X-19, C-CL04-13). No price changed.

##### Phase 3: Final Touch & Touch-Up Buff
* **Rate:** $0.10 - $0.15 per sq. ft. *(4 - 6 SAR per sqm)*.
* *Inclusions:* Wiping lingering settled dust right before client walkthrough.

> FLAG (C-CL04-10, X-19, C-CL04-13) - Phase 3 heading and lines: "Touch-Up Buff" is not defined; if it means floor buffing, no SOP step performs it and no buffing equipment is listed in the inventory (C-CL04-10). Sold alone on a small area, this phase can compute below the job minimum; whether the minimum is evaluated on the job total or per phase visit is an owner decision (Q-28-04; see the classification table below). The rate is an unsourced benchmark with a legacy SAR and per-square-metre parenthetical (X-19, C-CL04-13). No price changed.

##### 📦 All-In Commercial Bundle (أفضل خيار للمقاولين)
* If the developer signs a contract for all 3 phases combined, charge:
  * **$0.50 - $0.80 per sq. ft.** *(20 - 32 SAR per sqm)* flat.

> FLAG (C-CL04-11, C-CL04-13, X-19) - "All-In Commercial Bundle": its per-square-foot range equals the sum of the three phase ranges, so it offers no bundle saving although its Arabic subheading presents it as the best option for contractors (28 section 3.13; Q-28-20); "flat" here is a per-square-foot rate, not a flat fee. The Arabic subheading and the SAR and per-square-metre parenthetical are legacy artifacts (C-CL04-13). The offer to "the developer" is commercial wording, while the main plan markets the service to homeowners (C-CL04-11; Q-28-19). No price changed.

#### 🚨 Specialty Hardware Surcharges
- [ ] **High Ceiling Scaffolding Work (Above 12 ft):** Add **+$150 - $300 rental/hazard fee** if painters ladders or rolling scaffolding are needed to clean high light fixtures or beams.

> FLAG (C-CL04-01, X-12, C-CL04-08) - "High Ceiling Scaffolding Work (Above 12 ft)" line: HEIGHT WORK - PENDING SAFETY REVIEW; pending height/access policy (C-CL04-01). The line charges a "rental/hazard fee" for a hazard that no control covers: no height and access policy exists (02 G-005; X-12), the inventory lists no ladder or scaffold, and the SOP holds every step that needs access equipment until the policy exists. Whether work "Above 12 ft" is in scope, excluded or subcontracted is an open decision for the owner with the qualified safety professional (Q-31-09). The fee has no line in the agreement, which bills only checked milestones: a condition known at estimate time is an estimate line, and one found after signature needs an approved change order (C-CL04-08; spec EST-005, FIN-008; 28 section 3.7, D-28-08). An equipment rental is passed through at documented cost or as an approved flat amount, never both, and rent or buy is an owner decision (28 section 3.5; Q-28-15; Q-29-08). HUMAN REVIEW: qualified safety professional. No price changed.


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Three per-square-foot milestone phases (Phase 1 rough interior clean, Phase 2 detail clean, Phase 3 final touch); an "All-In Commercial Bundle" per-square-foot rate for all three phases whose range equals the sum of the phase ranges, so it offers no saving, and whose "flat" is a per-square-foot rate, not a flat fee; and a conditional high-ceiling "rental/hazard fee" for work "Above 12 ft". The agreement lets phases be selected and invoiced one by one. Proposed route: site assessment; a firm quote waits for the hazard decisions (area release, height, acid treatment, suspect lead or asbestos debris) (estimate-assistant playbook "Missing hazardous-condition decisions block a firm quote.") (source: 28 section 7.4 row CL-04; 28 section 3.13; 04 CL-04 pricing model and estimating method; headings "Financial Benchmarks", "The 3-Phase Milestone Pricing Model", "Specialty Hardware Surcharges")
- Units: Square feet per phase and for the bundle, with no stated area basis (28 section 3.3 requires one per item); the "Billing Structure" line also offers metres, a legacy artifact (C-CL04-13). The high-ceiling fee is one charge per job, stated as a range. Estimating drivers on the spec card (construction status, dust and debris type, surfaces, residue, punch activity, occupancy readiness) and glass count or ceiling height are not priced (source: 28 sections 3.3 and 7.4; spec CL-04.INT; 04 CL-04 estimating method)
