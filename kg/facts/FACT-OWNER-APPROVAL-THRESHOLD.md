**Value:** $2,500.00 (250000 cents), configurable by the Owner (a new audited configuration version).

- Estimate: Owner approval before publication when the final proposed total, after eligible discounts and adjustments, is greater than the threshold.
- Change order: Owner approval before publication when the absolute price adjustment is greater than the threshold, or when the revised total authorized project value (accepted estimate plus approved change orders plus this one) is greater than it.
- Comparison "greater than": exactly $2,500.00 needs no approval.
- Refunds, deposit waivers and the other owner-only financial actions stay owner-only at every amount ([[OWN-DEC-76]], [[OWN-DEC-118]]).
