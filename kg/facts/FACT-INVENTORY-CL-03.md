Required tools, materials and PPE for [[SVC-CL-03]] and [[SOP-CL-03]]. The vault inventory is a draft checklist (`Required inventory/inventory-move-in-move-out.md`, `owner_approved: false`): quantities, stock levels, vendors, reorder points and ratings are not defined, and brand or rating words are kept as written, not approved. A missing critical tool, material or PPE item blocks the affected tasks ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]).

## Inventory checklist as written (`Required inventory/inventory-move-in-move-out.md`)

#### 🧴 A. The Chemical Arsenal (المنظفات الكيميائية)
- [ ] **Heavy-Duty Disinfectant Concentrate:** To thoroughly sanitize completely empty, unhygienic cabinets and drawers.
- [ ] **Concentrated Floor Stripper / Tile Cleaner:** To remove layers of old wax or buildup left behind by past tenants.
- [ ] **Commercial Drain Opening Gel:** To clear out slow-moving bathroom and kitchen drains before new occupancy.
- [ ] **Adhesive Glue Remover (pH 5-6):** For removing sticker residue from shelving and cabinet interiors.

> FLAG (C-CL03-02, critical): this file lists four chemical products, including concentrates and a drain-opening gel, and items left by past tenants are to be bagged, but no PPE is listed anywhere in it. Until a qualified safety professional selects PPE from each product's SDS, the chemical and debris tasks stay blocked, and no chemical product without a reviewed SDS is carried to a job (29 section 8.3, PROPOSED; X-09). PPE is proposed in the review tables below as "selection by qualified safety professional"; no PPE type is chosen here.

#### 🧳 B. Hardware & Essential Tools (الأدوات والمعدات)
- [ ] **Heavy-Duty Plastic Putty Knives:** For safely scraping dried food out of cabinets without scratching the wood.
- [ ] **Extension Cord (50-foot, 12-gauge):** Crucial for plugging in vacuums when wall outlets are limited or sparse in empty houses.
- [ ] **High-Powered LED Headlamps / Work Lights:** For illuminating dark, empty closets, pantries, and cabinets.
- [ ] **Microfiber Flat Mop System:** With 5x replacement pads for fast wall and ceiling dusting.
- [ ] **Heavy Contractor Trash Bags (3 mil thickness):** For throwing away forgotten bulky items left by past tenants.

Proposed additions in the vault file (from the SOP and spec cross-check; pending owner and safety review, nothing approved for purchase or use): PPE for chemical handling (disinfectant concentrate, floor stripper, drain-opening gel, adhesive remover and any approved oven, toilet, soap-scum or floor product) - selection by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); Cut and puncture protection for handling unknown debris and bags - selection by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); Sharps container, with a handling and disposal rule (PROPOSED - type and disposal route by qualified safety professional); `vehicle-safety` kit reference: first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, eyewash, spill kit as review items (PROPOSED); SDS register field for each chemical product above (disinfectant concentrate, floor stripper, drain-opening gel, adhesive remover) (PROPOSED); Kit dependency: the CL-02 deep-clean kit named by the spec as this service's base kit (PROPOSED); Vacuum with crevice tool (PROPOSED); Floor mop and bucket (PROPOSED); Cloths (PROPOSED); Glass cleaner and squeegee - approved product per SDS (PROPOSED); Window-track brush (PROPOSED); Oven cleaner and scrub pads - approved product per SDS (PROPOSED); Toilet bowl cleaner and toilet brush - approved product per SDS (PROPOSED); Soap-scum remover - approved product per SDS (PROPOSED); Floor cleaner for "Deep Mopping" ("an industrial cleaner") - approved product per SDS (PROPOSED - product type by qualified safety reviewer); Liners (PROPOSED); Wet-floor sign (PROPOSED); Absorbent towels or a tray for freezer defrost water (PROPOSED); Detail brush for the garbage-disposal splash guard (PROPOSED - used only under the SOP's disposal hold (hands never inside the disposal)); Ladder or step access - per approved height/access policy (none exists, 02 G-005) (PROPOSED - type by qualified safety professional).
