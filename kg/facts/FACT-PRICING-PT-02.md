## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)

#### 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Average Square Foot Baseline:** $2.00 - $4.00 / sq. ft. for full strip, brighten, and stain cycle.
* **Linear Foot Baseline (Fences):** $8 - $14 / linear foot (based on a standard 6ft privacy fence, both sides included).
* **Target Margin:** 65% (Very high profit if using efficient airless sprayers and thick block back-brushes).

> FLAG (C-PT02-07) - "Linear Foot Baseline (Fences)" line: market benchmark only. It sits under "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks" next to the market square-foot baseline, while HandyHub's "Unified Estimation Methods" below are deck-only, so HandyHub has no fence pricing method although fences are part of the canonical service (spec Appendix B) and only the owner can withdraw them. The SOP has no fence procedure, and "both sides included" assumes access to the neighbor's side of the fence (HUMAN REVIEW: attorney, neighbor access). A short fence-only job priced per foot could fall below the \$150 minimum, so FIN-001 would apply (04 PT-02 "minimum charge behavior"). OPEN QUESTION (owner): which fence pricing method, scope and minimum apply? Not a customer price until approved.

> FLAG (X-19) - "Average Square Foot Baseline" and "Target Margin" lines: the benchmark heading names no source or date. The margin is internal and unapproved, with no cost basis; the file does not say whether it is a gross, contribution or net margin or a markup, and its "Very high profit" rationale is an unverified profit claim that leaves out the back-brushing the SOP requires immediately after spraying or rolling ("Mandatory Back-Brushing"). It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; FIN-007; Q-28-13).

#### 📐 Unified Estimation Methods

##### Method A: Square Footage of Flat Deck Floorboards
* **Full Service Package (Chem Wash + Brighten + Premium Oil Stain):** $2.50 - $3.75 per sq. ft.
* *Example (12' x 16' / 192 sq. ft. deck floor):* 192 x $3.00 = $576 baseline.

##### Method B: Detailed Linear Component Pricing (For Railings & Steps)
* Floors are easy, but railings take double the time. Bill them separately:
	- **Standard Wood Railings:** +$12 - $18 per linear foot.
	- **Deck Steps/Stairs:** +$35 - $50 flat per single step tread.
	- **Under-Deck Support Joists (Exposed Staining):** +$1.50 / sq. ft.

> FLAG (C-PT02-07) - "Under-Deck Support Joists (Exposed Staining)" line: priced as an add-on, but the SOP has no under-deck or overhead staining step and no access, chemical-drip or overspray control for overhead work; not sellable until an SOP step is drafted and competently reviewed (28 PRC-042, PROPOSED; 31 SOP-029). The "Deck Steps/Stairs" line likewise has no stair-specific SOP step (04 PT-02 "SOP reference"). The "railings take double the time" remark is the only labor basis in this file (28 section 7.3 row PT-02).

#### 🚨 Regional Guard Clauses
- [ ] **Minimum Trip Fee for Decks:** $450 absolute minimum to execute a stripping cycle due to two-phase scheduling (Wash day vs. Dry stain day).
- [ ] **Solid Stain Premium:** Add **15% to the final estimate** if the client requests Solid Acrylic Stain over Transparent/Semi-Transparent Oils, due to a stricter double-coat dry specification requirement.

> FLAG (C-PT02-03, X-18) - "Minimum Trip Fee for Decks" line: competing minimum - owner decision (Q-28-06). It sets a deck-only service floor above the \$150 job minimum, and its label mixes a trip fee, which could be added on top of the package price, with an "absolute minimum". The owner has not authorized it as an exception to the \$150 rule (spec BIZ-003, CX-001, FIN-001; only authorized roles change price rules, PERM-004). Under the PROPOSED bundle rule one minimum applies per separately quoted job/visit bundle before tax, service-level floors stay inactive until approved, and a visit that combines this work with HI-10 or HI-11 repairs carries one minimum (owner facts 2026-10-02; 28 D-28-04, D-28-05; 04 section 5.4 rule (c)). Fence-only work and maintenance coats without stripping have no stated minimum, so the \$150 rule applies to them. Not a customer charge until approved.

> FLAG (C-PT02-04) - "Solid Stain Premium" line: option not supported by the SOP, inventory or agreement - not sellable until defined (28 PRC-042, PROPOSED). The SOP, the agreement's moisture clause and the main plan describe oil-based stain only; no solid acrylic product, SDS, moisture rule, recoat step, cleanup method or quality check exists, and the "double-coat dry specification" is unsourced (manufacturer instructions required). The base of the percentage ("the final estimate") is ambiguous, an option must be separately identifiable on the estimate (spec EST-005), and a product change needs approval (spec JOB-007). OPEN QUESTION (owner, with manufacturer instructions): offer, define or withdraw the option? No price or percentage is changed here.


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Per-square-foot full-service package for flat deck floorboards ("Full Service Package" line, Method A); per-linear-foot railing, per-tread step and per-square-foot under-deck add-ons (Method B); a percentage "Solid Stain Premium" on the final estimate; a deck "Minimum Trip Fee" stated as an "absolute minimum". The "Average Square Foot Baseline" and "Linear Foot Baseline (Fences)" lines are market benchmarks, and fences have no HandyHub method (C-PT02-07). Registry estimating basis (PROPOSED): site assessment with a soundness check; deck floor area x package rate + railing length + step count + under-deck area; product chosen first, with oil and acrylic as separate alternates (EST-005); a two-visit plan and weather contingency stated on the estimate (source: 28 section 7.3 row PT-02; 04 PT-02 "pricing model" and "estimating method")
- Units: Square feet (deck floor, under-deck joists), linear feet (railings; fence benchmark), step tread, and a percentage of the final estimate. No square metres (source: 28 section 7.3 row PT-02; PRC-039)
