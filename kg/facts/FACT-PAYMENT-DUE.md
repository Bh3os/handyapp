**Value:** "Payment due on receipt": the balance is due when the invoice is issued, which happens only after the owner quality review.

- Disclosed on the estimate (written and uploaded) before approval; the payment-terms text and version are recorded with the approval ([[OWN-DEC-103]]).
- Stored per customer/project; new projects default to due on receipt; payment terms already agreed with an existing customer are kept, never overwritten.
- The deposit is due earlier, before confirmed work scheduling ([[FACT-DEPOSIT-RULE]]).
- No late fees or interest ([[OWN-DEC-112]]).
