Value: HandyHub never performs licensed gas, electrical (line-voltage), plumbing or HVAC work, structural work, or hazardous-material work (asbestos, lead, mold), in any of the 26 services.

- [[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]: "HandyHub never performs licensed gas, electrical, plumbing, HVAC or structural work, and never removes hazardous materials."
- [[OWN-DEC-51]]: every service page answers "Is there anything you don't do?" with the service's own limits, the home-age limits where they apply, and the licensed-trade (line-voltage electrical, gas, plumbing, HVAC), asbestos and lead, mold and structural limits; "How estimates work" lists "Work we don't do". HUMAN REVIEW of the wording: attorney licensed in KY and/or IN; qualified safety professional.
- Owner, 2026-10-12 (Step 4.3, [[OWN-DEC-100]]): work outside HandyHub's scope ("licensed gas, electrical or plumbing, structural, asbestos/lead/mold") is marked "Not a fit" with a reason; the customer sees "We're not able to take on this project", with no referral to named companies.
- [[OWN-DEC-80]]: "Workers must not attempt gas repairs or handle exposed potentially live wiring." Suspected gas leaks: evacuate and call emergency services or the gas utility from a safe location.
- Owner, 2026-10-05: for DW-01, "Licensed plumbing, electrical, gas and HVAC stay excluded" ([[FACT-SVC-DW01-REPAIR-SCOPE]]).
- Owner, 2026-10-05: "When a worker finds a problem no HandyHub service covers (roof, foundation, mold, licensed trades), what do you tell the customer?" = "Advise a licensed pro (Recommended)".
- The brief for the estimate terms v1.0 lists this limit among the agreed policies the terms carry: "HandyHub doesn't perform licensed gas, electrical, plumbing, HVAC or structural work, or hazardous-material (asbestos, lead, mold) work per the owner's emergency/scope rules" ([[OWN-DEC-127]]).

Each service node (SVC-*) quotes its SOP's "Do not perform" list, the PROPOSED field wording of these limits, pending review by the qualified safety professional and the licensing reviewer.
