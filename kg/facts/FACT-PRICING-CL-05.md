## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)


#### 📈 Financial Benchmarks
* **Target Net Profit Margin:** 60% (Highly repetitive, fast setups).
* **Ideal Crew Size:** 1 - 2 Cleaners max.
* **Speed Requirement:** Must finish within the 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM turnover window.

> FLAG (X-19) - "Target Net Profit Margin" line: internal target, unapproved, with no cost basis. "Net" profit implies an overhead allocation that no file defines, the line does not say whether a margin or a markup is meant, and the rationale "Highly repetitive, fast setups" is unverified. It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; FIN-007; Q-28-13).

> FLAG (C-CL05-02) - "Speed Requirement" and "Ideal Crew Size" lines: one fixed turnover window conflicts with the per-property checkout and check-in times collected at intake (spec CL-05.INT) and with this file's "Luxury / Pool Property" row, whose cleaning time does not fit the window if it is crew elapsed time; the "Max Allowed Cleaning Time" column does not say whether it means elapsed time or person-hours (job-costing playbook). The agreement turns the window into a completion guarantee (its "Operational Timeline" clause; critical, attorney review). Time pressure is a card hazard (spec CL-05.HAZ), and a schedule never overrides stop-work (31 section 8.5; master section 12). OPEN QUESTION (owner; attorney for the guarantee): time-cap unit, same-day capacity of the two employees, and per-property windows.

#### 📐 Flat-Rate Turnover Fees (Passed down to the guest via app)

| Property Type                | Flat Turnover Fee (USD) | Max Allowed Cleaning Time |
| :--------------------------- | :---------------------- | :------------------------ |
| **Studio / 1 Bed Condo**     | $75 - $100              | 1.5 Hours                 |
| **2 Bed / 2 Bath Apartment** | $120 - $160             | 2.5 Hours                 |
| **3 Bed / 2.5 Bath House**   | $180 - $250             | 3.5 Hours                 |
| **Luxury / Pool Property**   | $300 - $500+            | 5+ Hours                  |

> FLAG (C-CL05-03) - "Studio / 1 Bed Condo" row (whole range) and "2 Bed / 2 Bath Apartment" row (low end): below the \$150 job minimum per turnover event, the unit in which the agreement prices turnovers. FIN-001 requires the estimate to apply the minimum or record an authorized exception (spec BIZ-003, CX-001). Whether the minimum is evaluated per turnover visit (PROPOSED default, 28 D-28-07), per billing period or by a plan-level exception is an owner decision (Q-28-04). Not a customer price until approved; no price is changed here.

> FLAG (28 section 3.13; Q-28-18) - heading "(Passed down to the guest via app)": not adopted (28 section 3.13). It implies a booking-platform integration and a pass-through of the fee to guests, neither of which is configured (master section 1.4: no claims about "integrations that are not configured"). HandyHub's customer under the agreement is the host, who is invoiced (its "Billing Cycle" clause). HUMAN REVIEW: attorney licensed in KY/IN, CPA.

> FLAG (C-CL05-12, C-CL05-V01) - the table does not define what a turnover includes. There is no laundry line, although the SOP starts on-site wash loads and the inventory keeps clean linens "stored in your car vehicle"; the laundry model, linen ownership and par levels are undecided (C-CL05-12). There is no kitchen standard, although the main plan sells "Deep cleaning kitchens and bathrooms" while the SOP's only kitchen cleaning step is the fridge (C-CL05-V01). The "Max Allowed Cleaning Time" values cannot be checked until the content is defined, and the pool and hot-tub areas implied by "Luxury / Pool Property" have no SOP step (OPEN QUESTION, 31 section 10). Owner decision.

#### 💼 Co-Hosting & Operational Management Add-ons
*If you act as the complete property manager, managing laundry lines, and dealing with emergency damage, choose one of these models:*

- [ ] **Option A (The Payout Percentage):** Charge a flat **10% to 15% of the total reservation revenue** generated by the Airbnb listing. (Excellent during peak tourist seasons).
- [ ] **Option B (The Logistics Retainer):** Keep the cleaning fee separate, and add a **$50 - $100 monthly retainer** per property to manage restocking inventory logistics (buying soaps, coffee pods using the owner's budget).

> FLAG (C-CL05-07) - "Co-Hosting & Operational Management Add-ons" heading, its introduction and "Option A (The Payout Percentage)": not adopted (28 section 3.13) and not offered until legal and licensing review. A share of the host's reservation revenue is not a line-item price (spec EST-002) and needs access to host revenue data; whether acting as a property manager or co-host triggers licensing or trust-account rules is a question for Kentucky and Indiana separately (30 section 8 row 24); "dealing with emergency damage" may mean repairs that need their own estimate or change order (spec FIN-008). No agreement clause, SOP step or spec-card field covers this scope. HUMAN REVIEW: attorney licensed in KY/IN, CPA (Q-28-18).

> FLAG (C-CL05-05, X-22) - "Option B (The Logistics Retainer)": buying restock items "using the owner's budget" conflicts with the agreement's "Inventory Logistics" clause, under which the client procures and stores restock items in a locked supply closet on site, and with the inventory file, which lists restock items as HandyHub stock (Q-29-13). Handling a host's funds is a CPA and attorney question, and host-supplied restock needs the START-009 record and the common acceptance rule that X-22 calls for. The retainer has no basis in the agreement (C-CL05-14), and a retainer month without turnovers has no defined treatment under the \$150 minimum (Q-28-04). Owner decision (Q-28-18).

#### 🚨 Surcharges
- [ ] **Late Checkout Delay Fine:** Charge **+$45 / hour** if guests stay past check-out time, causing your cleaning crew to sit wait and risking the next guest's check-in.
- [ ] **Same-Day Rush Turnover Booking:** +$35 flat.

> FLAG (C-CL05-04, X-16) - "Late Checkout Delay Fine" line: "Fine" is penalty wording; the application uses "fee" (28 D-28-09, PROPOSED). The agreement charges the same hourly amount as a "waiting fee" when entry is prevented past 11:15 AM, while this line starts at check-out time; the billing increment and the effect on the agreement's completion window are undefined, and cleaning agreements word such charges differently (X-16). Workers do not enter while guests are present (SOP section 2, PROPOSED). Fee schedule decision Q-28-07; HUMAN REVIEW: attorney licensed in KY/IN.

> FLAG (C-CL05-14) - "Same-Day Rush Turnover Booking" line: no basis in the agreement, which lists only per-layout turnover rates. OPEN QUESTION (owner; attorney): must the rush charge be a separately identifiable option accepted on the estimate (spec EST-005) or an approved fee-schedule item (Q-28-07)? A same-day booking adds to the time pressure the card lists (spec CL-05.HAZ) and depends on the same-day capacity of the two employees (C-CL05-02).


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Flat fee per turnover event by property type, with a "Max Allowed Cleaning Time" per type ("Flat-Rate Turnover Fees" table); co-hosting add-ons: a share of the host's reservation revenue ("Option A", not adopted, 28 section 3.13) or a monthly retainer per property for restock logistics ("Option B", a recurring-plan option, 28 section 3.12); an hourly late-checkout charge and a flat same-day rush charge ("Surcharges"). Registry estimating basis: property type or size with a time cap; the card's intake drivers (checkout and check-in times, host checklist, linens, amenities and restock levels, damage-report rule, access policy) are not priced (source: 28 section 7.4 row CL-05; 04 CL-05 "pricing model" and "estimating method"; spec CL-05.INT)
- Units: Turnover event by property-type band; month (retainer); hour (late checkout); share of reservation revenue (Option A); per booking (rush). No square metres (source: 28 section 7.4 row CL-05)
