Draft agreement clauses for [[SVC-DW-04]], kept as written in `Service Agreements/contract-new-construction.md` (vault status `template-draft`, `owner_approved: false`). They are not approved terms and are never shown to customers as terms. Every clause is legal text that needs review by an attorney licensed in Kentucky and/or Indiana ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]], [[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]). Customers approve the estimate terms v1.0 ([[OWN-DEC-127]]), drafted only from agreed policies; draft clauses outside those policies are listed for attorney review. Placeholders such as "[Your Company Name]" stay placeholders: the business name on estimates and invoices is decided separately ([[FACT-BUSINESS-NAME]]).

Each clause below keeps the vault wording. A "Governed now by" line names the decided policy that already covers the clause's topic; where a clause conflicts with it, the decided policy applies and the clause waits for attorney review.

## Clauses as written

This Master Subcontract Agreement is executed by and between **[Your Drywall Company Name]** ("Subcontractor") and **[General Contractor / Developer Name]** ("Contractor") for high-volume production drywall services.

#### 1. Production Milestones & Turnover Parameters
* **Scope Metrics:** Subcontractor will deliver a standardized Level 4 paint-ready finish across **[Insert Total Sq. Ft.]** at an agreed unit rate of **\$[Insert Rate] / sq. ft.**, totaling a contract value of **\$[Amount]**.
  - Governed now by: price: set per job on the estimate, [[FACT-PRICING-PER-JOB]].
* **Pre-requisite Trades Sign-off:** The Contractor warrants that all MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) rough-in inspections are fully passed and signed off by municipal building inspectors before the Subcontractor hangs a single sheet. Any board removal forced by late mechanical changes will be billed at an emergency rate of **\$75 / hour per worker plus materials**.
  - Governed now by: price: set per job on the estimate, [[FACT-PRICING-PER-JOB]]; no warranty or guarantee is part of the approved terms ([[OWN-DEC-127]]).

#### 2. Site Climate & Winterization Terms
* **Drying Temperature Clause:** Contractor must maintain an enclosed structure with active, safe heating units keeping the internal building temperature **above 50°F (10°C) continuously** for 48 hours before joint compound application and throughout the sanding phase (crucial for Kentuckiana winter builds).
* **Failure Penalty:** If joint flashing or freeze-cracking occurs due to Contractor’s failure to maintain building heat, all repair punch-list hours will be billed as a paid change order.
  - Governed now by: price: set per job on the estimate, [[FACT-PRICING-PER-JOB]]; payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]]; change orders: [[OWN-DEC-108]].

#### 3. Mechanical Tooling & Scaffolding Waiver
* **Glass/Window Scraping Waiver:** Subcontractor is not liable for structural window scratches if glass cleaning requires popping heavy construction plaster drops left by past framing stages, unless pre-existing glass scratches were documented.
  - Governed now by: liability, insurance and waiver wording: attorney and insurance-broker review ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]); no insurance or liability statement is made from this draft ([[RULE-NO-INVENTED-FACTS]]).
* **Standby Crew Fee:** If the Subcontractor's production line is locked out or delayed by delivery trucks, late framing adjustments, or un-cleared debris from other trades, a **\$45 / hour per worker standby penalty** will be actively invozed to the Contractor.
  - Governed now by: price: set per job on the estimate, [[FACT-PRICING-PER-JOB]].

#### 4. Commercial Billing & Net Draw Terms
* Invoices will be submitted upon completion of individual unit blocks.
  - Governed now by: payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]].
* Payments are strictly bound to **[Net 15 / Net 30]** schedules from invoice presentation. Late draws accumulate 1.5% monthly interest.
  - Governed now by: deposit: [[FACT-DEPOSIT-RULE]] ([[OWN-DEC-104]]); no forfeiture, refund or late-fee terms are invented; payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]].
