Draft agreement clauses for [[SVC-PT-01]], kept as written in `Service Agreements/contract-interior-painting.md` (vault status `template-draft`, `owner_approved: false`). They are not approved terms and are never shown to customers as terms. Every clause is legal text that needs review by an attorney licensed in Kentucky and/or Indiana ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]], [[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]). Customers approve the estimate terms v1.0 ([[OWN-DEC-127]]), drafted only from agreed policies; draft clauses outside those policies are listed for attorney review. Placeholders such as "[Your Company Name]" stay placeholders: the business name on estimates and invoices is decided separately ([[FACT-BUSINESS-NAME]]).

Each clause below keeps the vault wording. A "Governed now by" line names the decided policy that already covers the clause's topic; where a clause conflicts with it, the decided policy applies and the clause waits for attorney review.

## Clauses as written

tags: #legal/contract #painting/interior #operations

This Service Agreement is entered into by and between **[Your Painting Company Name]** (hereinafter "Company") and **[Client Full Name]** (hereinafter "Client") for professional interior painting services.

#### 1. Scope of Work & Color Selection
* **Inclusions:** Service strictly covers the surface preparation, priming, and application of a minimum of two (2) coats of paint to the designated interior areas as outlined in the `sop-interior-painting.md` micro-checklist.
  - Governed now by: minimums: none, [[FACT-NO-MINIMUM]] ([[OWN-DEC-102]]).
* **Color Confirmation Waiver:** Client is solely responsible for final color choices, sheens (e.g., eggshell, satin, semi-gloss), and brand selection. Once the Client signs off on a color swatch, any change of mind after paint application will be treated as a new project and billed as a paid change order.
  - Governed now by: payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]]; change orders: [[OWN-DEC-108]]; liability, insurance and waiver wording: attorney and insurance-broker review ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]); no insurance or liability statement is made from this draft ([[RULE-NO-INVENTED-FACTS]]).
* **Exclusions:** Moving heavy electronic home theater setups, pianos, or fine art collections must be handled by the Client prior to crew arrival.

#### 2. Pricing & Progress Draw Schedule
* **Total Project Fee:** The agreed total for the defined workspace is **\$[Amount]**.
  - Governed now by: price: set per job on the estimate, [[FACT-PRICING-PER-JOB]].
* **Payment Milestones:**
  - Governed now by: payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]].
	- **Draw 1 (Retainer):** 30% non-refundable deposit to secure the calendar date and procure paint materials.
  - Governed now by: deposit: [[FACT-DEPOSIT-RULE]] ([[OWN-DEC-104]]); no forfeiture, refund or late-fee terms are invented.
	- **Draw 2 (Prep & Prime):** 40% due upon completion of masking, drywall patching, and priming.
  - Governed now by: deposit: [[FACT-DEPOSIT-RULE]] ([[OWN-DEC-104]]); no forfeiture, refund or late-fee terms are invented; payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]].
	- **Draw 3 (Completion):** 30% due immediately upon final walkthrough inspection on the final day.
  - Governed now by: deposit: [[FACT-DEPOSIT-RULE]] ([[OWN-DEC-104]]); no forfeiture, refund or late-fee terms are invented; payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]].

#### 3. Lockout & Late Cancellation
* **24-Hour Notice:** Client must provide at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a session. Failure to do so, or failure to provide home entry when the crew arrives, will incur a flat **\$50 Late Cancellation/Lockout Fee** to cover crew downtime and I-65 bridge toll expenses.
  - Governed now by: price: set per job on the estimate, [[FACT-PRICING-PER-JOB]]; cancellation and rescheduling: customer requests confirmed by staff, no cancellation fee configured ([[OWN-DEC-107]]).
