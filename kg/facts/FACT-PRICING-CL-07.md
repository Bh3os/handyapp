## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)


#### 📈 Financial Benchmarks
* **Target Net Profit Margin:** 70% - 80% (Very high margins after specialized heavy equipment is paid off).
* **Ideal Crew Size:** 1 - 2 Technicians.
* **Estimated Time:** 2 to 4 hours per system.

> FLAG (X-19) - the three lines under "Financial Benchmarks" are unsourced, undated and unapproved (owner facts 2026-10-02: no rates, wages, production factors or profit targets supplied). "Target Net Profit Margin" is an internal target with no cost basis; a net margin would need an overhead allocation that no file defines, and the equipment ownership and cost behind "after specialized heavy equipment is paid off" are not established (28 section 8; Q-28-13). It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (spec FIN-007). "Ideal Crew Size" at two technicians would commit both initial employees (owner facts 2026-10-02: initially two employees), and whether "Estimated Time" is crew time or person-hours is undefined (job-costing playbook: "Distinguish ... crew elapsed time from person-hours"). Values unchanged. Added 2026-10-03.

#### 📐 Flat System Models vs. Variable Vent Models

##### System Model A: The Base + Vent Breakdown (Highly Profitable for Large Homes)
* **Step 1: Base Inspection & Trunk Connection Fee:** $150 flat *(550 SAR)*. Covers connecting negative air machine to furnace and testing system lines.
* **Step 2: Per-Vent Surcharge:** Add **+$25 per supply/return register grille** on the walls.
* *Calculation Example:* A home has 15 vents total.
  * *Base ($150) + (15 vents x $25 = $375) = **$525 Grand Total**.*

> FLAG (C-CL07-V03, C-CL07-07, C-CL07-V02) - Model A lines above. "Base Inspection & Trunk Connection Fee": the inspection deliverable is undefined. No SOP step inspects the system or tests "system lines"; the SOP's only test is the fan run after cleaning, and what is charged if work stops at an inspection (for example on suspected significant mold, spec CL-07.SAF) is an owner decision (C-CL07-V03; Q-28-05). "connecting negative air machine to furnace" is not an SOP step, and whether it is licensed HVAC work in the job's Kentucky or Indiana municipality is unverified; it is not performed until the licensing review decides (C-CL07-07; spec CL-07.SAF "scope requiring licensed HVAC work"; HUMAN REVIEW: licensing authority, licensed HVAC contractor). The SOP attaches the vacuum to the trunk line instead. "Per-Vent Surcharge" counts registers "on the walls" only, while the SOP removes grilles from walls and ceilings; which register locations count is an owner decision (C-CL07-V02). The calculation example is an illustration, not an approved quote. Values unchanged. Added 2026-10-03.

##### System Model B: All-In Flat Rate Structure
* **Single AC Zone / Single Furnace System (Up to 12 Vents):** $350 - $450 flat *(1,300 - 1,700 SAR)*.
* **Dual Zone Systems (Large homes with 2 separate AC units):** $700 - $900 flat *(2,600 - 3,400 SAR)*.

> FLAG (C-CL07-09) - Model A and Model B price the same system with no rule for which governs (Q-28-20). For a single system, Model A totals fall below Model B's flat floor when few registers are counted; Model B has no price for a single system above its "Up to 12 Vents" limit or for more than two zones. One governing method per job type is an owner decision; until then neither model is a customer price. Values unchanged. Added 2026-10-03.

#### ➕ Ancillary Add-on Services (زيادة المبيعات)
- [ ] **Dryer Vent Exhaust Line Cleaning:** +$90 - $150 flat (cleaning fire-hazardous lint out of lines leading to outside walls).
- [ ] **Commercial Range Exhaust Hood (Kitchens):** +$150 - $250 flat (heavy scraping of industrial caked grease out of fan paths).
- [ ] **Anti-Microbial Sanitize Fogging:** +$75 flat (fogging an eco-friendly disinfectant mist through the entire duct network after vacuuming to kill lingering mold spores).

> FLAG (C-CL07-03, C-CL07-04, C-CL07-05, C-CL07-01) - the three add-ons above are not sellable pending SOP, licensing and SDS review (28 PRC-042, PROPOSED). No SOP step covers dryer-vent lines (C-CL07-04) or fogging (C-CL07-03, critical: no product, label check, SDS, ventilation or occupant controls, and no fogging equipment; whether a product label permits duct use and whether paid application needs a state applicator credential are open, 30 section 8 row 14). "Commercial Range Exhaust Hood (Kitchens)" is outside the documented residential scope (C-CL07-05; X-23; Q-04-11). "fire-hazardous lint", "eco-friendly disinfectant mist" and "to kill lingering mold spores" are claims that are not carried into price items or customer text (28 PRC-041, PROPOSED); the spec stops work on suspected significant mold (spec CL-07.SAF; C-CL07-01). Whether an add-on may be booked alone is open; if it may, FIN-001 requires the \$150 minimum or a recorded authorized exception. HUMAN REVIEW: qualified safety professional; attorney licensed in KY/IN; state pesticide regulator (fogging product); local fire code official and insurance broker (commercial hoods). Prices unchanged. Added 2026-10-03.


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Two alternatives with no selection rule: "System Model A: The Base + Vent Breakdown" (a base fee plus a per-register surcharge) and "System Model B: All-In Flat Rate Structure" (flat per system or zone: a single AC zone or single furnace system "Up to 12 Vents", or a dual-zone system); plus three "Ancillary Add-on Services" priced flat (flagged in place) (source: 28 section 7.4 row CL-07; labels quoted)
- Units: Register (a "supply/return register grille" counted "on the walls") in Model A; system or zone in Model B; flat per add-on. GAP: no unit for ceiling or floor registers, for a single system above the "Up to 12 Vents" limit or for more than two zones (source: 28 section 7.4; C-CL07-V02; C-CL07-09)
