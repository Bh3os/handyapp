**Changed by [[OWN-DEC-161]], [[OWN-DEC-163]], [[OWN-DEC-195]] (2026-10-21):** the under-24-hours rule applies only to confirmed work visits; a deposit under $50.00 is kept and nothing more is invoiced; $50.00 or more keeps at most $50.00 and refunds the rest; with no deposit the $50.00 is invoiced only where legally permitted and disclosed and accepted in advance; never for legally protected cancellations or cancellations caused by HandyHub, unsafe conditions or emergencies; estimate visits are never charged; arrival alone is not "work started"; Office processes routine customer-authorized work-visit cancellations within policy, the Owner decides waivers, refunds, disputes and exceptions; enforcement waits for attorney approval ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]).

- Rescheduling: always free.
- Final cancellation, 24 hours or more before the visit, before work starts: full deposit refund, no charge.
- Final cancellation under 24 hours (including no access then cancelling): $50.00 (5000 cents) kept; rest of any deposit refunded; with no deposit the $50.00 is invoiced. Materials bought do not add to it.
- Cancellation after work started: work done plus materials.
- HandyHub cancels or moves a visit: customer chooses free reschedule or full refund.
- Refunds are owner-only. Customer wording pending attorney review ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]).

**Value:** `cancellation.rules` version 2 (migration 0115 of [[TSK-0140]]): notice 24 hours, late-cancellation amount 5000 cents as the cap of the fee, a smaller deposit kept only, the no-deposit invoice only where disclosed and accepted in advance, estimate visits never charged, the reschedule initiator required, fee enforcement waiting for the attorney. The repeated-cancellation review numbers are `cancellation.repeat_review`, NOT CONFIGURED ([[OQ-CANCELLATION-RULES-DETAILS]]).
