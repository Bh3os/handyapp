- Rescheduling: always free.
- Final cancellation, 24 hours or more before the visit, before work starts: full deposit refund, no charge.
- Final cancellation under 24 hours (including no access then cancelling): $50.00 (5000 cents) kept; rest of any deposit refunded; with no deposit the $50.00 is invoiced. Materials bought do not add to it.
- Cancellation after work started: work done plus materials.
- HandyHub cancels or moves a visit: customer chooses free reschedule or full refund.
- Refunds are owner-only. Customer wording pending attorney review ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]).
