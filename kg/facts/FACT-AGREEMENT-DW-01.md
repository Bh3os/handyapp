Draft agreement clauses for [[SVC-DW-01]], kept as written in `Service Agreements/contract-drywall-patching.md` (vault status `template-draft`, `owner_approved: false`). They are not approved terms and are never shown to customers as terms. Every clause is legal text that needs review by an attorney licensed in Kentucky and/or Indiana ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]], [[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]). Customers approve the estimate terms v1.0 ([[OWN-DEC-127]]), drafted only from agreed policies; draft clauses outside those policies are listed for attorney review. Placeholders such as "[Your Company Name]" stay placeholders: the business name on estimates and invoices is decided separately ([[FACT-BUSINESS-NAME]]).

Each clause below keeps the vault wording. A "Governed now by" line names the decided policy that already covers the clause's topic; where a clause conflicts with it, the decided policy applies and the clause waits for attorney review.

## Clauses as written

This Service Agreement is entered into by and between **[Your Drywall Company Name]** (hereinafter "Company") and **[Client Full Name]** (hereinafter "Client") for localized drywall repair and patching services.

#### 1. Scope of Work & Structural Limits
* **Inclusions:** Service strictly covers the extraction and restoration of the targeted damaged partition(s) as verified in the `sop-drywall-patching.md` micro-checklist.
* **Exclusions:** Structural load-bearing stud replacement, framing remediation for foundational shifting, and final aesthetic color-matching paint coats are excluded unless explicitly itemized as a paid add-on.
  - Governed now by: payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]].
* **Texture Disclaimer:** Company will execute expert texture matching (e.g., Orange Peel, Knockdown). Client acknowledges that due to natural aging, fading, and paint accumulation on older existing walls, a 100% molecular texture blend cannot be legally guaranteed, though seamless flat feathering will be achieved.
  - Governed now by: payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]]; no warranty or guarantee is part of the approved terms ([[OWN-DEC-127]]).

#### 2. Financial Terms & Minimums
* **Agreed Flat Fee:** The fixed total for the defined repair area is **\$[Amount]**.
  - Governed now by: price: set per job on the estimate, [[FACT-PRICING-PER-JOB]].
* **Trip Minimum Notice:** Client acknowledges the Company’s regional baseline trip minimum fee of **\$150 - \$250** is factored into this estimate.
  - Governed now by: minimums: none, [[FACT-NO-MINIMUM]] ([[OWN-DEC-102]]); price: set per job on the estimate, [[FACT-PRICING-PER-JOB]].
* **Payment Due:** Payment must be captured in full immediately upon final work walkthrough on the day of service completion.
  - Governed now by: payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]].

#### 3. Structural Access & Safety
* **Work Workspace:** Client agrees to clear all furniture, fragile decorations, and hanging art pieces at least 5 feet away from the target wall or ceiling zone before the technician arrives.
* **Lockout Policy:** If the technician arrives at the scheduled hour block and cannot gain entry, a flat **\$50 Lockout/Dry-Run Fee** will apply to cover labor hours and bridge toll expenses across the I-65 corridors.
  - Governed now by: price: set per job on the estimate, [[FACT-PRICING-PER-JOB]]; cancellation and rescheduling: customer requests confirmed by staff, no cancellation fee configured ([[OWN-DEC-107]]).
