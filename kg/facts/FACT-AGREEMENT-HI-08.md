## Clauses as written

This Service Agreement is executed between **[Your Company Name]** ("Company") and **[Client Full Name]** ("Client") for commercial or residential shelving installations.

#### 1. Weight Parameters & Anchoring Standards
* **Inclusions:** Layout design verification, laser horizontal track setting, and structural fastener loading as outlined in `sop-closet-shelving.md`.
* **Load Rating Disclaimer:** The Company utilizes premium toggle bolts and direct wood stud structural screws to maximize weight limits. However, the ultimate load tolerance is bound to the structural integrity of the Client's drywall. The Company is not liable for drywall fracturing if the Client grossly exceeds the manufactured load rating of the installed shelving kits.
  - Governed now by: liability, insurance and waiver wording: attorney and insurance-broker review ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]); no insurance or liability statement is made from this draft ([[RULE-NO-INVENTED-FACTS]]).

#### 2. Pre-Service Prep Requirements
* **Closet Clearance:** Client must completely clear all clothes, boxes, and personal items out of the closet enclosure before the technician arrives. A **\$30 delayed access fee** applies if the technician has to wait for space clearing.
