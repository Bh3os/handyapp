## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)


#### 📈 Financial Benchmarks
* **Target Net Profit Margin:** 55% - 65%
* **Ideal Crew Size:** 2 Cleaners
* **Condition:** Property must be 100% vacant of furniture and trash.

> FLAG (C-CL03-11, X-19) - "Target Net Profit Margin" line: internal target, unapproved, with no cost basis. "Net profit" would need an overhead allocation that no file defines, and whether a gross, contribution or net margin is meant is an owner and CPA decision (Q-28-13). It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (spec FIN-007; 28 section 8). The "Financial Benchmarks" heading names no source, date or approver.

#### 📐 Pricing Tiers & Calculation Methods

##### Method A: Square Footage Billing (Highly Recommended)
* **Standard Rate:** $0.22 - $0.35 per sq. ft. *(9 - 14 SAR per sqm)*.
* *Why:* Vacant spaces allow fast movement, but detail inside empty cabinets adds labor time.

> FLAG (C-CL03-13) - "Standard Rate" line: below-minimum for small units; reconcile with Method B - owner decision. Priced by area, a small unit totals less than the \$150 job minimum and less than the "Small Condo / Studio (Vacant)" package below, so the quote depends on which method the estimator picks. No rule selects the method, and "Highly Recommended" names no source (Q-28-20). A job total below the minimum receives the minimum adjustment or a recorded authorized exception (spec BIZ-003, FIN-001; 28 section 4). The agreement's "cleanable square footage" has no measurement method. The SAR and per-square-metre figures are legacy artifacts and are never converted or imported (28 PRC-039). Not a customer price until approved.

##### Method B: Flat Rate Packages
* **Small Condo / Studio (Vacant):** $250 - $350 *(1,000 - 1,300 SAR)*
* **Standard 3-Bedroom House (Vacant):** $450 - $600 *(1,700 - 2,250 SAR)*
* **Large Multi-Floor Family Home:** $650 - $900+ *(2,450 - 3,400 SAR)*

> FLAG (C-CL03-10, C-CL03-12) - package lines: the tiers have no size bounds, and no tier covers homes between a studio and three bedrooms (Q-28-20). The agreement prices by "cleanable square footage" only, so a package-priced job does not fit the agreement ("agreement lacks package option", C-CL03-10). The SAR amounts are legacy artifacts (C-CL03-12). Occupied move-outs are not priced in any CL-03 file (Q-04-05). Not a customer price until approved.

#### 🚨 Surcharges & Penalties (شروط جزائية هامة)
- [ ] **Furniture/Trash Leftover Penalty:** Add **+25% to +50% premium surcharge** if the crew arrives and the tenant has left heavy trash, furniture, or boxes that need discarding.
- [ ] **Utilities Off Fine:** +$50 surcharge if electricity or hot water is turned off, forcing the crew to use specialized battery equipment or slow work.
- [ ] **Interior Windows Included:** Standard packaging includes interior window glass, tracks, and frames. If exterior window glass is requested, add **+$10 per window**.

> FLAG (C-CL03-01, critical) - "Furniture/Trash Leftover Penalty" line: CONFLICT - surcharge basis unreconciled; owner + attorney decision. This line gives a percentage range, while the agreement's "Trash Surcharge" clause fixes and pre-discloses a single percentage, so an estimator following this file could bill more than the agreement allows. The charge is triggered by the crew's judgement on arrival and covers disposal scope (hauling, heavy lifting, disposal fees) that the customer has not approved. RECOMMENDATION (pending approval): leftover items are described on the estimate as a possible change and priced only through a change order the customer approves before the extra work, with photos (spec FIN-008, JOB-005, AT-032; 28 D-28-08). Authority to discard property left by a tenant is an open legal question (Q-30-17; HUMAN REVIEW: attorney licensed in KY/IN), and disposal items stay inactive until a waste and disposal policy exists (28 section 3.5; 02 G-157). No percentage is changed here.

> FLAG (C-CL03-09) - "Utilities Off Fine" line: trigger mismatch - owner decision. This line charges when "electricity or hot water is turned off"; the agreement's "Utility Fine" clause charges when "electricity or running water is disconnected during service execution", so a utility that is already off on arrival triggers the charge here but not under the agreement. The spec captures "utilities/water availability" at intake (CL-03.INT) and flags missing prerequisites before dispatch (SCH-001); a site that is unsafe or missing prerequisites is recorded as No Start / Blocked (SCH-008), and unsafe utilities are a stop-work condition (04 CL-03 "hazards"). The inventory lists headlamps and work lights but no other "specialized battery equipment". Whether any utilities fee exists, and its trigger and wording, are owner and attorney decisions (Q-28-07, Q-30-10). No amount is changed here.

> FLAG (C-CL03-05) - "Interior Windows Included" line, exterior window glass: NOT OPERATIONALLY SUPPORTED - do not sell until SOP/equipment/safety review. No SOP step, equipment or height control covers exterior glass; the SOP, the agreement and the main plan cover interior windows only, and no company height and access policy exists (02 G-005; X-12). OPEN QUESTION (owner): is exterior glass offered at all, at what maximum height and with which equipment? Price unchanged.

> FLAG (X-16) - the "Surcharges & Penalties" heading (its Arabic part means penalty clauses, 28 section 7.4) and the "Penalty" and "Fine" labels: penalty and fine framing is held for attorney review; the application uses "fee" and charges only items in the approved fee schedule (28 section 3.7, D-28-09). The agreement's waiting fee for access delays has no line in this file: fee not in price book - owner to confirm (C-CL03-10; Q-28-07, Q-30-10).


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Two methods with no selection rule: Method A per square foot ("Square Footage Billing (Highly Recommended)") and Method B flat vacant packages by home type ("Flat Rate Packages"). Plus a percentage leftover-items surcharge, a utilities-off surcharge and a per-window exterior-glass add-on (source: 28 section 7.4 row CL-03; labels "Method A", "Method B", "Surcharges & Penalties")
- Units: Square foot (with a legacy per-square-metre SAR rate); package by home type; window (source: 28 section 7.4 row CL-03)
