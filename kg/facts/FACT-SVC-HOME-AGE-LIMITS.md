Value: no paint-disturbing work in homes built before 1978 on HI-09, DW-01, DW-02, DW-03, PT-01, PT-02, PT-03 and PT-04; popcorn removal (DW-02) only in homes built in 1981 or later.

- [[OWN-DEC-66]]: "home-age limits (no paint-disturbing work in homes built before 1978 on HI-09, DW-01, DW-02, DW-03, PT-01, PT-02, PT-03, PT-04; popcorn removal only in homes built in 1981 or later on DW-02)".
- Owner, 2026-10-05: "Homes built before 1978 may have lead paint. Federal rules (EPA RRP) require a certified firm and renovator for painting, drywall or sanding work that disturbs more than about 6 sq ft inside. What do you want?" = "Decline those jobs for now"; "Popcorn ceilings installed before about 1981 may contain asbestos. Before removal, a lab test is needed. Who pays for it?" = "Decline older ceilings".
- Owner, 2026-10-05: "If the customer doesn't know the year the home was built, is paint-disturbing work / popcorn removal declined?" = "Customer looks it up first"; "Do you plan to get EPA lead-safe (RRP) certification so pre-1978 painting/drywall can open later?" = "No plans".

An unknown year is not treated as safe: the customer looks it up first ([[HH-SVC-03]]). These are screening limits, not legal determinations (HUMAN REVIEW: EPA RRP-certified renovator, accredited asbestos inspector). The DW-02 vault files still name 1980 ("before 1980" in the SOP's "Asbestos Verification" step, "prior to 1980" in the draft agreement); the owner's 1981 limit governs.
