## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)

#### 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Handyman Baseline Hourly Fallback:** $65 - $85 / hour
* **Trip Fee / Minimum Visit Charge:** $125 - $175 (Covers first 3 small pictures or 1 large mirror)

> FLAG (C-HI01-03, X-18): competing minimum - owner decision (Q-28-06). This visit charge is a second, service-level minimum beside the job minimum of spec BIZ-003, FIN-001 and CX-001, and its range starts below that minimum. Unapproved; do not quote. Added 2026-10-03; see the review notes at the end.

* **Target Margin:** 80%

> FLAG (C-HI01-07, X-19): internal target, unapproved, no cost basis; gross margin or markup basis undefined (Q-28-13). Must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; spec FIN-007). Added 2026-10-03.

#### 📐 Flat-Rate Matrix by Artwork Demographics

##### Standard Frames (Up to 16"x20" / Under 10 lbs on Drywall)
* **Rate:** $35 - $50 per single frame.
* *Bundle:* 5-Frame Gallery Wall Grid = $180 - $250 flat.

##### Oversized / Heavy Items (Large Mirrors, Heavy Canvases 20–50 lbs)
* **Rate:** $110 - $160 per item.
* *Note:* Includes heavy-duty zinc toggle bolts and dual-anchor laser balancing.

> FLAG (C-HI01-02): load claim - manufacturer ratings only. The weight band in the heading above and the "heavy-duty zinc toggle bolts" in the note describe a price band and hardware, not a rated capacity. An item's hardware capacity comes from the manufacturer's rating for that weight and substrate, recorded for the job; items above the SOP's own weight bands stop and escalate (SOP section 12). No rating is added here. Added 2026-10-03.

##### Masonry Premium (Mounting onto Raw Brick / Concrete Fireplaces)
* **Rate:** Add **+$50 flat surcharge** per item to cover hammer-drill bit wear and specialized sleeve anchors.


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Hybrid with no precedence rule: an hourly fallback; a visit charge that "Covers first 3 small pictures or 1 large mirror"; a flat rate per standard frame; the "5-Frame Gallery Wall Grid" bundle; a flat rate per oversized or heavy item; a per-item masonry surcharge. Three methods can price the same job (Q-28-20). A reading that prices one large mirror twice is not an open contradiction (02 section 4.8); the precedence rule is still open (source: 28 section 7.1 row HI-01; line labels "Handyman Baseline Hourly Fallback", "Trip Fee / Minimum Visit Charge", "Rate", "Bundle", "Masonry Premium"; 02 section 4.8)
- Units: Hour; visit; single frame (standard band "Up to 16"x20" / Under 10 lbs on Drywall"); bundle; item (oversized or heavy band); per-item surcharge. No price band between the standard and the heavy weight bands and none above the heavy band (GAP) (source: Headings under "Flat-Rate Matrix by Artwork Demographics"; 02 G-046)
