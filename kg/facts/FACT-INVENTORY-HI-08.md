Required tools, materials and PPE for [[SVC-HI-08]] and [[SOP-HI-08]]. The vault inventory is a draft checklist (`Required inventory/inventory-closet-shelving.md`, `owner_approved: false`): quantities, stock levels, vendors, reorder points and ratings are not defined, and brand or rating words are kept as written, not approved. A missing critical tool, material or PPE item blocks the affected tasks ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]).

## Inventory checklist as written (`Required inventory/inventory-closet-shelving.md`)

#### 🧳 A. Track Systems & Mounting Anchors (أدوات ومسامير التثبيت الثقيلة)
- [ ] **Heavy-Duty Steel Wall Toggle Bolts (3/16" x 3"):** Capable of holding high load margins on open hollow drywall.
- [ ] **Self-Drilling Zinc Drywall Wall Anchors:** Fast thread variants for intermediate wall divider shelves.
- [ ] **Structural Wood Screws (2.5-inch T25 Star Drive):** For directly hitting framing studs with zero anchor flex.
- [ ] **Pre-Cut Wire Shelving Tracks & Clothing Rod End-Caps:** Standard sizes stocked inside the van rig.

#### 🛠️ B. Levelling & Fastening Hardware (أدوات الموازنة والقطع)
- [ ] **360-Degree Self-Leveling Green Laser Level Line:** Projects exact horizontal anchor paths across multi-wall configurations.
- [ ] **Cordless Professional Hacksaw / Portaband Saw:** For clean cutting of metal wire shelving grids without jagged burrs.
- [ ] **Heavy-Duty Rubber Mallet:** For snapping vertical standard brackets into top track locks without marring.
- [ ] **Magnetic Stud Finder with Center Target Laser:** Isolates wood vs. metal stud backings.

Proposed additions in the vault file (from the SOP and spec cross-check; pending owner and safety review, nothing approved for purchase or use): PPE for drilling, cutting and handling wire shelving and metal track, lifting and ladder work, including eye protection (02 C-HI08-02) and hand protection where sharp edges are handled (29 section 5.2 ppe-base) - selection by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); `vehicle-safety` kit reference: first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, eyewash and spill kit as review items; contents by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); SDS register field for chemical products and batteries (PROPOSED); Scanner able to locate studs and utilities in the drilling and fastening path, used per its manufacturer instructions (PROPOSED - model chosen by owner and lead installer); Drill/driver and a 1/2-inch drill bit (PROPOSED - bit size to be confirmed against the toggle manufacturer's instructions); Impact driver (PROPOSED - tightening per the anchor manufacturer's instructions (C-HI08-03)); Driver bit matching the listed structural wood screws (PROPOSED); Vertical shelf standards, wire shelf brackets, shelves and rods per the system manufacturer, where HandyHub supplies the system (PROPOSED - supply policy Q-29-14); Manufacturer instructions and load data (rating, spacing) for the listed anchors and screws and for each kit installed (PROPOSED); Measuring tools, including a means to check level and square (PROPOSED); Hand tools per the core-handyman kit (PROPOSED - kit contents Q-29-05); Ladder or step stool - per approved height/access policy (none exists, 02 G-005) (PROPOSED); Vacuum and cleanup supplies (PROPOSED); Trim/patch materials, only if included in the approved scope (PROPOSED - any chemical product needs a reviewed SDS first (29 section 8.3)); Lumber or wooden shelves and a wood-cutting saw, only if wood shelving is in scope (PROPOSED - only after the owner's scope decision).

## SOP section 7, "Tools and equipment" (`Standard Of Operations/sop-closet-shelving.md`)

- Laser or level: the listed green laser level, used in "Laser Level Baseline" (source: spec HI-08.TLS "laser/level"; inventory file).
- Stud finder and scanner: "Stud Mapping" names a "deep-scanning sensor" while the inventory lists a "Magnetic Stud Finder with Center Target Laser" (C-HI08-10), described only as isolating "wood vs. metal stud backings"; no file shows that it locates wiring or pipes. The hidden-service check needs a scanner able to locate studs and utilities, used per its manufacturer instructions (source: spec HI-08.TLS "stud finder", HI-08.SAF "locate studs/utilities"; inventory file, proposed additions).
- Drill/driver and bits, including the 1/2-inch bit named in "Top Track Drilling"; not yet in the inventory (source: spec HI-08.TLS "drill/driver, bits"; section 12).
- Impact driver, named in "Toggle Bolt Fastening"; not yet in the inventory (source: section 12).
- A driver bit matching the listed structural wood screws (source: inventory file; spec HI-08.TLS "bits").
- Saw only if authorized: the listed cordless hacksaw or portaband saw; no current step uses it (source: spec HI-08.TLS "saw if authorized"; inventory file).
- Measuring and hand tools, including a means to check level and square, and the listed rubber mallet (source: spec HI-08.TLS "measuring tools, hand tools"; section 12 "verify perfect square alignment").
- Ladder or step stool only under the approved height and access policy, which does not exist yet (source: spec HI-08.HAZ "ladder"; 02 G-005; X-12).
- Vacuum and cleanup supplies (source: spec HI-08.QAC "area clean", QA-002).
- Charged batteries and pre-use checks per each tool manufacturer (source: spec BRF-009 "charged-battery/consumable checks", START-005).

## SOP section 8, "Materials and consumables" (`Standard Of Operations/sop-closet-shelving.md`)

- Track, brackets, shelves and rods of the approved system, supplied by the customer (client kit) or by HandyHub as the accepted estimate states (source: spec HI-08.MAT "track/brackets/shelves/rods"; BRF-010 "who supplies them"; pricing file).
- Rated fasteners and anchors: toggle anchors for Option A and structural wood screws for Option B, as named in section 12 and listed in the inventory without manufacturer rating data; use them only within the manufacturer's rating and spacing recorded for the job (source: spec HI-08.MAT "rated fasteners, anchors", HI-08.SAF; C-HI08-03).
- The inventory also lists "Self-Drilling Zinc Drywall Wall Anchors", which no step uses; their rating is not stated (source: inventory file).
- Trim/patch materials only if included in the approved scope; a chemical product without a reviewed SDS is not carried to a job (source: spec HI-08.MAT; 29 section 8.3).
- Substitutions need approval according to policy (source: spec JOB-007).
