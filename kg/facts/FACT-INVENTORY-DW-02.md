## Inventory checklist as written (`Required inventory/inventory-popcorn-removal.md`)

tags: #inventory/tools #inventory/drywall #service/popcorn-removal

Optimized for 2-cleaner heavy containment scraping and acoustic texture spraying operations.

#### 🧴 A. Heavy Chemical & Slurry Softening Arsenal
- [ ] **Surfactant Softener Base:** Industrial liquid wetting agent or liquid dish soap packs to break surface tension in old acoustic sprays.
- [ ] **All-Purpose Ready-Mix Mud:** 3 buckets of lightweight joint compound (green lid) pre-thinned for Level 5 skim coating passes.
- [ ] **Pneumatic Wall Texture Concentrates:** 2 bags of dry texture compound specifically formulated for Orange Peel, Knockdown, or Skip-Trowel patterns.

#### 🧳 B. Scraping & Containment Machinery (معدات الكشط والعزل)
- [ ] **Industrial Garden Pump Sprayer:** 2-gallon heavy-duty sprayer with brass wand extension to mist ceilings fluidly.
- [ ] **Ceiling Scraper Pole System:** 12-inch wide ceiling scraping blades mounted on 6-to-12 foot extendable fiberglass poles (keeps workers off ladders during bulk scraping).
- [ ] **Heavy Poly Sheeting Containment (Bulk Supply):**
	- [ ] **Floor Protection:** 2 rolls of 6-mil thick 20'x100' black heavy-duty plastic sheeting.
	- [ ] **Wall Curtains:** 3 rolls of 3-mil thick 10'x100' clear plastic sheeting.
- [ ] **Professional Masking Tape (Bulk):** 1 box of 2-inch high-adhesion blue painters tape to hold heavy plastic curtains on walls securely without pulling paint later.

#### 🌀 C. Skimming, Sanding, & Texture Spraying Tools
- [ ] **Ergonomic Skimming Blades:** 18-inch and 24-inch wide stainless steel skimming blades with pole adapter attachments (for flawless Level 5 finishes).
- [ ] **Pneumatic Texture Hopper Gun Kit:** Heavy-duty hopper gun with 3 nozzle sizes (small, medium, large) linked to a portable air compressor running at 4-6 CFM @ 40-90 PSI.
- [ ] **Drywall Pole Sander (Dustless System):** Telescopic wall and ceiling sander connected directly to a high-capacity Class M HEPA Dust Extraction Vacuum.

## SOP section 7, "Tools and equipment" (`Standard Of Operations/sop popcorn-removal.md`)

- Containment system: plastic sheeting and painters tape ("Heavy Poly Sheeting Containment", "Professional Masking Tape") (source: spec DW-02.TLS "containment system"; inventory file; original "HVAC Isolation" and "Total Masking" steps).
- Sprayer: "Industrial Garden Pump Sprayer" (source: spec DW-02.TLS "sprayer as appropriate"; inventory file; original "Misting Protocol" step).
- Scraper: "Ceiling Scraper Pole System"; the step allows "a 10-inch or 12-inch floor scraper or specialty ceiling blade mounted on an extension pole" (source: spec DW-02.TLS "scraper"; inventory file).
- Skimming tools: "Ergonomic Skimming Blades" with pole adapters; the large trowel, wide taping knife and mixing drill that the steps name are not stocked (source: original Phase 4 steps; inventory file; C-DW02-12).
- Sanding: "Drywall Pole Sander (Dustless System)" with a HEPA dust-extraction vacuum; the dust class needed for this work is set by the safety reviewer (source: spec DW-02.TLS "HEPA vacuum, sanding tools"; inventory file; 29 section 5.2).
- Inspection light for "Exposing Defects" and "check with light": not stocked (source: original steps; C-DW02-12).
- Texture spraying: "Pneumatic Texture Hopper Gun Kit" with an air compressor, set up and operated only per their manufacturers' instructions; the inventory's settings are unapproved values (source: inventory file; 29 section 2.3). The rubber wipe blade that "Knockdown Execution" uses is not stocked.
- Phase 3 repair tools (a driver for drywall screws, a nail set): not stocked (source: original "Fastener Tightening" step; C-DW02-12).
- Access equipment: none stocked beyond the scraper poles; used only under an approved height and access policy, which does not exist yet (02 G-005; X-12); pending safety review (source: spec DW-02.TLS "access equipment"; pricing "High Ceiling Surcharge").
- Fixture protection and de-energizing aids: none approved; method pending qualified safety review and a licensed electrician (source: spec DW-02.SAF; C-DW02-04).

## SOP section 8, "Materials and consumables" (`Standard Of Operations/sop popcorn-removal.md`)

- Plastic and containment: floor sheeting and wall curtains as the "Total Masking" step describes (source: spec DW-02.MAT "plastic/containment"; inventory file).
- Wetting: liquid dish soap in water as the "Misting Protocol" step describes; the mixing quantity is flagged UNSOURCED in section 12. The inventory's "Industrial liquid wetting agent" option has no step (source: original step; inventory file; 29 section 8.2 control 8).
- Joint compound: lightweight ready-mix for skim coating (source: spec DW-02.MAT "joint compound"; inventory file; original Phase 4 steps). The setting compound that "Seam Re-Tape" uses is not stocked (source: original step; C-DW02-12).
- Fiberglass mesh tape and drywall screws for Phase 3: not stocked (source: original Phase 3 steps; C-DW02-12).
- Texture materials: texture compound for Orange Peel or Knockdown (source: spec DW-02.MAT "texture materials"; inventory file; original "Texture Spray Application" step).
- Mesh sanding paper: not stocked (source: original "Dustless Sanding" step).
- Primer: required by the card and by "The Level 5 Choice" step; not stocked (source: spec DW-02.MAT "primer"; C-DW02-12).
- Disposal bags: required by the card; not stocked (source: spec DW-02.MAT "disposal bags").
- Substitutions that affect specification, safety, cost or customer expectation need approval (source: spec JOB-007).
- Every chemical product (wetting agent, compounds, texture, primer) needs a reviewed SDS before it is carried to a job (source: 29 section 8.3, PROPOSED; 02 G-008).
