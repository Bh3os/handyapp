## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)

#### 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Average Cleaning Time:** 1.5 to 3 hours based on grill size.
* **Chemical Surcharge:** ~$20 per job for commercial carbon stripping agents.
* **Target Margin:** 70%

#### 📐 Flat-Rate Matrix by Grill Layout

##### Standard 2-3 Burner Grill (e.g., Weber Spirit / Genesis Series)
* **Price Range:** $175 - $225 flat.
* *Inclusions:* Full soak, cookbox scrape, burner purge, and exterior stainless steel polish.

##### Large 4-6 Burner Premium Grill (e.g., Built-in Island Grills / Large Smoker Units)
* **Price Range:** $250 - $350 flat.
*  *Inclusions:* Full soak, cookbox scrape, burner purge, and exterior stainless steel polish.

##### Extremely Neglected Commercial Restorations
* **Rate:** Switch to a flat **$75 / hour rate** (Minimum 3 hours) if the grill has heavy grease fire risk crust layers that require absolute chemical asset stripping.
