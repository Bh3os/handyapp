## Clauses as written

This Agreement governs the relationship between **[Your Painting Company Name]** ("Company") and **[Client Full Name]** ("Client") for wallpaper stripping and drywall restoration.

#### 1. Scope of Restoration & Hidden Conditions
* **The Past Paste Unknown Clause:** Because vintage wallpapers conceal the exact condition of the underlying wall sheets, the Company cannot determine if the original paper was applied directly to unprimed raw drywall paper.
* **Damage Disclaimer:** The Company utilizes professional enzymes and steaming machinery to dissolve glue safely. If the paper was originally installed improperly by past trades over raw unprimed drywall, minor paper tearing of the sheet core may occur during removal.
* **Inclusions:** Contract includes paper stripping, glue cleansing, and a Level 4 skim coat mud repair as outlined in `sop-wallpaper-removal.md`. Extensive structural drywall reconstruction required by hidden black mold behind old paper will be treated as a paid change order.
  - Governed now by: payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]]; change orders: [[OWN-DEC-108]].

#### 2. Utility & Environmental Constraints
* **Active Electrical Isolation:** Client grants permission for the Company to turn off wall outlet circuit breakers in the active workspace to prevent liquid shorts. Client must ensure the home has active power lines on separate circuits or a reachable junction box for steaming equipment.
* **Running Hot Water:** Property must have running water accessible on-site for chemical mixture setups.

#### 3. Pricing & Payments
* **Total Quoted Value:** **\$[Amount]** based on a flat room count or total wall square footage.
  - Governed now by: price: set per job on the estimate, [[FACT-PRICING-PER-JOB]].
* **Payment Due:** Final balance is due instantly upon the final sanding and dry-wiping phase, before any new interior primer or paint coatings are applied under separate agreements.
  - Governed now by: payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]].
