Required tools, materials and PPE for [[SVC-HI-01]] and [[SOP-HI-01]]. The vault inventory is a draft checklist (`Required inventory/inventory-picture-hanging.md`, `owner_approved: false`): quantities, stock levels, vendors, reorder points and ratings are not defined, and brand or rating words are kept as written, not approved. A missing critical tool, material or PPE item blocks the affected tasks ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]).

## Inventory checklist as written (`Required inventory/inventory-picture-hanging.md`)

#### 🧳 A. Precision Hardware & Fasteners (المسامير وأدوات التثبيت)
- [ ] **Heavy-Duty Zinc Self-Drilling Toggle Anchors:** Rated for 50–100 lbs on hollow drywall.
- [ ] **Standard Plastic Expansion Sleeves & Anchor Assortment:** For light to medium frames.
- [ ] **Monkey Hooks / Gorilla Hooks Steel Wire Assortment:** For tool-free drywall hanging under 10 lbs.
- [ ] **Heavy-Duty Galvanized Masonry Screws (Tapcon style):** For secure brick/concrete mounting.
- [ ] **Adhesive Clear Rubber Bumper Pads:** Placed on frame back corners to prevent wall scuffing.

#### 🛠️ B. Alignment & Mechanical Tools (أدوات القياس والثقب)
- [ ] **Self-Leveling Cross-Line Green Laser Level:** With a heavy-duty tripod or telescoping pole mount.
- [ ] **20V Cordless Brushless Hammer Drill & Impact Driver Combo:** For fast substrate boring.
- [ ] **Carbide-Tipped Masonry Drill Bits:** 3/16-inch and 1/4-inch bits specifically for brick fireplace mantels.
- [ ] **Deep-Sensing Stud Finder:** Capable of reading center columns through thick sheetrock and plaster.
- [ ] **Magnetic Pocket Bubble Levels & 24-inch Box Level:** For physical cross-checking.

Proposed additions in the vault file (from the SOP and spec cross-check; pending owner and safety review, nothing approved for purchase or use): PPE for drilling, hammer drilling into brick or concrete, and heavy-item handling - selection by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); `vehicle-safety` kit reference: first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, eyewash, spill kit as review items; contents by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); SDS register field for every chemical product or battery on this list (the drill battery; the bumper-pad adhesive if the reviewer classes it) (PROPOSED); Tape measure (PROPOSED); Hanging scale (PROPOSED); Wood or other non-masonry pilot drill bits (PROPOSED); Driver bits (PROPOSED); Rubber mallet (PROPOSED); Hanger hooks (PROPOSED); Ladder or step stool - per approved height/access policy (none exists, 02 G-005) (PROPOSED); Drop cloth and wall protection (PROPOSED); Dust collection or vacuum - dust class by safety reviewer (PROPOSED); Patching and touch-up materials - only if touch-up is in the approved scope (PROPOSED - conditional); Masonry anchor sleeves of the type the SOP names ("plastic or lead expansion masonry anchor sleeve") (FLAG - pending safety review; not proposed for purchase); Customer-supplied hardware and artwork - job record only, never stock (source `customer_provided`) (PROPOSED).

## Service-card defaults (spec section 17, HI-01)

- Tools and equipment (HI-01.TLS): stud finder/scanner, laser level, tape, drill/driver, bits, anchors/fasteners, ladder as needed, hand tools, dust protection.
- Materials and consumables (HI-01.MAT): rated anchors/fasteners, bumpers, patching touch-up materials if included.

## SOP section 7, "Tools and equipment" (`Standard Of Operations/sop-picture-hanging.md`)

- Stud finder or scanner: stocked ("Deep-Sensing Stud Finder"); used for the hidden-service check before any hole, per its manufacturer instructions; whether it detects wiring or pipes is not stated (source: spec HI-01.TLS; inventory file; C-HI01-01).
- Laser level: stocked ("Self-Leveling Cross-Line Green Laser Level") (source: spec HI-01.TLS; inventory file; section 12 "Laser Level Setup").
- Tape: not stocked; proposed in the inventory review notes (source: spec HI-01.TLS "tape").
- Drill/driver and bits: the hammer drill and impact driver combo and carbide masonry bits are stocked; non-masonry pilot bits and driver bits are not (source: spec HI-01.TLS; inventory file; C-HI01-11).
- Scale for "Weight Audit" and rubber mallet for "Sleeve Setting": not stocked (source: section 12; C-HI01-11).
- Levels for cross-checking: pocket and box levels are stocked and are used by the level check in section 13 (source: inventory file "For physical cross-checking"; spec HI-01.QAC).
- Ladder as needed: not stocked; only under an approved height/access policy, none exists (source: spec HI-01.TLS; 02 G-005; X-12).
- Hand tools: not itemized (source: spec HI-01.TLS "hand tools").
- Dust protection: not stocked; dust collection or vacuum and dust class per the safety reviewer (source: spec HI-01.TLS; 29 section 5.2 `dust-control` kit).
- Inspect each tool before use per its manufacturer instructions (source: 31 section 3.1 row 7).

## SOP section 8, "Materials and consumables" (`Standard Of Operations/sop-picture-hanging.md`)

- Rated anchors and fasteners matched to the item weight and the substrate from the hardware manufacturer's rating; no rating is assumed (source: spec HI-01.MAT "rated anchors/fasteners", HI-01.SAF "confirm fastener capacity"; risk-triage playbook). Stocked: zinc self-drilling toggle anchors (their rating in the inventory file is unverified, C-HI01-02), plastic expansion sleeves, monkey or gorilla hooks, galvanized Tapcon-style masonry screws (source: inventory file).
- Bumpers: rubber bumper pads are stocked (source: spec HI-01.MAT "bumpers"; section 12 "Bumper Placement").
- Masonry anchor sleeves of the type named in "Sleeve Setting" (plastic or lead): the stocked plastic sleeves are not stated as masonry-rated, lead sleeves are not stocked, and lead-containing hardware is a hazardous-material handling item, not a lead-paint item (source: section 12; inventory file; 31 section 10; C-HI01-11).
- Hanger hooks for "Bracket Fastening": not stocked (source: section 12; C-HI01-11).
- Patching and touch-up materials only if the approved scope includes them (source: spec HI-01.MAT).
- Customer-supplied hardware: record condition, model and quantity before installation and check its rating against the manufacturer instructions (source: spec HI-01.INT, START-009; 29 section 9.1; X-22).
- Substitutions need approval according to policy (source: spec JOB-007).
