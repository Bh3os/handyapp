Required tools, materials and PPE for [[SVC-CL-04]] and [[SOP-CL-04]]. The vault inventory is a draft checklist (`Required inventory/inventory-post-construction.md`, `owner_approved: false`): quantities, stock levels, vendors, reorder points and ratings are not defined, and brand or rating words are kept as written, not approved. A missing critical tool, material or PPE item blocks the affected tasks ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]).

## Inventory checklist as written (`Required inventory/inventory-post-construction.md`)

#### 🧴 A. The Chemical Arsenal (المنظفات الكيميائية)
- [ ] **Phosphoric Acid Cleaners (Muriatic-free):** For removing heavy cement film, plaster drops, and grout haze from tiles.

> FLAG (C-CL04-02, critical): CHEMICAL - SDS + REVIEWED SOP STEP REQUIRED; NOT ACTIVE. No SOP step uses the product in the line above, and it is not used until a reviewed SOP step written from its SDS and label exists; nobody mixes or dilutes it except as that label states. The agreement's "Chemical Use" clause, which authorizes acid treatment of grout lines, is under attorney and safety review; whether acid grout-haze removal is offered at all is an open owner decision (Q-31-08). A chemical product without a reviewed SDS is not carried to a job, and until segregation groups exist this acid is not packed in the same caddy or container as the CL-02 chlorine-based product; that separates products and declares no pair compatible (29 section 8.3, PROPOSED). HUMAN REVIEW: qualified safety professional. Item kept as listed. (source: 02 C-CL04-02; 29 sections 8.1 to 8.3)

- [ ] **Industrial-Grade Adhesive & Silicone Remover:** To break down window caulking and glue film lines.
- [ ] **Dust-Binding Mop Solution:** Anti-static chemical additive mixed with water to prevent drywall dust from floating back into the air.

#### 🧳 B. Hardware & Essential Tools (الأدوات والمعدات)
- [ ] **Industrial HEPA Wet/Dry Vacuum (Class M):** Specifically rated to trap ultra-fine silica and drywall dust without burning the motor.
- [ ] **Professional Window Squeegees & Scrapers (4-inch):** With sharp carbon steel replacement blades to pop off paint droplets from glass at a 45-degree angle.
- [ ] **Floor Scraper Tools:** For removing drywall drops stuck to bare subfloors before flooring installation.
- [ ] **Fine Dust Sweeping Compound:** Wax/oil-based grit sprinkled on floors before sweeping to lock fine dust down.
- [ ] **Microfiber Duster Sleeves (Bulk Supply):** Drywall dust destroys cloths instantly; you will need at least 20-30 cloths per job.

#### 🛡️ C. PPE & Safety (أدوات الحماية)
- [ ] **NIOSH-Approved N95 / P100 Dust Respirator Masks:** Mandatory to protect workers' lungs from airborne silica dust particles.

> FLAG (C-CL04-03, critical): the line above stays mandatory. The SOP's dry dust pull and sweeping steps carry a "DUST-CONTROL METHOD PENDING SAFETY REVIEW" hold; respirator type, selection, fit testing, medical evaluation and the respiratory program, and whether silica exposure applies, are decided by the qualified safety professional (OSHA applicability not concluded here). No field SOP is active until competent review (owner facts 2026-10-02). (source: spec CL-04.TLS "PPE/respiratory protection as plan requires"; CL-04.SAF "respiratory/dust control"; 29 section 7.2 item 1; 31 section 10 CL-04)

## Service-card defaults (spec section 17, CL-04)

- Tools and equipment (CL-04.TLS): HEPA vacuum, microfiber systems, scrapers appropriate to surface, floor tools, PPE/respiratory protection as plan requires.
- Materials and consumables (CL-04.MAT): cleaners, bags, filters, cloths, surface-safe residue removers.

## SOP section 6, "PPE" (`Standard Of Operations/sop Post-Construction Cleaning Checklist.md`)

TBD - pending safety review. No PPE type, class or respirator is selected here (source: 31 section 3.5 rule 6; risk-triage playbook "do not invent respirator selection, fit-test clearance").

- Respiratory protection: required by the card ("PPE/respiratory protection as plan requires"). The inventory lists "NIOSH-Approved N95 / P100 Dust Respirator Masks" as "Mandatory to protect workers' lungs from airborne silica dust particles."; that statement is not weakened here. Type, selection, fit testing, medical evaluation and the respiratory program are decided by the qualified safety professional (source: spec CL-04.TLS; inventory file; C-CL04-03; 31 section 10).
- Eye protection: "ANSI-Approved Safety Glasses" are listed for overhead vacuuming; selection pending qualified safety review (source: inventory file; spec CL-04.TLS "PPE").
- Hand, eye and face protection for chemical steps, and cut protection for blades and sharp debris: not stocked; selection by the qualified safety professional (source: spec CL-04.HAZ "sharp debris", "chemicals"; 29 section 7.3 row CL-04).
- Footwear for construction debris and wet floors: pending qualified safety review (source: spec CL-04.HAZ "sharp debris"; original step "Double Floor Clean").
- PPE is the last layer after elimination, substitution, engineering and administrative controls (source: safety-liability-manager "Prefer the hierarchy of controls: eliminate, substitute, engineer, administratively control, then PPE.").
- Suspect asbestos or lead debris is not a PPE matter: the work stops (source: 31 section 7.2 ranks 1 and 2; 29 section 8.1).

## SOP section 7, "Tools and equipment" (`Standard Of Operations/sop Post-Construction Cleaning Checklist.md`)

- HEPA vacuum: "Industrial HEPA Wet/Dry Vacuum (Class M)"; its rating claims are verified against manufacturer data, and the dust class needed for this work is set by the safety reviewer (source: spec CL-04.TLS "HEPA vacuum"; inventory file; 29 section 5.2).
- Microfiber systems: "Microfiber Duster Sleeves"; the microfiber flat mop that "Wall Damp-Wipe" uses is not stocked (source: spec CL-04.TLS "microfiber systems"; inventory file; C-CL04-12).
- Scrapers appropriate to surface: "Professional Window Squeegees & Scrapers (4-inch)" with replacement blades; blade scraping only after the glass inspection hold in section 12, and only by a method the glass and window manufacturers' instructions allow (source: spec CL-04.TLS "scrapers appropriate to surface"; inventory file; spec section 17 "Service-card defaults never override a site-specific hazard, manufacturer instruction, permit/licensing requirement, or approved change order").
- Floor tools: "Floor Scraper Tools" are stocked, but no step uses them (floor scraping is not covered); the floor mop and bucket and the broom and dustpan that the steps need are not stocked (source: spec CL-04.TLS "floor tools"; inventory file; C-CL04-12).
- Access equipment: none stocked; used only under an approved height and access policy, which does not exist yet; the pricing file names ladders or rolling scaffolding for work "Above 12 ft" (source: spec CL-04.HAZ "ladders"; 02 G-005; C-CL04-01; X-12).
- Power: corded equipment on temporary or permanent site power; power safety pending qualified review (source: spec CL-04.SAF "verify power/water safety"; agreement "On-Site Safety"; 29 section 7.3 row CL-04).
- Photo device for the required photos: not listed (source: spec START-008, QA-003; 29 section 7.4 row CL-04).
- Required, optional and proposed items are listed in the inventory.

## SOP section 8, "Materials and consumables" (`Standard Of Operations/sop Post-Construction Cleaning Checklist.md`)

- Cleaners: general-purpose and glass cleaners are not stocked (source: spec CL-04.MAT "cleaners"; C-CL04-12).
- Bags: trash or contractor bags are not stocked (source: spec CL-04.MAT "bags"; C-CL04-12).
- Filters: replacement HEPA filters and vacuum bags are not stocked (source: spec CL-04.MAT "filters"; C-CL04-12).
- Cloths: "Microfiber Duster Sleeves (Bulk Supply)"; the inventory's count per job is not an approved quantity (source: spec CL-04.MAT "cloths"; 29 section 2.1).
- Surface-safe residue removers: "Industrial-Grade Adhesive & Silicone Remover" (inventory) and "an adhesive remover (like Goo Gone)" in the "Adhesive Removal" step; a product is used only if it is on the approved list with its SDS, and the brand in the step is a quotation of the original (source: spec CL-04.MAT "surface-safe residue removers"; 31 section 3.5 rule 8). Surface compatibility follows the product label and the finish manufacturer (source: spec CL-04.QAC "residues treated without damage").
- Dust-binding product: "Dust-Binding Mop Solution" for the "Double Floor Clean" rinse, mixed only as the product label states (source: inventory file; 29 section 8.2 control 8).
- Sweeping compound: stocked "to lock fine dust down", but the "Bulk Debris" step sweeps without it; its use is part of the dust-control decision (source: inventory file; C-CL04-03).
- Metal polish: not stocked; a surface-specific product per the fixture manufacturer's care instructions (source: original step "Metal Polish"; C-CL04-12).
- Phosphoric acid cleaner: stocked, but no step uses it, and it is not used under this SOP until an SDS-based reviewed step exists (source: inventory file; C-CL04-02).
- Every chemical product needs a reviewed SDS before it is carried to a job (source: 29 section 8.3, PROPOSED; 02 G-008). Substitutions that affect specification, safety, cost or customer expectation need approval (source: spec JOB-007).
