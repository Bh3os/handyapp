## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)

tags: #finance/pricing #painting/interior #market/louisville

#### 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Average Labor Rate:** $45 - $65 / hour per painter.
* **Materials Surcharge:** Standard practice is to mark up retail paint costs by 15% to 20% or pass down your contractor discount (Sherwin-Williams/PPG) with a minor logistics handling fee.
* **Target Margin:** 65% - 75%

> FLAG (C-PT01-09; X-19): the three benchmark lines above are unsourced and unapproved (owner facts 2026-10-02: no rates, wages, production factors or profit targets supplied). "Average Labor Rate" does not say whether it is a customer billing rate or a wage or cost rate (Q-28-02). "Materials Surcharge" is an internal, unapproved material policy: the "Standard practice" statement is unverified, the handling fee has no amount, and a brand name is not a vendor relationship (Q-28-14, Q-29-03). "Target Margin" is an internal, unapproved target with no cost basis and no stated margin basis; it must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; FIN-007). Owner and CPA review.

#### 📐 Flat-Rate Matrix by Room Type (Includes Wall Prep + 2 Coats)

##### Standard Bedroom (Up to 12' x 12' with 8ft Ceilings)
* **Walls Only:** $350 - $450 flat
* **Walls + Trim & Baseboards:** $450 - $600 flat
* **Add Ceiling:** +$100 flat

##### Large Master Suite or Open Living Room (Up to 15' x 20')
* **Walls Only:** $600 - $850 flat
* **Walls + Trim, Baseboards & Doors:** $850 - $1,200 flat
* **Add Ceiling:** +$175 flat

##### Small Bathroom / Powder Room
* **Walls Only:** $200 - $300 flat (High cutting detail around fixtures)

> FLAG (C-PT01-04; C-PT01-06; C-PT01-10): the matrix heading says "Includes Wall Prep + 2 Coats" and is silent on primer, while the main plan promises a full "foundation primer" coat and the SOP spot-primes repairs only: full prime coat included, spot-prime only, or a separate option (EST-005)? Owner decides (C-PT01-04). The trim, baseboard, door and ceiling scopes priced above have no SOP procedure yet (C-PT01-06). Whether paint is included in these prices is not stated (Q-28-14). The room ranges are a reference only: an estimate records surface area, preparation and coats (spec BIZ-004, EST-002; C-PT01-10). No price is changed here.

The "Closet Interiors" line has no SOP procedure (C-PT01-06). A single accent wall, ceiling or closet sold alone has no base price (Q-28-01). No price is changed here. ## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Flat price ranges by room type and scope ("Flat-Rate Matrix by Room Type": "Standard Bedroom", "Large Master Suite or Open Living Room", "Small Bathroom / Powder Room"); flat add-ons ("Add Ceiling", "Accent Wall Premium", "Closet Interiors"); a percentage surcharge on the room total ("High Ceiling Surcharge"); an hourly labor benchmark ("Average Labor Rate") that no line uses as a billing method; a materials markup or contractor-discount pass-through with a handling fee ("Materials Surcharge"). This is a room catalog, while estimates must be project-specific, with surface area, preparation and coats as the painting basis (C-PT01-10) (source: 28 section 7.3 row PT-01; line labels as quoted; spec BIZ-004; handyhub-estimate-assistant playbook ("surface area/preparation/coats for painting"))
- Units: Room size band (two capped room types and a small bathroom); wall (accent wall); closet; room total (surcharge base). No rule for rooms larger than the largest cap, for ceilings between the bedroom heading's "8ft Ceilings" basis and the surcharge's lower bound or above its upper bound, or for doors in the bedroom tier (source: 28 section 7.3; 04 "estimating method")
