**Value:** 30 days, a configurable value set now (not NOT CONFIGURED).

- Applies to a request closed "Closed—no response". A customer reply within 30 days of closing reopens the same request for staff review with its history; it never restores an expired estimate or appointment.
- After the period a reply starts a new request (PROPOSED handling; the decision sets only the period).

**Changed by [[OWN-DEC-197]] (2026-10-21), built by [[TSK-0148]]:** the 30 days stay. After them a reply no longer reopens the request: staff with authorization may reopen it with a written reason, or the customer is guided to send a new request ([[OQ-QUESTION-AMENDMENT-DETAILS]]).
