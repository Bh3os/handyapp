**Value:** 10% of the quote for military members, seniors and returning customers; one discount per job, the biggest applies ([[OWN-DEC-66]]). Eligibility as the owner stated it on 2026-10-05: "60+, spouses, last 12 months"; how it is verified is open ([[OQ-DISCOUNT-ELIGIBILITY]]).

**Presentation ([[OWN-DEC-145]]):**
- The discount is a separate row in the totals, shown before the total, for example "Discount (military) -10%". It is never a line item; every line stays above $0.00.
- It is computed in integer cents, half-up ([[RULE-MONEY-CENTS]]), on the subtotal of the approved lines and selected options.
- The deposit is computed on the total after the discount ([[FACT-DEPOSIT-RULE]]).
