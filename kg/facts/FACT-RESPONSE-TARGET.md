**Value:** first meaningful staff response within 1 hour, counted only during [[FACT-HOURS]] in [[FACT-TIMEZONE]].

- Customer wording (receipt page and receipt email, exact): "We'll review your project and contact you using your preferred method. We aim to respond within one hour during our daily hours, 8 AM–10 PM. Requests received outside those hours are reviewed when we reopen." ([[OWN-DEC-94]]). It replaces "We aim to reply within an hour, every day." ([[OWN-DEC-35]]).
- The word "guaranteed" is never used for it ([[OWN-DEC-35]]: attorney approval needed).
- Staff see it as an internal target: remaining time or overdue, paused outside hours ([[OWN-DEC-115]]).
- The clock stops only when a meaningful staff response is successfully sent or a completed customer conversation is recorded; opening, assigning, drafting, the automatic receipt and failed deliveries never stop it ([[OWN-DEC-116]]).
- The Owner Dashboard reports time to first meaningful response and the share within target ([[OWN-DEC-125]]).
