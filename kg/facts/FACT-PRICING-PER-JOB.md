Value: every job is priced individually on its own estimate. There is no price book and no fixed price list; no price item has a pre-approved value; no minimum charge applies ([[FACT-NO-MINIMUM]]).

- Owner, 2026-10-05 ([[OWN-DEC-32]]): "each job should priced individually based on the job estimate will be generated after the visit or online if it doesn't require".
- [[OWN-DEC-102]]: no price-book requirement; authorized staff write an estimate (scope, lines, prices, options, exclusions, any deposit) or upload one (PDF or Word, total and amounts entered as structured fields and confirmed to agree); estimates are valid 14 days ([[FACT-ESTIMATE-VALIDITY]]), carry no tax line ([[FACT-TAX-NO-LINE]]) and no card surcharge ([[FACT-NO-SURCHARGE]]); money is exact integer cents ([[RULE-MONEY-CENTS]]).

The 26 vault price lists (FACT-PRICING-HI-01 to FACT-PRICING-CL-07) are benchmark drafts superseded by this fact; their figures are never quoted, imported or applied.
