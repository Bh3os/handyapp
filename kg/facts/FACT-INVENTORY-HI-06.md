Required tools, materials and PPE for [[SVC-HI-06]] and [[SOP-HI-06]]. The vault inventory is a draft checklist (`Required inventory/inventory-gutter-guards.md`, `owner_approved: false`): quantities, stock levels, vendors, reorder points and ratings are not defined, and brand or rating words are kept as written, not approved. A missing critical tool, material or PPE item blocks the affected tasks ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]).

## Inventory checklist as written (`Required inventory/inventory-gutter-guards.md`)

#### 🧴 A. Waterproof Sealants & Flushes (المواد الكيميائية ومعجون العزل)
- [ ] **Self-Leveling Copolymer Gutter Sealant (e.g., Geocel or OSI GutterSeam):** Heavy waterproof joint injector.
- [ ] **Industrial Wire Brush Grinder Wheel:** Mandrel drill attachment to instantly scrape crusty old caulking off aluminum.
- [ ] **High-Volume Downspout Flushing Nozzle Hose Attachment:** Blasts packed clogs out of subterranean drains.

#### 🧳 B. Gutter Guard Hardware & Hand Tools (أدوات القطع والشبكات)
- [ ] **Heavy-Gauge Aluminum Micro-Mesh Gutter Guard Screens:** 4-foot sections packed in bulk cases (leaf screens).
- [ ] **Professional Offset Aviation Tin Snips:** For slicing through aluminum guard screens at complex miter angles.
- [ ] **Self-Tapping Hexagonal Zip Screws (1/4-inch head, waterproof washers):** Magnetic outdoor screws.
- [ ] **Extendable Telescoping Ladder (16-to-24 foot, ANSI Type IA rated):** Fitted with rubber stabilizer standoff bars.
- [ ] **Ergonomic Gutter Scoop Hand Shovels:** For fast manual leaf mud extraction.

Proposed additions in the vault file (from the SOP and spec cross-check; pending owner and safety review, nothing approved for purchase or use): PPE for the card hazards and the wire-wheel step - selection by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); `vehicle-safety` kit reference: first-aid kit, fire extinguisher, eyewash, spill kit as review items; contents by qualified safety professional (PROPOSED); SDS register entry for the gutter sealant (PROPOSED); Fall-protection equipment - only if the approved policy requires it (PROPOSED); Hose (PROPOSED); Debris bags (PROPOSED); Gutter spikes or screws for re-pitching - type and size per gutter manufacturer (PROPOSED); Drill/driver (PROPOSED); Slope level or other pitch-measuring tool (PROPOSED); Rags (PROPOSED); Caulk gun (PROPOSED); Tape measure (PROPOSED); Replacement hangers, brackets and end caps - only if included in the approved scope (PROPOSED).

## Service-card defaults (spec section 17, HI-06)

- Tools and equipment (HI-06.TLS): ladders/access equipment, stabilizer, hand scoop/tools, hose, drill/driver, fall-protection equipment where policy requires, PPE.
- Materials and consumables (HI-06.MAT): fasteners, sealant, guard system, bags, replacement minor hardware if included.

## SOP section 8, "Materials and consumables" (`Standard Of Operations/sop-gutter-maintenance.md`)

- Fasteners: gutter spikes or structural timber screws for Pitch Realignment and self-tapping zip screws for Fastener Locking. Types, sizes and spacing follow the gutter and guard manufacturers' instructions; the values written in section 12 are not yet verified against them (source: spec HI-06.MAT "fasteners"; spec section 17 implementation note).
- Sealant: the copolymer gutter sealant in the inventory. SDS required; a chemical product without a reviewed SDS is not carried to a job (source: spec HI-06.MAT "sealant"; 29 section 8.3, PROPOSED; X-09).
- Guard system: aluminum micro-mesh screens as stocked; the stainless option is not stocked (C-HI06-08); the standard product and model are an owner decision (Q-29-14) (source: spec HI-06.MAT "guard system"; inventory file).
- Bags for debris (source: spec HI-06.MAT "bags"; proposed in the inventory).
- Replacement minor hardware (hangers, brackets, end caps) only if included in the approved scope (source: spec HI-06.MAT "replacement minor hardware if included").
- Rags for drying joints (source: Joint Resealing step; proposed in the inventory).
- Substitutions only with approval (source: spec JOB-007).
