## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)

#### 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Average Removal Rate:** $2.50 - $4.50 / square foot of wall surface area.
* **Hourly Safe-Fallback Rate:** $65 - $85 / hour per technician if glue is completely crystallized or non-water-soluble.
* **Target Margin:** 55% (Highly labor-intensive, low tool-chemical overhead).

> FLAG (C-PT04-01): the "Hourly Safe-Fallback Rate" line above switches the billing basis to hourly when the glue proves "completely crystallized or non-water-soluble", a condition found on site. After the estimate is accepted, such a switch applies only through a change order that the customer approves before the extra work, with photos of the condition (spec FIN-008, JOB-005; 28 section 3.7, D-28-08). The agreement does not disclose this line. No value is changed; the owner and the attorney decide (Q-28-07, Q-28-16).
>
> FLAG (C-PT04-09; X-19): the "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks" figures name no source, date or approver and are not HandyHub rates (28 section 2.2). The "Target Margin" line is an internal target, unapproved, with no cost basis, and the file does not say whether it is a margin or a markup (Q-28-13). It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; spec FIN-007). Values unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; CPA (margin basis).

#### 📐 Flat-Rate Matrix by Room Demographics

##### Single Standard Room (Approx. 10' x 12' / ~350 sq. ft. of wall surface)
* **Standard Single Layer Paper:** $750 - $1,100 flat.
* **Inclusions:** Perforation scoring, enzyme saturation, steam removal, glue residue scrub wash, and basic Level 4 skim coat mud touch-up.

> FLAG (C-PT04-08): for the same standard room, the "Standard Single Layer Paper" flat price and the "Average Removal Rate" per square foot give different results at the low end, although the flat price also includes skim-coat work. No rule says which basis governs; the owner decides (Q-28-20). Values unchanged.
>
> FLAG (C-PT04-06): the "basic Level 4 skim coat mud touch-up" inclusion is a spot repair, while the main plan promises a "flawless, paint-ready Level 4 finish" and the agreement includes "a Level 4 skim coat mud repair" of undefined extent. The deliverable and its acceptance criteria are an owner decision with a drywall finishing lead. No price is changed.
>
> FLAG (X-04): no deposit rule covers a room job at the flat range above. Deposits apply to large jobs, and the threshold, amount, timing and terms are still owner decisions (owner facts 2026-10-02; Q-28-09).

##### Commercial Corridor or Multi-Layer Kitchen
* **Rate Range:** $3.50 - $5.50 per sq. ft.
* *Operational Fact:* Multiple layers of wallpaper stacked over each other require double the chemical dwell time and double the tool labor passes.

> FLAG (X-23): the heading above joins a commercial location ("Commercial Corridor") with a site condition ("Multi-Layer Kitchen"). No commercial-work policy exists; the owner decides whether PT-04 includes commercial work (X-23). The "Operational Fact" line is not a verified production factor: dwell times come from the product label (31 section 3.5 rule 3), and production factors are not supplied (owner facts 2026-10-02). How this rate relates to the other methods is part of Q-28-20.

#### 🚨 Important Pricing Disclaimers (الشروط والتحذيرات)
- [ ] **Unprimed Drywall Fine:** If the wallpaper was installed directly onto raw unprimed drywall paper by past trades, and removal causes wide structural tearing of the sheetrock paper face, stop work. Add a mandatory **+$150 - $300 drywall reconstruction surcharge** per room to apply oil-base sealers and extensive wide trowel patching.

> FLAG (C-PT04-01; X-16; C-PT04-05): the "Unprimed Drywall Fine" line above adds a "mandatory" per-room charge after work stops for a condition found on site. It requires an approved change order (FIN-008): the customer approves the charge before the extra work, with photos of the condition (spec JOB-005; 28 section 3.7, D-28-08). The label "Fine" is held for attorney review; the application uses "fee" (28 section 3.7, D-28-09; X-16). The agreement treats the same condition as possible "minor paper tearing" and includes a skim-coat repair without saying where included repair ends and this charge begins (30 section 7), while the SOP and the main plan call the process "damage-free" (C-PT04-05). Amounts unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; attorney licensed in KY/IN.

- [ ] **Border-Only Stripping Surcharge:** If removing only an aesthetic top border strip along the ceiling, charge a flat **$150 - $250 fee** per room.

> FLAG (C-PT04-09): the "Border-Only Stripping Surcharge" line above is a per-room price for border-only jobs, labelled a surcharge; its low end equals the job minimum (28 section 7.3). Border work along the ceiling involves ladder work (spec PT-04.HAZ "ladder"; 04 PT-04 PPE row "ladder work at ceiling borders"), and no height and access policy exists yet (02 G-005). Values unchanged.


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Four bases with no precedence rule: a per-square-foot wall rate ("Average Removal Rate"); a flat price for a standard room ("Standard Single Layer Paper" under "Single Standard Room"), which includes scoring, enzyme, steam, residue wash and a basic skim-coat touch-up; a per-square-foot rate for a commercial corridor or multi-layer kitchen ("Rate Range"); and an hourly per-technician fallback when the glue resists ("Hourly Safe-Fallback Rate"). Two per-room charges: the "Unprimed Drywall Fine" after a stop for wide tearing, and the "Border-Only Stripping Surcharge" for border-only jobs (source: 28 section 7.3 row PT-04; line labels as quoted)
- Units: Square foot of wall surface area (gross or net of openings not stated), room, technician hour (source: 28 sections 3.3 and 7.3)
