## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)

tags: #finance/pricing #painting/brick-coatings #market/louisville

#### 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **All-In Exterior Masonry Rate:** $3.50 - $6.00 / square foot of brick wall surface area.
* **Masking & Safety Overhead:** Roughly 25% of your total project timeline will be spent masking off windows and landscapes.
* **Target Margin:** 60% - 65% (High ticket sizes, usually $4,000 to $12,000+ total contracts).

> FLAG (C-PT03-10, X-19, C-PT03-08) - the three lines under "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks" are unsourced, undated and unapproved (owner facts 2026-10-02: no rates, wages, production factors or profit targets supplied). "All-In Exterior Masonry Rate" does not say whether coatings, lime, cleaners, masking, access equipment or a test section are included, or whether wall area is measured gross or net of windows and doors (Q-28-14; 04 PT-03 estimating method). "Masking & Safety Overhead" is a labor-share assumption, not a customer price (28 section 8); it budgets time for masking landscapes, which no SOP step performs (C-PT03-08). "Target Margin" is an internal, unapproved target with no cost basis and no stated margin basis; it must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; spec FIN-007; Q-28-13). Its "usually ... total contracts" range describes a typical contract size, not a floor. Values unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner; CPA (margin basis).

#### 📐 Unified Estimation Breakdown

##### Option A: Authentic Distressed European Limewash (Class-A Slaked Lime)
* **Rate Range:** $4.25 - $6.00 per sq. ft. of wall face.
* *Why:* High skill ceiling. Includes masonry wash, custom mixing slurry, cross-hatch block brush pass, and artistic water-pressure distressing for the antique look.

> FLAG (C-PT03-07) - Option A block: the "Why" line describes a brush-applied limewash ("cross-hatch block brush pass") with "artistic water-pressure distressing", while the agreement promises a test section "for Client sign-off before spraying the full facade"; the SOP has no test-section or sign-off step. Whether a test section is offered and included in this rate, and whether limewash is brushed or sprayed, are owner decisions (open question recorded with C-PT03-07 in 02; HUMAN REVIEW: owner, and an attorney for the agreement wording). The masonry wash and water-pressure distressing included here are held in the SOP until pressure-washing and chemical controls are approved. Value unchanged.

##### Option B: Solid Elastomeric Masonry Coating (Full Waterproof Shield)
* **Rate Range:** $3.50 - $4.50 per sq. ft. of wall face.
* *Why:* Requires high-power commercial airless spray rigs and thick 1-1/4 inch back-rolling to force thick polymer layers into porous mortar cells.

> FLAG (C-PT03-02) - Option B heading "(Full Waterproof Shield)": technical claim pending product data and attorney review. The agreement calls both options "premium breathable coatings" and the SOP intro speaks of "breathable protections", while the spec card lists "moisture trapping/wrong coating" as a hazard (spec PT-03.HAZ) and requires a compatible coating system (spec PT-03.SAF). Which product is used, and what permeability claim its data sheet supports, is an open question recorded with C-PT03-02 in 02; no claim is rewritten here, and neither wording is used in customer material until it is resolved (32 register A row 18 holds the "Breathable Protection" headline for the same reason). Whether the airless rig named in the "Why" line is owned or rented is not stated (Q-29-08). HUMAN REVIEW: attorney licensed in KY/IN; coating manufacturer technical data; qualified masonry or building-envelope professional. Value unchanged.

#### 📋 Standard Minimum Estimates for Invoicing
* **Small Single-Story Ranch Home Brick Facade:** $3,500 - $5,000 minimum.
* **Large Two-Story Southern Indiana Suburban Home:** $7,500 - $12,000+ flat rate.

> FLAG (C-PT03-V01, X-18, C-PT03-10, X-12) - basis conflict - owner decision. The same facade is priced per square foot by option above and by house type here, and no rule says which governs (Q-28-20). The ranch line is a service-level project floor labelled "minimum": it is above the \$150 job minimum (spec BIZ-003, FIN-001, CX-001) but unapproved and not disclosed in the agreement, and it must not stack with another service's floor on a combined visit; the owner's proposed default is one minimum per separately quoted job or visit bundle (owner facts 2026-10-02; 28 section 4.4; Q-28-06). The two-story line is called a "flat rate" although its range is open-ended, so it is not flat, and two-story work depends on height and access controls that do not exist yet (X-12; Q-31-09). "for Invoicing" in the heading does not make these values approved invoice amounts (C-PT03-10). Values unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: owner.

#### ➕ Mandatory Add-ons
- [ ] **High-Reach Lift Rental Surcharge:** If the structure is a heavy two-story or sits on a steep walk-out basement slope requiring a towable boom lift or hydraulic scissor lift, add **+$450 - $700 flat** to pass down the rental yard invoice directly to the client.

> FLAG (C-PT03-11, C-PT03-04, X-12) - "High-Reach Lift Rental Surcharge" line: the charge is stated both as a flat range and as passing "the rental yard invoice directly to the client". The owner chooses one basis, never both on one job (28 section 3.5; Q-28-15), and the agreement calls the total "fixed" (C-PT03-11); any rental charge must appear on the estimate or an approved change order, with no silent pass-through (spec FIN-008; 29 section 9.2). Lift use itself is on hold: no access equipment, height and access policy, operator qualification or fall controls exist (C-PT03-04; X-12; Q-31-09). Rent or buy, the vendor, the rental's damage coverage and who may sign rental terms are open (Q-29-08). HUMAN REVIEW: owner; qualified safety professional (lift operation); attorney licensed in KY/IN (rental terms); insurance broker (rental coverage). Value unchanged.


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Per square foot of wall face by coating option: a "Rate Range" line for "Option A: Authentic Distressed European Limewash (Class-A Slaked Lime)" and for "Option B: Solid Elastomeric Masonry Coating (Full Waterproof Shield)", plus an "All-In Exterior Masonry Rate" benchmark spanning both options. House-type ranges under "Standard Minimum Estimates for Invoicing": the ranch line labelled "minimum", the two-story line "flat rate" with an open upper end. A conditional "High-Reach Lift Rental Surcharge". No rule says whether the per-square-foot rates or the house-type ranges govern (C-PT03-V01; Q-28-20). Options A and B are exclusive alternates of one service and must be separately identifiable on the estimate (spec EST-005; 28 section 5.4) (source: 28 section 7.3 row PT-03; 04 PT-03 pricing model; headings "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks", "Unified Estimation Breakdown", "Standard Minimum Estimates for Invoicing", "Mandatory Add-ons")
- Units: Square foot "of wall face" or "of brick wall surface area", with no rule for windows and doors (gross or net); house type (single-story ranch, two-story); the lift charge as a flat range or as the rental invoice. No unit for coats, height band, test section, drying or weather revisits; no square metres (source: 28 sections 3.3 and 7.3; 04 PT-03 estimating method)
