## Price list as written (original lines and the FLAG lines added 2026-10-03)

#### 📈 Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks
* **Deck Board Replacement Average:** $6 - $12 per linear foot of rotted wood pulled and swapped.
* **Hourly Repair Diagnostic Fallback:** $75 - $95 / hour per structural carpenter.
* **Target Margin:** 55% - 65%

> FLAG (structural wording; X-19) - "Hourly Repair Diagnostic Fallback" line: "per structural carpenter" names a role and a qualification that HandyHub has not established (owner facts 2026-10-02: "Reported capability is not documentary proof of insurance, qualifications or equipment"), and no structural repair is in the approved scope: major structural, ledger or foundation issues outside the approved repair stop the job (spec HI-11.SAF). HUMAN REVIEW: structural engineer or building official (HI-11.SAF) for any structural scope; owner for the role and its qualification. The line sits under a market-benchmark heading, so the file does not establish whether it is a HandyHub rate; whether a diagnostic or estimate visit is charged at all, and how the job minimum applies to it, is an open owner decision (Q-28-05). Unapproved; value unchanged.

> FLAG (X-19) - "Target Margin" line: internal and unapproved, with no cost basis; the file does not say whether it is a gross, contribution or net margin or a markup (Q-28-13). It must never appear in customer documents, field views or agreements (28 section 8; spec FIN-007). The "Deck Board Replacement Average" and the other figures under this heading have no source or date. Values unchanged.

#### 📐 Component Repair Estimation Matrix

##### Rotted / Cupped Floorboard Swap-Out (Pressure-Treated Lumber Included)
* **Rate:** $8.50 - $13.00 per linear foot of lumber replaced.
* *Example (Three 12-foot rotted boards = 36 linear feet):* 36 x $11 = **$396 total floor patch bill**.

##### Structural Post Tightening / Safety Stabilization (Through-Bolt Method)
* **Rate:** $45 - $65 per vertical handrail post connection.
* *Inclusions:* Boring bolt shafts, installing heavy-duty 5-inch carriage through-bolts, thick washers, hex nuts, and high-torque socket compression locks.

> FLAG (structural wording) - "Structural Post Tightening / Safety Stabilization" heading and block: the post being tightened carries a guard, no vault file states load criteria for guards, and "Safety Stabilization" is safety-outcome wording that is not for customer use until reviewed. Whether a single through-bolt is an acceptable guard-post connection, and the bolt length named in "Inclusions", are questions for the structural engineer or building official (spec HI-11.SAF; 32 register B row 49, disposition "Hold"). The SOP flags the bolt length as a hypothesis to field-measure and holds work at a loose guard until fall control is reviewed. The job total is subject to the approved minimum (FIN-001). Unapproved; value unchanged. HUMAN REVIEW: structural engineer or building official.

##### Individual Step Tread Replacement
* **Price:** $60 - $85 flat per broken stair step board swapped out safely.

> FLAG (C-HI11-04) - "Individual Step Tread Replacement" line: no approved procedure - pending SOP. Tread replacement is priced here, the agreement names "stair tightening as defined in `sop-deck-repair.md`" and the main plan promises to "replace weak stair steps", but no SOP step covers stairs; stair work is not schedulable until a procedure is drafted and competently reviewed, and offering, defining or withdrawing it is an owner decision. "swapped out safely" is safety-outcome wording, not for customer use. The job total is subject to the approved minimum (FIN-001). HUMAN REVIEW: owner (scope); competent reviewer (procedure). No price changed.


## Classification from the vault review notes

- Pricing model(s): Per-unit components plus an hourly fallback: "Rotted / Cupped Floorboard Swap-Out" per linear foot of lumber replaced, pressure-treated lumber included, with a worked example; "Structural Post Tightening / Safety Stabilization" per vertical handrail post connection (through-bolt method; bolts, washers and nuts included); "Individual Step Tread Replacement" flat per stair tread, a line with no SOP procedure (C-HI11-04). Under the "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks" heading, which has no source or date (X-19): a "Deck Board Replacement Average" per linear foot with no matching HandyHub line, and an "Hourly Repair Diagnostic Fallback" per hour "per structural carpenter", which the file does not establish as a HandyHub rate (source: 28 section 7.1 row HI-11; 04 HI-11 pricing model; headings "Kentuckiana Local Market Benchmarks", "Component Repair Estimation Matrix")
- Units: Linear foot of lumber replaced; vertical handrail post connection; stair step board; hour. The worked example's arithmetic is consistent (deterministic check). No unit for structural remediation, joist or ledger work, disposal of removed lumber, a return visit, or deck height and access (source: 28 section 7.1; 04 HI-11 pricing model)
