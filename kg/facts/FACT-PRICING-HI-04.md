> FLAG (X-22; C-HI04-10): the two benchmark lines above assume different bracket suppliers ("Client supplies TV and bracket frame" versus "You supply premium tilt/fixed bracket") - customer-provided item rule pending (X-22; 29 section 9.1); no line prices bracket supply or markup (Q-28-14).
