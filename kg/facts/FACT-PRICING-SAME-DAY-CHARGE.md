**Value ([[OWN-DEC-140]]):** there is no extra charge for same-day service. Forms, estimates and invoices show none.

- Same-day service is offered when there is an opening ([[OWN-DEC-66]]).
- The request form's "Need help today?" is an optional request for staff review, never a guaranteed appointment ([[OWN-DEC-92]]).
- Recorded interpretation of the owner's answer "don't require same day job charge": no same-day charge. A different meaning needs a new decision ([[OWN-DEC-140]]).
