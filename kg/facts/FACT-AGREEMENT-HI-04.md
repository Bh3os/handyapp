## Clauses as written

This Agreement governs the relationship between **[Your Company Name]** ("Company") and **[Client Full Name]** ("Client") for secure flat-screen TV wall mounting and optional wire hiding.

#### 1. Inclusions & Weight Tolerances
* **Structural Guarantee:** The Company warrants that all TV brackets will be fastened using structural lag bolts anchored directly into the center of load-bearing wood studs or verified commercial toggle anchors, completely meeting safety weight parameters.
  - Governed now by: warranty: only the 90-day workmanship warranty of [[FACT-WARRANTY]] ([[OWN-DEC-135]]); no other warranty or guarantee is part of the approved terms ([[OWN-DEC-127]]).
* **Component Clause:** Standard packaging includes mounting the screen bracket. Running low-voltage wires behind the drywall is excluded unless itemized below as a paid add-on.
  - Governed now by: payment timing and methods: [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]], [[FACT-PAYMENT-METHODS]].

#### 2. Hidden In-Wall Obstruction Waiver
* **Wire Hiding Disclaimer:** When executing in-wall low-voltage cable fish lines, the Company utilizes deep-scanning wall sensors. Client releases the Company from liability for cutting into unmapped cross-bracing fire blocks, internal insulation, or plumbing ventilation pathways hidden behind the drywall surface.
  - Governed now by: liability, insurance and waiver wording: attorney and insurance-broker review ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]); no insurance or liability statement is made from this draft ([[RULE-NO-INVENTED-FACTS]]).
* **Hardware Sufficiency:** Client must supply matching functional HDMI lines of sufficient length (minimum 10–15 feet recommended for wire-hiding paths).
  - Governed now by: minimums: none, [[FACT-NO-MINIMUM]] ([[OWN-DEC-102]]).
