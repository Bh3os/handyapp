A field employee (initially two). The guide's Supervisor column has no separate role: supervision and quality review belong to the Owner.

- Sees My Day and only assigned jobs inside the access window: address from visit confirmation through the service day; access codes only online on the visit date, audited.
- Records On my way, Arrived, Finished, checklist steps, photos, notes, walkthrough outcomes, issue and incident reports; drafts change orders; requests administrative exceptions; Stop Work at any time without permission.
- Starts work only through the Start Work gate on an assigned visit.
- Records on-site payments only with the Owner-granted collect permission (PROPOSED mapping), never clears or reconciles them.
- Never: prices, publishing, approving for the customer, unrelated customer records, cost and margin, finance changes, exports, restricted incident details beyond their own report.

Full permissions: [[ROLE-MATRIX]].
