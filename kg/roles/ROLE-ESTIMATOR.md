Prepares and publishes estimates and change orders.

- Writes or uploads estimates and publishes them directly, while the Owner's publishing policy grants it (default yes); revises through new versions; records a customer's decline.
- Reviews and publishes change orders drafted by workers.
- Sees intake records and project history needed for estimating; sees internal cost inputs on estimates they prepare (PROPOSED).
- Never: approving for the customer, waivers, refunds, scheduling commits, payment recording, field start.

Full permissions: [[ROLE-MATRIX]].
