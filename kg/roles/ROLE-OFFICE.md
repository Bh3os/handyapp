Runs intake, customer communication, scheduling, invoice issue and routine payment recording. Combines the guide's Dispatcher and Customer service columns; the guide's Bookkeeper finance tasks other than Owner-only decisions are mapped here as PROPOSED.

- Sees the office action queue, the unified inbox, customer and property records, the scheduling board.
- Merges and relinks records with a reason and preview (the Owner can reverse); releases message drafts; offers windows, confirms, reschedules and reassigns visits; handles reschedule and cancellation requests.
- Issues invoices after the Owner's quality review, enters invoice-specific Square payment links and records verified payments ([[OWN-DEC-138]]); never for payments they collected (PROPOSED). Routine reconciliation matching is PROPOSED.
- Sees their own audit actions only, plus the operational history their role needs.
- Never: marking a check cleared, waivers, refunds, credits, write-offs, reopening, deletion decisions, role changes, cost and margin, restricted incident details (Office sees only that a case exists and its status, [[OWN-DEC-143]]), bulk exports.

Full permissions: [[ROLE-MATRIX]].
