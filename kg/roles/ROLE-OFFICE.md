Runs intake, customer communication, scheduling and routine payment recording. Combines the guide's Dispatcher and Customer service columns; the guide's Bookkeeper finance tasks other than Owner-only decisions are mapped here as PROPOSED.

- Sees the office action queue, the unified inbox, customer and property records, the scheduling board.
- Merges and relinks records with a reason and preview (the Owner can reverse); releases message drafts; offers windows, confirms, reschedules and reassigns visits; handles reschedule and cancellation requests.
- Records verified payments and checks (PROPOSED for clearing and reconciliation), never for payments they collected.
- Sees their own audit actions only, plus the operational history their role needs.
- Never: waivers, refunds, credits, write-offs, reopening, deletion decisions, role changes, cost and margin, restricted incident details, bulk exports.

Full permissions: [[ROLE-MATRIX]].
