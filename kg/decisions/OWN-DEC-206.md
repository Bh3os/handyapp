Final confirmation, with amendment, of reading 7 of [[OQ-READINGS-2026-10-21]] (coverage by ZIP only, [[OWN-DEC-179]]).

Owner answer (round 14, 2026-10-22), verbatim:
> Confirm all 13 readings, subject to the following final amendments.
> Item 7 — ZIP coverage
> Approve the four-outcome ZIP classification logic.
> However, treat ZIP-only results as preliminary screening. Verify the complete property address and county before confirming service-area eligibility and the first applicable appointment.
> Do not reject an otherwise legitimate request merely because its ZIP is marked EDGE or UNKNOWN.
> Never represent a ZIP-only match as conclusive address verification.

Effect:
- The ZIP-only classification of the reading is approved: SERVED only for a ZIP wholly inside approved counties per the verified source; EDGE when it also reaches other counties; UNKNOWN for PO Box and Unique ZIPs and unverifiable ZIPs; OUTSIDE for a verified location outside all approved counties ([[OWN-DEC-179]]); the four existing customer messages of [[OWN-DEC-88]] map to SERVED, EDGE, OUTSIDE and UNKNOWN.
- A ZIP-only result is preliminary screening. Service-area eligibility and the first applicable appointment are confirmed only after the complete property address and its county are verified (the site checks at confirmation, [[OWN-DEC-167]]).
- A request whose ZIP is EDGE or UNKNOWN is never rejected for that reason alone; it is accepted and reviewed.
- No screen, message or record presents a ZIP-only match as address verification: the stored coverage result keeps its basis (ZIP only, or verified address and county).
