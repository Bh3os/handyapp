Owner wording (owner facts, 2026-10-02): "Use service-specific estimating; numeric rates, wages, production factors, tax rates and profit targets are not supplied."

Effect:
- Each service is estimated on its own basis.
- No rate, wage, production factor, tax rate or profit target is supplied, assumed or seeded. The amounts in the vault pricing drafts (`FACT-PRICING-*`) are unapproved benchmarks and are never used as prices.
- Without cost data, a price can lose money unnoticed until job costing exists (RISK).

Partly replaced by [[OWN-DEC-32]]: each job is priced individually on its own estimate. [[OWN-DEC-102]]: there is no price book, so nothing about a versioned price book applies.

- Tax treatment: [[OQ-TAX-CPA]].
