Owner wording (2026-10-20): Q: "Warranty: an earlier answer mentioned a 90-day workmanship warranty, but no decision adopted it. What should apply?" Answer: "90-day workmanship warranty".

Effect:
- HandyHub gives a 90-day workmanship warranty, counted from the reviewed completion date.
- It goes into the next estimate terms version after v1.0 and is shown on estimates and invoices. The exact wording and any exclusions get attorney review ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]); no exclusions are invented.
- A warranty claim opens a case with return-visit punch items ([[OWN-DEC-124]]).
