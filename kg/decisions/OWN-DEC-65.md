Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-12): asked whether request receipts and status emails should go automatically, the owner chose "Staff release each email".

Effect:
- Every customer message starts as a draft that staff release, unless it is on the automatic-message exceptions list ([[FACT-AUTOMATIC-MESSAGES]]).
- One-time codes and secure links are sent immediately because they expire within minutes or hours.

Partly replaced by [[OWN-DEC-95]]: the request receipt also sends automatically, and the exceptions list grows with later decisions: published staff questions ([[OWN-DEC-98]]), visit options and confirmations ([[OWN-DEC-99]]), "not a fit" notices ([[OWN-DEC-100]]), estimate-ready notices ([[OWN-DEC-102]]), approval receipts and appointment confirmations ([[OWN-DEC-105]]), on-my-way notices ([[OWN-DEC-107]]), invoice-ready notices and payment receipts ([[OWN-DEC-110]], [[OWN-DEC-111]]), and staff invite and reset emails ([[OWN-DEC-114]]).
