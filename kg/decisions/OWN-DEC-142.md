Owner wording (2026-10-20): Q: "How long are audit-log records kept? (Your retention schedule has no row for them.)" Answer: "7 years".

Effect:
- Audit-log records are kept 7 years, then go to owner-reviewed deletion; there is no automatic deletion ([[OWN-DEC-70]]).
- Records under a case or legal hold are kept until the hold ends.
