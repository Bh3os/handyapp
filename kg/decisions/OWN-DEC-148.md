Owner wording (2026-10-20): Q: "Recurring services (for example routine cleaning): how much notice must a customer give to change or stop the recurring schedule?" Answer: "24 hours".

Effect:
- A customer can change or stop a recurring schedule from the workspace. The change applies to visits more than 24 hours away; a visit within 24 hours follows the cancellation rules of [[OWN-DEC-134]].
