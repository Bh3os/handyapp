Owner wording (2026-10-20): Earlier owner answers, adopted 2026-10-20 ("Yes, adopt all six"): cancel after paying the deposit = "Refund -50 usd for the service usage"; materials already bought = "Only the $50 is kept"; under 24 hours = "rescudle free but if final cancellation 50 use charge"; the $50 applies = "Only under 24 hours"; cancel after work started = "Work done + materials (Recommended)"; HandyHub cancels or moves a visit = "Free reschedule or full refund (Recommended)"; notice to cancel or reschedule = "24 hours, no fee (Recommended)". Clarified 2026-10-20: a final cancellation with more than 24 hours notice before work starts = "Full deposit refund".

Effect:
- Rescheduling is always free.
- Final cancellation with 24 hours or more notice, before work starts: full deposit refund and no charge.
- Final cancellation with less than 24 hours notice, including when nobody lets the crew in and the customer then cancels: $50.00 is kept. It comes from the deposit when one was paid and the rest of the deposit is refunded; with no deposit it is invoiced. Materials already bought do not add to the $50.
- Cancellation after work has started: the customer pays for the work done plus materials.
- HandyHub cancels or moves a visit (weather, safety stop, sick worker): the customer chooses a free reschedule or a full refund.
- Refunds are owner-only ([[ROLE-MATRIX]]). The customer-facing wording goes into the estimate terms after attorney review ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]).
