Owner, 2026-10-12, journey Step 11, afterwards.

Question asked:
> Step 11 - Afterwards: a repeat-service reminder is drafted on the agreed schedule (only if the customer agreed to one). A review request is NOT sent automatically: staff can send one only after a positive satisfaction check and only with consent. Marketing messages require the separate marketing permission. The customer can download their documents anytime, ask for a data export, or request deletion (checked for open complaints, disputes, money owed and legal retention first; the owner decides). OK?

Owner wording:
> After completion:
>
> - Prepare repeat-service reminders only on a customer-agreed schedule. Staff release them, and customers can change or stop the reminders.
> - Offer “Request This Service Again,” reusing authorized property details while confirming they are still current. New pricing and appointments require review.
> - Review requests remain staff-released. Invite honest feedback using the same eligibility rules regardless of satisfaction score. No incentives, pressure, or positive-review filtering.
> - Pause review invitations during an active complaint; reconsider them after resolution regardless of the outcome.
> - Respect channel permissions, separate marketing consent, and opt-outs before sending.
>
> Customers can download their retained, customer-visible documents through verified access and request a data export or deletion.
>
> Show deletion as a request for owner review, with confirmation of identity and checks for required retention, relevant holds, and linked evidence. Explain what will be deleted or retained and why. Account closure must remain separate from retaining necessary financial or dispute records; an unpaid balance must not automatically prevent closing login access.
>
> Record actions and notify the customer of the outcome. Implement, test, and publish on private staging using test notifications.

Effect:
- Repeat-service reminders only on a customer-agreed schedule, staff released; customers can change or stop them from the workspace ([[FACT-REMINDER-CADENCES]]).
- "Request This Service Again" reuses authorized property details, asks the customer to confirm they are current, and creates a new linked request; new pricing and appointments need review.
- Review requests are staff-released with the same eligibility whatever the satisfaction score: no incentives, pressure or positive-review filtering (any score-gating logic is removed). They pause during an active complaint and are reconsidered after resolution regardless of outcome ([[OWN-DEC-124]]).
- Channel permissions, separate marketing consent and opt-outs are checked before every send.
- Customers download their retained, customer-visible documents through verified access and can request a data export or deletion.
- Deletion is a request for owner review with identity confirmation and checks for retention ([[FACT-RETENTION-SCHEDULE]]), holds and linked evidence; the customer is told what will be deleted or kept and why ([[OWN-DEC-70]], [[OWN-DEC-71]]). Closing login access is separate from keeping financial or dispute records; an unpaid balance never blocks closing login access.
- Actions are recorded and the customer is notified of the outcome (a staff-released message; it is not on the automatic list).
