Owner wording (2026-10-20): Q: "Office staff and money: which finance actions may Office/Dispatcher do (Owner can always do all)?" Answer: "Issue invoices, Enter Square payment links, Record payments" (mark checks cleared not chosen).

Effect:
- Office/Dispatcher may issue invoices (after the owner quality review), enter invoice-specific Square payment links and record payments.
- Only the Owner marks a check cleared.
- Refunds, waivers, credits and write-offs stay owner-only.
