Owner wording (2026-10-20): Q: "Who may see restricted incident details (injury, damage, disputes)?" Answer: "Owner + reporting worker (Recommended)".

Effect:
- Only the Owner and the worker who reported it see restricted incident details (injury, damage, disputes).
- Office sees only that a case exists and its status.
- Customers see only the acknowledgement, progress and next step.
