Owner wording (2026-10-20): Q: "Security review before real customer data: you answered AI review only, but earlier decisions required a qualified human reviewer. Which wins?" Answer: "AI review only".

Effect:
- Before real customer data, AI agents run a full multi-angle security review and fix every finding. No human reviewer is required for go-live.
- The review, its findings and the fixes are recorded as release evidence.
- This replaces the qualified-human-reviewer requirement of earlier decisions for the security review.
