Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-05): "answer guaranteed in less than hour". In-chat choices the same day: for when it applies, "Every day, all hours"; for the website, "Softer wording now" (offered as: show "We aim to reply within an hour" now, switch to "guaranteed" after attorney review).

Effect:
- HandyHub aims to answer a customer's inquiry in less than an hour.
- Until an attorney approves the word "guaranteed", the website states only: "We aim to reply within an hour, every day." The word "guaranteed" is not used.
- This is a reply expectation only: no emergency, 24/7 or same-day service is promised.
- RISK: a missed reply breaks a published expectation while the owner holds every office role.

Partly replaced by [[OWN-DEC-94]] and [[OWN-DEC-115]]: the one-hour target applies during the daily hours 8 AM to 10 PM (business time zone), not at all hours; the receipt says "We aim to respond within one hour during our daily hours, 8 AM–10 PM. Requests received outside those hours are reviewed when we reopen." The contact details are decided in [[OWN-DEC-47]], [[OWN-DEC-49]] and [[OWN-DEC-126]].
