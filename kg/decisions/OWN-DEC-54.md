Owner wording (design brief, project chat, 2026-10-11, as recorded): "For HandyHub, I would go with a Premium Home-Improvement Company identity: elegant, modern, professional, and trustworthy, with a strong local presence in Kentucky and Southern Indiana. The website should feel like an established home-services company that customers trust with their properties, not a gig-work marketplace or a generic handyman template." Colour system: Deep Navy #1A2B49, Warm Ivory #F7F5F0, Champagne Gold #C79A55, Charcoal #303942, Stone #E7E2D9 ("champagne gold adds elegance without the aggressive look of bright construction-yellow buttons"). Visual inspiration: architectural photography, clean compositions, restrained colours, a premium residential atmosphere. Home page direction: the label "Your property. Our priority.", the headline "Professional care for every corner of your property." and the actions "Request an estimate" and "Explore services". Site structure: hero; trust and credentials ("genuine insurance, qualifications, guarantees, and verified reviews"); services; our work ("real project portfolios"); why HandyHub; how it works; who we serve; service areas "with ZIP-code verification"; testimonials ("authentic reviews"); final call to action. Features: "a guided estimate request that lets them select a service, describe the job, upload photos, specify their property location, and choose a preferred appointment window", "clear confirmation", review of the estimate before accepting; the admin and technician portal handles estimates, assignments, schedules, SOPs, safety checks, tools, materials, job photographs, customer communication, invoicing and payment status, "without making unsupported claims about live dispatch or technician availability". Design rules: editorial-quality layouts, beautiful residential and project photography, deep navy and warm neutral colours, refined typography, generous whitespace, subtle motion, exceptional mobile usability; "Avoid gig-economy marketplace aesthetics, generic SaaS dashboards on public pages, excessive gradients, cartoon illustrations, aggressive animations, misleading fleet claims, and fabricated credentials or reviews"; the public website designed for conversion and customer confidence, the internal platform for speed, clarity, safety and efficiency, one HandyHub identity across both. Next deliverable: "a complete HandyHub UI/UX design specification".

Effect:
- One premium residential identity across both sites ([[FACT-BRAND-IDENTITY]]): the five named colours, editorial layouts, real photography, restrained motion, excellent mobile use.
- Credentials, reviews, projects and the owner introduction appear only when real, verified and permitted; their sections render only then, and no placeholder is visible on the public site.
- Words the copy check blocks (premium, luxury, best, expert, team, insured, licensed, guarantee, years of experience and similar claims) stay off the site until approved and, where needed, reviewed by the attorney.
- The ZIP check answers from the service-area configuration ([[OWN-DEC-37]]).
- Preferred visit windows are a request preference, never an appointment ([[OWN-DEC-56]]).
- The staff app keeps fast, clear operational screens in the same identity.

Partly replaced by [[OWN-DEC-55]]: typefaces and the service plate. [[OWN-DEC-86]]: the homepage trust facts and the Why HandyHub section use the owner's exact later wording.
