Owner answer (round 13, question 1), corrections 1, 4 and 8, verbatim:
> 1. For "Closed—no response," require the established reminder and waiting period, but also account for delivery failures, customer responses, active appointments, accepted work, and received payments.
> 4. Preserve the rule that work stays paused when the Owner cannot be reached, except that employees must immediately contact emergency services for an imminent emergency without waiting for Owner authorization.
> 8. Keep customer-facing content English-only and preserve Spanish support for workers.

Owner answer (round 14, 2026-10-22), verbatim:
> Confirm all 13 readings, subject to the following final amendments.
> Items 2, 3, 9, 10, 11, 12, and 13
> Approved as written, subject to existing mandatory safety, financial, and security protections.
> For item 10, serious emergencies always require immediate contact with 911 without waiting for Owner permission.
> Treat this as my final approval of all 13 readings with these amendments. Continue implementation without additional routine approval questions.

"As written" is the wording presented to the owner in the review of the 13 readings that followed round 13; readings 3 and 13 were presented with the round 13 corrections built in. The readings as recorded in [[OQ-READINGS-2026-10-21]]:

> 2. **A1 hazard inspection ([[OWN-DEC-193]]):** while an intake hold is open, only a visit the Owner authorizes as a hazard-evaluation inspection, naming the qualified person and the safety conditions, can be confirmed; the hold stays open.
> 3. **Closed—no response ([[OWN-DEC-153]]):** available 24 hours after the single question reminder was actually sent.
> 9. **Free estimates wording ([[OWN-DEC-163]]):** the existing "Free estimates—online or at your property." is kept as the statement the owner quoted.
> 10. **Escalation backup ([[OWN-DEC-172]]):** the 2026-10-05 backup contact ("My field worker") is not part of the new procedure; when the Owner is unreachable the work stays paused.
> 11. **Subcontractor cost ([[OWN-DEC-188]]):** when tracked, entered as a direct job cost.
> 12. **Greeting:** "Hi there," (R2 question 4) is used where the specification's section 2 said "Hello,".
> 13. **Dashboard language:** English only ([[OWN-DEC-162]]) where R3 question 3 item 6 said English and Spanish.

Final wording of reading 3, as presented to and approved by the owner: "Closed—no response" becomes available 24 hours after the single question reminder was actually sent. It is not available if that reminder failed to deliver (the request goes to staff follow-up instead), if the customer replied, if an appointment exists, if work was accepted or if a payment was recorded.

Final wording of reading 13, as presented to the owner: the customer side and the Owner Dashboard are English. Worker-side screens, including safety text and the emergency instruction, are available in Spanish.

Effect (all subject to the existing mandatory safety, financial and security protections):
- 2: while an intake review hold is open, the only visit of that project that can be confirmed is one the Owner authorizes, with a recorded reason, as a hazard-evaluation inspection naming the qualified person and the safety conditions; the hold stays open and ordinary service visits stay blocked; HandyHub never performs licensed or hazardous work ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]], [[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]). Confirms the reading in [[OWN-DEC-193]] A1. Built by [[TSK-0145]].
- 3: "Closed—no response" as in the final wording above; a queued but unsent reminder never counts, and a failed delivery opens staff follow-up, never customer non-response ([[OWN-DEC-197]] E4). Confirms the reading in [[OWN-DEC-153]] and [[FACT-REMINDER-CADENCES]]. Built by [[TSK-0121]] and [[TSK-0148]].
- 9: the approved wording "Free estimates—online or at your property." ([[OWN-DEC-86]]) stays; no copy change ([[OWN-DEC-163]]).
- 10: the 2026-10-05 backup contact is not part of the escalation procedure; when the Owner cannot be reached and there is no emergency, the affected work stays paused. A serious emergency always means calling 911 at once, without waiting for the Owner's permission ([[OWN-DEC-172]], [[FACT-SVC-ESCALATION-PATH]]). Built by [[TSK-0129]].
- 11: subcontractor cost, when the Owner tracks it, is entered as a direct job cost in job costing ([[OWN-DEC-188]], [[OWN-DEC-189]]); no pay rates or payroll in the app. Built by [[TSK-0131]].
- 12: the greeting is "Hi {customerFirstName}," or "Hi there," when no verified first name exists; "Hello," is not used ([[OWN-DEC-158]], [[OWN-DEC-151]]). Built by [[TSK-0119]].
