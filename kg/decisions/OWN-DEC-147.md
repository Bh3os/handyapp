Partly replaced by [[OWN-DEC-190]] (2026-10-21): consent is collected at the request form and on the receipt with the owner's wording; the registration package is prepared now.

Owner wording (2026-10-20): Q: "Texting registration (A2P 10DLC) through Bird must be filed with your business details before real texts go out. Who does it?" Answer: "make me website satisfy all the 10 dlc". Q: "Which is the legal entity for the registration?" Answer: "ODD JOBS & MORE LLC" (Kentucky; DBA not filed; brand shown to customers: HandyHub).

Effect:
- The public site meets every A2P 10DLC campaign-vetting requirement:
  - a privacy policy with an SMS section saying mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data are never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing;
  - SMS terms naming the program, the message types, the frequency, "Message and data rates may apply", "Reply STOP to opt out", "Reply HELP for help", and the support contact;
  - an SMS opt-in checkbox on the request form, unchecked by default, separate from accepting the terms and separate from marketing consent, with the exact disclosure text next to it;
  - the same business and brand name across the site, the privacy policy, the SMS terms and the registration;
  - working links to the privacy policy and the SMS terms from the form and the footer.
- Transactional texts and marketing texts are separate consents. Marketing texts need the separate marketing permission.
- STOP, HELP and START are honoured through Bird ([[INT-Bird]]) and update the consent record immediately.
- Registration uses the legal entity ODD JOBS & MORE LLC with the brand HandyHub. Filing it in Bird is an owner action ([[OQ-BIRD-10DLC]]); the EIN and registered address are entered by the owner in Bird and never stored in the repository.
- The wording of the privacy policy and SMS terms gets attorney review ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-PRIVACY]]).
- Documents still show "Handyhub LLC" per [[OWN-DEC-126]]; whether that name matches the legal entity is open in [[OQ-LEGAL-ENTITY-NAME]].
