Owner answer (round 10, question 3, how subcontractors are paid): "Outside the app (Recommended)". The option chosen said the app records their hours and jobs only, with no payroll or pay rates.

Effect:
- Subcontractor pay is worked out and paid outside the software. The app records their sign-in, assigned jobs and per-worker Arrived and Finished times ([[HH-WORK-06]]); it holds no pay rates, payroll or subcontractor payments.
- Reading: when the Owner wants subcontractor cost in job costing, it is entered as a direct job cost ([[OWN-DEC-189]]).
- Answers the payment part of [[OQ-SUBCONTRACTOR-POLICY]]; the hiring trigger and the agreement wording stay open (attorney and CPA packets, [[OWN-DEC-186]]).
