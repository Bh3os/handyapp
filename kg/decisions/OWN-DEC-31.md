Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-05): "forget all the way the 150 usd min job don't consider it at all in all the stages and continue what you working at".

Effect:
- HandyHub has no minimum job value. The $150 minimum of [[OWN-DEC-06]] is withdrawn.
- No minimum is considered in pricing, estimating, invoicing, agreements, SOPs, the website, the request form, the software or any review packet: no minimum rule, disclosure, acknowledgement, validation or automatic top-up.
- Every mention of a $150 minimum in the vault business files is superseded by this decision; those files are not reworded.
- Small jobs are priced only by their written estimate.

Reaffirmed by [[OWN-DEC-102]].
