Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-12): the owner chose "10 photos, no PDFs".

Effect:
- A request takes up to 10 photos in total: JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 15 MiB each. PDFs are not accepted.
- File type (by content signature, not extension), size and count are validated on the server.
- Photos are stored privately and kept per the retention schedule ([[OWN-DEC-70]]).

Reaffirmed and detailed by [[OWN-DEC-91]] (photos optional; per-photo states with Remove and Retry; submission with failed photos excluded; the warning not to upload cards, IDs or passwords).
