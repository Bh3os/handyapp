Owner answer (round 12, Rules Review), section verbatim:

> A — Visit Confirmation
> A1: Maintain mandatory holds. Distinguish ordinary service work from a separately authorized, qualified, safe inspection intended to evaluate a hazard. Never bypass applicable safety, legal, permit, or qualification restrictions.
> A2: Separate estimate-visit and work-visit eligibility.
> Estimate visits do not require an approved job, since estimates are free and normally occur before a job exists.
> Work visits require an approved estimate/job and all applicable confirmation checks.
> Staff can publish appointment options only when the relevant visit type satisfies its required conditions.
> A4: Completed jobs may authorize follow-up visits only where an existing contractual obligation, warranty, punch-list process, or approved return-work authorization permits them. New paid work requires an approved estimate or change order.
> Approve A3, A5, and A6 as built.

Rules as built, as the owner reviewed them on the Rules Review page (2026-10-21):

- **A1** An open intake review hold (hazardous material, licensed trade, structural, permit or other) blocks confirming every visit of that project, estimate visits included, until it is resolved.
- **A2** Staff can only offer appointment windows when the job is approved and no blocking hold is open, so customers are never offered times that can't be confirmed.
- **A4** A job counts as approved while it is approved or completed (for follow-up work). A job under cancellation review, cancelled or closed blocks confirmation.

Effect:
- A1: an open intake review hold keeps blocking confirmation of ordinary service visits of its project. A separately authorized, qualified and safe inspection whose purpose is to evaluate the hazard is distinguished from ordinary service work; no safety, legal, permit or qualification restriction is ever bypassed. Reading (PROPOSED, [[OQ-READINGS-2026-10-21]]): such an inspection visit can be confirmed only with the Owner's recorded authorization naming the qualified person and the safety conditions, while the hold itself stays open; HandyHub still never performs licensed or hazardous work ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]).
- A2: estimate visits need no approved job (estimates are free and come before a job exists); work visits need an approved estimate and job plus every confirmation check. Staff publish options only when the visit type meets its own conditions. Replaces A2 as built (approval required for every offer).
- A4: a completed job allows a follow-up visit only under an existing contractual obligation, a warranty ([[FACT-WARRANTY]]), the punch-list process or an approved return-work authorization; new paid work needs an approved estimate or change order ([[OWN-DEC-169]]).
