Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-12, as recorded): "Delete the login account separately from the business records." The confirmation shows what is deleted, what is retained, why, and the review date; sign-in, sessions and project-access links are revoked; eligible personal data and preferences are deleted; invoices and payments (7 years), accepted estimates and authorizations (10 years) and incident records (10 years) are retained; "Do not rewrite signed approvals or issued financial documents to anonymize them"; holds block deletion but access can still be disabled; owner, time, reason and deleted and retained categories are recorded.

Effect:
- The customer's login account is deleted separately from business records.
- Before confirming, the owner sees what will be deleted, what is retained, why, and the review date.
- On confirmation: sign-in, sessions and project-access links are revoked; eligible unnecessary personal data and account preferences are deleted.
- Retained, subject to longer requirements or holds: invoices and payments (7 years), accepted estimates and authorizations (10 years), incident records (10 years); supporting photos, messages and property details when their own schedule or a protected record requires them ([[FACT-RETENTION-SCHEDULE]]).
- Signed approvals and issued financial documents are never rewritten or anonymized.
- Retained records are restricted to authorized staff, with only the identifying information they need. Relationships are preserved without cascading deletion into protected records.
- A hold blocks deletion of the affected records, but account access can still be disabled.
- The owner, time, reason, deleted categories and retained categories are recorded; retained records enter the review queue when their periods end.

Partly replaced by [[OWN-DEC-113]]: the customer starts deletion as a request for owner review with identity confirmation and an explanation of what is deleted or retained; an unpaid balance never blocks closing the login.
