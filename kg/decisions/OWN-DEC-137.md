Owner wording (2026-10-20): Q: "Who may release the job-level Hold (the Hold/Ready note) on a job? The strict rule says nobody releases a hold they raised themselves ..." Answer: "Owner can release any Hold/Ready note".

Effect:
- The Owner may set Ready on any job-level Hold/Ready note ([[OWN-DEC-82]]), including one the Owner set.
- The rule that nobody releases a hold they raised themselves ([[OWN-DEC-38]]) stays for critical safety holds from hazard reports. It does not apply to job-level Hold/Ready notes.
- Safety gates are unchanged ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]).
