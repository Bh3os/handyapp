Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-12, as recorded): "Draft the complete English privacy notice and website terms now. Put them on staging with "Draft - not reviewed by an attorney." Do not use empty "pending review" pages." Vendors are described only after verifying the implementation. Marketing consent and photo-publication permission stay separate. The terms reflect guest requests, optional accounts, estimates needing approval, separately confirmed appointments, approved scope changes and configured payment and deposit policies, with no minimum job charge and no invented cancellation fees, warranties, liability waivers or refund rules. An attorney review checklist accompanies the drafts. "Production release requires the reviewed pages, verified practices, and documented owner approval. Record the policy version presented with each request or acceptance."

Effect:
- A complete English privacy notice and website terms exist, shown on staging with the banner "Draft - not reviewed by an attorney". No empty "pending review" pages.
- Vendors are named only as actually used and verified in the implementation, each with its purpose.
- The privacy notice covers collected information, purposes, authorized staff and vendor access, photo handling, retention, customer requests and contact details.
- Marketing consent and permission to publish photos stay separate from service requests.
- The terms reflect the actual process: guest requests, optional accounts, estimates that need approval, separately confirmed appointments, approved scope changes, the configured payment and deposit policies, no minimum job charge. No cancellation fee, warranty, liability waiver or refund rule is invented.
- An attorney review checklist goes with the drafts.
- The policy version presented is recorded with each request and each acceptance ([[ENT-PolicyVersion]]).
- Production needs the attorney-reviewed pages, verified practices and documented owner approval.
