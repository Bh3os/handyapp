Owner wording (owner facts, 2026-10-02): "Deposits for large jobs; threshold, amount, timing and terms remain owner decisions."

Recorded approach (2026-10-02): deposits apply to large jobs; until the policy existed, deposit enforcement stayed off and no draft deposit clause (the fixed 25% to 40% deposits in five draft agreements) could be used.

Superseded by [[OWN-DEC-42]] (20% on estimates over $1,000), [[OWN-DEC-66]] (paid before materials are bought) and [[OWN-DEC-104]] (the full deposit rule: final approved total including options, checks pending until cleared, owner-only waiver, credited to the invoice). The draft agreements' deposit and retainer clauses stay unused.
