Owner wording (owner facts, 2026-10-02): "No brand assets or verified portfolio/reviews supplied; never fabricate proof."

Effect:
- No testimonial, review, portfolio item, credential or project photo is shown unless it is real, verified and permitted.
- Every customer-facing claim passes a claims check; launch requires no unverified claim in customer-facing content.
- Any compliance, insurance or licensing statement needs attorney review before it is published.

Partly replaced by [[OWN-DEC-54]]: brand assets now exist (the premium identity, colours and typefaces, [[FACT-BRAND-IDENTITY]]). The rule against fabricated proof is unchanged.
