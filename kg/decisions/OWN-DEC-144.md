Partly replaced by [[OWN-DEC-194]] (2026-10-21): the 24-hour hold starts when the offer is published to the customer.

Owner wording (2026-10-20): Q: "When staff offer 2-3 appointment windows to a customer, should those windows be held (blocked for others) until the customer picks one?" Answer: "Hold for 24 hours".

Effect:
- Windows offered to a customer (estimate visits and work visits) hold the needed workers and resources for 24 hours from the offer.
- Selecting a window turns its hold into a confirmed reservation and releases the other offered windows. Unselected holds release automatically after 24 hours.
- Selection still rechecks availability; if a hold has expired and the slot is gone, alternatives are offered.
