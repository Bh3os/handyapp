Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-12): asked whether email or phone is required on the request form, the owner answered "make both required".

Effect:
- Name, email and phone are all required on the public request form and validated on the client and the server.
- Project access codes can go to either contact once it is verified.
- Each contact's verification state stays visible to staff.

Reaffirmed by [[OWN-DEC-93]] (verified account details prefill the fields; the customer can correct them).
