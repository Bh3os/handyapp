Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-05): "Kentuckiana refers to the metropolitan area that includes Louisville, Kentucky, and the surrounding counties in both Kentucky and Southern Indiana." In-chat county answers the same day: Kentucky side "Meade county, clark county in, Floyd county in , including salem,coyrdon,and Madison in,Jefferson (Louisville),Oldham,Bullitt,Shelby"; Southern Indiana side "Clark,Floyd,Harrison,Scott". Asked whether Salem and Madison mean the towns or the counties: "then whole counties".

Effect:
- The service area is eleven whole counties ([[FACT-SERVICE-AREA]]):
  - Kentucky: Jefferson (Louisville), Oldham, Bullitt, Shelby, Meade.
  - Indiana: Clark, Floyd, Harrison (includes Corydon), Scott, Washington (Salem), Jefferson (Madison).
- The ZIP coverage check answers from this area; the ZIP list is derived from the official Census ZCTA-to-county relationship file, never typed by hand or guessed.
- Both states are served, so each needs its own legal, tax and permit assessment.

Partly replaced by [[OWN-DEC-122]]: the area is active configuration, with no Business-settings activation step. [[OWN-DEC-100]]: staff, not the ZIP checker, decide whether an outside-area request is accepted.
