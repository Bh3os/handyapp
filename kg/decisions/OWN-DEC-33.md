Owner wording (in-chat question, 2026-10-05): "which service categories do you want to open first at launch?" answered "cleaning, dry wall , painting.and home improvement".

Effect:
- The launch set is all 26 services in the four categories; the website shows all of them.
- Being in the launch set is not being bookable for work: a service is offered only when its SOP and hazard templates are active after competent review ([[OWN-DEC-17]]), insurance is confirmed, and the owner's go decision is given ([[OWN-DEC-22]]).
- RISK: the review chain covers 26 services instead of a few; leads may arrive for services that cannot yet be delivered.

Partly replaced by [[OWN-DEC-102]]: no price-book status is needed for a service to be offered.

Open:
- Competent review of each service's SOP and hazard templates (all services at once, per the owner): [[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]].
- Insurance coverage for each activity: [[OQ-INSURANCE-EVIDENCE]].
