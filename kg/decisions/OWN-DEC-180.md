Owner answer (round 8, question 3), option chosen "Approve all drafts now", subject to the publishing and quality requirements:

> Choose **Approve all drafts now**, subject to the following publishing and quality requirements.
>
> I authorize you to finalize and publish the 26 HandyHub service pages and their after-job care notes without requiring individual Owner approvals.
>
> Before publishing:
> 1. Review all 26 service pages for accuracy, clarity, professionalism, completeness, and consistency with HandyHub's approved business requirements.
> 2. Correct grammar, unnatural AI-generated wording, duplicated content, and inconsistent formatting.
> 3. Ensure every page has service-specific descriptions, inclusions, exclusions, estimate procedures, customer preparation guidance, FAQs, and after-job care instructions where applicable.
> 4. Do not invent prices, guarantees, warranties, certifications, licenses, insurance coverage, or service capabilities.
> 5. Keep uncertain or unverified claims hidden until verified. Do not silently treat AI-generated claims as established facts.
> 6. Ensure after-job care instructions are accurate, service-specific, and safe. Any safety-critical instructions requiring qualified review must remain unpublished until properly validated.
> 7. Verify all pages follow HandyHub's premium, professional home-improvement branding.
> 8. Ensure the customer-facing content is in natural American English.
> 9. Add appropriate internal links between related services and the estimate request form.
> 10. Make every page mobile-responsive, accessible, and optimized for appropriate local SEO.
> 11. Ensure service-area information is consistent with HandyHub's 11 approved Kentucky and Indiana counties.
> 12. Test navigation, buttons, service selection, forms, and page rendering.
> 13. Publish all validated content to private staging and promote eligible content to production using the existing deployment controls.
> 14. Do not leave completed, validated pages unnecessarily blocked by a redundant Owner approval requirement.
> 15. Provide a final report showing all 26 services, their publishing status, corrections made, and any content withheld for safety or factual verification.
>
> My approval applies to content that meets these requirements. It does not authorize publication of unsupported claims or unverified safety instructions.
>
> Proceed with implementation, validation, and deployment without requesting further routine content approvals.

Effect:
- The owner authorizes finalizing and publishing all 26 service pages and their after-job care notes without individual approvals, for content that meets the 15 requirements; it replaces the per-service owner approval of [[OWN-DEC-84]] (production) and of [[OWN-DEC-81]] (care notes).
- Before publishing: accuracy, clarity and consistency review; grammar and unnatural wording fixed; every page has its service-specific description, inclusions, exclusions, estimate procedure, preparation guidance, FAQs and care instructions where applicable; natural American English; internal links to related services and the request form; mobile, accessible and local-SEO ready; service-area text consistent with the 11 counties ([[OWN-DEC-179]]); navigation, buttons, service selection, forms and rendering tested.
- Never invented: prices, guarantees, warranties, certifications, licenses, insurance or capabilities. Uncertain or unverified claims stay hidden until verified. The decided 90-day workmanship warranty ([[FACT-WARRANTY]]) appears only as a registered claim.
- Care instructions must be accurate, service-specific and safe; product-dependent lines still come only from stored manufacturer guidance ([[OWN-DEC-81]]); any safety-critical instruction needing qualified review stays unpublished until validated ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]]).
- Validated content goes to private staging and, through the existing deployment controls, to production when the launch phases allow it ([[OWN-DEC-191]]); validated pages are not held back by a redundant owner approval.
- A final report lists all 26 services with their publishing status, the corrections made and anything withheld for safety or factual verification.
