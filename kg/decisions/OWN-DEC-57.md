Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-12): "I strongly agree with launching fewer, better pages. However, I'd distinguish between providing 2-3 authentic lines of local insight and creating an entire county landing page with only 2-3 lines. For a page to deserve its own URL, I'd include its real coverage, offered services, relevant communities, an authentic local introduction, and useful project or service details." "If you don't yet have sufficient material, keep the county on a well-designed, consolidated service-area page rather than generating a generic SEO landing page." Approved version: "Publish dedicated county pages only when authentic, location-specific information is available. Require at least 2-3 meaningful lines of original local context, supported by useful coverage and service details. Do not create repetitive, keyword-stuffed county pages. Keep other supported counties discoverable through the main service-area page until dedicated pages provide distinct value."

Effect:
- One consolidated Areas page carries a section per county with an anchor (towns, kinds of work, the request action) and the ZIP check.
- A dedicated county page exists only with at least two or three meaningful lines of original local context from the owner, plus real coverage and service details. None has been supplied yet.
- A county URL without its own page answers 301 to its anchor on the Areas page, so earlier county URLs never break.
- The sitemap lists only pages that exist; the footer links to the page or the anchor.
