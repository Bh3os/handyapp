Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-12): "Record the end-of-job walkthrough with the customer or their authorized representative. Outcomes: accepted, accepted with punch items, customer unavailable, or disputed. Record the participant, worker, timestamp, comments, and linked punch items. Customer unavailable is never treated as acceptance. Share the completion summary and approved photos through the secure project link so the customer can review them later. No response is not acceptance. Disputed work goes to the owner for review. Customer acceptance does not bypass required quality checks or close unresolved punch items. A signature is optional unless the approved service agreement requires it."

Effect:
- The walkthrough is recorded with the customer or their authorized representative, with one outcome: accepted, accepted with punch items, customer unavailable, or disputed; plus participant, worker, timestamp, comments and linked punch items.
- "Customer unavailable" and no response are never acceptance.
- The completion summary and approved photos are shared through the secure project link for later review.
- Disputed work goes to the owner for review.
- Customer acceptance never bypasses the quality review ([[OWN-DEC-64]]) and never closes an unresolved punch item.
- A signature is optional unless the approved service agreement requires it.

Reaffirmed by [[OWN-DEC-109]].
