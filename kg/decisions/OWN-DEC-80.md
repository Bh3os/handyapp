Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-12): "The worker may resolve a problem without waiting for the owner only when the fix is within their training, qualifications, approved scope, and approved SOP, with no unresolved safety hold. If resolved, record what happened, the steps taken, materials used, and how the result was checked. Add photos only when safe and notify the owner. If the problem is unsafe, uncertain, outside their authority, or cannot be resolved, stop affected work, keep people away, and call the owner. Perform only approved, safe stabilization steps within the worker's training. Suspected gas leaks require immediate evacuation and contacting emergency services/the gas utility from a safe location. Workers must not attempt gas repairs or handle exposed potentially live wiring. Emergency action must not wait for the owner."

Effect:
- A worker may resolve a problem on site without the owner only when the fix is within their training, qualifications, approved scope and approved SOP, and no safety hold is unresolved. The worker then records what happened, the steps, the materials used and how the result was checked, adds photos only when safe, and the owner is notified.
- If the problem is unsafe, uncertain, outside the worker's authority or cannot be resolved: the affected work stops, people are kept away and the worker calls the owner. Only the make-safe (stabilization) steps listed in the approved SOP and within the worker's training are recorded and allowed.
- A suspected gas leak means immediate evacuation and calling emergency services or the gas utility from a safe location. Workers never attempt gas repairs or handle exposed, potentially live wiring.
- A suspected gas leak or exposed potentially live wiring creates a critical hold in the same transaction as the report and never offers a resolve-on-site option.
- Emergency action never waits for the owner.
- The Start Job gate, stop-work rights and critical holds stay as strict as before or stricter.
