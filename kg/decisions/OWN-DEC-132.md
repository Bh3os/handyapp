Owner wording (2026-10-20): Q: "Estimate reminders: ... drafted at +3 days and a final one at +7 days ... Keep that?" Answer: "Faster: +24h / +3 days". Q: "Estimate reminder drafts (+24h, +3 days): should they also wait for contact hours 8 AM-10 PM like the others?" Answer: "No restriction".

Effect:
- Unanswered-estimate reminder drafts are prepared 24 hours and 3 days after the estimate is sent. This replaces the 3/7-day estimate cadence of [[OWN-DEC-78]].
- Staff still release each draft. Estimate reminders have no contact-hours restriction.
- The stop rules of OWN-DEC-78 stay: acceptance, decline, expiry or any customer response stops them.
