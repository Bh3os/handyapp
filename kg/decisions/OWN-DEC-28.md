Partly replaced by [[OWN-DEC-173]] (2026-10-21): estimates and change orders over $2,500.00 need the Owner's approval before publication.

Owner wording (project chat, 2026-10-04): "owner handles dispatch, estimating, customer service, approvals, and field work at launch. Owner has final approval authority. Keep specific monetary approval limits NOT CONFIGURED until the related pricing/payment policies are decided."

Effect:
- At launch the owner holds every office role (administration, dispatch, estimating, customer service) and also does field work.
- The owner has final approval authority.
- No monetary approval limit is configured or invented; a feature that needs one shows it as missing.
- Self-approval refusals stay in force even though one person holds all roles: nobody overrides or releases their own critical hold ([[OWN-DEC-38]]), the quality verifier is never the corrector, the collector never clears their own deposit. Safety gates are unchanged.
