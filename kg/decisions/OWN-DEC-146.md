Owner wording (2026-10-20): Q: "Upload limit for photos and documents: you said 15 MB. Keep 15 MB per file, and how many photos per request?" Answer: "15 MB per file, up to 10 photos".

Effect:
- Each uploaded photo or document may be up to 15 MB, implemented as 15 MiB so no file the owner allows is refused.
- A request accepts up to 10 photos.
