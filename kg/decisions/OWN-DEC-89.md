Owner, 2026-10-12, journey Step 1 rules.

Question asked:
> Step 1 rules: the site shows only facts you approved (no prices, times, guarantees, licenses, reviews, 24/7); payment methods, deposit, discounts and same-day charge appear only after you activate them; coverage is never a booking; English only; works on phones and at 200% text; staging hidden from search. OK?

Owner wording:
> Yes, with these clarifications:
>
> - Keep approved facts visible: free online or onsite estimates, individually priced jobs, and visits daily from 8 AM–10 PM by appointment. Do not promise completion times or 24/7 availability.
> - Do not invent prices, guarantees, credentials, or reviews. Use only genuine approved information.
> - Show only activated payment and business policies. Approved card wording names Square; no card surcharge applies. Keep any approved same-day service charge separate.
> - Coverage checks never confirm a booking. Customers may submit requests from outside or unknown areas for review.
> - Customer pages remain English; staff pages support English and Spanish.
> - Verify phone usability and 200% text zoom.
> - Keep staging behind its password gate and hidden from search, with all implemented features available for my testing.
>
> Publish completed edits to private staging for review; do not leave them hidden in a draft queue. Production remains unchanged.

Effect:
- Approved card wording names Square: where payment methods are listed the site says cards are taken with Square (for example "credit or debit cards (Square)"), and never mentions a surcharge ([[FACT-NO-SURCHARGE]]). This replaces the "keep the card provider unnamed" wording of [[OWN-DEC-67]]; the rest of OWN-DEC-67 (Square on staff phones, payments recorded by hand) stays.
- Any approved same-day service charge is shown separately from the card wording (same-day work is priced per job on the estimate, [[OWN-DEC-66]]).
- The site keeps the approved facts visible: free online or onsite estimates, individually priced jobs, visits daily 8 AM–10 PM by appointment ([[OWN-DEC-86]]). It never promises completion times or 24/7 availability and never invents prices, guarantees, credentials or reviews.
- Coverage checks never confirm a booking; requests from outside or unknown areas are accepted for review ([[OWN-DEC-88]]).
- Customer pages stay English; staff pages support English and Spanish ([[RULE-LANGUAGES]]).
- Phone usability and 200% text zoom are verified. Staging stays behind its password gate and hidden from search ([[OWN-DEC-73]]), with completed edits published there directly; production remains unchanged until [[OQ-GO-DECISION]].
- Under [[OWN-DEC-122]] the payment methods and policies the owner decided are active, so "only activated policies" means only the decided ones.
