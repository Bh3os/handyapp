Goal: Workers follow the job-specific checklist in six ordered stages, with evidence, pause, resume and handoff.

Scope:
- `tasks` instantiated from the pinned SOP version; stage order enforced; evidence per step; pause, resume, handoff.

Done when:
- Integration tests: evidence stays with its worker and work package; handoff keeps history.
- The `tested_by` scenarios pass for this slice; tests are tagged with the `implements` IDs.
- CI green: `secrets`, `test` (lint, typecheck, unit, integration) and `e2e`.
- Graph: this task set to `status: done`; `node tools/kg.mjs index && node tools/kg.mjs lint` reports 0 problems.

Built 2026-10-23 ([[OQ-CHECKLIST-DETAILS]] lists the PROPOSED details): `work.checklist_steps`, `work.checklist_step_events` and `work.checklist_handoffs` (migration 0083). The steps are the pinned SOP version's own; the database refuses a step out of stage order, a completion without the evidence the step asks for, a write by someone who did not start work on the job, and any write while the job or package is not in progress or a hold is open. Step pause and resume, skip and reopen with a reason, handoff notes; the history keeps every change with its worker. The staff page `/checklist/<job>` (EN and ES chrome; SOP text in English as imported) is linked from the job packet; the Owner's quality review shows each package's checklist summary. NOT built here: change-order-dependent steps ([[TSK-0081]]), offline ([[TSK-0079]]), job-level pause and resume ([[TSK-0094]]), the walkthrough and Finished ([[TSK-0083]]).
