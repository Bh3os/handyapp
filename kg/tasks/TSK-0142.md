From [[OWN-DEC-210]] and [[OWN-DEC-212]] (2026-10-22): the checklist includes the unpaid-invoice reminder scenarios (day 3, day 7, payment before dispatch, partial payment, dispute, payment plan, revised due date, voided invoice, duplicates, failed delivery and retry); no customer is invited before the Owner completes this phase.

Built (2026-10-22, on the mandatory-MFA branch TSK-0154; migration `0076_launch_phase.sql`):
- Launch phases: configuration `launch.phase` (owner_acceptance_testing, invited_customers, public), seeded phase 1 with every switch off; rules in `@handyhub/domain/launch`. `GET /staff/launch` and `PUT /staff/launch/phase` (Settings → Launch phase): the Owner only (policy_values.change, fresh sign-in, the Owner role checked again), one step forward or back to an earlier phase, the typed words of the move and a written approval, recorded as a policy version with the approval as its reason plus an audit event in one transaction. A database trigger refuses any other change (not an active Owner, no reason, a skipped phase). Nothing moves a phase automatically.
- Enforcement in production only (staging stays behind the access gate and noindex whatever its phase): before phase 3 the public request submission is refused (CONFLICT launch:SUBMISSIONS_CLOSED in phase 1, launch:INVITATION_REQUIRED in phase 2) and audited, the request form shows a closed notice (`submissions` in the form settings), every response is noindex, robots.txt disallows everything and the sitemap is not served. A phase that cannot be read is phase 1.
- Test accounts: Staging Tools → Test accounts (`POST /staff/staging/test-accounts`, staging only, the Owner with a fresh sign-in and a reason): Worker, Estimator, Office and Owner as invited staff accounts with a single-use invite link through the outbox; the Customer as a registered account through the real request, verification and account offer, designated reusable; the Guest has no account. All on example.test; no password is set, stored or returned. On staging the invite links and codes reach only TEST_EMAIL_DESTINATIONS (EMAIL_PROVIDER=log writes them to the private service log).
- `docs/owner-acceptance-testing.md` (the journey A–G with staging links and expected outcomes, the reminder scenarios of [[OWN-DEC-210]], results, the switches) and the GitHub issue template `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/owner-acceptance-issue.yml` for reporting problems.
- Tests: `packages/domain/test/launch.test.ts`, `apps/server/test/integration/launch-phase.test.ts`, `apps/server/test/integration/owner-acceptance.test.ts`, `apps/staff/test/LaunchPhase.test.tsx`, `apps/staff/test/StagingTools.test.tsx`, and two e2e tests in `apps/e2e/tests/staff-staging-tools.spec.ts`.
- NOT DONE: the checklist has not been walked on staging (staging does not run this repository yet, [[TSK-0112]]); the results table says so. Manual accessibility tests stay UNVERIFIED ([[OWN-DEC-181]]).
- NOT CONFIGURED: customer invitations: no way to invite a customer exists, so phase 2 accepts no public submission at all (fail closed) until a follow-up builds invitations. The test accounts' addresses must be added to TEST_EMAIL_DESTINATIONS by the Owner in Render.

PROPOSED (no owner decision covers these):
- The phase model: three phases with derived switches (invited customers on in phase 2 only; public submissions and search indexing on in phase 3 only), moves one step forward or back to any earlier phase, and the confirmation words `APPROVE PHASE 2`, `APPROVE PHASE 3`, `RETURN TO PHASE <n>`.
- The switches apply in production only; outside production the access gate (staging) or an isolated test system (local, ci) keeps everything testers-only and noindex, so a phase may be moved there to test the switch.
- Reading the phase is the Owner Dashboard's action (dashboard.view); moving it is policy_values.change; creating the test accounts is staff.invite. The indexing check keeps the phase for 5 seconds per process (cleared at once on the Owner's change).
- The test account addresses and names (`oat-<role>@example.test`, labelled fictional) and a second Owner account (`oat-owner@example.test`) on staging.
- The closed request form wording ("Online requests are not open yet").
