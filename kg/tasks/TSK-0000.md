Ordered plan that rebuilds both websites in this repository: the public site and customer portal on handyhub247.com and the staff app on staff.handyhub247.com ([[OWN-DEC-133]]), on [[ARCH-STACK]]. When every task is done, every active requirement is implemented and T01 to T75 pass. This note is the index; it is not a unit of work.

Phases:
1. **Foundation** ([[TSK-0001]] to [[TSK-0018]], 18 tasks): Monorepo, CI, deploy config, database, shared packages, audit, outbox, auth and the two origins.
2. **Public site** ([[TSK-0019]] to [[TSK-0032]], 14 tasks): Service catalog, coverage, homepage, legal and SMS pages, request wizard, uploads and receipt.
3. **Identity and portal** ([[TSK-0033]] to [[TSK-0039]], 7 tasks): Verification, project access, customer accounts, projections and the workspace tabs.
4. **Staff core** ([[TSK-0040]] to [[TSK-0051]], 12 tasks): Staff shell, action queue, requests, customers and properties, messaging and the unified inbox.
5. **Estimates** ([[TSK-0052]] to [[TSK-0060]], 9 tasks): Write or upload, publish, approval, receipt, deposit and terms.
6. **Scheduling** ([[TSK-0061]] to [[TSK-0067]], 7 tasks): Visits and time zones, capacity, windows held 24 hours, the board, changes and reminders.
7. **Field** ([[TSK-0068]] to [[TSK-0084]], 17 tasks): Packet, My Day, offline, Start Work gate, SOP checklist, photos, hazards and holds, change orders, completion, quality review.
8. **Invoicing and payments** ([[TSK-0085]] to [[TSK-0092]], 8 tasks): Invoices, payment records, Square links, cash and checks, exceptions, reconciliation, unpaid follow-up, after-completion.
9. **Cases, dashboard, staff and privacy** ([[TSK-0093]] to [[TSK-0101]], 9 tasks): Cases, Owner Dashboard, staff management, audit log, retention and privacy.
10. **Hardening and release** ([[TSK-0102]] to [[TSK-0112]], 11 tasks): Spanish staff catalogs, accessibility, performance, resilience, security review, traceability, runbook, staging.
11. **Later (P1/P2)** ([[TSK-0113]] to [[TSK-0118]], 6 tasks): Requirements that are P1, P2 or open, after the P0 release.

Ordering notes:
- Take the lowest-numbered `todo` task whose `depends_on` tasks are all `done`; run `node tools/kg.mjs ctx <TSK>` and build only what it links.
- Numbers give a valid order; tasks within a phase whose dependencies are done may run in parallel on separate branches.
- `implements` names the requirements a task fully delivers. Foundation tasks build shared mechanisms that later tasks finish, so cross-cutting requirements (authorization, transactions, security, traceability) are implemented in the hardening phase.
- `tested_by` lists the acceptance scenarios the task must make pass for its slice; the traceability task makes all of T01 to T75 pass together.
- Owner-blocked values stay NOT CONFIGURED in software and never block a merge; the release runbook lists the open owner actions.
- The last phase holds P1, P2 and open requirements; AI features stay disabled until the owner approves them ([[RULE-AI-WAVE2]]).
- Each task is one pull request of roughly 1 to 3 days of agent work; Claude merges it once the required CI checks pass, production releases stay with the owner.
