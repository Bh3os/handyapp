Goal: Invoice, payment, allocation, refund, fee and payout are separate records; balances never double-credit; Paid only at $0.00.

Scope:
- Extend `payments`, `payment_allocations`; add `refunds`, `fees`, `payouts`; balance computed from allocations.
- Financial actions as separate capabilities in `packages/policy`.

Done when:
- Integration tests: partial, deposit and overpayment cases; over-allocation refused; Paid only at zero balance.
- The `tested_by` scenarios pass for this slice; tests are tagged with the `implements` IDs.
- CI green: `secrets`, `test` (lint, typecheck, unit, integration) and `e2e`.
- Graph: this task set to `status: done`; `node tools/kg.mjs index && node tools/kg.mjs lint` reports 0 problems.

Built (2026-10-22, migration 0077): `finance.payment_allocations` now target an invoice or a deposit requirement (exactly one); `finance.refunds` (refunds, credits and write-offs: requested by staff, approved by the Owner only from the Owner's own live session), `finance.payouts` and `finance.provider_fees` (Square, recorded by hand) are separate records. An invoice's invoiced, credits, confirmed, pending and remaining amounts are computed from them; the database refuses an allocation or credit beyond the invoice and a refund beyond what its payment can still give, and keeps the state in line with the money: Paid exactly when confirmed payments and credits reach $0.00 (`invoice.paid`, once; the receipt message it triggers is [[TSK-0087]]). Recording a verified payment on an issued invoice applies it under the invoice's lock; an overpayment's remainder is unallocated money for the Owner (the `unallocated_credit` item in Owner Decisions). Financial capabilities: `payout.record` and `fee.record` (PROPOSED, in the reconcile row) beside the existing finance actions in `packages/policy`. Tests: partial, deposit, pending-check and concurrent overpayment cases; over-allocation refused; Paid only at zero. The refund, credit, void and write-off workflows are [[TSK-0089]].
