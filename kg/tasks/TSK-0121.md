Goal: Routine reminders send automatically on the cadence of [[OWN-DEC-153]] instead of waiting as staff-released drafts.

Scope:
- New `reminders.cadences` configuration version from [[FACT-REMINDER-CADENCES]]: one estimate reminder 3 days after successful delivery per version; one question reminder 3 days after the notice's delivery; the appointment reminder 24 hours before every confirmed visit; all automatic. Remove the +24 h estimate draft and the 24 h / 48 h question drafts; turn existing waiting drafts into scheduled automatic messages or cancel them with a reason.
- Cancel on every status change that ends the condition; recalculate after reschedules; no "24-hour" reminder for a visit booked less than 24 hours ahead; idempotency keys; staff see scheduled, sent and cancelled reminders.
- "Closed—no response" follows the single reminder (PROPOSED reading, [[OQ-READINGS-2026-10-21]]) and [[OWN-DEC-197]] E4.
- Update the policy-config integration test that asserts the old cadence values.

Done when:
- Timing tests (3 days, 24 hours, Eastern and Central, DST changes), cancellation and duplicate tests; no reminder for approved, rejected, expired, withdrawn, cancelled or superseded records.
- The `tested_by` scenarios pass for this slice; tests are tagged with the `implements` IDs.
- CI green: `secrets`, `test` (lint, typecheck, unit, integration) and `e2e`.
- Graph: this task set to `status: done`; `node tools/kg.mjs index && node tools/kg.mjs lint` reports 0 problems.

**Confirmed by [[OWN-DEC-208]] (2026-10-22):** the "Closed—no response" reading is final: "Closed—no response" is available 24 hours after the single question reminder was actually sent; not if that reminder failed to deliver (staff follow-up instead), the customer replied, an appointment exists, work was accepted or a payment was recorded.

**Built (2026-10-22):** migration 0075 records `reminders.cadences` version 2 ([[FACT-REMINDER-CADENCES]], [[OWN-DEC-153]], [[OWN-DEC-157]]: estimate 72 hours after delivery, question 72 hours after the notice with "Closed—no response" 24 hours after the reminder was sent, appointment 24 hours before; satisfaction, unpaid-invoice and repeat-service values unchanged and staff-released) and `messages.automatic` version 3 with the three reminders ([[FACT-AUTOMATIC-MESSAGES]] rows 22-24). Each reminder is a scheduled automatic delivery: one queued row per destination, due at its cadence, sent by its `notify.release` job after the send-time recheck of its record ([[HH-COM-05]]), in the owner's wording with its values read at send time ([[OWN-DEC-151]]; never an amount or the address, [[OWN-DEC-158]]). The estimate reminder is scheduled from the first successful delivery of the version's estimate-ready notice, the question reminder from the notice's successful delivery (a failed notice never starts either clock), the appointment reminder from the confirming or rescheduling event (recalculated per window version; `booked_within_interval` when booked less than 24 hours ahead). Every change that ends a condition cancels the waiting rows in its own transaction with the reason (accepted, declined, expired, withdrawn, superseded, a customer response, message or question reply, a cancellation, a reschedule, On my way); the database backstop of migration 0060 stays. The delivery tuple and its provider key send each reminder once per subject and destination. Staff see each reminder scheduled, sent, failed (a delivery issue with Retry) or cancelled on the estimate, the board's visit and the request's questions; the release and dismiss routes and the reminder queue items are gone. "Closed—no response" follows the single reminder's actual send ([[OWN-DEC-208]]) and checks accepted work, appointments and recorded payments ([[OWN-DEC-197]] E4). Waiting drafts were cancelled as `replaced_by_automatic` and every open version, question and visit re-planned. Texts stay off: no reminder text wording is released, so a text is recorded as `template_not_configured` with a delivery issue until A2P 10DLC ([[TSK-0133]]).

PROPOSED readings (no owner rule): a reminder goes to every destination of the customer (the email, and the mobile number, whose text needs the SMS opt-in, rechecked at send), as the automatic notices of [[TSK-0120]] do, replacing the "preferred channel only" reading of the drafts; the estimate reminder's clock is the first successful delivery of the estimate-ready notice on any channel; a reminder released or sent before this task counts as the record's one reminder (no second one), and so does a question's first reminder for closing; a visit already within 24 hours at migration time gets no late reminder; nobody stops an automatic reminder by hand (its conditions do); the booking time of a visit is when its confirming or rescheduling event was committed; a reminder the provider accepted reads "Sent"; an automatic reminder cancelled before any send attempt is erased with its estimate like an unreleased draft; accepted estimates and recorded payments are obligation facts (also for Not a Fit, [[OWN-DEC-100]]), and a returned or voided payment still counts; the single question reminder's send is recorded as the question's final reminder.

OPEN QUESTION (legal wording, human review): the published SMS terms still say "Up to two reminders" about an unanswered estimate or question (`legal.sms.messages.reminderEstimate`, `reminderQuestion`). One automatic reminder stays within that wording, so the legal text was not changed; whether to reword it is for the owner and the legal reviewer.
