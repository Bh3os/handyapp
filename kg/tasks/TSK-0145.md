Goal: Apply the amendments of [[OWN-DEC-193]] and [[OWN-DEC-194]] to confirmation and offers.

Scope:
- A1: holds keep blocking ordinary visits; an Owner-authorized hazard-evaluation inspection visit (qualified person and safety conditions recorded) may be confirmed while the hold stays open (PROPOSED reading).
- A2: estimate-visit offers and confirmations no longer require an approved job; work visits still do.
- A4: follow-up visits on completed jobs only with a recorded contractual, warranty, punch-list or return-work authorization.
- B1: the 24-hour clock starts at successful publication to the customer; publication time and exact expiry stored.
- B3/B4: one active offer per visit, no overlaps, capacity held across all windows and released on selection; withdraw or replace only with a reason and a customer notice; no silent edits to a published offer.
