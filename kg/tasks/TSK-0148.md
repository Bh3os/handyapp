Goal: Apply [[OWN-DEC-197]] to staff questions and no-response handling.

Scope:
- E2: staff mark any question as allowing "I'm not sure" / "I don't know" (not only measurements).
- E4: closing checks the reminder policy, the reminder's actual delivery state and no accepted work, appointment or payment; delivery failures open a staff review item. Coordinate with the single-reminder cadence of [[TSK-0121]].
- E5: after the 30-day reopen period, staff may reopen with authorization, or the customer is guided to a new request.

Done when:
- Tests for each rule, including a queued-but-unsent reminder that never allows closing.
- The `tested_by` scenarios pass for this slice; tests are tagged with the `implements` IDs.
- CI green: `secrets`, `test` (lint, typecheck, unit, integration) and `e2e`.
- Graph: this task set to `status: done`; `node tools/kg.mjs index && node tools/kg.mjs lint` reports 0 problems.

**Confirmed by [[OWN-DEC-208]] (2026-10-22):** E4 final wording: "Closed—no response" is available 24 hours after the single question reminder was actually sent; not if that reminder failed to deliver (staff follow-up instead), the customer replied, an appointment exists, work was accepted or a payment was recorded. Tests for each exclusion.

Done (2026-10-26): migration 0110. E2: `intake.questions.not_sure_allowed`, set by staff when publishing (`notSureAllowed`, always true for a measurement, immutable); the portal offers "I'm not sure" and the server accepts it only there (`not_sure_not_offered`). E4: the close check, shared by the staff view and `POST /staff/requests/:id/actions/close-no-response`, counts a reminder only while one of its deliveries is sent or delivered, from its earliest such send (`FINAL_REMINDER_NOT_SENT` for queued or scheduled, `REMINDER_FAILED`, `TOO_EARLY`, `CUSTOMER_REPLIED`, `OWNER_REVIEW_REQUIRED`); the request machine refuses closing while a customer response stands. The `questions.reminder_outcome` automation opens the request's `question_reminder` item as `question_reminder_failed` when every delivery of a reminder failed (at send time or a later bounce) and resolves it once one is sent; `questions.close_available` never says "may close" then. E5: `POST /staff/requests/:id/actions/reopen` (`request.reopen`, the request-handling row; a written reason; the `reopen_by_staff` event; the decision `reopen` in the history), within or after the reopen period; the portal points to a new request after it, also when a reply is refused for it. Staff app: the "I'm not sure" option in the composer, the failed-reminder hint and Reopen request (EN and ES). The PROPOSED details are in [[OQ-QUESTION-AMENDMENT-DETAILS]].
