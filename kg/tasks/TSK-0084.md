From [[TSK-0085]] (2026-10-22): the final invoice's issue already calls `finance.invoice_quality_gate(job)` (migration 0077), which answers `quality_review_not_recorded` until this task replaces its body with the review's outcome: `passed` (Owner or Office may issue), `owner_may_issue` (only minor punch items: the Owner only) or `blocked` (major defects). Nothing else in invoicing changes.

Built 2026-10-23 ([[OQ-QUALITY-REVIEW-DETAILS]] lists the PROPOSED details): `quality.reviews`, `quality.punch_items` and `quality.review_events` (migration 0082). The review screen opens from the Owner Decisions item (`/quality-reviews/<job>`): what to review, the four area results, punch items with kind, severity, work package or area, required evidence, worker and due date, corrections, re-checks and the history. `finance.invoice_quality_gate` now answers from the review (`quality.review_gate`): not recorded, passed, Owner only with minor items, blocked with a major one. The database makes a job operationally complete only when its review passed and refuses to close it while a punch item is not re-checked. Open punch items stay in the action queue with their due dates and stay visible after an invoice; the customer sees "Follow-up work".
