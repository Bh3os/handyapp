Scope:

Done (2026-10-26, migration 0122): workers write internal notes (`POST /staff/jobs/:jobId/notes`, `job.add_note`: the project's internal conversation) and proposed customer replies (`comms.reply_proposals`, `POST /staff/jobs/:jobId/reply-proposals`, kept as a draft or sent for review) from an assigned job, linked from My Day; My Day lists their own proposals with the status Draft, Awaiting Review, Sent or Rejected, the reviewer's open question and its answer form (`/staff/reply-proposals/mine`, `.../clarification-answer`). A submitted proposal opens the Owner/Office queue item `reply_proposal`; the review page (`/reply-proposals/<id>`) shows the worker's original as internal and lets the reviewer ask for clarification, reject with a reason, or write the English message and send it with explicit approval as their own staff reply ([[HH-COM-08]] rules, English template). The original stays with the proposal, which names the sent message, and beside the reply on the staff conversation; the history is append-only (`comms.reply_proposal_events`) and audited without texts. The database refuses a decision or send by anyone but a live Owner or Office grant, any change to a sent or rejected proposal, and an internal message in a customer conversation.
