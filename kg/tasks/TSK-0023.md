Goal: County content only for the approved service area, a truthful sitemap, and private routes kept out of search.

Scope:
- `/areas` with one anchored section per county; dedicated county pages only with approved local content; 301 from county URLs to anchors.
- `sitemap.xml` of existing public pages only; `robots.txt`; `noindex` on private routes; 404 and 500 pages; structured data naming the counties.
- `tools/content-lint.mjs` in CI: flags availability, response-time, review, credential and price claims not in an allowlist of approved wording.

- e2e: unverified county URL redirects to its anchor and is absent from the sitemap; content lint passes.
