Scope:
- Wire the automatic templates whose events exist: `request_received`, `secure_access_link` (24-hour link, reissue, [[OWN-DEC-160]]), `estimate_visit_options`, `estimate_visit_confirmed`, `estimate_approval_confirmation` (link only, [[OWN-DEC-155]]), `work_appointment_windows` ([[OWN-DEC-156]]), `work_appointment_confirmed`, `visit_cancelled` (a committed cancellation only, never a reschedule; it cancels pending reminders), `estimate_follow_up` (when a staff answer to an estimate question is released), `staff_reply_wrapper` (on Send), `staff_customer_replied` (authorized assigned staff only, deduplicated, grouped), `account_email_changed` (old and new verified address; this unblocks the email change of TSK-0036).
- Every update shows in the portal immediately, independent of delivery.
- Production delivery stays off until the owner authorizes it; texts stay off until A2P 10DLC ([[OWN-DEC-190]]).

- The `tested_by` scenarios pass for this slice; tests are tagged with the `implements` IDs.
- CI green: `secrets`, `test` (lint, typecheck, unit, integration) and `e2e`.
- Graph: this task set to `status: done`; `node tools/kg.mjs index && node tools/kg.mjs lint` reports 0 problems.
