Goal: The routine transactional messages of [[OWN-DEC-152]] send automatically when their committed events occur, with staging test destinations only.

Scope:
- Wire the automatic templates whose events exist: `request_received`, `secure_access_link` (24-hour link, reissue, [[OWN-DEC-160]]), `estimate_visit_options`, `estimate_visit_confirmed`, `estimate_approval_confirmation` (link only, [[OWN-DEC-155]]), `work_appointment_windows` ([[OWN-DEC-156]]), `work_appointment_confirmed`, `visit_cancelled` (a committed cancellation only, never a reschedule; it cancels pending reminders), `estimate_follow_up` (when a staff answer to an estimate question is released), `staff_reply_wrapper` (on Send), `staff_customer_replied` (authorized assigned staff only, deduplicated, grouped), `account_email_changed` (old and new verified address; this unblocks the email change of TSK-0036).
- Every update shows in the portal immediately, independent of delivery.
- Production delivery stays off until the owner authorizes it; texts stay off until A2P 10DLC ([[OWN-DEC-190]]).

Done when:
- Scenarios A, B, C, D, G (email part), H and I of [[OWN-DEC-152]] pass on staging test accounts; each trigger is suppressed when its conditions are unmet; double clicks, webhook redelivery, concurrent workers and restarts produce one message.
- The `tested_by` scenarios pass for this slice; tests are tagged with the `implements` IDs.
- CI green: `secrets`, `test` (lint, typecheck, unit, integration) and `e2e`.
- Graph: this task set to `status: done`; `node tools/kg.mjs index && node tools/kg.mjs lint` reports 0 problems.

From [[OWN-DEC-209]] (2026-10-22): the seven added templates send automatically only after their event happened (legitimate code request; published question; officially published estimate version; issued invoice; formal decline by authorized staff; confirmed revised appointment; On my way for a confirmed visit by the assigned worker), idempotently, under preferences, consent and quiet hours; codes are single-use, short-lived, rate-limited and never logged.

**Built (2026-10-22):** the resolvers of the automatic messages whose events exist supply the values of their owner-worded (or owner-authorized, [[OWN-DEC-209]]) email templates, so they render and go through the one outbox and delivery path ([[HH-ARC-08]]): request received; secure access link (with its 24-hour expiry, also on reissue); estimate-visit options; estimate visit confirmed; not a fit; estimate ready; estimate approval confirmation (link only); work-appointment windows (publishing a work-visit offer now writes `work_visit.windows_published`); work appointment confirmed; appointment changed; visit cancelled (a committed cancellation only; its transaction already cancels the visit's waiting reminders); on the way; account email changed (old and new address; the sign-in email change is available); the staff reply email (values read from the reply at send time); the staff notice of a customer reply (the responsible staff member only, with an active account and a role that may see the record; one notice per open reply queue item, so replies are grouped; a portal or inbound message writes `conversation.customer_replied` when it opens the item). The one-time code and the question notice were already wired. Every message stays one delivery per subject and destination (retries, redelivered events and concurrent workers send once); a missing value is a visible failure, never a send. Texts: no text wording is released, so every text is recorded as `template_not_configured` until A2P 10DLC ([[TSK-0133]]); codes and access links are never held by hours or reminder queues. A reissue also needs the access message on the automatic list.

Not wired, because their events do not exist yet: `estimate_follow_up` (no estimate-question record; a staff answer goes out as the staff reply), `deposit_invoice` ([[TSK-0085]]), `proposed_change` ([[TSK-0082]]), `invoice_ready` (no invoice issuing yet), `job_complete` ([[TSK-0084]]), `payment_receipt` ([[TSK-0087]]); `work_paused` stays Owner-released ([[TSK-0077]]). The reminders are [[TSK-0121]], delivery hours [[TSK-0122]].

PROPOSED readings (no owner rule): a visit's customer-facing reference is `<project reference>-V<n>`; a job is named by its project reference; times show the zone's short name at that instant (EDT, CST) in the visit's or project's display zone ([[OWN-DEC-159]]); the support contact of the account notice is the configured public phone and email, or the labelled placeholders of [[FACT-PUBLIC-CONTACT]] while they are NOT CONFIGURED; the staff notice of a reply names the project's state label in the staff member's language.
