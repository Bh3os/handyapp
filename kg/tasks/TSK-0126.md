Goal: The three site checks of [[OWN-DEC-167]] gate the first visit's confirmation.

Scope:
- Three checkboxes with the address, site contact and coverage result shown beside them (Spanish staff text); persisted with verifier, time and versions; rechecked when the address or contact changes.
- SERVED confirmed by staff; EDGE or OUTSIDE needs the Owner's exception approval with a reason; nobody overrides legal, licensing or safety restrictions.
- The server's confirmation transaction refuses a first visit while any check is missing and names it.

- Tests: valid and invalid addresses, missing or unauthorized contacts, coverage exceptions, changed address and contact, blocked confirmation, owner-approved exception, audit and role permissions.

Built (2026-10-25, migration 0106):
- The three checks on the project's visits, each a checkbox with what it rests on, in English and Spanish: (1) the address-and-county check of [[TSK-0127]] for the current address version, after an automated pre-validation (PO box street, mailing ZIP, ZIP of another state); (2) the site contact, the customer prefilled from their record or a person they named, with the authority staff confirm (give access, coordinate the visit; never money or contracts), versioned; (3) the coverage of the verified county, SERVED accepted by the Owner or Office, OUTSIDE only with the Owner's exception and reason (`visit.approve_coverage_exception`, a new Owner-only [[ROLE-MATRIX]] row).
- Every confirmation path (staff confirmation, window offers, the customer's selection) names each missing check (COUNTY_NOT_VERIFIED, SITE_CONTACT_MISSING, SITE_CONTACT_NOT_AUTHORIZED, SITE_CONTACT_CHANGED, COVERAGE_NOT_ACCEPTED, COUNTY_OUTSIDE_AREA); the database refuses the confirmation without them and records the checks used on the visit. An exception never lifts a hold, the approval or the deposit. Every check is append-only and audited (no phone or email in the audit).
