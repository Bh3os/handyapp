Goal: Business changes and their events commit together; background consumers are idempotent, bounded and visible when they fail.

Scope:
- `outbox_events` and `jobs` tables; `outbox.add(tx, event)` inside `withTx`.
- In-process worker with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, scheduled `run_at`, bounded exponential retry, a `failed` state exposed for the staff queue, and a reconciliation sweep.
- Automation declaration (trigger, identity, inputs, idempotency key, retry, exception path) required for every consumer.

Done when:
- Integration tests: rollback leaves no event; a consumer run twice has one effect; exhausted retries end in `failed`.
- CI green: `secrets`, `test` (lint, typecheck, unit, integration) and `e2e`.
