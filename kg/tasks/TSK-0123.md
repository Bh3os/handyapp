Goal: Apply [[OWN-DEC-159]] everywhere: business time America/Kentucky/Louisville, appointment times in the property's zone.

Scope:
- New `business.timezone` configuration version citing [[OWN-DEC-159]] without the PROPOSED flag (update the policy-config test that expects `proposed: true`).
- Store the property's IANA zone from its verified location; unknown zones are flagged and time-sensitive messages wait for verification; every staff screen labels the zone.
- Appointment dates, windows, options, reminders and expiries shown in the property's zone; estimate validity stored as an explicit expiry timestamp; the response clock in business hours.

Done when:
- Integration and e2e tests with synthetic Eastern and Central properties, DST gaps and repeated hours, 24-hour holds and reminder delays.
- The `tested_by` scenarios pass for this slice; tests are tagged with the `implements` IDs.

Built (2026-10-22, migration 0066; final owner amendments 2026-10-22):
- `business.timezone` version 2, America/Kentucky/Louisville, citing [[OWN-DEC-159]], not PROPOSED.
- A property's zone is unknown, derived or verified, with its basis and counties, mirrored on the property from an append-only history. Derived only when every county of the ZIP code maps to one zone in `geo.county_time_zones`, which is NOT CONFIGURED ([[OQ-COUNTY-TIME-ZONES]]); otherwise unknown, flagged in the staff queue (`property_time_zone`) and never assumed Eastern. Office and the Owner verify it on the property record from the county of the verified address (Eastern or Central zones only, read from the time-zone data). An address change derives it again. The zone the existing properties carried was the withdrawn assumption: recorded in their history, now unknown.
- No visit is planned on an unknown zone (PROPERTY_ZONE_UNKNOWN); windows, gaps and repeated hours are resolved in the property's zone; staff screens name the zone; the portal shows offer holds in the property's zone and the estimate's end of validity (computed in business time, stored as an explicit timestamp) in the property's zone, or as business time, said so, while it is unknown.
- Texts: the recipient's own zone is recorded on the contact (never taken from the property); unknown is flagged on the customer record and resolves to the configured fallback `sms.unknown_zone_hours` (9 AM–9 PM Eastern, final owner amendments 2026-10-22, reading 8). Applying the windows to deliveries stays with [[TSK-0122]]; authentication codes never pass through quiet hours.
- Holds and reminder delays are elapsed hours on the UTC timeline (tested across both DST changes in Eastern and Central).
