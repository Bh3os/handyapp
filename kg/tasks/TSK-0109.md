- The `tested_by` scenarios pass for this slice; tests are tagged with the `implements` IDs.

Done (2026-10-26, no migration): the review was carried out by AI agents, as [[OWN-DEC-136]] decides; no human reviewer took part and no human sign-off is claimed. The review, its threat model, its findings and their fixes are in `docs/security/review-2026.md` (release evidence). Thirteen findings were fixed, each pinned by a tagged test (`apps/server/test/integration/security-review.test.ts` and the tests the review names):
- AVAIL-1 (High): a write route held a transaction's connection while its handler took a second one from a pool that waited forever, so ten parallel sign-ins (no session needed) stopped the API until a restart; the server's pools now wait at most 10 seconds (PROPOSED).
- LINK-1, AVAIL-2, CONF-2, MON-1 (Medium): staff invite and reset tokens are masked in request logs and leave the staff app's address bar and history once read; `/readyz` no longer opens a connection per call; production refuses to start without a measured `TRUST_PROXY_HOPS` or without two hosts (CONF-1); the Owner's system health screen shows security signals ([[HH-SEC-07]]).
- AUTH-1, LOG-2, LOG-3, AI-1, AI-2, AI-3, SEC-1 (Low): staff sign-in, second-factor and re-authentication attempts are counted before they are checked; logged database errors lose the fields that quote values; the 404 answer no longer echoes the query; the disabled AI routes are rate limited and AI redaction covers Spanish code words, codes a few words after their word and street addresses (AI stays off, [[RULE-AI-WAVE2]]); CI scans the built bundles for secrets.
Residual items and PROPOSED values: [[OQ-SECURITY-REVIEW-DETAILS]].
