Built (2026-10-26, migration 0123): a service's default is text the Owner approves for it, kept as versions (`catalog.preparation_defaults`): the reviewed "Getting ready" steps of its approved page version ([[TSK-0132]]) as they are, or the Owner's edit; the Owner may remove it. No default is seeded: every service is NOT CONFIGURED until the Owner approves one on the settings screen (`service_content.approve`, the Owner only). The offer form is prefilled with the defaults of the visit's services and names the services without one; staff may change the text, and the offer keeps exactly what was sent with its basis (default, edited, staff, none) and the default versions it started from; the confirmed visit shows it. The unsafe-instruction rules (`@handyhub/domain` preparation.ts: hazardous materials, licensed trade work, structural work, heights and confined spaces, a hazard inspection) refuse unsafe text when an offer is saved, when a draft is published and when the Owner approves a default; staff see the same check while typing.
