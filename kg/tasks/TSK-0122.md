**Built (2026-10-22):** `messages.delivery_hours` (migration 0075, [[OWN-DEC-154]], [[OWN-DEC-152]] sections 3.3-3.4, [[OWN-DEC-207]]) replaces the PROPOSED `replies.contact_hours` (superseded): emails, automatic messages and released replies go at once at any hour; a non-urgent customer text goes only inside `contact.hours` (8 AM–10 PM) in the recipient's verified zone, or inside `sms.unknown_zone_hours` (9 AM–9 PM Eastern) while that zone is unknown. A text the send finds outside its window stays queued with its next allowed time (`waiting_text_hours`, shown as scheduled) and its job runs again then, rechecking the record, consent, opt-out, the template and the window; an obsolete one is cancelled, an opt-out suppresses it. The delivery records the window it waited for or was sent in (`text_window`: recipient zone, unknown-zone fallback, not held). Codes (authentication), account security notices, the secure access link and the On my way notice ([[FACT-HOURS]]) and staff notices never wait. The composer says the email goes at once and when a text would go, naming an unverified zone. `sms.live_delivery` (off, [[OWN-DEC-190]]) keeps every text from reaching a carrier: such a text is recorded as failed (`sms_disabled`), never as sent. While the rule, the hours or the fallback is NOT CONFIGURED a text is not sent (`text_hours_not_configured`) and an email still goes.

PROPOSED readings (no owner rule): the live-text switch holds back carrier adapters only; the local and ci fakes and the staging log sender intercept every message and reach no one, so they are not live texts; an unknown zone is flagged on the held text (its `text_window`) and in the composer, with no separate verification queue item (staff record the contact's zone, [[TSK-0123]]); the checks run in the order template, sender, consent, live switch, values, window, so nothing waits that could never be sent; staff notices are not held to a customer's hours.
