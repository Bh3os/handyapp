Goal: Organization accounts (for example landlords or hosts) with explicit memberships and project scopes.

Scope:
- Organization, membership and scope tables; membership changes audited; access checked per project.

Done when:
- Integration tests: a member sees only projects in scope; removal revokes access.
- CI green: `secrets`, `test` (lint, typecheck, unit, integration) and `e2e`.
- Graph: this task set to `status: done`; `node tools/kg.mjs index && node tools/kg.mjs lint` reports 0 problems.

Built (2026-10-26, migration 0125): a signed-in account sets up an organization on Organizations (`/portal/organizations`) and administers it: it shares projects its own account is linked to, invites exact email addresses, each with a scope of shared projects and the approve and pay privileges, changes access, withdraws invitations, removes members and sees the append-only history. The account that signed in with the invited address accepts or declines the invitation; members find the projects in scope on their dashboard ("Shared with you by …") and leave when they want. What a member reaches is read on every request (scope, the organization's sharing, the administrator's own link), so every removal ends access on the next request; the policy's organization table limits a member to reading, plus approving and paying with those privileges. Closing a login account ends its memberships and those of the organizations it administers. The PROPOSED details are in [[OQ-ORGANIZATION-ACCOUNTS-DETAILS]].
