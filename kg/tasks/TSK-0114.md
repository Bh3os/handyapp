The Owner or Office records the schedule the customer agreed to on the project (`/staff/repeat-service/<project>`, from the request record): the next service date and how often, in the property's zone, or one date; nothing is suggested. Each occurrence is a new request with its own records and gates, linked to its date by staff; nothing is copied. A reminder draft is prepared on each occurrence's date for staff to release; its wording is NOT CONFIGURED, so none can be sent yet; an open case holds it and an opt-out of optional follow-ups stops it, rechecked at send. The customer turns reminders off or on, changes or stops the schedule in the project workspace; a change applies beyond the 24-hour notice and keeps the dates inside it.
