**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the walkthrough and Finished?

- **Who answers:** the owner.
- **Decided, in use:** the walkthrough outcomes "Everything looks good", "Accepted with follow-up items", "I have a concern" and "Customer unavailable", with comments and the identity of the customer or authorized representative; absence or silence is never acceptance; disputed work goes to the Owner; Finished sends the job to "Awaiting quality review", never complete; before and after photos per work area; a skipped required step or missing evidence blocks Finished ([[OWN-DEC-75]], [[OWN-DEC-109]], [[FACT-SVC-PHOTO-MINIMUM]]).
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - One walkthrough and one Finished per job, recorded together by any assigned worker who started work on the job; afterwards nobody edits them. Return work after the review is [[TSK-0094]].
  - Finished needs, for every work package: every required checklist step completed (none skipped, none open), at least one before and one after photo that processing has not rejected (counted per work package: "work area" is read as the work package); no review, job-level or critical safety hold open; the job in progress.
  - "I have a concern" opens a dispute case (restricted, Owner-notified, [[OWN-DEC-124]]) with the worker's comments, and the job still goes to the Owner's review. Comments are required for a concern and for follow-up items.
  - The participant's standing: the customer, or an authorized representative. No signature is captured (optional unless the approved service agreement requires one; none does yet).
  - Finished marks the job's work packages complete and opens the Owner's quality review ([[TSK-0084]]). The customer sees "Completion review".
- **Waiting for later tasks or the owner:** the customer's later review of the completion summary through the project link beyond the status shown now; the job-complete message (MESSAGE 16), which [[OWN-DEC-152]] sends at the authorized final completion; the upload-failure exception's pending photos ([[OWN-DEC-68]]) do not count as photos until uploaded.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code.
