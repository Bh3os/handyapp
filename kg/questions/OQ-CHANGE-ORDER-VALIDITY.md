**Answered by [[OWN-DEC-168]] (2026-10-21).**

**Question:** how long may a customer approve a published change order before it expires?

- **Who answers:** the owner (wording reviewed with [[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]).
- **Known:** estimates are valid 14 days ([[FACT-ESTIMATE-VALIDITY]]); change orders follow the estimate approval rules ([[OWN-DEC-108]]); no period is set for them.
- **Until answered:** a change order stays approvable until it is superseded or withdrawn, and the server always rejects a stale version; no expiry date is invented on the document.
