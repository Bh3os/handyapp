**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the escalation procedure in every job packet?

- **Who answers:** the owner; the escalation wording and its Spanish text also with the qualified safety reviewer ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]], [[OWN-DEC-186]]).
- **Decided, in use:** owner first for every problem that is not life-threatening; 911 at once, without waiting for anyone's approval, for an actual or reasonably suspected immediate threat to life or serious injury; the Owner is told as soon as it is safe ([[OWN-DEC-172]]). Every packet shows "Call Owner — Report a Problem" and "Call 911 — Immediate Emergency"; Call 911 always works without a network. The Owner's number is read from the Owner's staff profile, where the Owner enters it, and is never stored in the repository; while it is empty Call Owner says NOT CONFIGURED and to call 911 in an emergency ([[HH-PKT-09]]). With the Owner unreachable and no emergency the affected work stays paused; there is no backup contact; Call 911 is never gated by the Owner ([[OWN-DEC-208]] reading 10). The Spanish staff portal shows the owner's Spanish instruction exactly as given. Escalation actions are recorded.
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - **Where the number lives.** On the Owner's own account (My account, "Escalation phone number"), in its own table (`identity.staff_escalation_phones`, migration 0099) so no staff query returns it by accident. Entering or clearing it uses the Owner's "Business details" row of the permission matrix (`business_details.edit`) with a fresh sign-in and the version shown, no written reason (it is the Owner's own profile). Only an account with a live Owner grant holds a number (the database refuses any other); clearing keeps the row without a number. The change is audited with the state and the versions, never the number.
  - **Format.** A North American number, typed as (502) 555-0100, 502-555-0100, 502.555.0100 or +1 502 555 0100, stored as +1 and ten digits and shown as (502) 555-0100. Anything else is refused, never guessed.
  - **Which number.** The number of an active account with a live Owner grant; with more than one, the one entered last. A disabled account's number is not used.
  - **Not part of the packet version.** The number is read whenever a packet is read, so entering or changing it makes no new packet version and asks nobody to acknowledge again.
  - **Who sees it.** Everyone who may open the job's packet or its Stop Work reports (the assigned workers, Office, the Estimator, the Owner). The phone keeps the last answer for Call Owner offline (browser storage, like the offline packet copy) and removes it at sign-out. The printed packet and its PDF show it, so the paper copy works without the app.
  - **Where the actions are.** On every job screen that has Stop Work (the My Day visit card, the packet, the loadout, the checklist, the Start Work briefing and the holds review): Call 911 first, then Call Owner with the number (or NOT CONFIGURED), then "I could not reach the Owner". The packet's communication section also states the procedure and the Owner-unreachable rule. A phone that never received the number says so ("not on this phone yet"), never NOT CONFIGURED.
  - **Recording the taps.** A Call Owner, Call 911 or "I could not reach the Owner" tap is recorded after the call started (the phone link is never held back): the phone's UUID, who, the job and, when known, the visit and the Stop Work report, the phone's time and the server's (`work.escalation_calls`, append-only, recorded once per UUID; offline it waits on the phone and is sent later). Whoever may stop work may record one (`hazard.report` row). The Owner sees every recorded tap of a job with its reports; everyone else their own. Each is audited (`hazard.record_escalation_call`). They are job and safety records for retention.
  - **Owner unreachable.** The record and the owner's rule shown at once: keep the affected work paused, secure the area if it is safe, document the problem (Stop Work / Report Hazard or Report an issue); call 911 if it becomes dangerous. It creates no hold, queue item or notification of its own (the Stop Work's safety queue item is the Owner's item), and nothing resumes work: the Owner alone authorizes resuming ([[OWN-DEC-172]] item 3), critical holds clear only with a recorded qualified assessment ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]).
  - **The English wording.** The packet's procedure and the notes beside the actions are the owner's English of [[OWN-DEC-172]] items 1, 2 and 4 addressed to the worker ("stop the affected work, make the area safe if you can do so without risk, and contact the Owner first"). The Spanish procedure is item 6 word for word; beside the actions its first sentence is the Call Owner note and the other two the Call 911 note, in its order.
  - **Missing number.** Nothing waits for it: the packet, its acknowledgment, Start Work and Stop Work work as before (no decision makes the number a Start Work prerequisite).
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** the Owner's phone number itself (entered by the Owner on staging and production; never in the repository, the graph or a fixture other than a synthetic 555-01XX number); a text or app alert to the Owner when a worker records that the Owner could not be reached (SMS waits for A2P 10DLC, [[OWN-DEC-190]]).
- **Human review:** the owner's Spanish instruction is the owner's own wording, not a translation. The other new Spanish staff texts (the Call Owner label, the Owner-unreachable rule, the not-configured and not-synchronized lines, the account card) are AI translations shown as they are; they are not released as "AI-reviewed", because the separate comparison of [[HH-LANG-04]] (`tools/compare-safety-es.mjs`) covers the SOP texts only. They go to the safety reviewer with the other Spanish safety text ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]], [[OQ-SPANISH-SAFETY-DETAILS]]).
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code.
