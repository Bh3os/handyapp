**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of return work and of resuming after an incident?

- **Who answers:** the owner. Warranty wording and exclusions go to the owner's attorney ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]); the resume and hold-release rules to the qualified safety reviewer ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]]). The software decides neither.
- **Decided, in use:** return work is classified by people into the seven classes of [[HH-QA-07]]; a workmanship problem reported within 90 days of the reviewed completion date is a warranty claim ([[FACT-WARRANTY]], [[OWN-DEC-135]]) with return-visit punch items; coverage and price are the Owner's decision, never applied automatically, with no invented exclusion; return work is a linked return visit (purpose `return_work`) or job and never reopens the original job's completion evidence or costs ([[HH-QA-08]]); return-work costs are recorded apart ([[HH-FIN-17]]); resuming needs the hold released with evidence by someone other than the person who raised it ([[OWN-DEC-38]]), a new packet version, each worker's own review and the Start Work gate passing again online ([[HH-INC-03]]); the app never says a released hold certifies the site safe.
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - The record: one return-work record per case (`quality.return_work`, migration 0102), on the case's own project and job (the project's job when the case names none). Staff who work the case classify it (a complaint: the Owner or Office; a restricted case: the Owner) and may reclassify it while the case is open; the history keeps each.
  - The warranty window: the reviewed completion date is the local date, in the business time zone, on which the Owner's quality review passed; the report date is the case's reported date; the window ends on the 90th day after it, inclusive. A workmanship problem (workmanship callback or warranty claim) is recorded as `warranty_claim` inside the window and `workmanship_callback` after it, and is refused before the review passed (it is then a punch item of the review, `REVIEW_NOT_PASSED`). `open_punch` is accepted only before the review passed.
  - The Owner's decision (`case.triage`): coverage `covered` or `not_covered` and price `no_charge` or `chargeable`, with a note. Chargeable records no amount: the work is priced through its own estimate or change order. A later decision replaces it; the history keeps both. A case with return work closes only once this decision is recorded and every return-visit punch item passed its re-check.
  - Return visits: planned from the case (a tentative visit of the original job with purpose `return_work`, the ordinary window rules, needing `visit.offer_windows`) or an existing return visit linked. A return visit passes the same Start Work gate (assignment, packet viewed and acknowledged, SOP, holds, site check, loadout, before photos of each work package, deposit) and may start once the job is awaiting quality review, operationally complete or closed; its start moves neither the job nor its work packages. The job packet itself does not list the return items: they are shown on the case and on the worker's Start Work screen of the return visit.
  - A return job: a job of another project of the same customer (new scope through its own request, estimate and approval), linked from the return work; never the original job.
  - Return-visit punch items (`quality.return_work_items`): added by the Owner (`quality.manage_punch_items`) with a work package or area, what to correct, the required evidence, an active worker and a due date; corrected by that worker (`quality.correct_punch_item`) only on a linked return visit they started through the gate and are on (arrived); re-checked by an Owner who did not correct it (passed, or failed back to open). They are not punch items of the job's passed quality review, which stays as it was.
  - Costs: materials, approved purchase, rental or other, in integer cents up to $100,000.00 per entry, with a note, recorded by staff who work the case; voided once with a reason, never changed. Labor on return visits is the elapsed Arrived-to-Finished minutes per worker; its cost is shown as incomplete.
  - Resuming: a worker who started work on the job before its latest pause by a hazard report, and has not started or resumed since, resumes through the Start Work screen (`work.resume`): every prerequisite again, with a site check recorded after the pause; one resumption per worker per pause. The first resumption moves a paused job and its blocked work packages back to in progress; afterwards each other worker who started before the pause resumes before writing the checklist, the walkthrough or Finished (`RESUME_REQUIRED`; the database refuses it too). A worker who never started on the job starts it once it has resumed. Resuming on a new visit also records the worker's start on that visit.
  - No separate queue item for return work: the case's own action-queue item stays open until the case closes.
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** the warranty wording shown to customers and any exclusion ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]: none is invented, and nothing customer-facing is sent from return work); a labor cost rate for return-work labor ([[TSK-0131]]); the job's estimated-versus-actual cost report that will read the return-work costs ([[TSK-0095]]); the review and satisfaction requests that pause while the case is active ([[TSK-0092]], reading the case).
- **Human review:** whether a claim is covered, any compensation, and any legal or financial exposure are the Owner's, with their professional reviewer ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]); the qualified safety reviewer reviews what a resumption checks again.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code.
