**Answered by [[OWN-DEC-134]] (2026-10-20).**

**Question:** what happens to a paid deposit, and is anything charged, when a customer cancels (before work, within 24 hours of a visit, or after work has started), or when HandyHub must cancel?

- **Who answers:** the owner, with the attorney for the terms wording.
- **Known:** on 2026-10-05 the owner answered: refund minus $50 for the service usage; only the $50 kept even if materials were bought; rescheduling free, a $50 charge only for a final cancellation under 24 hours; after work started the customer pays work done plus materials; when HandyHub cancels, a free reschedule or full refund (owner answers 2026-10-05T16:13, 16:15, 17:36, 17:41). Later decisions did not adopt these: no late fees, forfeiture or refund terms are invented ([[OWN-DEC-104]]), no cancellation fee is configured ([[OWN-DEC-107]]) and terms v1.0 contain none ([[OWN-DEC-127]]).
- **Until answered:** no cancellation fee is shown or charged; any deposit or refund decision goes to the owner case by case.
