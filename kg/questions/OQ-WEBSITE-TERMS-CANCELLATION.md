**Question:** how should the "Rescheduling and cancellation" section of the website terms read, now that the owner decided the late-cancellation rules after it was drafted?

- **The conflict.** Website terms v1 (prepared 2026-10-21, draft, not attorney-reviewed) say: cancelling less than the notice hours before "the visit" keeps the late-cancellation amount from the deposit and refunds the rest, and "without a deposit, the [amount] is invoiced". The decisions say:
  - a deposit under $50.00: only that deposit is kept and the difference is never invoiced ([[OWN-DEC-161]]);
  - no deposit: the $50.00 invoice only where it is legally permitted and was disclosed and accepted in advance ([[OWN-DEC-161]]); estimate terms v1.0 leave the cancellation rules out, so the software charges nothing in that case today ([[OQ-CANCELLATION-RULES-DETAILS]]);
  - estimate visits are never charged, whatever the notice ([[OWN-DEC-163]]); the terms speak of "the visit" without that exception.
- **Not resolved by choosing one.** The software follows the decisions (`cancellation.rules`, fee enforcement waiting for the attorney). The published text was not changed: customer legal wording needs the owner's and the attorney's approval ([[RULE-NO-INVENTED-FACTS]], [[OQ-ATTORNEY-PRIVACY]]). The page already says "The wording of these rules is waiting for attorney review."
- **Who answers:** the owner with the attorney (review packet question A-03, [[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]).
- **Until answered:** website terms v1 stay published as drafted, marked as a draft not reviewed by an attorney; no cancellation fee is charged automatically; the Owner applies or waives each outcome by hand.
