**Question:** when are more workers or subcontractors brought in, and what is the subcontractor policy?

- **Who answers:** the owner (with the attorney for the agreement and the CPA for W-9 and payment).
- **Known owner answers (2026-10-05):** subcontractors get their own sign-in as workers; they show proof of their own insurance before their first job; they need an agreement and a W-9 on file and never see job prices; cleaning jobs planned for 3–5 people are taken, hiring as needed; the owner trains them on each service's safety procedure as paid time (owner answers 2026-10-05T16:29, 18:20).
- **Open:** the hiring trigger, the agreement wording and how subcontractors are paid.
- **Until answered:** subcontractor features follow the worker role only; no subcontractor-specific workflow is built.

- **2026-10-21 ([[OWN-DEC-188]]):** subcontractors are paid outside the app; the hiring trigger and the agreement wording stay open (attorney and CPA packets).
