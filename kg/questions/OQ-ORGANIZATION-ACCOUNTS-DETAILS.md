**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of customer-side organization accounts (property managers, landlords, businesses)?

- **Who answers:** the owner. Whether a member may approve an estimate or a change order for the project's customer is a question of authority to bind the customer; the owner may want the attorney's view ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]).
- **Decided, in use ([[HH-ID-13]], [[T20]], [[HH-ID-03]], [[HH-ID-02]]):** explicit membership, scopes, approval and payment privileges, invitations, revocation and history; sharing an email domain never grants anyone access; access attaches only after verified linking; every access is checked on the server.
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging (migration 0125):**
  - **Who sets one up.** Any signed-in customer account, from Organizations (`/portal/organizations`, linked from the dashboard). That account is the organization's one administrator; there is no second administrator and no transfer yet. The name is the customer's own words, 1 to 80 characters. Staff have no organization screen; the audit log shows every change.
  - **What it shares.** Only projects the administrator's own account is linked to (code-verified links, [[ENT-CustomerIdentityLink]]), one by one. The organization never holds more than the administrator: a revoked link, a corrected link or a closed account ends that project's sharing on the next request. Stop sharing removes a project from every member's scope at once. No customer, property or contact record changes.
  - **Invitations.** By one exact email address, never a domain, phone or name. Nothing is sent (no invitation wording is approved): the invitation waits on the Organizations page of the account that signs in with exactly that address (its sign-in email, proved by a code), and grants nothing until that account accepts it. Before accepting it shows only the organization's name, the privileges and how many projects it covers. Invitations stay open until accepted, declined or withdrawn (no expiry). An address is invited once at a time; the administrator's own email is refused. A phone-only account cannot receive invitations.
  - **Scope.** 1 to 50 of the organization's shared projects per membership, chosen explicitly; a membership reaches nothing else, and not the organization's other projects.
  - **Privileges.** Every membership reads the projects in its scope: the Overview, Documents (estimate versions and their PDFs), Photos, Appointments, estimates and change orders, and the released case updates. `approve`: approve, decline or ask for changes to an estimate, approve or reject a change order, choose an offered window, ask to reschedule or cancel a visit (with the same fresh verification as the customer, [[HH-ID-07]]); an approval through a membership records the member's account and is checked again, locked, at the moment it is written. `pay`: the Payments tab, invoices (listed, counted and downloaded), the deposit amount and the Pay by Card links. Never for a member, whatever the privileges: answering HandyHub's questions, the project conversation (the Messages tab and writing to HandyHub), contact, property and access-code changes, privacy requests, Request This Service Again and the repeat-service schedule with its reminders ([[TSK-0114]]: read, change or stop); they stay the project customer's own. Without `pay` the Overview says payments are not shared; without `approve` the estimate and change-order screens say the membership does not include approving.
  - **No extra verification to grant.** Inviting, changing access and accepting need a signed-in account (its sign-in is a one-time code); granting `approve` asks for no fresh verification.
  - **Ending access.** The administrator withdraws an invitation, removes a member, takes a project out of a scope or a privilege away; the member leaves; each ends that access on the member's next request. When a login account closes, its memberships end, and so do the memberships of the organizations it administers. A removed address may be invited again (a new membership).
  - **What members see.** Their organizations' names, their privileges and the projects their membership reaches now; never the other members, the administrator's details or the organization's other projects. On the dashboard a shared project says "Shared with you by {organization}".
  - **History.** Append-only: set up, projects shared and no longer shared, invitations, acceptances, declines, withdrawals, changes (privileges and scope size), removals, departures and memberships ended by a closed account; the administrator sees the latest 200 with the invited email and the project reference. A request erasure removes the shares, scopes and history entries that name the erased project.
  - **Limits and records.** Changes: 120 per network per hour (the account settings limit). The audit log records the organization, the membership, the project, the privileges and the scope size; never a name, an email address or anything the customer typed.
  - **Wording.** Customer catalog `portal.organizations.*`, `portal.account.organizations`, `portal.projects.sharedBy`, `portal.payment.notShared.*`, `portal.estimate.approval.reason.no_approval_privilege` and `portal.changeOrders.noApprovalPrivilege`.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code.
