**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of cases?

- **Who answers:** the owner. Wording that touches insurance, legal exposure or injury goes to the owner's attorney or insurer through the owner ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]); the software decides none of it.
- **Decided, in use:** complaints from walkthroughs, messages or calls become cases linked to the project with severity, assignee, due date, notes and photos, and a second open complaint of the same category is offered "add to existing case" ([[OWN-DEC-124]]); workers report incidents quickly from the job; damage, injury and disputes notify the Owner (an Owner Decisions item and the staff notification); the Owner handles and records every insurer, attorney or other professional referral; the software never decides fault, admits liability or promises compensation; restricted incident details are read only by the Owner and the staff member who reported them, Office sees that a case exists and its status ([[OWN-DEC-143]]); customers see only an acknowledgment, progress and the next step, released by staff after review, and "Work is paused for safety review." for a safety pause ([[HH-INC-02]]); closing needs a recorded resolution and any necessary re-check; history is kept.
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - Kinds and types: the five kinds of [[ENT-Case]]. A report names one of the incident types of [[HH-INC-01]] or a dispute; injury makes an injury case, property damage a damage case, dispute a dispute case, the others an incident case. Every non-complaint case is restricted (its report, notes and photos in the `restricted_incident` class). A damage claim is reported as a damage case, not a complaint category.
  - Complaint categories (work quality, conduct, schedule, billing, other) and severities (low, moderate, high, critical), taken from the planning domain model (05 Complaint and Incident; incident severities still wait for the qualified safety professional, [[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]]).
  - Who does what, in the permission matrix: `case.view` and `case.manage` sit in the "Record a complaint case" row (the Owner, Office); `case.triage` sits in the "Professional referral, case closure" row (the Owner). Office records and works complaints up to action pending; the Owner triages incidents, resolves, reopens, closes, links the hold and works every restricted case. Office, the Estimator and an assigned worker may report an incident; afterwards only the Owner and the reporter read it.
  - Lifecycles: complaint `open → acknowledged → investigating → action_pending → resolved → closed`, reopened (with a reason) to investigating; incident `reported → triaged → action_pending → resolution_review → closed`, with `resolution_review → action_pending` when the resolution is not enough. A case is active, pausing review and satisfaction requests and holding records, until it is closed (also while resolved). Closing needs the recorded resolution, the re-check when the resolution said one is needed, and no open follow-up item.
  - The responsible reviewer is the Owner: the only active Owner at reporting, else the Owner who triages. Review areas flagged per kind: injury (injury, insurance), damage (insurance), dispute (disputes, legal).
  - Owner notification: one Owner Decisions item per damage, injury or dispute case while it is reported (an injury in the safety band), resolved at triage; one action-queue item per open case for the Owner and Office (Office sees a restricted case's reason as "case opened"). The staff notification goes by email to every active Owner, without any detail.
  - Customer updates: four neutral templates (acknowledgment "We received your report and recorded it for review.", "Progress update:" and "Next step:" with the staff member's detail, and the safety pause), shown in the project Overview once released; a safety pause only while the linked hold is open or under review; wording with fault, liability, coverage, compensation, refund or warranty statements is refused; a released update is never changed or withdrawn; nothing is sent by email or text from a case (staff reply in the project thread for that).
  - Deletion and retention: an open complaint, dispute or incident case on a project is a "found" complaints, disputes or investigations check (a hold) in deletion reviews and privacy plans; none open is still the Owner's check, since one handled outside the software cannot be ruled out. Complaints are job records; restricted cases, their reports and the Owner's referrals are safety records (10 years after closure, [[FACT-RETENTION-SCHEDULE]]).
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** the wording of the staff notification `staff_notice.case_opened` (deliveries are recorded as template not configured; the queue item is the signal); the hazard report link (`hazard_report_id`) waits for [[TSK-0077]]; review and satisfaction requests that read the active-case pause wait for [[TSK-0092]]; return-work classification and resuming after an incident wait for [[TSK-0094]].
- **Human review:** whether a claim is covered under the 90-day workmanship warranty ([[FACT-WARRANTY]], [[OWN-DEC-135]]), fault, liability, compensation and every insurer or attorney contact are the Owner's; the templates make no coverage statement.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code.
