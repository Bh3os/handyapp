**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the erasure of a request that never became a job?

- **Who answers:** the owner; the attorney for what a kept privacy request, consent record or link history must keep ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]], [[OQ-RETENTION-REVIEW]]).
- **Decided, in use:** the Owner's Delete on the request's review entry, after the checks and with a reason and a fresh sign-in, erases the request and keeps only its reference, creation date, closure date and final status (`privacy.request_remainders`), with the review entry, the deletion log and the audit entries (references only). Nothing is deleted automatically. Holds, open cases and protected evidence block it; Archive is offered. A restore re-applies it from the exported deletion log.
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** how long backups keep the erased rows (the backup-retention and secure-expiration policy of OWN-DEC-176 item 5); the business time zone, if it is ever unset, leaves the consent evidence without a review date.
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - **What goes.** The submission (description, answers, timing and preferences, the original submission), photos and their stored files, internal notes, logged calls, questions and replies, conversations and messages with their deliveries and provider receipts, the handling history (decisions with their notes, assignments, qualification, response clock and events, resolved holds, queue items), work packages, discount requests, the project's estimates never accepted with their publication approvals, site contacts and coverage acceptances, guest, receipt and upload links, account links to the project, sessions started from those links, closed access-help requests naming the reference or an erased email or phone, verification codes sent to an erased email or phone, stored responses of earlier writes that name an erased record (idempotency records are kept after they expire), and waiting jobs (cancelled). The project record goes too; the remainder keeps the reference.
  - **Records other projects need.** The customer and contact records stay unchanged while another project or request, a login account, a privacy request, a message, a site contact, a link or a duplicate-merge history names them; the property while another project, request or customer does; the project record (with its customer and property) while a privacy request or another project's message names it. The confirmation lists each under what stays. A login account follows its own deletion ([[OWN-DEC-71]]).
  - **Consent evidence.** When the contact records go, the consent records of their email and phone move to the restricted compliance record (`privacy.consent_evidence`), unless another record on file uses that email or phone (then they stay where they are). Its review date is the last relevant use (the latest consent record or message sent to that address) plus the 5 years of [[OWN-DEC-177]], as a calendar date in the business time zone. No screen or route reads it; nothing is deleted at that date. Once the consent-records retention of [[TSK-0138]] is configured, the review date should come from that configuration and the compliance records should join the Owner's review queue.
  - **What blocks it besides holds and evidence.** The request or project not closed, no recorded closing decision, an estimate still open, an open access-help request naming it, and linked records this erasure does not remove: site visits and their field records (worker times are payroll-relevant), cases (open or closed), photo publication permissions, and links between customer records (relink or merge history).
  - **Estimates on the project.** An estimate never accepted is erased with the request, and its own review entry, if any, is decided as deleted by the same deletion. Anything that blocks one of them blocks the request.
  - **Statistics.** No separate anonymous statistics store exists yet (the Owner Dashboard is [[TSK-0096]]); the remainders are the anonymous request history (reference, dates, final status), kept apart from customer records.
  - **Search and caches.** Staff search reads the tables themselves and the server keeps no cache of request content, so removing the rows removes them from search. The staff app's offline store keeps job packets only, and a request that never became a job has none.
  - **Privacy deletion requests.** A customer's deletion request lists their closed requests as kept for their 2-year period (then the Owner's review erases them), instead of "not deleted yet".
