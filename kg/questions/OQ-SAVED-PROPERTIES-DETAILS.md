**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of an account's saved properties, its preferences and "Request This Service Again"?

- **Who answers:** the owner.
- **Decided, in use ([[OWN-DEC-113]], [[OWN-DEC-160]]):** "Request This Service Again" reuses authorized property details while asking the customer to confirm they are still current, and creates a new request; new pricing and appointments need staff review. Channel permissions, separate marketing consent and opt-outs are checked before every send. Customers see only what is linked to their verified identity (HH-ID-03).
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging (migration 0121):**
  - **Where.** An account's dashboard (`/portal/`) links to Saved properties (`/portal/properties`) and Preferences (`/portal/preferences`), and offers "Request This Service Again" on each of its projects, in every section. A guest link's session offers neither.
  - **Saved properties come from the account's history only.** The list is the addresses of the projects the account's active links open, one per address (case and spacing aside; every request makes its own property record), each shown with its latest project. The customer gives an address a name of 1 to 60 characters, unique within the account (case aside), one name per address; renames it; removes it (only the name goes: the property and its projects stay). The customer never edits the address there: an address that changed is corrected on the next request, and the property record stays staff's ([[ENT-Property]]). A revoked link or a closed account hides the address and its name at once. A name goes with its property record when the Owner erases a request that never became a job.
  - **"Request a service here".** A saved property starts a new request from its address and the contact of its latest project there, with no service chosen, linked to that project like "Request This Service Again".
  - **What "Request This Service Again" reuses.** The services of the earlier request the form still offers, its "not sure" choice, the property address and type as they read now, the request contact's name, email and phone as they read now, and the account's contact-method preference. Never reused: the description, the answers and notes, photos, timing, the estimate choice, a requested discount, the SMS opt-in and the marketing permission (both unchecked, as on every request), prices, verification and consent.
  - **Confirming the details.** The property step says where the details came from ("These details come from your project …") and the customer checks "The property details above are still current." before going on. The form collects no access details today, so there is nothing else to reconfirm.
  - **The new request.** It is a new request with its own customer, contact, property and project records, like every submission (nothing is matched or merged), linked to the earlier project with the time the details were confirmed and whether the address is the earlier one. Staff see "Requested again from" on the request and may merge or relink records through the reviewed merge ([[HH-CRM-04]]). It is accepted only from a signed-in account linked to the earlier project; otherwise it is refused, nothing is written, and the form says the link is gone so the customer can send it as a new request. The new project joins the account only once its own contact is verified on its receipt (the receipt's account offer), never by itself. A form started this way keeps no browser draft.
  - **Preferences.** The contact method new requests start with (email, phone call, text or none; each request still records its own choice, and texts still need the SMS opt-in on that request). The permissions of the email and phone the account signs in with, as the send-time checks read them: marketing, optional follow-ups after a job (the satisfaction check and review requests, [[TSK-0092]]) and, for the phone, texts. Each choice is an append-only consent record (source portal). Any of them can be stopped; stopping texts also ends the consent to verification codes by text, as STOP does. Only email permissions are given back in the portal (marketing with the privacy notice version shown beside it): texts are agreed to on the request form, the opt-in path the SMS terms and the A2P 10DLC registration describe ([[HH-COM-12]], [[OWN-DEC-147]]). The choices apply to the account's own sign-in values; a project contact with another email or number keeps its own permissions.
  - **Repeat-service reminders.** No customer-agreed schedule exists yet, so there is nothing to change or stop; the customer's control of it comes with the schedule itself ([[TSK-0114]], [[HH-SCH-10]]).
  - **Limits and records.** Changes: 120 per network per hour. The audit log records each saved-property change (the saved property and the property), each contact-method change and each permission choice (channel, permission, given or stopped, whether a record was written), and a request's link to the earlier project; never a name the customer typed, an address or a contact value.
  - **Data export.** The customer's data export lists their consent records but not yet their property names and contact-method preference; PROPOSED to add them when the attorney reviews what an export holds ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-PRIVACY]]).
  - **Wording.** Customer catalog `portal.account.*`, `portal.projects.again*`, `portal.properties.*`, `portal.preferences.*`, `request.again.*` and `request.review.repeatOf`; staff catalogs `request.repeatOf*`. "Request This Service Again" is the owner's.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code.
