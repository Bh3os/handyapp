**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of job costs and of the source states every report declares?

- **Who answers:** the owner; the accounting definitions (what counts as revenue, cost and money received, and when a deposit is recognized) go to the owner's CPA ([[OWN-DEC-186]], [[OWN-DEC-189]] item 15, [[OQ-TAX-CPA]]). The software decides none of them.
- **Decided, in use:** the direct cost lines of [[OWN-DEC-189]] (labor, burden where known, materials, subcontractors as a direct job cost when tracked ([[OWN-DEC-208]]), equipment rentals, disposal, directly attributable expenses); estimated and actual kept apart; missing cost is incomplete, never zero, and no provisional figure is shown as final ([[HH-FIN-17]]); elapsed time per worker from Arrived to Finished with no payroll ([[OWN-DEC-69]]); every report names its sources and no figure mixes pending checks, failed charges, estimates, invoices, fees and payouts as "cash"; deposits counted once ([[HH-DATA-10]], [[OWN-DEC-125]]).
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - Cost entries (`finance.job_costs`, migration 0109): one entry per cost in integer cents, in one of six categories: materials, approved purchase, subcontractor, equipment rental, disposal, other direct expense. Up to $100,000.00 per entry; a date incurred no later than today in the business time zone; a note. Never changed; voided once with a reason. Labor and burden are never entered as amounts.
  - Completeness: a category's actual figure is final only once the Owner confirms that it holds every cost of the job; a confirmed category with no entry is the only $0.00 the report shows. An entry is recorded or voided only in a category that is not confirmed; the Owner reopens a confirmed category with a reason. The history (`finance.job_cost_events`) keeps every entry, void, confirmation and reopening.
  - Who: reading and recording job costs (`job_cost.view`, `job_cost.record`) are the Owner's, the new ROLE-MATRIX row "View and record job costs, estimated versus actual" (P); Office and Estimators are refused. The list of report definitions is `dashboard.view` (the Owner).
  - Estimated costs: the internal material and labor cost of each counted line (base lines and the selected options) of the estimate version the job was approved from. Complete only when every counted line carries it; an approved change order leaves the estimate incomplete, since change orders carry no cost estimate. The other categories are "not estimated".
  - Time: the Arrived-to-Finished minutes per worker, with the Owner's corrections, on the job's service and follow-up visits once they went on the way; a missing or unfinished time is flagged and makes the time incomplete. Return visits are the return work's.
  - Return work: its costs ([[OQ-RETURN-WORK-DETAILS]]) are shown apart from the job's own and never added to the job's total; they are "recorded so far", never final.
  - A variance (actual minus estimated) is shown only between two final figures. Each figure is complete, incomplete (with the part known), not recorded, not estimated or not configured.
  - Report definitions (`@handyhub/domain` REPORT_DEFINITIONS), each with one basis: estimates sent (estimate versions published or later, counted once per estimate); invoiced revenue (final invoices issued, partially paid or paid; the deposit invoice left out because the final invoice credits it); money received (payments recorded, reconciled or cleared, once per payment; pending checks, returned and voided payments, fees and payouts left out); pending checks (received or deposited); refunds and credits (refunds succeeded, credits issued or applied; write-offs left out); provider fees; payouts (settlement); outstanding balances (final invoices issued or partially paid, from their ledger, overdue shown apart); job costs; return-work costs. Two figures are added only when they share a basis. The Owner Dashboard and its CSV ([[TSK-0096]]) and job margin ([[TSK-0131]]) read these definitions.
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** a labor cost rate per worker (the Owner enters it, [[TSK-0131]]), so labor cost is not configured and every actual total is incomplete; an employee burden rate; any overhead (never a job cost); job gross margin and the low-margin flag ([[TSK-0131]], [[FACT-MARGIN-TARGET]]); when a deposit is recognized as revenue (the CPA's); how write-offs are presented on the dashboard ([[TSK-0096]]).
- **Human review:** the accounting definitions above and any tax treatment of costs are the CPA's ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]); the app computes no payroll, rate or tax.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code.
