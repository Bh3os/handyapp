**Answered by [[OWN-DEC-142]] (2026-10-20).**

**Question:** how long are audit records (sign-ins, approvals, payment entries and corrections, merges, role changes, safety events) kept?

- **Who answers:** the owner, with the CPA and attorney.
- **Known:** [[OWN-DEC-123]] says audit records follow the agreed retention rules rather than being kept forever, but the [[OWN-DEC-70]] schedule ([[FACT-RETENTION-SCHEDULE]]) has no row for audit records.
- **Until answered:** PROPOSED: an audit entry is kept at least as long as the record it describes (for example 10 years for an approval, 7 years for a payment) and then enters the owner's deletion-review queue; nothing is deleted automatically.
