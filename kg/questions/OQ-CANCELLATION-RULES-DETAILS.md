**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the cancellation and reschedule rules, and which numbers does the repeated-cancellation review use?

- **Who answers:** the owner (the attorney for the fee wording and its enforcement, [[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]).
- **Decided, in use ([[OWN-DEC-161]], [[OWN-DEC-163]], [[OWN-DEC-195]]):** `cancellation.rules` version 2 (migration 0115): under 24 hours before a confirmed work visit the fee is capped at $50.00 (5000 cents); a smaller verified deposit is kept and nothing more is invoiced; $50.00 or more keeps at most $50.00 and the rest is refunded; with no deposit the $50.00 is invoiced only where it was disclosed and accepted in advance; estimate visits are never charged; HandyHub's own cancellations give the customer the choice of a free reschedule or a full refund; every reschedule records who started it and why; only Start Work is the start of contracted work; Office processes routine customer-authorized work-visit cancellations; waivers, refunds, disputed charges and exceptions are the Owner's. Nothing is collected or refunded by the software: the Owner applies each outcome.
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** `cancellation.repeat_review` (how many short-notice cancellations and no-shows, within how many calendar days, flag a customer's next estimate booking for the Owner's review, [[OWN-DEC-163]] 7). "Repeatedly" has no number, so nothing is flagged until the Owner sets it (two numbers: a count from 1 and a period in calendar days in the business time zone).
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - **Disclosure of the fee.** The no-deposit invoice needs the accepted estimate terms to state the fee. Terms v1.0 leaves the cancellation rules out on purpose ([[HH-EST-13]]), so no accepted terms disclose it yet and a late cancellation with no deposit charges nothing (`late_no_charge`). The next terms version, once the attorney has reviewed its wording, is added to the list of terms that disclose it. "Legally permitted" is the attorney's answer, not the software's.
  - **Fee enforcement.** `cancellation.rules.feeEnforcement` is `attorney_approval_pending`: staff see the computed fee with "Charging the fee waits for the attorney's approval of the cancellation terms." The Owner still applies (or waives) each outcome by hand.
  - **Routine cancellation (Office).** A cancellation the customer asked for (their portal request, or staff recording the customer's own instruction as initiator "customer"), with no circumstance, whose outcome is full refund, the capped late fee, or a late cancellation with nothing to charge (and any estimate visit). Office may then cancel the work visit (new matrix row "Cancel a work visit for a routine customer-authorized cancellation within policy"). HandyHub's own cancellation of a work visit, a cancellation after contracted work started and any circumstance stay the Owner's ("Cancel accepted, scheduled or paid work (financial review)"). Declining a customer's cancellation request of a work visit stays the Owner's. The Owner Decisions item "Work cancellation (Owner review)" opens for a customer's request only when the request, as asked, is not routine; a routine one is the staff queue item only. Money in a routine outcome (a refund, a kept deposit) still opens the Owner's cancellation-outcome item.
  - **Circumstances.** Staff may record one of: unsafe conditions, an emergency, a legally protected cancellation right, another circumstance for the Owner to review. Any of them makes the outcome "Owner review" with no amount computed; nothing is charged automatically. Whether a right is legally protected is the attorney's question ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]).
  - **Fee waiver or reduction ([[OWN-DEC-161]] 8).** The Owner enters the fee after the waiver (0 waives it; never more than the computed fee) and a documented reason, with a fresh sign-in, when recording the cancellation; the change record keeps the fee before it. New matrix row "Waive or reduce a cancellation fee, with a documented reason" (F). A waiver after the cancellation was recorded is not built here; applying the outcome's refund or fee stays with the Owner's finance actions ([[TSK-0089]]).
  - **Work-start markers ([[OWN-DEC-195]] C4).** Worker arrived: the first Arrived tap on the job's visits. Preparation started: the first Start Work site check or before photo of the job. Contracted work started: the first Start Work ([[TSK-0073]]), or the job in a state only Start Work reaches. Work completed: the first Finished tap on a visit where contracted work started. Only contracted work started gives the "after work started" outcome; staff see the four apart on the cancellation.
  - **Repeat pattern.** Counted per customer across their projects: customer cancellations under the notice (24 hours) before contracted work started, estimate or work visit, and the visit outcomes "No access" and "Customer absent" as the no-shows. Checked when an estimate visit is booked (staff confirmation or the customer's window choice); the Owner Decisions item "Estimate booking after repeated cancellations" opens for that visit and resolves when the visit is no longer booked. It never charges and never blocks.
  - **Staff text.** The labels, hints and the waiver form in English and Spanish (staff catalogs, `change.*`), written for this build. No customer-facing wording was added: the cancellation policy text for customers waits for the attorney-reviewed terms ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]).
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used and `cancellation.repeat_review` stays NOT CONFIGURED; each is a small change to the nodes, the configuration or the code.
