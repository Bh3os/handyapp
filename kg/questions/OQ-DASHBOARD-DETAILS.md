**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the Owner Dashboard and the accountant's files?

- Periods: dates in the business time zone, both ends included, at most 366 days; the previous period is the same number of days just before. The screen opens on this month so far. A figure is counted over the period ("period") or as things stand ("as of now"); only period figures have a previous-period value.
- What places a record in a period: a request by when its response clock started; an estimate by when its first version was sent (counted once per estimate, by where its latest sent version stands now: approved, declined, expired, withdrawn or awaiting); a completed job by when its quality review passed; a review's pass rate by its first decision; a return visit by its visit date; an invoice by when it was issued; a payment by the date received; a refund or credit by when the Owner approved it; a Square fee by the date incurred; a payout by the date paid out; return-work costs by when recorded.
- Overview counts: new requests are requests still "received"; estimates awaiting approval have their latest sent version published; scheduled jobs have a confirmed visit from now on; jobs awaiting quality review have a review pending, in review or awaiting re-check; overdue invoices are final invoices with a balance whose due date (the latest revised one, else the date issued: due on receipt, [[FACT-PAYMENT-DUE]]) is before today; "Overdue—owner decision" items are the open Owner Decisions items; open cases are cases not closed; Owner approvals are pending publication approvals ([[OWN-DEC-173]]); privacy requests open, past an internal deadline (acknowledgment or the internal target, [[FACT-BUSINESS-CALENDAR]]) and past a recorded legal deadline, shown apart ([[OWN-DEC-205]]).
- Money figures read their report definitions ([[OQ-JOB-COST-DETAILS]]): invoiced is final invoices issued (the deposit invoice left out); money received is verified payments by method, added only within that one basis; pending checks, refunds (succeeded), credits (issued or applied), Square fees and payouts are apart; outstanding balances are what issued final invoices still ask for, with the overdue part shown under them.
- Job costs on the dashboard: the jobs completed in the period, how many have a complete or an incomplete actual cost, and the actual cost known so far (incomplete while any job's is; labor and burden cost are NOT CONFIGURED, so today every job's is). Return-work costs are "recorded so far", never final. No margin or flag here ([[TSK-0131]]).
- Cases: open complaints (not resolved or closed), open damage and injury reports, open cases without an assignee, overdue follow-ups, and open cases by assignee; drill-down lists show the case number, kind and state only.
- Drill-down lists show at most 500 records; their totals count every record and equal the figure.
- NOT CONFIGURED, for the CPA or the Owner ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]], [[OQ-TAX-CPA]]): how deposits are recognized as revenue, tax treatment and how write-offs are presented. The write-off figure stays "not configured"; write-offs are listed as recorded in the accountant's refunds file.
- Who: the dashboard and its CSV are `dashboard.view` / `dashboard.export_csv` (the Owner); each export (the dashboard CSV and every accountant file) also needs a sign-in within the last 5 minutes. The accountant's files are `export.accounting` (F: the Owner, fresh sign-in and a reason). Every export is audited with its period, dataset, row count and reason, never its contents. At most 30 exports per staff member per hour.
- The dashboard CSV: one row per figure in screen order with section, figure, staff member (per-assignee rows), report, basis, unit, scope, period, state, value (integer cents, counts, minutes or basis points), the amount in dollars and the previous period beside it.
- The accountant's files, one per kind, with exact dollar amounts from integer cents: invoices (issued in the range, with charges, credits, discount, total, deposit credit, credits applied, written off, confirmed and pending payments and balance), payments (every state, with whether it counts as received), payment allocations, refunds, credits and write-offs (every state, with the reason), deposits (required amount, confirmed and pending payments, the deposit invoice and the final-invoice credit), Square fees, Square payouts (with the fees recorded against each), job-cost entries (voided ones kept) and Arrived/Finished times (the [[TSK-0070]] file). The files hold only what the Owner reads on the staff screens: no access code, token, message text or case detail.
