**Question:** what margin or profit target, if any, should job costing compare estimates against?

- **Who answers:** the owner (with the CPA).
- **Known:** each job is priced individually on its own estimate with no fixed price list ([[OWN-DEC-32]]); no rate, wage, production factor or profit target is supplied ([[OWN-DEC-07]]); estimates are written or uploaded by staff without a price book ([[OWN-DEC-102]]).
- **Until answered:** no margin is computed or shown as a target; costs are recorded only where staff enter them.
