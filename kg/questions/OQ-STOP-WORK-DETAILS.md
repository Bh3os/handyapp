**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of Stop Work / Report Hazard, critical holds and issue reports?

- **Who answers:** the owner; the hazard kinds, the guidance texts, the whole-job list, the reviewer named per hazard and the stabilization record also with the qualified safety reviewer ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]], [[OWN-DEC-186]], [[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]). The software decides none of the safety questions: it flags, names the reviewer and stops the work.
- **Decided, in use:** Stop Work and Report Hazard are available to every assigned worker, Office, the Estimator and the Owner (ROLE-MATRIX "A, always, no permission needed"), with no fresh sign-in, reason, version or rate limit, and nothing about the job's state refuses them ([[HH-WORK-09]]); the critical safety hold is written in the same database transaction as the report, under the job's row lock, never through the outbox (migration 0079 refuses a critical report without its own hold of the same transaction, [[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]); the job moves from in progress to paused; a top-priority safety queue item is opened in the same transaction; the customer sees only "Work is paused for safety review." ([[T48]]); a suspected gas leak shows evacuate and call 911 or the gas utility from a safe place, live wiring is never touched, and neither offers an on-site fix ([[OWN-DEC-80]], [[HH-WORK-13]]); Call 911 comes first on every Stop Work and is never put behind anything ([[OWN-DEC-172]]); an on-site resolution only within the worker's training, qualifications, the approved scope and SOP, with no unresolved safety hold, recording what happened, the steps, the materials and the check, and the Owner is notified ([[HH-WORK-08]]); injury and incident details only for the Owner and the reporting worker ([[OWN-DEC-143]]); the work-paused notice is an Owner-released draft, never automatic ([[OWN-DEC-152]]); the hold is released only by the documented path (the Owner, never on a hold they raised, with evidence, the reviewer and their qualification, [[OWN-DEC-38]]).
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - Every Stop Work / Report Hazard is critical and writes a critical hold (unknown is not safe); so do an issue of category "unsafe or unexpected condition" and any issue reported as critical. A hold entered by mistake is cancelled by the Owner as entered in error (never by the reporter).
  - The hazard kinds: suspected gas leak, exposed potentially live wiring, suspected hazardous material, structural concern, needs a licensed trade, injury or medical emergency, other unsafe condition; the hold's category is the same, injury and other as "other". The issue severities: critical, major, minor; the next owner: the Owner (default), Office or the worker.
  - The Stop Work body is entirely optional (the hazard defaults to other unsafe condition); a long text is cut to 2,000 characters, a work package or visit the server cannot place and an unreadable device time are left out, never refused; an unknown field is refused, so no flag can ask for less.
  - Affected work ([[HH-CHG-04]]): the named work packages are blocked; every package that is not complete is blocked when none is named or the hazard cannot be separated safely (gas leak, live wiring, hazardous material, structural, injury). The job pauses only when work was under way. Unaffected packages keep their state, but no new Start Work passes while the job-scoped critical hold is open: continuing unaffected work under a hold waits for task-level execution ([[TSK-0075]]) and the Owner's decision on resuming ([[TSK-0094]]), which is stricter.
  - The hold's note is the reporter's text for an internal report; for a restricted one (injury, incident) only its code, so the packet and the review page never carry restricted details.
  - The reviewer named per hazard (a role, never a person): the gas utility or a licensed gas professional; a licensed electrician; a qualified hazardous-materials assessor; a structural engineer; a licensed trade professional; for an injury the Owner with any insurer or other professional referral; otherwise the Owner with the qualified safety reviewer.
  - The guidance shown per hazard, from the owner's wording of [[OWN-DEC-80]] and [[OWN-DEC-172]]: evacuate and call from a safe place, never a gas repair, never touch potentially live wiring, do not disturb a suspected hazardous material, no licensed-trade work, stop the affected work, keep people away, call the Owner, and for a serious injury call 911 now. No repair procedure is given for any of them.
  - Restricted reports show others only that they exist, their severity and the hold's state; the reporter's name is masked too. The Owner's reads of restricted details are audited.
  - Issue reports: an on-site resolution is refused for a hazard, a critical issue, an unsafe condition and an incident, and while any critical hold of the job is unresolved; the worker's statement of training and scope is recorded, never verified by the software. Every non-critical report opens one Owner item per job (field_issue, time-sensitive band) until the Owner reviews it (issue_report.review, a PROPOSED action in the "Professional referral, case closure" row). A critical report is reviewed only through its hold.
  - Photos, only when safe: the Owner and the assigned workers (job.add_photo), on a restricted report only its reporter and the Owner; stored privately with the report's visibility, never served or shared (field photos, [[TSK-0076]], add processing and viewing).
  - Emergency stabilization is recorded only on a Stop Work (trigger, actions, why necessary, evidence, Owner and customer notification times, follow-up), by the Owner or an assigned worker (on a restricted report its reporter and the Owner), and updates the hold's safety queue item; the app lists no make-safe steps.
  - The safety queue item: one per critical hold, seen by every role that may stop work on the job, linked to the job's review page; it is resolved when the hold is released or cancelled.
  - The work-paused notice: drafted after the report commits (event hazard.reported), by email to the project's customer contact with their first name and the project reference as the job number; one waiting draft per project; the Owner releases or dismisses it; a released notice is not sent once the hold was released or cancelled.
  - The customer portal shows "Work is paused for safety review." while any critical hold of the project is open or under review.
  - Offline: a stop is saved on the phone with its key, shown at once as "Saved on this phone" and "Not yet synchronized" (the office has not been told), and sent again with the same key when the phone is online, so it is recorded once.
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** Call Owner (the Owner's phone number in the staff profile is [[TSK-0129]]: until then the Stop Work panel says the number is NOT CONFIGURED and to call 911 in an emergency); the Owner's text alert (SMS waits for A2P 10DLC and an Owner number; the safety queue item is the in-app alert); make-safe steps from an approved SOP (none until competent review, [[OWN-DEC-17]]); the incident case record of an injury or incident ([[TSK-0093]]); the change-order draft of a customer change ([[TSK-0082]]); resuming after a hold ([[TSK-0075]], [[TSK-0094]]); the offline sync queue with its visible states ([[TSK-0080]]); the Spanish safety release of the new staff texts ([[TSK-0103]]).
- **Human review:** the guidance texts, the hazard kinds, the whole-job list, the reviewer roles and the stabilization record go to the qualified safety reviewer ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]]); the Spanish Stop Work and guidance texts are AI translations pending the separate comparison of [[TSK-0103]] ([[OWN-DEC-79]]); an injury's insurer or attorney referral is the Owner's ([[HH-INC-04]]); no text decides fault, admits liability or promises compensation.
- **RISK:** a Stop Work the Owner reports is a hold the Owner raised: it stays open until a second administrator exists ([[OWN-DEC-38]]); the remedy is an owner decision, never a weaker rule.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code.
