**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the Start Work gate, the hard holds and the three administrative exceptions?

- **Who answers:** the owner; the hold release and void rules and the boundary of the exceptions also with the qualified safety professional ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]], [[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]). The software decides none of the safety questions.
- **Decided, in use:** the ten prerequisites of [[HH-WORK-01]], evaluated on the server with every missing item named; Start Work re-evaluates everything in one transaction under the job lock and records the evaluation, the versions, the acknowledgment, any exception and the server time ([[HH-WORK-04]]); the worker viewed the latest packet version ([[OWN-DEC-196]]); the start is "actual contracted work started", distinct from Arrived ([[OWN-DEC-195]]); only the three exceptions of [[OWN-DEC-68]] exist, requested by an assigned worker and approved only by the Owner; nobody releases a critical safety hold they raised ([[OWN-DEC-38]]); the Owner sets Ready on any job-level Hold / Ready note, including one the Owner set ([[OWN-DEC-137]]); Call 911 is always available at once on the Start Work screen, before anything else ([[OWN-DEC-172]], [[OWN-DEC-208]]).
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - Outcome: `blocked` when anything blocks (a hold, a critical resource, an SOP NOT CONFIGURED, the deposit, an assignment problem), else `requires_review` (an SOP pending competent review in production, a Spanish safety text not yet released as AI-reviewed, a site difference), else `needs_attention` (the worker's own steps), else `ready`. Only `ready` starts work. Each missing code has a resolution owner: the worker, the Owner or the Office.
  - Start Work is per worker and visit: each assigned worker passes their own gate (their own packet view and acknowledgment, their own site check, their own language). The first start moves the job from pending preparation (or ready) to in progress and its planned or ready work packages to in progress; a later worker or visit of a job already in progress records its own start. A paused job resumes through resuming (TSK-0075, TSK-0094), not here.
  - Start needs the worker's own Arrived on an arrived visit (HH-WORK-12 sequence), and the packet version the briefing showed: a newer one refuses the start.
  - The site check is the worker's own answers on arrival: property and site identified, access available, the customer (or authorized representative) present, hazards and controls of the packet reviewed, and the site as the packet describes it or different with a note. A difference raises a job-level Hold (source "site check", category "site condition") in the same transaction; it counts as assessed once the Owner sets Ready on it. Customer presence is a start requirement only so that the "customer not present" exception has something to cover.
  - Before photos: at least one per work package of the job, uploaded on this visit, internal (a minimal record: field photos, [[TSK-0076]], add processing, sharing, labels and after photos).
    - Added by [[TSK-0076]] (2026-10-22), PROPOSED: they are field photos of the before phase; a photo the processing rejects does not count ([[OQ-FIELD-PHOTO-DETAILS]]).
  - Intake review holds (HH-LEAD-04) block Start Work like job-level and critical holds, as they block confirmation (TSK-0064). The database refuses a start, and any move of a job or work package to in progress, without a passing evaluation in the same transaction, and while any of these holds blocks.
  - Exceptions: one live request or approval per visit and kind. "Customer not present" needs the accepted scope, the property's recorded access instructions (an access notes or access windows detail) and the worker's site check first; its evidence is how the customer authorized unattended access and the contact who is reachable. "Before photos captured, upload failed" names the work packages and how many photos are on the device, and covers those packages only. "Deposit paid, bookkeeping pending" names the provider (Square only: the only approved provider), the transaction reference and an amount at least the deposit still due; the Owner approves only with the statement that the provider's own records show it. Approval needs a fresh sign-in, the follow-up owner and the deadline; nobody approves their own request. An exception expires at the end of the visit's service day or when the visit ends, and is invalidated by a critical packet change since it was requested (scope; holds, read as site conditions; site access; assignment) or when its requester is no longer assigned.
  - Follow-ups: the pending upload stays open until each named package has a before photo; the reconciliation until verified money or the Owner's waiver covers the deposit; the deferred walkthrough of "customer not present" until the visit is completed (the walkthrough record is [[TSK-0083]]). The Owner's queue lists a job's exceptions while one is requested or an approved one's follow-up is open (synchronized when the exception, the photo upload or a start changes; a deposit payment recorded elsewhere shows on the review page and updates the queue item at the next of these writes).
  - Job-level Hold categories: permit or licensing, licensed trade, structural, hazardous material, site condition, other. Office and the Estimator set a Hold (their PROPOSED cells); only the Owner sets Ready, with a note; the Owner is recorded as the reviewer.
  - Critical safety holds: the Owner (hold.release_critical) starts the review, releases with how it was resolved, the evidence, the reviewer and the reviewer's qualification (fresh sign-in), or cancels one entered in error with the reason (fresh sign-in); never on a hold they raised, including its review, and the database refuses the same. With one administrator a hold the Owner raised stays open (the RISK of [[OWN-DEC-38]]).
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** the hazard report that writes a critical safety hold ([[TSK-0077]]: until then nothing in the app raises one; the kind, its job lock and its release path exist); the Spanish release of SOP safety text ([[TSK-0103]]: until then every Spanish-reading worker's gate shows "Spanish safety review pending" and is not ready); the offline sync queue ([[TSK-0080]]: an offline start is impossible because the start is a server call, and the briefing disables Start while the device is offline).
- **Human review:** the safety boundary of the exceptions and the hold release and void rules wait for the qualified safety professional ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]]); the Spanish emergency text on the Start Work screen is the owner's verbatim instruction of [[OWN-DEC-172]] and goes to the same reviewer with the other Spanish safety text.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code.

**Updated by [[TSK-0077]] (2026-10-22):** the hazard report now exists: Stop Work / Report Hazard writes the critical safety hold (source hazard_report) in the same transaction as its report, under the job lock, so the first NOT CONFIGURED item above is built; its own PROPOSED details are in [[OQ-STOP-WORK-DETAILS]].
