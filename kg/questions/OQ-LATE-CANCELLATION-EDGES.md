**Question:** what applies in these two cases of a final cancellation with less than 24 hours notice?

1. **A verified deposit above zero but below $50.00** (for example a partly paid deposit): is all of it kept, is the rest of the $50.00 invoiced, or something else? The rules say "$50.00 kept; rest of any deposit refunded; with no deposit the $50.00 is invoiced".
2. **An Estimate visit** (free, [[OWN-DEC-99]]): does the $50.00 late-cancellation amount apply to an estimate visit cancelled late, or only to work visits?

- **Who answers:** the owner (with the attorney for the customer wording, [[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]).
- **Until answered:** the outcome of either case is "Not decided by the rules: Owner review" with no amount computed; the Owner decides and applies it ([[HH-FIN-14]]). Nothing is refunded, kept or invoiced automatically.
