**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the visit-confirmation and window-offer amendments?

- **Who answers:** the owner.
- **Decided, in use ([[OWN-DEC-193]], [[OWN-DEC-194]], [[OWN-DEC-208]]):** holds stay mandatory; while an intake hold is open, only a visit the Owner authorizes as a hazard-evaluation inspection, naming the qualified person and the safety conditions, can be confirmed, and the hold stays open. Estimate visits need no approved job; work visits need their approved job and every check; options are published only when the visit type meets its own conditions. A completed job allows a follow-up visit only under a contractual obligation, a warranty, the punch-list process or an approved return-work authorization. The 24 hours run from the successful publication to the customer, never from a saved draft, and the publication time and the exact expiry are recorded. One active offer per visit, no overlapping windows, capacity held across every window and released by a valid selection, atomic booking; withdraw or replace only with a recorded reason and a customer notice; no silent change to a published offer (migration 0120).
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - **What "successfully published" means.** The publication is the committed transaction that makes the offer visible in the customer's Appointments tab and writes its holds (every condition and window checked again then). Its time is the database clock at that commit; the expiry is exactly the configured hours (24) later. The automatic options notice ([[FACT-AUTOMATIC-MESSAGES]] #5 or #11) is sent after it; a failed delivery shows staff a delivery issue with Retry and does not move the clock.
  - **Drafts.** A draft keeps its windows, team and instructions as they will be published; it holds nothing, has no customer-facing number and is invisible to the customer. One draft per visit; a draft is never edited (staff discard it and save another). A draft whose visit planning changed since it was saved, or whose window is now in the past, is refused at publication. Staff may still save and publish in one step.
  - **Replacement and its notice.** Replacing a published offer publishes the visit's new windows with a written reason in the same transaction; the old offer ends as "replaced" and its holds are released. The customer's one notice of the change is the new offer's owner-worded options notice; the replaced offer sends nothing more and disappears from the Appointments tab.
  - **Withdrawal notice.** No owner-approved wording exists for a notice that offered times were withdrawn, so none is sent (NOT CONFIGURED). The withdrawn times leave the Appointments tab at once and staff get the time-sensitive item "Offered windows need new times" (reason: staff withdrew the offered windows), resolved when new windows are published, whose notice tells the customer. Wording proposed for the owner to approve or change (not used): "Hi {customerName}, the appointment times we offered for {reference} are no longer available. We'll send you new times shortly. You can view your project here: {projectLink}".
  - **The hazard-evaluation inspection.** The Owner records it (a new Owner-only row of [[ROLE-MATRIX]], "Authorize a hazard-evaluation inspection visit while an intake hold is open, with a reason", no fresh sign-in) on a tentative site visit (an Estimate visit: no work is performed); it names the qualified person, their qualification (recorded, never judged by the software), the safety conditions and a reason, and covers the intake review holds open at that moment. Those holds stay open and keep blocking every other visit and Start Work; an intake review hold raised later, a job-level Hold and a critical safety hold still block the inspection visit itself. The authorization is kept as recorded (no revocation: a mistaken one is handled by not confirming or cancelling the visit). The audit summary names the visit and the holds, not the qualified person.
  - **Follow-up visits on a completed job.** A job counts as complete when it is operationally complete (the Owner's quality review passed), closed or reopened. The basis is recorded on the tentative work visit by the Owner or Office (a new row of [[ROLE-MATRIX]], "Record a follow-up visit's authorization on a completed job", Office marked P): one of contractual obligation, warranty, punch-list process or approved return-work authorization, with a note saying which. A warranty names the job's return work classified as a warranty claim ([[FACT-WARRANTY]], [[TSK-0094]]); a return-work authorization names the job's return work the Owner decided. A return visit linked to return work still needs its basis recorded. While the job is in progress no authorization is needed.
  - **Staff text.** The draft, replacement, inspection and follow-up wording in English and Spanish (staff catalogs `offers.*`, `visits.inspection.*`, `visits.followUp.*`), written for this build.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes, the catalogs or the code.
