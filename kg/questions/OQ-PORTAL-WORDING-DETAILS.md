**Question:** does the owner approve the corrections below, and decide the places where the owner's own wording does not match what the software does?

- **Who answers:** the owner.
- **How the review was done:** every customer catalog sentence of the portal and the access pages that claims behaviour (a state, an action, a time, a notice, a link's life, what appears where) was traced to the code and configuration that makes it true, with the owner's list first: confirmed versus preferred appointments, real versus tentative holds, what the customer must do, pending deposits, estimate approval and expiry, payment state, and completed versus open jobs. Each corrected or added sentence is registered with the tests that pin its behaviour (`apps/portal/test/Wording.test.tsx`).
- **Checked and true, unchanged:** window offers ("These times are held for you until {time}." is a real capacity hold of the whole team for `scheduling.window_offers` 24 hours from publication, and the time shown is the one enforced; "Choose this time" confirms at once; Request other times releases the holds and opens a staff item; the expired, unavailable and closed messages); the reschedule and cancellation requests (the appointment stays as booked until HandyHub changes it); the deposit lines (a check is pending and never counts until cleared; Pay by Card only with a staff-pasted link; nothing marks a payment paid on return from Square); estimate expiry (`estimate.validity` days, to the end of the day in business time, refused at approval under the lock); "Approving does not book an appointment or pay a deposit."; the questions, reply, reopen and Closed—no response wording; the 24-hour guest link and the five-minute download links; Sign out everywhere; the repeat-service schedule wording except the reminder line below.
- **Corrected behaviour (the wording was the owner's or right, the software did not do it), built:**
  - The Overview now reads every record that changes what the customer must do. A change order open for a decision is "Review the change" (Documents tab); an issued final invoice with a balance not covered by a pending check is "Pay your invoice" (Payments tab), and its balance shows in the payment summary ("Invoice balance due: {amount}."), so "Nothing is needed from you" and "Nothing to pay yet" are never said while one is owed; a started job is "Work in progress"; a job paused by a hazard report whose safety hold was released, not yet resumed, is "Update pending". Before, these records were not read: a completed job with an unpaid invoice said "Nothing is needed from you".
  - A recorded "Arrived" no longer says "HandyHub recorded that your visit is on the way."; the step is "HandyHub recorded that your visit has arrived." (PROPOSED wording).
  - An estimate whose terms were re-versioned after it was sent is no longer offered for approval (the server refuses it, TERMS_NOT_CURRENT); the page says "The estimate terms changed after this version was sent, so this version can't be approved." (PROPOSED wording).
  - The Documents tab and the change orders show "Valid until" with the time and zone the estimate itself uses, never a date in the browser's zone that can be a day off.
  - A change order that another approved change blocked shows "Can't be decided" (PROPOSED), never "Waiting for your decision".
- **Corrected wording (ours, PROPOSED earlier), in use:** Documents empty state "Estimates, your approval receipt, change orders and invoices appear here once HandyHub shares them." (payment receipts are in Payments; care notes reach no portal page yet), and the line "Your approval receipt, change orders, receipts and care notes appear here…" is removed; "Your deposit and invoice amounts appear here once HandyHub shares them." (no estimate amount is shown there); a returned or voided payment's receipt "this payment was returned or voided, so it no longer counts" (the receipt itself is not voided); Request Changes "This version stays as it is until HandyHub sends a new one, withdraws it or it expires."; a blocked change order "…can't be approved or declined. Only a new version from HandyHub can be decided." (nothing creates or announces a new version by itself); the approval failure "This estimate can't be approved here yet. Please try again later or contact HandyHub." (a 503 is not always the terms); "Your approval receipt is being prepared. It appears here once it is ready." (no time is promised); Appointments "Your visits" (canceled and past visits are listed) and "No upcoming appointment"; the dashboard "Cancelled projects, and projects closed before any work was completed, appear here." and "Projects linked to your account appear here."; Add a project "If that email or phone was confirmed on the request, we'll send a one-time code there…"; Preferences "Request This Service Again and a request for a saved property start with this choice." (a plain new request reads no account preference; this narrows [[OQ-SAVED-PROPERTIES-DETAILS]] "the contact method new requests start with"); Saved properties "the places from your requests" (also requests that were never worked); Messages "Your conversation with HandyHub about this project appears here. Automatic notices aren't shown here."; Photos "Photos you upload on your project page appear here…"; access help "HandyHub will review your message and, if these details match…, contact you…" (the Owner may close it without contact).
- **Owner wording that differs from behaviour, not reworded, PROPOSED changes for the owner to decide:**
  1. "Estimate approved—choose your work appointment." ([[OWN-DEC-105]]) shows, with "Waiting on HandyHub" and no button, from approval until HandyHub publishes work windows, when there is nothing to choose yet. PROPOSED: until windows are offered show the owner's [[OWN-DEC-103]] sentence "Estimate approved—scheduling is next.", and "Estimate approved—choose your work appointment." once they are.
  2. "Review care notes and invoice" ([[HH-PORT-03]], Work complete) shows although care notes reach no portal page yet and an invoice may not be issued. PROPOSED: name only what exists ("Review the invoice." while care notes are not shared), or keep the sentence until care notes are shared.
  3. "View date, window and preparation" ([[HH-PORT-03]], Scheduled): preparation instructions show only when the window offer carried them; a visit staff booked directly has none. PROPOSED: drop "and preparation" when there are none, or require instructions on every booking.
  4. "View shared updates" ([[HH-PORT-03]], Work in progress): no work-progress updates are shared in the portal yet (only shared photos and concern updates). PROPOSED: point it at shared photos, or keep it until progress updates exist.
  5. "On the way" ([[HH-PORT-03]]) stays the label after a recorded "Arrived" until work starts (the table has no Arrived row); only the step says the visit arrived. PROPOSED: an "Arrived" label for that state.
  6. "Please pay your deposit so we can confirm your work appointment." ([[HH-FIN-02]]): paying confirms nothing by itself; HandyHub offers work windows once the deposit is received and the customer then chooses one. PROPOSED: keep (it is true of the sequence) or add "then choose a time".
- **Reviewed, not changed, for the owner's decision (minor, or owned by another open question):** Payments "Balance due" counts issued invoices only, so it shows $0.00 beside a deposit still due with no deposit invoice (PROPOSED: "Balance due on invoices"); "Paid (confirmed)" counts a disputed payment until its outcome ([[OQ-PAYMENT-EXCEPTION-DETAILS]]) and refunds are not shown to the customer; the invoice line "Total · credits · paid · balance" leaves out a write-off; "Deposit received: {amount}" shows the full amount when a partly paid deposit was waived and credited; Pay by Card stays offered while a pending check already covers the deposit; "To see your project again, open the secure link HandyHub sent you." is also shown to an account whose session ended (it should sign in); "This project is closed, so messages can't be sent here." also shows during a cancellation review; "a new secure link is on its way" is not sent at the daily reissue limit or after a revoked link; "We'll follow up on it by {date}." is the Owner's review target, not a message ([[OQ-PRIVACY-DEADLINE-DETAILS]]); "We prepare a reminder for each date." while the reminder wording is NOT CONFIGURED, so none is sent ([[OQ-REPEAT-SERVICE-DETAILS]]); "Check your connection" also after a server error.
- **Until answered:** the corrected wording and behaviour above are in use on private staging; changing any sentence is a catalog edit plus its register entry.
