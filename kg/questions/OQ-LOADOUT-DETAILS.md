**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the loadout and of critical shortages, and who sets the missing values?

- **Who answers:** the owner; which items are critical, and any PPE, chemical, respiratory, height-access or digging item, with the qualified safety professional ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]], [[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]). The software decides none of these.
- **Decided, in use:** item lists come from each service's inventory file (the 26 `FACT-INVENTORY-<code>` nodes, imported verbatim by `tools/import-loadouts.mjs`, every line with its vault line) and quantities are never invented ([[HH-PKT-05]]); a shortage of a critical item creates a task-specific block with an owner and an action, and Start Work stays blocked for the affected work package only ([[HH-PKT-06]], [[HH-WORK-01]]); no checkbox, later statement, timer, AI output or override clears a block ([[HH-WORK-02]], [[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]); nothing is silently marked equivalent; a substitute that affects the customer is a change order ([[HH-CHG-02]]).
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - Every imported item is "required": the inventory files do not say which items are only nice to have (unknown is not safe, as for the SOP steps). The owner marks items nice to have in the inventory nodes and the list is imported again as a new version.
  - The imported lists keep the files' own group headings; the five HH-PKT-05 categories (shop and vehicle tools, consumable materials, PPE, rental equipment, customer-supplied products) are not assigned to the imported items (`not_classified`) rather than guessed from a heading.
  - Only the "Inventory checklist as written" items are on a loadout. The files' "Proposed additions" paragraphs (pending owner and safety review, approved for nothing) are shown as not approved and are never items. Items under a FLAG review note stay on the list as written, with the note shown beside their group; whether such an item is loaded at all is for the owner and the safety reviewer.
  - A work package's list is the current version of its service's list, pinned on the package the first time its job's packet or loadout is read after the version exists, and never changed afterwards; a later import is a new version for later jobs only.
  - Workers state per item: loaded (loaded and available), missing or damaged, with an optional note. Stating is the "Checklist steps, photos, notes, walkthrough record, issue reports" row of the matrix (Owner and Worker, assigned jobs only, inside the access window); Office reads the loadout but does not state it.
  - A missing or damaged required item raises one task block (an ENT-Hold of kind `task_block` on the work package, category `critical_resource`) in the same transaction as the statement, under the job's lock, and the packet gets a new version (a hold is a critical packet change). A missing nice-to-have item is recorded and blocks nothing. A task block never blocks a visit confirmation ([[HH-SCH-06]]).
  - The block's owner and action: the Owner provides the item or approves a substitute. Office (and the Estimator, PROPOSED cells) may start the review (`job_hold.set`); only the Owner resolves or cancels it (`job_hold.set_ready`). The Owner's action queue shows one "Critical loadout shortage" item per job while a block stands.
  - A block is resolved only with a note and who assessed it, either "item provided" (the worker stated the item loaded after the block was raised, and that is still its latest statement) or "substitute approved" (the substitute is named, never "equivalent", and the approver records what it affects: safety, appearance, specification, price, warranty, and whether the customer is affected). A later "loaded" statement on its own releases nothing. A block entered in error is cancelled with the reason; the item then still needs a "loaded" statement.
  - A work package's loadout is ready only when every required item's latest statement is loaded or an approved substitute and no block stands; the Start Work gate ([[TSK-0073]]) reads this for its seventh prerequisite. The database refuses to move a work package to in progress while one of its blocks stands.
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** a substitute that affects the customer needs a change order, which does not exist until [[TSK-0081]] and [[TSK-0082]]: it is refused with CHANGE_ORDER_REQUIRED, never approved here. A service without an imported list shows NOT CONFIGURED and its package's loadout is never ready (all 26 services have a list today).
- **Waiting for later tasks:** photos as shortage evidence ([[TSK-0076]]: until then the worker's note is the evidence); items from the approved scope, rental equipment and customer-supplied products added per job (with the change orders of [[TSK-0081]] and [[TSK-0082]]); the Start Work gate itself ([[TSK-0073]]) and the three owner-approved administrative exceptions ([[TSK-0074]]: none of them clears a task block); the offline loadout ([[TSK-0079]]); Spanish item text (the lists stay as the vault files write them; labels are translated).
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code.
