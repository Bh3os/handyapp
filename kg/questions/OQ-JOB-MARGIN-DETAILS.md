**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of job gross margin, the labor cost rate and the low-margin reasons?

- **Who answers:** the owner; what counts as net revenue and direct cost, and when a deposit is recognized, go to the owner's CPA ([[OWN-DEC-186]], [[OWN-DEC-189]] item 15, [[OQ-TAX-CPA]]). The software decides none of them.
- **Decided, in use:** margin = (net job revenue − direct job costs) / net job revenue, from integer cents; the target 30% (3000 basis points, `job_margin.target`, migration 0116), configurable by the Owner (`PUT /staff/settings/margin-target`: a fresh sign-in and a reason, audited); the flag is advisory and never blocks accepting, publishing, completing or invoicing a job; incomplete data is labelled and never shown as a final margin; the Owner records reasons for low-margin jobs; overhead and business net profit stay apart ([[OWN-DEC-189]], [[FACT-MARGIN-TARGET]]).
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - Net revenue (actual): the job's final invoices issued, partially paid or paid (their totals hold approved discounts, change orders and credit change orders, and credit the deposit, so the deposit counts once) less refunds that succeeded and credits issued or applied. Payments never count, so a partial payment changes nothing. With no final invoice issued, nothing is recognized as revenue yet (a deposit is not revenue before then). A refund or credit still requested, approved, processing or needing review leaves revenue incomplete; so does a write-off, since how write-offs count is NOT CONFIGURED. The report definition is `job_margin` (basis "estimated profit") in `@handyhub/domain` REPORT_DEFINITIONS.
  - Estimated margin: the job's contract price (its latest scope snapshot: the accepted estimate plus approved change orders) against the estimated cost total of the job cost report (the accepted version's internal material and labor costs); incomplete while any counted line lacks them or a change order is approved (change orders carry no cost estimate). In the approval queue of [[TSK-0130]] the estimated margin of the document is shown against the same target, advisory only: it never adds an approval reason.
  - Labor cost: each worker's Arrived-to-Finished minutes on each of the job's visits at the rate in force on the visit's local date, rounded once per worker-visit, half-up, to the cent. A time with no rate is NOT CONFIGURED; labor is final only once the job is operationally complete (or closed) and every time is known and rated. Burden the same way from the burden rate; a rate entered with no burden leaves burden NOT CONFIGURED, so the margin stays incomplete until the Owner states it (0 allowed).
  - Labor cost rates (`finance.labor_cost_rates`): entered by the Owner per staff member in cents per hour, $0.00 to $1,000.00 (0: the time carries no labor cost, e.g. a subcontractor whose cost is entered as a direct job cost, [[OWN-DEC-188]]), from a local date, with a reason; a later entry replaces the rate from its date; never changed or deleted; audited without the amounts; kept by a staging reset with the staff accounts. Who: `labor_rate.manage`, the Owner.
  - The flag: a final margin below the target, compared exactly (29.995% is below 30%); a final loss on zero or negative revenue is below any target; no flag while the margin is not final or the target is NOT CONFIGURED. The percentage shown is basis points rounded half-up.
  - Reasons (`finance.job_margin_reviews`): underpricing, material overrun, additional labor, unexpected site conditions, or other with a note; recorded only for a job flagged below the target, against its final figures and the target in force; a reason is current while those figures and the target are unchanged, otherwise the job asks for a new one. Append-only. Who: `job_margin.review`, the Owner.
  - Owner Dashboard ([[TSK-0096]]): of the jobs completed in the period, how many have a final margin, how many are not final, how many are below the target and how many of those have no current reason; the target in force; each opens its jobs.
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** every labor cost rate and burden rate (until the Owner enters them); any overhead (never a job cost); when a deposit is recognized as revenue (the CPA's); how write-offs count.
- **Human review:** the accounting definitions above are the CPA's ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]); the app computes no payroll or tax.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code.
