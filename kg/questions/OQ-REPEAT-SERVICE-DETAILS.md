**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of a customer-agreed repeat-service schedule and its reminders, and what is the reminder's wording?

- **Who answers:** the owner.
- **Decided, in use ([[OWN-DEC-113]], [[OWN-DEC-148]], [[FACT-REMINDER-CADENCES]]):** repeat-service reminders are prepared only on a schedule the customer agreed to and released by staff; the customer can change or stop the schedule and its reminders; a change applies to visits more than 24 hours away and a visit within 24 hours follows the cancellation rules (`repeat_service.change_notice`, migration 0124); channel permissions, marketing consent and opt-outs are checked before every send; new pricing and appointments need staff review.
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** the reminder's wording (no template of `reminder.repeat_service` is released): staff see each prepared draft but cannot release it, so no repeat-service reminder is ever sent until the Owner releases the wording. No lead time before an occurrence is decided (see "When" below). No interval, no default date and no list of offered frequencies is configured: the software proposes none, and a schedule exists only because staff recorded the customer's agreement.
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging (migration 0124):**
  - **What a schedule is.** One per project (the project the service was done in, its property and the request's contact), recorded by the Owner or Office (`service_series.manage`, Office P) only with the box "The customer agreed to this schedule." and how staff heard it (phone, in person, email, a message): the agreed next service date and, when the customer agreed to a repeat, every 1-366 days, 1-52 weeks or 1-24 months (input limits only); or one date with no repeat. Whether the customer wants reminders. A stopped schedule stays stopped; a new agreement is a new schedule.
  - **Dates.** Local calendar dates of the property's zone, read each time; never recorded while the zone is unknown (never assumed Eastern). Occurrence k is the agreed date plus k intervals, counted from that date each time (31 January + 1 month is 28 February, and the next one is 31 March again); a clock change never moves a date.
  - **Each occurrence is a new request.** The customer sends it (Request This Service Again, [[OQ-SAVED-PROPERTIES-DETAILS]], or any request), with its own customer, contact, property and project records, its own staff review, estimate, approval, deposit and confirmed appointment; nothing is copied (no price, discount, verification or consent). [[FACT-SVC-RECURRING-SERVICE]] "its price holds until a new estimate" is read as: staff may write the new request's estimate at the earlier price; the software never copies it. Staff link the request to its date ("Link the request"): only a request whose contact email or phone is the schedule's, or that was sent again from the schedule's project or one of its occurrences, never the schedule's own project, one request per occurrence and one occurrence per request. Nothing is created automatically from a schedule.
  - **When a reminder is prepared.** On each occurrence's date, at the opening of the contact hours ([[FACT-HOURS]], 8 AM) in the property's zone, once per date; for staff to release (the Owner or Office, `message.release_draft`), by email only while texts are off. Not for a date that was skipped or already has its request. The Owner may ask for a lead time (for example a week before) instead.
  - **Consent.** The reminder is service communication the customer asked for, never marketing (no marketing consent needed). An opt-out of optional follow-ups (TSK-0113 preferences: service communication withdrawn on the channel) stops it, checked when it is prepared, when it is released and right before it sends.
  - **Active complaint.** Any case open on the schedule's project or on a project an occurrence was requested in holds the draft: it cannot be released and is offered again once every case is closed ([[OWN-DEC-124]]).
  - **The customer's control** (the project workspace, `service_series.change`, customer V, also from a guest link's session): turn reminders off (any waiting draft is cancelled at once) and back on; change the next date and how often; stop the schedule (after a confirmation). The new next date must start more than the notice away; dates of the current schedule inside the notice keep their date ("kept"), with their reminder and any request. Stopping is immediate for the schedule and its reminders; a request already sent stays its own, and its appointment is rescheduled or cancelled the usual way. Changes: 60 per network per hour.
  - **Staff's control.** Record a change, a stop or a skip of one upcoming occurrence the customer asked for, each with how they heard it and an optional staff note (never shown to the customer).
  - **Records.** The history of a schedule is append-only (who: staff or the customer, the schedule then, kept dates, a skip or link, how staff heard it, staff notes); the audit log keeps identifiers and codes only. The staff page is `/staff/repeat-service/<project>` (linked from the request record, below Customers in the menu); the queue item "Repeat-service reminders to release" opens it. The customer sees the schedule's state, its next three dates (each marked requested once its request is linked), how often, whether reminders are on and the notice; never how staff heard it, a note or the drafts.
  - **Wording.** Staff catalogs `repeat.*`, `enum.series*`, `enum.repeatReminderBlock.*`, `enum.repeatRefusal.*`; customer catalog `portal.repeat.*` and the follow-ups preference now names repeat-service reminders. Interface wording only; the reminder message itself has none.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small change to the nodes or the code. The reminder stays unsendable until its wording is released.
