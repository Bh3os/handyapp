**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the wider re-review triggers and the print metadata?

- **Who answers:** the owner; which property details count as a hazard or a restriction with the competent safety review ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]]).
- **Decided, in use ([[OWN-DEC-196]]):** any new or materially changed hazard, SOP, authorization restriction, site-access requirement or scope is a critical revision; every assigned worker reviews and acknowledges it before the Start Work gate passes or work resumes ([[TSK-0072]], [[TSK-0073]], [[TSK-0094]]). The packet is generated from the latest records whenever it is opened, listed, printed or downloaded, and the gate brings it up to date before it decides. Printed and downloaded packets carry the packet revision, its generation time, the job reference and a notice that printed copies may be out of date; printing keeps the "View job packet" permission and the PDF its limit of 60 per staff member per hour; a print never holds an access code, a code label, a price, a photo or a link, and a worker's print follows the visit-day access of [[OWN-DEC-171]] (address, site-access details and the site contact only during the access, [[TSK-0128]]).
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - **Hazard and restriction details.** Four of the property's occupant and environment details are critical when their value is recorded, changed or removed: occupants and pets, restricted areas, power and water access, and known site flags (a hazard, an authorization restriction or a site-access requirement). Customer preparation stays a new version that is not critical. Confirming the same value again is never critical.
  - **What the site contact may authorize.** The packet holds the project's authorized site contact ([[TSK-0126]]) as its version, whether it is the customer or a person the customer named, and what staff confirmed the contact may do (give access, coordinate the visit); never the person's name, phone or email. A change of that authority, or of who the contact is (the customer or another person), is critical; the same contact recorded again with the same authority (a corrected phone, say) is a new version that is not critical. A worker sees the authority only during their visit-day access, like the site contact. The first version of each packet after this change lists the site contact once, not critical.
  - **Unchanged rules.** The critical changes already built stay: a new accepted scope or its lines and texts (an approved change order, with its hazards), a blocking hold raised, changed or ended (a hazard report, a loadout task block), a new pinned SOP version or a service added, a worker added or removed, an address, property-type, site-access detail or access-code change. A moved window, a visit state, photos, equipment, the loadout list, the job, package and deposit states and an SOP sign-off of the same version are new versions that are not critical, so the normal course of the work never ends an acknowledgment.
  - **"Applicable" revisions.** Every critical revision affects every worker assigned to the job; nothing is narrowed to one visit, service or task (stricter than "when relevant", as [[OQ-PACKET-ACKNOWLEDGMENT]] proposes).
  - **Job reference.** A job belongs to one project, so the job reference printed is the project's reference.
  - **Print layout.** Every PDF page carries the print time and "Printed copies may be out of date; check the app before starting" at the top (set smaller when the Spanish text does not fit one line), and the job reference, the packet revision and its generation time with "no access codes" and the page number at the bottom; the first page lists the job reference, the revision, its generation time, the print time and when the packet was first generated, after the same notice. The staff app's print view carries the same notice, the print time, the job reference, the revision and its generation time. Wording in English and a plain Spanish translation, written for this build.
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small code change.
