**Answered by [[OWN-DEC-211]] (2026-10-22):** "Notes in Spanish": workers write notes and proposed replies in Spanish; an Owner or Office review queue prepares and approves the English customer message; the Spanish original is kept internally ([[HH-COM-16]], [[TSK-0155]]).

**Question:** customer messages must be professional English ([[OWN-DEC-162]]) and translating Spanish drafts waits for wave 2 ([[OWN-DEC-166]]). Until then, how do Spanish-speaking employees contribute to customer messages?

- **Who answers:** the owner.
- **PROPOSED (not decided):** customer-facing replies are written in English by the person who releases them (the Owner or Office); Spanish-speaking workers write internal project notes in Spanish (never sent to customers), and staff turn them into English replies.
- **Until answered:** the reply composer sends English text only; no translation feature exists.
