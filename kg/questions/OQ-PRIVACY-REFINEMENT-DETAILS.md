**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the privacy request refinements?

- **Who answers:** the owner; what an export must disclose or may withhold, and the identity verification a request needs, with the attorney ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-PRIVACY]]); the table-to-retention mapping with the CPA and the attorney ([[OQ-RETENTION-REVIEW]], [[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]).
- **Decided, in use:** every request needs a fresh one-time code in the same session; an Owner-verified alternative exists for a person who cannot complete it; the export contains the requester's own data and does not leave out data only because it is in the audit log; editable records keep their change history and protected records are never rewritten; before a deletion the accounting records are checked, an unrecorded external payment is not assumed, an unresolved discrepancy goes to authorized review, and unrelated eligible data is still deleted; no destructive production deletion rests on an unreviewed table mapping ([[OWN-DEC-201]]).
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** the attorney's list of disclosure exceptions for audit entries, and any legal identity-verification standard. Until then the PROPOSED choices below apply on private staging.
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - **I1, risk levels.** A correction is standard: the fresh code is enough. An export (it discloses the person's data) and a deletion (it removes data) are elevated: the Owner also ticks "I checked the identity evidence above" before completing one; the confirmation is recorded with the decision (Owner and time). Declining needs no confirmation. No second code or second channel is asked of the customer.
  - **I1, the secure alternative.** A person who cannot complete the code asks HandyHub (or uses access help, [[TSK-0149]]). The Owner verifies them another way, chosen from: a call to the phone on file, a reply from the email on file, a photo ID seen in person; writes a note (never an ID number); and records the request on the Privacy requests screen with a fresh sign-in, naming the project by its reference and the person by the email or phone on file for a contact who asked on that project. The request then has the same dates, review and decision as one made in the portal, and the person sees it in their project page once they have access. It is elevated like any export or deletion.
  - **I2, audit entries in the export.** The export's `activity` lists every audit entry of the requester's own actions, and the entries about their own privacy requests, contact record and login accounts: time, action code, record kind, result, and who acted ("you", "staff" without a name, "automatic"). Left out as restricted internal records or third-party information: the entries' summaries and reasons, staff identities, and the entries about staff handling of requests, estimates, jobs, cases, notes and other records. The `excluded` list names `audit_entry_details` and `restricted_audit_entries` instead of `audit_entries`.
  - **I3, corrections.** Customer, contact and property details are corrected in their own screens (their history keeps the values before and after, the reason and the staff member). The request shows those changes made since it was received (record and fields only). A record never rewritten (signed approval, estimate version, invoice, payment, receipt, refund, audit entry, job record, message or other) is corrected by a new entry the Owner records on the request: the record's kind and reference and the correction in the Owner's words, append-only and audited, with no fresh sign-in (nothing is disclosed or removed). A correction request is completed only once at least one change or entry is recorded; otherwise it is declined with a reason. The customer's outcome text is unchanged ("corrected").
  - **I4, accounting checks.** Issued invoices, payments, deposit records, refunds, credits and write-offs and reconciliation items linked to the record are found (protected evidence: they block) or none found; issued invoices and payments are no longer confirmed by hand. "Linked" is: for an estimate, its approvals' jobs, deposits and payments; for a request erasure or a privacy request, the whole project. An unresolved discrepancy is any entry of the reconciliation queue for those records: an open reconciliation item, a check received or deposited but not cleared, an open dispute, a refund processing or needing review. It blocks a Delete (`financial_discrepancy`) and is resolved in the reconciliation queue, where the Owner reviews it; no new queue item is opened. In a privacy deletion request it is shown as a check and never blocks the deletion of an estimate never accepted, which has no financial record of its own.
  - **I5, the mapping review.** The report is `docs/retention-mapping-review.md`, generated with the data inventory: the mapping's SHA-256 digest, each category with where its rule comes from (decided, provisional, no period) and its tables, and five finding kinds (no scheduled period, provisional period, start not recorded, several categories, partly protected). The Retention reviews screen adds the live audit of the database the server runs on (tables without an annotation, annotations without a table). The Owner records a review of one digest with a fresh sign-in and a note, ticking whether the CPA and the attorney confirmed it (`records.delete`). In production every Delete waits until the latest review names the mapping in force with both confirmations: Confirm Delete on a review entry and the audit-retention delete refuse `MAPPING_NOT_REVIEWED`; a privacy deletion request keeps its estimates ("can't be deleted yet") and still closes access. Any later change of an annotation is a new digest to review. Outside production nothing is gated.
