**Question:** who resolves the questions the vault SOPs and inventory files leave open inside their own text, and how is each answer recorded?

- **What they are.** The SOPs and inventories linked above carry inline markers from the owner's vault and its cross-check: "OPEN QUESTION" (for example whether a permit applies, the maximum item weight for one technician, who verifies the authority to change locks, the disposition of removed hardware), "FLAG ... CONFLICT" (a step that promises more than the procedure covers, or an outcome claim that conflicts with the service agreement's waiver) and "UNSOURCED" values (keep-off times or mixing quantities with no manufacturer source). Each is kept word for word in its node; none was resolved by choosing one source ([[HH-AUD-02]]).
- **Who answers:** the qualified safety professional with the owner for procedures, PPE, quantities and hazards ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]], packet `docs/review-packets/safety.md`); the attorney where a step conflicts with a service agreement clause ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-TERMS]]); the owner for business policy (scope, disposition, which work is offered).
- **Until answered:** every imported checklist stays "Pending competent review" and is not used for real jobs ([[OWN-DEC-17]]); the flagged steps and holds stay as written; unknown is treated as unsafe; no inventory addition is approved for purchase or use. An answer is recorded as a new SOP or inventory version through the review, never by editing the imported text in place.
