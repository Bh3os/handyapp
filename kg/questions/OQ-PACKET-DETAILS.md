**Answered by [[OWN-DEC-192]], [[OWN-DEC-196]], [[OWN-DEC-172]] (2026-10-21).**

**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the job packet, and who sets the missing values?

- **Who answers:** the owner; the emergency and stop-work wording, and its Spanish text, with the competent safety review ([[OQ-SAFETY-REVIEW]], [[HH-PKT-04]]).
- **Decided, in use:** one versioned packet per job, generated by the server from stored records only; every version keeps what changed and whether a change is critical; "What changed since you reviewed this?" compares with the version the worker last viewed; nobody inherits another person's view; access codes are never in a packet, a print or a download, and are revealed separately, online, on the visit date, to the assigned worker, audited ([[OWN-DEC-121]], [[HH-CRM-03]]); every print and PDF is recorded and audited with who, when and which version, and carries "Printed <time>; check the app for changes".
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - Critical changes (they invalidate the review, [[TSK-0072]]): a new accepted scope version, a scope line or text; a hold raised, changed or no longer blocking; a new pinned SOP version; a worker added to or removed from a visit, or a changed role; the address version, the property type, a site-access detail (site contact, access windows, access notes, parking and loading) or an access code added, replaced or removed. Not critical, but a new version: a moved window or a visit state, occupant details (pets, restricted areas, utilities, customer preparation, site flags), photos, equipment, the job's state, the deposit state, a reconfirmation of an unchanged detail, a sign-off of the same SOP version.
  - The packet is brought up to date whenever it is read (opened, listed, printed or downloaded), so a version's "last updated" time is when the server first saw the change; a writer can also publish a version inside its own transaction (the change-order approval of [[TSK-0082]]).
  - A worker reaches a job's packet only with an active confirmed reservation on one of its live visits (confirmed, on the way or arrived), until the end of that visit's service day in its zone; a completed visit ends it earlier ([[OWN-DEC-40]]). The Estimator column of "View job packet" stays the matrix's PROPOSED cell.
  - Printing and the PDF use the "View job packet" permission (the matrix has no export row); at most 60 PDFs per staff member per hour. The print and the PDF leave out photos, access-code labels, prices and links; the on-screen packet shows a code's kind, label and last confirmation.
  - Prior evidence is the project's ready customer and staff photos that are internal or customer-shared (never restricted incident photos), with 5-minute signed links.
  - The directions link opens the address in a maps app through a public directions URL; nothing is sent until the worker taps it.
  - The scope in a packet keeps description, quantity and unit only: no price, total, deposit amount or discount.
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** the Owner's escalation contact (the `<owner decision: emergency contact>` and `<owner decision: escalation order>` of [[FACT-SVC-ESCALATION-PATH]]): the packet says NOT CONFIGURED and to ask the office.
- **Waiting for later tasks:** the materials list and loadout ([[TSK-0071]]: the packet says no list is recorded and never guesses a quantity); approved care notes ([[OWN-DEC-81]]: none is shown until the owner approves them); hazard reports and critical safety holds ([[TSK-0077]]: shown with the other blocking holds once they exist); acknowledgments and readiness ([[TSK-0072]]); the offline copy ([[TSK-0079]]); Spanish safety text, including the packet's emergency line ([[TSK-0103]]: until then the Spanish line is a plain translation pending the AI-reviewed comparison, and the SOP text stays English).
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small code change.
