**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of the privacy request acknowledgment, the 3-business-day and 30-day dates, the extensions and the legal deadline field?

- **Who answers:** the owner; the legal deadline and any legal notice period with the attorney ([[OQ-ATTORNEY-PRIVACY]], [[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]).
- **Decided, in use:** the business calendar is Monday to Friday in the business time zone, without the 11 US federal holidays on their observed dates, configurable and audited (`business.calendar` version 1, [[OWN-DEC-205]]). Each request is acknowledged automatically on submission, is due a substantive acknowledgment within 3 business days, and has an internal completion target of 30 calendar days from receipt (`privacy.request_targets` version 1, [[OWN-DEC-175]]). The legal deadline is a separate field, never computed from the calendar or the target. Nothing is rejected or closed when a date passes. Extensions are Owner decisions with a reason the customer is told. Pending requests, their dates and overdue alerts are shown to the Owner.
- **NOT CONFIGURED:** the applicable legal deadline of each request (empty until the Owner records the date the attorney defines), the legal notice period for an extension, and the wording of the acknowledgment and extension messages ([[OQ-MESSAGES-OUTSIDE-THE-21]] item 4). Until then nothing is sent: the customer sees the acknowledgment and any extension in their project page only.
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - **Counting.** The receipt date is the local date of the submission in the business time zone. The 3 business days start the day after the receipt date, whatever day the request arrives: a request received on a Friday afternoon or a Saturday has Monday as its first business day. "Due by" a date means by the end of that local day. The 30 days are calendar days added to the receipt date, so a clock change never moves them.
  - **Holidays.** A holiday's own date is not a business day either, as well as its observed date. This only matters if the Owner makes Saturday or Sunday a business day.
  - **Versions.** The dates are counted once, when the request is received, with the calendar and target versions then in force, and stored with those versions. A later calendar change applies only to requests received afterwards.
  - **Substantive acknowledgment.** Opening the Owner's review ("Start the review") counts as the substantive acknowledgment. The customer then sees that the review started.
  - **What the customer sees (English, portal only).** "We received this request on {date}. HandyHub's owner will review it." / "We'll follow up on it by {date}." / "HandyHub's owner started reviewing it on {date}." / "We aim to complete it by {date}." / "We need more time: we now aim to complete it by {date}. Why: {reason}". Showing the internal target date to the customer is itself PROPOSED: the 30 days are a customer-service commitment, not a statement of the law. The legal deadline is not shown to the customer.
  - **Extensions.** Recorded by the Owner (`privacy.decide`) on an open request, with a new date after the target in force and after today, at most 366 days ahead, and a reason of up to 1,000 characters that the customer is shown. Any number of extensions; each starts from the target then in force. The software cannot check that more time is legally permitted; the Owner confirms that. No fresh sign-in is asked for, because an extension neither discloses nor removes data.
  - **Legal deadline.** The Owner records it per request (`privacy.decide`) with a note saying where it comes from, or clears it with a note. It may be any date from the receipt date up to 366 days ahead; a date already passed is overdue at once. No fresh sign-in.
  - **Overdue alerts.** Three alerts: substantive acknowledgment overdue (no review started after its date), internal target overdue, and legal deadline overdue. Each open request has one Owner Decisions item, "Privacy request deadlines" (`privacy_request`, time-sensitive band), due at its next date, with a reason saying which date is due or overdue. A job at each date updates the item. The item resolves when the Owner decides. The Owner Dashboard tile itself is built with [[TSK-0096]]; until then Owner Decisions and the Privacy requests screen list the requests with their dates and alerts.
  - **Calendar screen.** The Owner reads the calendar (`dashboard.view`) and changes it (`policy_values.change`, with a fresh sign-in and a reason) on the Settings screen. The workdays, the holidays left out and further closed dates (up to 366) can all be changed. The observed holidays of this year and the next are shown as the software counts them.
  - **Guests.** A customer without an account submits from the secure project link verified with a one-time code (the existing guest path). A person with no working link asks for access help ([[TSK-0149]]); a secure alternative path by request risk is [[TSK-0152]] (I1).
  - **Access requests.** As in [[OQ-PRIVACY-REQUESTS]]: answered by the data export; there is no separate access request kind.
  - **Requests received before this change.** Open requests get their dates counted from their receipt, with the versions above, by a one-time job (migration 0103). Their automatic acknowledgment is recorded at their submission time, since the portal has shown them since then. Requests already decided are final and keep no dates.
