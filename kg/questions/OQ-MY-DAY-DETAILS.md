**Question:** does the owner approve, or change, these PROPOSED details of My Day and the Arrived and Finished times?

- **Who answers:** the owner; the accountant for the export's columns ([[HH-FIN-16]]). Payroll, overtime and deductions stay outside the software and are not proposed here ([[RULE-HUMAN-REVIEW]]: payroll is a human-review area).
- **Decided, in use:** On my way is the assigned worker's own tap, from three hours before the arrival window (`visit.on_my_way`, seeded from [[OWN-DEC-107]]); time alone records nothing; a canceled visit, or a worker reassigned away, is refused; repeated taps record nothing more and send nothing more; the first accepted tap sends the automatic on-the-way notice once, with first names and the arrival window, never an ETA or tracking ([[OQ-ON-THE-WAY-WORDING]] for its wording). Arrived and Finished are one server timestamp each per assigned worker; Arrived never starts work or passes the Start Work gate; there is no check-in between Arrived and Finished ([[OWN-DEC-149]]); a missing value is flagged, never invented; the Owner corrects with a reason and the original is kept; the times export as CSV for the accountant with no pay calculation ([[OWN-DEC-69]]). The customer sees "On the way" and "Arrived" only once recorded.
- **PROPOSED, in use on private staging:**
  - Arrived is refused until someone tapped On my way (the visit's states follow [[ENT-Visit]]: confirmed, on the way, arrived), so the customer never sees an arrival that skipped the recorded departure. A teammate who has not left yet may still tap On my way: it records that worker's departure only (no second notice); a teammate who joins an arrived visit taps Arrived directly.
  - Finished needs the worker's own Arrived. The visit is completed when every worker assigned to it has a Finished value; the Owner's correction that supplies the last missing Finished completes it. A correction never moves a visit any other way.
  - A visit that went on the way cannot go back to confirmed (for example a worker who turned back): staff handle it outside this flow until the owner decides.
  - At send time the on-the-way notice goes only while the visit is still on the way (an arrived or completed visit sends nothing). It uses the customer's permitted channels like the other automatic notices.
  - My Day lists the visits of today in each visit's own zone, and any visit still on the way or arrived, from the worker's own active reservations: a tentative hold shows no address and nothing to record. The address, directions link, access notes and preparation show only for a confirmed live visit ([[OWN-DEC-40]]); a completed visit shows none.
  - Readiness on My Day lists what the records show: blocking holds, a deposit not yet covered, a work package without an SOP, a job packet not viewed or changed since (critical or not), the worker's own packet acknowledgment missing or ended ([[TSK-0072]]), and each work package's loadout: a shortage's task block (listed once, as a loadout shortage, not also as a hold), required items not yet confirmed loaded, or no list (NOT CONFIGURED) ([[TSK-0071]]). It is not the Start Work check ([[RULE-SAFETY-GATES]]) and never refuses or allows a tap: On my way, Arrived and Finished do not depend on it, and a tap neither restores an acknowledgment nor releases a block.
  - A worker can tap until the end of the visit's service day in its zone; after that a forgotten value is the Owner's correction. Arrived is due once the visit arrived (or its day ended after it went on the way); Finished once the visit is completed or its day ended after the worker arrived.
  - Only the Owner sees and corrects the times (`visit.correct_timestamps`). A correction is in the past, keeps Arrived before Finished and is entered in the device's zone (the visit's zone is shown beside it).
  - The CSV has one row per worker and visit date (in the visit's zone): date, zone, project reference, visit number and purpose, worker name and ID, Arrived and Finished (local and UTC, with recorded, corrected, pending or missing), whole minutes between them when both exist, and whether a value was corrected. One export covers at most 366 days, needs the Owner's fresh sign-in and a reason (the accounting-export rule), is audited, and is limited to 30 per hour.
  - The notice's first names are the first word of each display name; a worker without one is left out.
  - The first On my way cancels the visit's waiting appointment reminders (a draft, or a released one not yet with the provider) in the tap's transaction, audited with the reason `visit_en_route`, and resolves their queue item ([[HH-COM-11]]: an outdated reminder is never sent; migration 0060 stays the database's backstop).
  - In a privacy request ([[TSK-0100]]) the departures, the Arrived and Finished times and their corrections are job records, retained with the visits; they name a visit, never an estimate version, so deleting an estimate never accepted leaves them.
  - The device's clock is kept beside the server's time when the app sends it. A tap without a connection is not saved on the phone: the screen says it was not recorded (offline drafts and their sync are [[TSK-0079]]).
- **Waiting for later tasks:** offline timestamps and sync ([[TSK-0079]]); the sequence from Arrived to Awaiting quality review ([[TSK-0083]]); the Owner Dashboard and the other accountant-ready exports ([[TSK-0096]]).
- **Until answered:** the PROPOSED details above are used; each is a small code change.
