id: RULE-DECIDED-VALUES-ACTIVE
type: rule
title: Decided values are active configuration
status: active
area: platform
links:
  decided_by: ["[[OWN-DEC-122]]"]
source: "OWN-DEC-122"
---
- Every value the owner decided is seeded as active, versioned and audited configuration, citing its decision. No separate settings-activation step may block a workflow.
- Values that are genuinely missing stay missing and are never invented. They show a narrow "missing" state only on the exact output that needs them; on staging they use clearly labelled TEST values.
- Safety, access and customer-approval protections stay exactly as agreed.
