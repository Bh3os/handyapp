These areas are flagged with a proposal and a named reviewer, never decided by software or AI: legal, tax, accounting close, payroll, licensing, insurance, structural work, licensed electrical/plumbing/HVAC/gas work, hazardous materials (asbestos, lead, mold), OSHA, injury and disputes. HandyHub never performs licensed gas, electrical, plumbing, HVAC or structural work, and never removes hazardous materials.
