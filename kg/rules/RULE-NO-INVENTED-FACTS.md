---
id: RULE-NO-INVENTED-FACTS
type: rule
title: Never invent business facts
status: active
area: business
source: "vault CLAUDE.md rule 1"
---
Never write prices, rates, discounts, deposit amounts, tax rates or treatments, licensing, permit or insurance claims, warranties, guarantees, legal wording, testimonials or measurements that are not in a fact or decision node. Unknown is not safe: an unknown value becomes a `question` node or a fact with `status: open`, and the software shows a clearly labelled missing state only on the output that needs it.
